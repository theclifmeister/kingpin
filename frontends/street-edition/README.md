# Street Edition

An alternative illustrated browser frontend for Kingpin, tracked by #383 (named Ink & Ambition until #409, after its art style; renamed for the street the game is played on, and the paper it reads like). Paper, navy ink, coral, teal and gold surround a hand-drawn city. The original Go engine runs in WebAssembly; all actions go through the JSON-RPC session.

## Build and play

Requirements: Python 3.11+, the Go version declared in the repository's `go.mod`, and Node 20+ for the smoke check. Run from the repository root:

```sh
python3 frontends/street-edition/build.py
node frontends/street-edition/smoke.mjs
python3 -m http.server 8080 --bind 127.0.0.1 --directory frontends/street-edition/dist
```

Open http://127.0.0.1:8080. `KINGPIN_GO=/path/to/go` selects a Go executable. The generated `dist/` folder can be served by any static host; there is no server-side game process. Generated WASM, shared JS, loader and derived content are not committed. The builder refuses a different protocol/view contract until this frontend is reviewed. It copies `session.js`, `alerts.js`, `police.js` and `lieutenants.js` from the reference web client, and reads front roles, trait descriptions and three crew numbers no rule serves (a blank investigation's loyalty, a war's enforcers, the sloppy skill) and a cop's price and accuracy from the same checkout's TOML.

## Deploying on Vercel

The site that plays is `dist/`, which the build makes; `src/` alone hangs on "Opening the city ledger…", because it has no engine or helpers. `vercel.json` has Vercel run `vercel-build.sh`, which fetches the Go version `go.mod` names (Vercel's build image has no Go), picks a Python 3.11+ and runs `build.py`, then serves `dist/`. The build stamps the commit from `VERCEL_GIT_COMMIT_SHA`, because the checkout has no `.git`. Set the project's root directory to `frontends/street-edition` and keep "Include files outside the root directory in the Build Step" on (Vercel's default), because the build compiles the engine from the whole repository (#411).

## Play

Start on seed 41 as the default character; a new story picks any of the six characters the engine lists, each with its start. Buy stock in Market, queue sales, preview tonight and end the day. Your story offers new games, export and import. Saves stay in this browser under `kingpin-street-v1` (a save still under the old `kingpin-ink-v1` is read and moved over); exported `.gob` saves move between the browser and the other engine frontends. Loading failures retain the existing saved bytes. There are no cloud saves or multiplayer.

## Controls

- Streets: city travel, corner assignment/abandonment, enforcer strikes, police tips (confirmed with the police's attention, the raid and arrest lines, the trust, the page's odds and a broken peace, #552) and undercutting.
- Market: purchases, max-buy capacity, each lot's quality (amber under the street's), the lab (`src/lab.js`, #557): cook a batch with the chemist (the products a chemist makes, the precursors against a connect's price, the quality, batch and days the chemist and the lab give, blank a batch held to the room and the till) and cut a lot (the most the product, the room and the till allow, blank the most, what the lot becomes with the chemist's hand), the lots on their way, reviewed restocking, queued sales, private buyers, routes (each on its dial saying why it sends nothing: the till, the stash, no target, shut or its target met, and its checkpoint or customs agent bought through a confirm with its price, cut, days and the risk the file knows, #552) and reviewed built-in operation presets. The connects, the market pane and the routes (`src/market.js`, `src/routes.js`, #582): the SUPPLIERS block (each connect's lot, what they have left today, the relationship and a note: frozen, waiting for a word, owed, a tip, out for the day, credit or cash only) with each connect's pane (temper, band, price against the street and at the next band, lot and small-lot premium, credit and debt, the door, missed payments and the rules); a buy from the connect you pick, for cash or on their book (the credit terms, the due day, what the book has left); a product's pane (range, glut, margin, the demand your corners serve, quality, cooking, a shock, ELSEWHERE and NOTES); each route's pane (dial, days and capacity, fare and seizure odds, targets, the road, the driver, the skies for the plane) with its target dialog (units or days, what the days mean today and the shortfall it sends) and its driver; the preset review in names with each change's estimate; and an upgrade's prerequisites by name. The routine (`src/routine.js`, #556): a product's standing order (blank keeps the whole stash every night, the approach, the edit words) and supply contract (keep at, what it brings in the morning, holding on the road, the room, where you stand without your carry, and the warning on leaving) on its row and in its Routine dialog, set, changed, cancelled and cleared there; tonight's orders are the engine's (the day's buys by hand, on credit and by contract this morning, each with its return; each order, each standing order selling tonight and each contract, with its take and a remove), and a sale says when what lands tonight comes after the sales.
- Crew: hire and fire, a chemist's hand (what a candidate would cook at; the best chemist's cook and cut; another waits), (the fire confirm names what it costs the rest, who it takes to the walk line and a war it can lose, #551), ask around (price and odds, the `talking` alert lands on it), pay-offs and bail with their costs, the pay dial's wages a day, `N of M` on the payroll, each member's post, lines, SNITCH mark and days laid up or jailed, a crew summary, assignments, veteran traits, city captains with nightly budgets, and lieutenants: a city to run, their temper once it shows, and the role's terms (cut, crew slots, tempers, the loyalty risk) where you hire and assign them (#455).
- Empire: the wash and the till (`src/wash.js`, #553): the till with its control (blank is the float, never over the rot line), the wash idle under it, a route waiting on a lot and the till that would save for it, the fronts the night is expected to shut on their upkeep, and what the wash takes tonight; each front's status and why (shut on unpaid upkeep, audited, opens tomorrow, idle under the till, its covered nights); each offer's terms, its lock (peak cash to go, or the asset it waits on) and a shut warning that only a confirm buys through; front roles, investment levels, upgrades, properties and assets (each asset's upkeep and heat floor); export lanes (what each waits on, or its capacity, order, rate abroad and loads out, and an order form) and trophies (owned, and the offers to buy with clean cash). Properties lists your houses (#581): the status, block, stash, rent and unpaid days, guard and robbery odds, with move stock (the drive's heat), guard and drop; and your assets with what each does, standing or shut, upkeep and heat floor.
- Rivals (`src/rivals.js`, `src/endings.js`, #554): the crown's card (what it waits on, each crew off the count with why and for how long, the last clock, or the reign's day and income); each faction's stance, line (temper, corners, muscle, the corner it eyes), what keeps it off the crown's count, trust and war against the crackdown line, its deals with their terms and days left, where you stand (a betrayal's days of distrust), allies, and the war on it; propose with the standard truce lengths and tribute cuts and the three split lines, each with the odds the dice use, the tribute's basis, the split's side, withdraw, and a confirm before tonight's proposal is replaced; declare war and call it off, each through a confirm naming the target, the odds, the heat, a deal it breaks and the taken-out line; offers worded by who pays whom with the days to answer, what each does and breaks; the rules and the lifetime; expansion warnings and confront scouts.
- Ledger: the law (`src/law.js`, #552): the chief and the DA (the chief's temper once seen, a favour owed and called in from the card, the days left in the term; the DA's ticket and the days to the election; the pressure and goodwill), the DA RACE block while the tickets take money (the vote's day, the odds per ticket, who sits, a row a city with what your money holds and the points it buys, what it costs; the `da_race` alert lands on it), the fund (a city, goodwill with its preview, blank keeping tonight's upkeep back, and the ticket and the money behind it while the race runs), the PAYOFFS card (the live deals and when each runs out, what a fixer hears, a bribe to the chief or the DA with what it is likely to do, a cop's word with how straight it is); offshore transfers and cash out (clean back to dirty at the quoted fee), each filled in with an amount that keeps tonight's upkeep back and confirmed with a warning when it would not, community funding, the ambitions with each step in words by its unit and the crews off the crown's count, the PLAN line with what reset the quiet days (also atop the paper), and the walk away (#554): what this morning would leave unsettled (the pages due, a reserve not yet landed), each way out with its terms, what it is short and what it scores, each confirm in its own words with the score and what is left behind. The ending screen leads with the score (the account over one plus the bodies) and tells the epilogue, the story and the money, the people and the city. The sweep offshore (`src/property.js`, #581): the line it keeps, tonight's move and fee, on and off.
- Paper: engine-ordered reports, leading stories, cash-flow reconciliation with the MONEY section's itemised lines and cash before→after, and 90 report editions.
- Persistent risk panel: a warrant marked, the LAW lines (the chief, the DA, the pressure here and elsewhere, a call-favour button while the chief owes one, #552), heat ladder with the other cities' heat, evidence (one file for every city), cash exposure, tonight's count (the pile the police will count after tonight's landings and wages, before the wash) and its heat, every danger alert in red and alert navigation; each alert lands on the tab that answers it (`src/landing.js`, #549) and a morning that opens on a danger rings a red toast.

A quiet day says who sells nothing (a lieutenant's corners too) and asks first when a handoff is queued (#552). Dilemmas display the engine's consequence chips, and a choice shows what happened. The day preview is explicitly an estimate and explains what it cannot predict. It requires a separate end-day action.

## Boundaries and artwork

This UI does not yet expose every engine command: undercover work is not included. Built-in presets can operate on routines in imported saves; custom saved presets remain a TUI-profile feature. Bayport reuses the city illustration. Optional, feature-detected WebMCP tools read state and navigate sections; they do not play turns. `?test=1` exposes a local browser test hook.

`src/assets/city.webp` is original AI-generated artwork created for this frontend in the accompanying development session. It is decorative: the labelled controls carry real game state. It is not a geographic map. Go's loader license is copied into the build output. No Sites project metadata or user playthrough saves are included.

See `VALIDATION.md` for the prototype's validation, and `docs/web.md` for the repository's reference client and protocol contract.
