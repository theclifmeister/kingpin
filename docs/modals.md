# Modals are one component

**Modals are one component** (#81, `ui/modal.go`): `modal(title, body []string, footer []binding)` is `min(width-4, 76)` wide, border included, for every modal and every step of one dialog, sits on body row 2 (one blank row under the title bar) centred, in a gold `DoubleBorder` with `Padding(0, 1)`, and draws the title in caps, a blank, the body cut line by line to its width (`modalTitled(rendered, body, footer)` is the same box with the title row handed in rendered: #157's stage prints its title in, and `modal` is one call to it) (never wrapped: the card wraps its prose first through `wrapLines`), a blank and a footer line of `k()` pairs; `modalFooter()` reads each mode's footer off the key table's `modeBindings` (`modeKeys`; confirmations `y <verb>` / `esc close`, any other key still declining: the fourteen yes-or-no confirmations (a new run, a slot deleted, a fire, an upgrade, asking around, a pay-off, a trip, a house dropped, a scout, a boost, a tip, a checkpoint, bail, a deed) are one `modeConfirm` with a payload, `confirm{verb, view, act, back}` on `Model.cfm` set by each `askX` through `ask(verb, view, act)` (`back` is `modeStart` for the slot deletion, `modePlay` otherwise), so `handleKey` and `View` have one arm each and the key table one `y <verb>` row whose label reads the payload's verb (#242; the end of the day, fast-forward and buy off keep their own modes, taking `enter` or a number); pickers and dialogs `enter <verb>` / `esc close`, `q` still closing; the end of the day also takes `enter` and says so; the report, the card's outcome and help `enter esc close`) and `viewFooter` repeats it in the status bar while a modal is open. **Back is one key and close is one key** (#110): `esc` closes every modal whole, from any step of a dialog (`esc back` is nowhere); in the dialogs with steps (`keyDialog` for buy and sell, `keyTarget`, `keyCart`, `keyLab` for the cut and the cook (#47), the propose case of `handleKey`) `shift+tab` goes back a step keeping what the earlier steps hold (the product, the line or the kind stays under the cursor; a quantity is cleared on leaving its step and kept on coming back to it from the dial) and is silent on the first, and `tab` goes forward once the step is complete (a product you can buy or sell, a quantity that reads, a line or a kind under the cursor) and is silent otherwise and on the last step, where `enter` alone commits; the footer lists `⇧tab back` on every step past the first (`pastFirstStep`; the key column draws `⇧tab`, `shift+tab` being too wide at 80 columns). A modal with no pages (`Model.hasPages`: the confirmations, the pickers, the fund dialog while no campaign is open (#193: it has a second page, the campaign, while one is; `docs/law.md`), the card, the report, help and the overlay) leaves `tab` and `shift+tab` alone rather than closing on them; on the play screen they stay the next and the previous screen, one binding answering to both. `TestBackIsOneKey`, `TestTabBindingTakesShiftTab` and `TestTabIsSilentWhereThereIsNoPage` (`back_test.go`) pin it. **One stepper, one picker** (#243, `ui/modal.go`): every dialog with pages embeds `stepper{step, err}` (buy and sell, target, cart, move, cut and cook, bribe, fund, spy, exit, new run, the buy picker `frontPicker` and the propose dialog `proposeDialog`, the last two moved off the `Model`'s loose fields; `fireID` is `subjectID`), and `stepper.back(fieldAt)` is the one back rule: a step back blurs and clears the number field the step leaves and focuses the one it lands on, keeping what the earlier steps chose (the product, the line, the kind, the city; the new run's seed too, being nobody's step's), so the cart's, the bribe's and the campaign's fields clear on leaving as the buy's always did; `closes(key)` is `esc` and `q`, and every dialog and picker closes on either from any step (the new run to the start menu). The six one-page pickers (post, strike, undercut, assign, guard, driver) share one `picker{cursor, role}` on the `Model` (`pick`; each `askX` sets the cursor as it opens), one handler `pickerKey(key, rows, pick)` (`↑↓` and `j` `k` move within the rows, `1-9` select and commit, `enter` commits, `esc` and `q` close) and one modal `pickerModal(title, head, cols, cells, cursor, notes...)` (the head lines, the table with the cursor kept in view by `modalFollow`, a blank and the notes), which the spy's two lists and the buy picker's front page draw through too. The key table reads the open dialog's state through `paged` (`page()`, `field()`: the page it is on and the number field on it, nil off one; `openPaged()` returns the state for the mode, the number-only dialogs and the picker included), so `modalStep` and `numberStep` are one line each and the field's four rows, `⇧tab back` and `esc close` are listed for whatever dialog is open (`binding.dialogs`) rather than by mode list; `labList` and `labNumber` are `step(0)` and `step(1)`. Every modal page renders byte-identical to before (a capture diff of `TestModalsFit`'s scripts at 80 and 120 columns). **Every number field is one helper** (#112, `ui/number.go`, `numberField`, a `textinput` with a `max` the caller sets before handing it a key or drawing it, and a width of its prompt plus its placeholder, because the input's `Width` counts the prompt and the cursor cell and a narrower field cuts the placeholder to `blank = …` (#234; `TestNumberField` checks every field it walks shows its placeholder whole)): the buy and sell quantities (`qtyMax`: the stash in the dialog's city, `maxBuy`), the cart's (`cartMax`: the order's stash; a buy's units today plus `maxBuy` where you stand), the route target's number (`targetMax`: `World.Capacity` at the far end, a target being stock to keep, not a shipment, or for a days target the days of demand that holds) and the fund's amount (`maxFund`: clean cash up to what fills goodwill), its campaign page's (`maxBack`, #193: the clean cash left after the goodwill up to what fills the swing; blank is nothing there), the invest dialog's levels (#192, `investMax`: the levels left to the front's top that the clean cash pays for, at least one), the reserve dialog's amount (#195: the clean cash in hand; blank is a lot), and the cut's percent and the cook's units (`labMax`, #47: `cutMax`, the product's `cut_max` held to the room and the till; `cookMax`, the chemist's batch, the room with what is on its way counted and the till) take digits and backspace and nothing else (a letter never lands in a quantity, so `0` is the typed quantity that does not read and `tab` stays), `m` (`a` an unlisted alias) is max, `h` half of it rounded down, `↑↓` ±1 and `pgup pgdn` ±10, clamped to `[0, max]`; the field draws `/ 340 max` in `Subtle` after the number (`money` for the fund, nothing at a max of 0), a blank still means what it did, and typing is not clamped: a typed number over max is refused by the game as it always was (`Only 3 Weed in Eastside.`; a target over the capacity is set as it always was). The footer of every field lists `m max  h half  ↑↓ ±1  pgup pgdn ±10` before its `enter` (`numberStep` in `modeBindings`), one line at 80 columns; `TestNumberField` walks the six fields. A body taller than `modalRoom()` scrolls (`modalScroll`, reset when the mode changes; `↑↓`/`pgup`/`pgdn`/`j`/`k` through `scrollModal` where the modal has no cursor of its own, a picker keeping its cursor in view with `modalFollow`) and the footer ends in `↓ more` / `↑ more`. `richModel` in `ui_test.go` is the fixture with something behind every modal; `TestModalsFit` opens every mode of the enum from it (`modeCount` is the sentinel) at 80x24 and 120x40 and pins the width, the row, the footer and the status bar, and `TestReportScrolls` the scrolling. Bodies carry no key hints; the footer does. **A modal's rows are the pane's rows** (#236): every labelled line in a dialog body is `row(label, value)`, lowercase, the label at `paneLabelW`, never hand-padded or capitalised (`TestModalsFit` rejects a body line opening with a capitalised word padded to a value), and every dialog that spends (the buy, the fund and its campaign page, reserve, invest, pay a cop, bribe, buy off, the cook and the cut, and the front, house, asset and deed purchases) opens its rows with the cash in hand, `in hand     $452K dirty · $50K clean` (`inHand`), so the player reads the same row in the same place before every purchase and no dialog spells the cash its own way after an amount (the buy's `total` keeps `credit $X` beside it while the buy is on the book).
