# Modals are one component

**Modals are one component** (#81, `ui/modal.go`): `modal(title, body []string, footer []binding)` is `min(width-4, 76)` wide, border included, for every modal and every step of one dialog, sits on body row 2 (one blank row under the title bar) centred, in a gold `DoubleBorder` with `Padding(0, 1)`, and draws the title in caps, a blank, the body cut line by line to its width (`modalTitled(rendered, body, footer)` is the same box with the title row handed in rendered: #157's stage prints its title in, and `modal` is one call to it) (never wrapped: the card wraps its prose first through `wrapLines`), a blank and a footer line of `k()` pairs; `modalFooter()` reads each mode's footer off the key table's `modeBindings` (`modeKeys`; confirmations `y <verb>` / `esc close`, any other key still declining; pickers and dialogs `enter <verb>` / `esc close`, `q` still closing; the end of the day also takes `enter` and says so; the report, the card's outcome and help `enter esc close`) and `viewFooter` repeats it in the status bar while a modal is open. **Back is one key and close is one key** (#110): `esc` closes every modal whole, from any step of a dialog (`esc back` is nowhere); in the dialogs with steps (`keyDialog` for buy and sell, `keyTarget`, `keyCart`, `keyLab` for the cut and the cook (#47), the propose case of `handleKey`) `shift+tab` goes back a step keeping what the earlier steps hold (the product, the line or the kind stays under the cursor; a quantity is cleared on leaving its step and kept on coming back to it from the dial) and is silent on the first, and `tab` goes forward once the step is complete (a product you can buy or sell, a quantity that reads, a line or a kind under the cursor) and is silent otherwise and on the last step, where `enter` alone commits; the footer lists `⇧tab back` on every step past the first (`pastFirstStep`; the key column draws `⇧tab`, `shift+tab` being too wide at 80 columns). A modal with no pages (`Model.hasPages`: the confirmations, the pickers, the fund dialog while no campaign is open (#193: it has a second page, the campaign, while one is; `docs/law.md`), the card, the report, help and the overlay) leaves `tab` and `shift+tab` alone rather than closing on them; on the play screen they stay the next and the previous screen, one binding answering to both. `TestBackIsOneKey`, `TestTabBindingTakesShiftTab` and `TestTabIsSilentWhereThereIsNoPage` (`back_test.go`) pin it. **Every number field is one helper** (#112, `ui/number.go`, `numberField`, a `textinput` with a `max` the caller sets before handing it a key or drawing it): the buy and sell quantities (`qtyMax`: the stash in the dialog's city, `maxBuy`), the cart's (`cartMax`: the order's stash; a buy's units today plus `maxBuy` where you stand), the route target's number (`targetMax`: `World.Capacity` at the far end, a target being stock to keep, not a shipment, or for a days target the days of demand that holds) and the fund's amount (`maxFund`: clean cash up to what fills goodwill), its campaign page's (`maxBack`, #193: the clean cash left after the goodwill up to what fills the swing; blank is nothing there), the invest dialog's levels (#192, `investMax`: the levels left to the front's top that the clean cash pays for, at least one), and the cut's percent and the cook's units (`labMax`, #47: `cutMax`, the product's `cut_max` held to the room and the till; `cookMax`, the chemist's batch, the room with what is on its way counted and the till) take digits and backspace and nothing else (a letter never lands in a quantity, so `0` is the typed quantity that does not read and `tab` stays), `m` (`a` an unlisted alias) is max, `h` half of it rounded down, `↑↓` ±1 and `pgup pgdn` ±10, clamped to `[0, max]`; the field draws `/ 340 max` in `Subtle` after the number (`money` for the fund, nothing at a max of 0), a blank still means what it did, and typing is not clamped: a typed number over max is refused by the game as it always was (`Only 3 Weed in Eastside.`; a target over the capacity is set as it always was). The footer of every field lists `m max  h half  ↑↓ ±1  pgup pgdn ±10` before its `enter` (`numberStep` in `modeBindings`), one line at 80 columns; `TestNumberField` walks the six fields. A body taller than `modalRoom()` scrolls (`modalScroll`, reset when the mode changes; `↑↓`/`pgup`/`pgdn`/`j`/`k` through `scrollModal` where the modal has no cursor of its own, a picker keeping its cursor in view with `modalFollow`) and the footer ends in `↓ more` / `↑ more`. `richModel` in `ui_test.go` is the fixture with something behind every modal; `TestModalsFit` opens every mode of the enum from it (`modeCount` is the sentinel) at 80x24 and 120x40 and pins the width, the row, the footer and the status bar, and `TestReportScrolls` the scrolling. Bodies carry no key hints; the footer does.
