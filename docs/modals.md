# Modals are one component

**Modals are one component** (#81, `ui/modal.go`): `modal(title, body []string, footer []binding)` is `min(width-4, 76)` wide, border included, for every modal and every step of one dialog, sits on body row 2 (one blank row under the title bar) centred, in a gold `DoubleBorder` with `Padding(0, 1)`, and draws the title in caps, a blank, the body cut line by line to its width (`modalTitled(rendered, body, footer)` is the same box with the title row handed in rendered: #157's stage prints its title in, and `modal` is one call to it) (never wrapped: the card wraps its prose first through `wrapLines`), a blank and a footer line of `k()` pairs; `modalFooter()` reads each mode's footer off the key table's `modeBindings` (`modeKeys`; confirmations `y <verb>` / `esc close`, any other key still declining: the fourteen yes-or-no confirmations (a new run, a slot deleted, a fire, an upgrade, asking around, a pay-off, a trip, a house dropped, a scout, a boost, a tip, a checkpoint, bail, a deed) are one `modeConfirm` with a payload, `confirm{verb, view, act, back}` on `Model.cfm` set by each `askX` through `ask(verb, view, act)` (`back` is `modeStart` for the slot deletion, `modePlay` otherwise), so the mode table (`modes.go`, #274) has one row for it and the key table one `y <verb>` row whose label reads the payload's verb (#242; the end of the day, fast-forward and buy off keep their own modes, taking `enter` or a number); pickers and dialogs `enter <verb>` / `esc close`, `q` closing too, from every step (`closes`, #241); the end of the day also takes `enter` and says so; the report, the card's outcome and help `enter esc close`) and `viewFooter` repeats it in the status bar while a modal is open.
**Back is one key and close is one key** (#110): `esc` closes every modal whole, from any step of a dialog (`esc back` is nowhere); in the dialogs with steps (`keyDialog` for buy and sell, in `ui/dialogs.go`, whose `viewDialog` draws one block a step reached through `connectStepRows`, `quantityRows`, `buyTermsRows`, `sellDialRows` and `sellRepeatRows` (#275; the connect helpers are in `suppliers.go`, `dialCells` in `dial.go`), `keyTarget`, `keyCart`, `keyLab` for the cut and the cook (#47), `keyPropose`) `shift+tab` goes back a step keeping what the earlier steps hold (the product, the line or the kind stays under the cursor; a quantity is cleared on leaving its step and kept on coming back to it from the dial) and is silent on the first, and `tab` goes forward once the step is complete (a product you can buy or sell, a quantity that reads, a line or a kind under the cursor) and is silent otherwise and on the last step, where `enter` alone commits; the footer lists `⇧tab back` on every step past the first (`pastFirstStep`; the key column draws `⇧tab`, `shift+tab` being too wide at 80 columns).
A modal with no pages (`Model.hasPages`, the mode table's `pages` column (#274): the confirmations, the pickers, the fund dialog while no campaign is open (#193: it has a second page, the campaign, while one is; `docs/law.md`), the spy dialog with one faction on the ground (#272: with two or more it has the factions' page before the crew's, `TestSpyDialogTurnsItsPages`), the card, the report, help and the overlay) leaves `tab` and `shift+tab` alone rather than closing on them; on the play screen they stay the next and the previous screen, one binding answering to both.
`TestBackIsOneKey`, `TestTabBindingTakesShiftTab` and `TestTabIsSilentWhereThereIsNoPage` (`back_test.go`) pin it.
**One stepper, one picker** (#243, `ui/modal.go`): every dialog with pages embeds `stepper{step, err}` (buy and sell, target, cart, move, cut and cook, bribe, fund, spy, exit, new run, the buy picker `frontPicker` and the propose dialog `proposeDialog`, the last two moved off the `Model`'s loose fields; `fireID` is `subjectID`), and `stepper.back(fieldAt)` is the one back rule: a step back blurs and clears the number field the step leaves and focuses the one it lands on, keeping what the earlier steps chose (the product, the line, the kind, the city; the new run's seed too, being nobody's step's), so the cart's, the bribe's and the campaign's fields clear on leaving as the buy's always did; `closes(key)` is `esc` and `q`, and every dialog and picker closes on either from any step (the new run to the start menu).
The six one-page pickers (post, strike, undercut, assign, guard, driver) share one `picker{cursor, role}` on the `Model` (`pick`; each `askX` sets the cursor as it opens), one handler `pickerKey(key, rows, pick)` (`↑↓` and `j` `k` move within the rows, `1-9` select and commit, `enter` commits, `esc` and `q` close) and one modal `pickerModal(title, head, cols, cells, cursor, notes...)` (the head lines, the table with the cursor kept in view by `modalFollow`, a blank and the notes), which the spy's two lists and the buy picker's front page draw through too.
The key table reads the open dialog's state through `paged` (`page()`, `field()`: the page it is on and the number field on it, nil off one; `openPaged()` returns the state for the mode, the `paged` column of the mode table (#274), the number-only dialogs and the picker included), so `modalStep` and `numberStep` are one line each and the field's four rows, `⇧tab back` and `esc close` are listed for whatever dialog is open (`binding.dialogs`) rather than by mode list; `labList` and `labNumber` are `step(0)` and `step(1)`.
**One one-field dialog** (#275, `ui/amount.go`): the six dialogs that are one page and one number field (invest, reserve, pay a cop, buy off, fast-forward, restock: `modeInvest`, `modeReserve`, `modePayCop`, `modeConfirmBuyOff`, `modeConfirmFast`, `modeRestock`, #356) are one `amountDialog{numberField; subject; err}` on one `Model.amt`, only one being open at a time (`subject` is the invest dialog's front id and the restock's city, `""` for the rest); each `askX` opens it through `openAmount(mode, placeholder, max, money, subject)`, each `keyX` is one call to `keyAmount(k, max, commit)` (the error cleared on any key, `esc` and `q` close, `enter` commits, the rest to the field with `max()` set first), and each view draws `amountField(max)`, the field with the max the world gives it now.
Their keys, messages and views are byte-identical to the five structs they replace.
The list keys are two helpers beside `clamp` (`ui/format.go`): `stepCursor(&cur, ±1, n)` is `↑↓` and `j` `k` on a list that stops at its ends (the card, the pickers, the buy dialog's connect and product steps, the target's product, the cart, the move, the lab, the exit, the front picker's offers, the spy's two lists, the propose dialog), `wrapCursor` the two that go round (the start menu, the new run's first page), and `digit(key)` is `1-9` as the row it picks (every picker's select-and-commit); the bribe's two rows and the front picker's kind page keep their own `max`/`min`, which differ from `stepCursor` for a cursor past the end.
Every modal page renders byte-identical to before (a capture diff of `TestModalsFit`'s scripts at 80 and 120 columns).
**One rule per gesture** (#241): `i` is information (investigate, intel, scout; invest is `u` on the ledger, beside `u buy upgrade`: spend on a level); `t` is tip (cut is `%` on the market, the dialog asking a percent, since `k` is the cursor's; assign is `l` on the crew screen, the lieutenant's, shadowing `l lie low` there as `b bail` shadows `b buy`); every number dialog commits on `enter`, fast-forward and buy off included, and `y` is a confirmation's yes and nothing else; every picker takes `1-9` as select-and-commit and lists `1-9 choose` (the bribe's `1-2`, the new run's `1-6`, the walk-away's, the spy's two lists and the propose dialog too); `←→` turns any dial or toggle inside a modal (`h` and `l` too where the page has no number field: the fund's amount page keeps `h` for half and takes `←→` alone, no longer `[ ]`), the undercut is a `←→` dial like the sale's with `1-3` its notches, and `[ ]` turns the tab on the screens (city, faction); `esc` and `q` close every modal from every step (help closes on any key); the walk-away confirmation declines on any other key; `enter` no longer doubles `u` on the upgrades screen nor `b` and `r` on the ledger (`b` opens the picker on its kind page wherever the ledger's cursor sits; a route's dial is the map's `r`), and `a` is no alias for max.
`TestBackIsOneKey` presses `q` on every dialog's last step and `TestPickersTakeDigits` walks every picker.
**Every number field is one helper** (#112, `ui/number.go`, `numberField`, a `textinput` with a `max` the caller sets before handing it a key or drawing it, and a width of its prompt plus its placeholder, because the input's `Width` counts the prompt and the cursor cell and a narrower field cuts the placeholder to `blank = …` (#234; `TestNumberField` checks every field it walks shows its placeholder whole)): the buy and sell quantities (`qtyMax`: the stash in the dialog's city, `maxBuy`), the cart's (`cartMax`: the order's stash; a buy's units today plus `maxBuy` where you stand), the route target's number (`targetMax`: `World.Capacity` at the far end, a target being stock to keep, not a shipment, or for a days target the days of demand that holds) and the fund's amount (`maxFund`: clean cash up to what fills goodwill), its campaign page's (`maxBack`, #193: the clean cash left after the goodwill up to what fills the swing; blank is nothing there), the invest dialog's levels (#192, `investMax`: the levels left to the front's top that the clean cash pays for, at least one), the reserve dialog's amount (#195: the clean cash in hand; blank is a lot), and the cut's percent and the cook's units (`labMax`, #47: `cutMax`, the product's `cut_max` held to the room and the till; `cookMax`, the chemist's batch, the room with what is on its way counted and the till) take digits and backspace and nothing else (a letter never lands in a quantity, so `0` is the typed quantity that does not read and `tab` stays), `m` is max (#241: no alias, `a` being accept and abandon), `h` half of it rounded down, `↑↓` ±1 and `pgup pgdn` ±10, clamped to `[0, max]`; the field draws `/ 340 max` in `Subtle` after the number (`money` for the fund, nothing at a max of 0), a blank still means what it did (`numberField.Read(blank)` reads it, #275: `blank` for a blank, `Enter a whole number above zero.` for text that does not read or reads as zero; `readQty(field, max)` adds `Nothing to do.` for a blank with nothing allowed, and the fund's campaign page, whose blank is nothing and which takes 0, keeps `backAmount`), and typing is not clamped: a typed number over max is refused by the game as it always was (`Only 3 Weed in Eastside.`; a target over the capacity is set as it always was).
The footer of every field lists `m max  h half  ↑↓ ±1  pgup pgdn ±10` before its `enter` (`numberStep` in `modeBindings`), one line at 80 columns; `TestNumberField` walks the six fields.
A body taller than `modalRoom()` scrolls (`modalScroll`, reset when the mode changes; `↑↓`/`pgup`/`pgdn`/`j`/`k` through `scrollModal` where the modal has no cursor of its own, a picker keeping its cursor in view with `modalFollow`) and the footer ends in `↓ more` / `↑ more`.
`richModel` in `ui_test.go` is the fixture with something behind every modal; `TestModalsFit` opens every mode of the enum from it (`modeCount` is the sentinel) at 80x24 and 120x40 and pins the width, the row, the footer and the status bar, and `TestReportScrolls` the scrolling.
Bodies carry no key hints; the footer does.
**A modal's rows are the pane's rows** (#236): every labelled line in a dialog body is `row(label, value)`, lowercase, the label at `paneLabelW`, never hand-padded or capitalised (`TestModalsFit` rejects a body line opening with a capitalised word padded to a value), and every dialog that spends (the buy, the fund and its campaign page, reserve, invest, pay a cop, bribe, buy off, the cook and the cut, and the front, house, asset and deed purchases) opens its rows with the cash in hand, `in hand     $452K dirty · $50K clean` (`inHand`), so the player reads the same row in the same place before every purchase and no dialog spells the cash its own way after an amount (the buy's `total` keeps `credit $X` beside it while the buy is on the book).
**The buy says what it leaves** (#356, `afterRow`): under the buy's `total` is `after       $8.9M dirty · room 460/810 in Eastside`, the cash (or `of their book` on credit) and the stash as they would stand after the typed quantity, each `Bad` when the buy is over it, so a capacity refusal is never one the player could not see coming. A quantity refused for the room, the cash, the connect's day or their book (`game.ErrNoRoom`, `ShortError`, `ErrSupplierCapacity`, `ErrCreditLimit`) is set to what fits (`maxBuy`), the error line saying so (`Can only hold 40 more units in Eastside; the quantity is now 40, what fits.`), and the next enter buys it (`TestBuyOffersWhatFits`); `TestModalsFit` opens the buy past the room and the restock.
**The end of the day previews the night** (#353): the END THE DAY? modal's body is the day's preview (`ui/daypreview.go`, `docs/cart.md`): what needs you first, then the cart, then tonight's money as a flow table, estimated.
It scrolls like the report (`keyConfirmEnd` hands the scroll keys to `scrollModal`). `[ ] alert` and `o open alert` pick and open one of its alerts as the dashboard's do (`previewHasAlerts`), and any other key but `y` and `enter` still declines.
**The presets dialog** (#357, `ui/presets.go`, `modePresets`, `docs/presets.md`) is one more with pages: a picker's list (`↑↓`, `1-9` select and commit to the review, `enter`) and the review, a body that scrolls with `↑↓` under `enter apply`; `s` and `x` on the list save and delete a preset of your own, and `enter` on the review with nothing to change says so and stays.
