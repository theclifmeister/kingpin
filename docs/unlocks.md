**Unlocks are announced** (#148, `events.Unlocked`, `sim/news` UNLOCKED, `ui/unlocks.go`, `ui/fast.go`). Every gate the game keeps behind a line fires one `Unlocked{Day, Gate, ID, Name, City, Why, Price, Cost}` the morning it opens, from the sim that owns the gate and never the news sim: the market for a product listed (`Gate: "product"`, in every city at once, `Why` `peak cash $3,000`, `Price` its base price; was `ProductUnlocked`) and a connect who will deal with you (`"connect"`, in their `City`, `Why` `peak cash $25K and Cass at 60`, `market.unlockWhy`; was `SupplierUnlocked`), the laundering sim for a front's offer opening (`"front"`, `Cost`; `laundering.Sim.announce`, **last in its step**, reading the line against the peak the clock is about to stamp, `max(Stats.PeakCash, Cash())`, because nothing after the laundering sim moves cash, so the report line, the headline and the ledger's `open to you` are the same morning and a front bought that morning was announced; `World.Laundering.Offered map[string]bool` fires it once, a front already owned when its line is first read stamped silently, `TestFrontOpensTheMorningTheLedgerSays`), and the crew sim for a role that joins the pool (`"role"`: the accountant the morning `rolesFor` first holds a front, `Why` `a front owned`, the lieutenant the morning `LieutenantsWanted` first holds, `corners in two cities`; `crew.Sim.announce` at the top of its step, `World.Crew.Offered`). The field is `Gate`, not `Kind`, because `Kind()` is the event's. Zero values are today's state, so no schema bump, and a save from before the fields catches up the first morning it is stepped. The product and the connect fire the morning after the peak that crosses their line is stamped, as they always did (the thing itself lists then); the front and the roles the morning the thing opens. **The news sim** puts one line per gate in the report's **UNLOCKED** section (`DayReport.Unlocked`, drawn by `viewReport` after #147's TIER and before PRICES, `news.unlockLine`, every line under 70 characters so it fits the modal at 80 columns: `Heroin is on offer, around $400.00 a unit: peak cash $3,000.`, `Designer is on offer in Bayport only: the road brings it here.` where the supplier where you stand does not sell it, `The Laundromat is open to you on the ledger screen (7): $25K.`, `The Dutchman will deal with you now in Bayport: peak cash $500K.`, `Lieutenants want work on the crew screen (4): corners in two cities.`) and a headline under the **`unlock` source** (in `journalSources`, the ledger's gold; neither notoriety nor the law counts it, `headline_sources` unchanged), keyed `Unlocked` + the gate capitalised (`UnlockedProduct`, `UnlockedFront`, `UnlockedConnect`, `UnlockedRole`, all in `TestEveryEmittedEventHasTemplate`);
