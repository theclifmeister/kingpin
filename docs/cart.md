# The cart is the day's shopping

**The cart is the day's shopping** (#103, `ui/cart.go`, `game/actions.go`). The buy and sell dialogs stay open after a buy or an order (`nextLine`: back on the product step, the quantity cleared, the CART block under the table; `esc` closes the dialog from any step and `shift+tab` goes back one, #110), so several lines go in one visit. **The trade is one dialog** (#168): `s` on the buy dialog's product step (or its connect step) and `b` on the sell dialog's turn it to the other side in place (`switchSide`: `modeBuy` ↔ `modeSell`, no new mode; the product stays under the cursor, the quantity, repeat and pay are cleared as `shift+tab` clears them, a buy's connect step is re-entered only where it has one, the sale's dial is seeded as opening would seed it, the CART block stays, and a side that cannot open, lying low or no connect dealing, is the dialog's error line, `cannotOpen`, never a close), the footer listing the other side's key on that step alone (`s sell` / `b buy` in `modeBindings`, gated `buyList` / `step(0)`); on a `numberField` step the field ignores the letters, so a typed quantity is never lost; and where the turn changes the city the dialog is about (a buy is where you stand, or the shown city where a lieutenant runs it for you, #174; a sale in the shown city) the first body line says so, `Selling in Bayport.` / `Buying in Eastside.` in `Subtle` (`dialog.turned`, `sideLine`; `TestDialogTogglesSide`, `TestDialogToggleRefusalStays`, `TestDialogToggleNamesTheCity`, and `TestBuyThenSellFlow` turns rather than closing). The cart is UI over the world's per-day scratch, `w.Buys` and `w.Orders`, and its standing orders, `w.Standing` (#114), nothing new saved and no schema bump: `cartLines` is every buy made today merged per city and product (units, the price paid a unit, the cost), then every sell order in city and ladder order (units, dial, the expected take and heat from `orderEstimate`, the sell dialog's expect and heat rows), a standing order standing in for the order of the day where you placed none and not on a lie-low day (a line marked `standing`, edited like an order through `placeLine`, `x` cancelling it for good), `totals` the bottom line and `cartSummary` the sentence the END THE DAY? modal opens with (`Buying 3 lines for $12K, selling 2 lines (1 standing), ~$30K, +4.2 heat.`, `endDayLine`; lying low still wins, and an empty cart reads `No sales queued.`). It is drawn as one `table()` (`cartCols`: `line product city units price dial cash heat`, a buy's dial and heat and an order's price blank) in the dialogs' CART block (`cartBlock`, at the bottom of the body; the `Pick a product.` line goes once the cart has lines), and as the first section of the dashboard's and the market's pane (`cartSection`: the totals first, so the strip at 80 columns carries them, then one row a line, `in <city>` under a line elsewhere, and `c  edit: quantity, dial, remove`), nothing while the cart is empty. `c` on the dashboard and the market opens it as the cart modal (`modeCart`, `keyCart`, `viewCart`; on the map `c` still posts a runner, and `pointer` spells a two-screen pointer `Cart on the dashboard screen (1) or the market screen (2).`): `↑↓` pick a line, `enter` opens a quantity for it (`setCartQty`: an order is placed again through `PlaceSell`; a buy is returned down to it, or added to with `Buy` where you stand or through the lieutenant who runs its city, #174, refused elsewhere), `←→` and `1-3` turn an order's dial (`PlaceSell` at the same units), `x` removes the line (`CancelSell`, or `Return` whole), `esc` closes from either step and `shift+tab` returns the quantity step to the line list; the footer is `modeBindings` gated by `cartHasLines` and `cartOnSell`; an empty cart says `Nothing in the cart.`. **A return is the exact inverse of a buy**: `Purchase.Prior` records the supplier price the buy found, `World.Bought(city, product)` is what today's receipts hold, and `World.Return(city, product, qty)` takes the last buy first, refunds what `Buy` charged for the units (`Cost` less what it would charge for those kept, so a part is proportional and the whole exact), walks the supplier price back to `Prior` scaled by what is kept (read off the price, never the pressure), takes the units out of the stash (refused with `ErrReturnGone` when they are no longer there, e.g. on the road, and the modal and the status bar both say so), `BoughtToday` down and the receipt off `w.Buys` (a returned buy is never reported: it never happened); `ErrNothingBought` once the day has ended and the scratch is gone. `w.Buy` keeps its signature and behaviour and no harness policy returns anything, so no pinned number moved (`game/cart_test.go`, `ui/cart_test.go`; `TestModalsFit` opens the cart and the dialogs with a full cart through `fillCart`, and `TestTablesAreConsistent` reads the CART table, a dial column now taking a bare `quiet` / `aggr.` as it did `normal`). **The dialogs and the cart show the price delta and the margin** (#138, `ui/market.go` `priceFacts`, `facts`, `priceLine`; `ui/dialogs.go` `dialogRows`, `priceRows`): the product step of both dialogs carries the market table's `Δ` and spark after the price (a buy's `product price/unit street margin Δ Nd have`, the margin signed over the supplier and `-` where the supplier does not sell it; a sale's `product price/unit Δ Nd have demand/day`; a column the modal has no room for is dropped from the right, never wrapped), the quantity and dial steps carry one `Subtle` line under the quantity (`price      $38 · +6% today · range 30d $30 – $45` for a sale, `supplier   $21 · street $38 · margin +82%` for a buy, and `shock      ×1.40, 3 days more` on a row of its own while one runs, as the pane names it), the cart's table has a `Δ` column after `price` for the sell lines (a buy's `-`, as its dial and heat are) and the cart modal carries the same line for the selected line under its quantity; every number comes off one `priceFacts` (the market table's `Δ` and spark cells, the market pane's `range 30d`, `margin +82% over supplier` and shock rows and the dashboard pane's `price` and `supplier` rows read it too; in the buy dialog the facts are read against the chosen connect's price, `factsAt` through `connectFacts`, #72, so the row and the line are what this buy pays and makes, `-` and `not from them` where the connect does not deal in it), so the dialog, the cart and the pane cannot disagree (`TestDialogShowsTheDelta`, `TestDialogPriceMatchesPane` in `ui/delta_test.go`).
