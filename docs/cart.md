# The cart is the day's shopping

**The cart is the day's shopping** (#103, `ui/cart.go`, `game/actions.go`).
The buy and sell dialogs stay open after a buy or an order (`nextLine`: back on the product step, the quantity cleared, the CART block under the table; `esc` closes the dialog from any step and `shift+tab` goes back one, #110), so several lines go in one visit.
**Where the dialog opens** (#462, `productInView`): `b` and `s` on the dashboard and the market open on the product their table has selected (the two tables share `Model.cursor`, and the pane's `s  sell ~N` reads it), and the title names it, `SELL · EASTSIDE · PILLS`, `BUY · EASTSIDE · CASS · WEED`, following the cursor as it moves (the connect step, before the product, names none); from any other screen, where no product table shows a selection, the dialog opens on its first row (a sale then lands on the first product held there), never on what another screen left selected: a playtest sold Pills for Weed and bought Coke for Pills off a selection the screen did not show (`TestDialogOpensWhereItSays`). A product kept by contract still opens at `keep at` (#443, the `Editing the contract` warning), now under a title naming it.

**The trade is one dialog** (#168): `s` on the buy dialog's product step (or its connect step) and `b` on the sell dialog's turn it to the other side in place (`switchSide`: `modeBuy` ↔ `modeSell`, no new mode; the product stays under the cursor, the quantity, repeat and pay are cleared as `shift+tab` clears them, a buy's connect step is re-entered only where it has one, the sale's dial is seeded as opening would seed it, the CART block stays, and a side that cannot open, lying low or no connect dealing, is the dialog's error line, `cannotOpen`, never a close), the footer listing the other side's key on that step alone (`s sell` / `b buy` in `modeBindings`, gated `buyList` / `step(0)`); on a `numberField` step the field ignores the letters, so a typed quantity is never lost; and where the turn changes the city the dialog is about (a buy is where you stand, or the shown city where a lieutenant runs it for you, #174; a sale in the shown city) the first body line says so, `Selling in Bayport.` / `Buying in Eastside.` in `Subtle` (`dialog.turned`, `sideLine`; `TestDialogTogglesSide`, `TestDialogToggleRefusalStays`, `TestDialogToggleNamesTheCity`, and `TestBuyThenSellFlow` turns rather than closing).
The cart is UI over the world's per-day scratch, `w.Today.Buys` and `w.Today.Orders`, and its standing orders, `w.Standing` (#114), nothing new saved and no schema bump: `cartLines` is every buy made today merged per city and product (units, the price paid a unit, the cost), then every sell order in city and ladder order (units, dial, the expected take and heat from `orderEstimate`, the sell dialog's expect and heat rows), a standing order standing in for the order of the day where you placed none and not on a lie-low day (a line marked `standing`, edited like an order through `placeLine`, `x` cancelling it for good), `totals` the bottom line and `cartSummary` the sentence the END THE DAY? modal opens with (`Buying 3 lines for $12K, selling 2 lines (1 standing), ~$30K, +4.2 heat.`, `endDayLine`; lying low still wins, and an empty cart reads `No sales queued.`).
It is drawn as one `table()` (`cartCols`: `line product city units price dial cash heat`, a buy's dial and heat and an order's price blank) in the dialogs' CART block (`cartBlock`, at the bottom of the body; the `Pick a product.` line goes once the cart has lines), and as the first section of the dashboard's and the market's pane (`cartSection`: the totals first, so the strip at 80 columns carries them, then one row a line, `in <city>` under a line elsewhere, and `c  edit: quantity, dial, remove`), nothing while the cart is empty.
`c` on the dashboard and the market opens it as the cart modal (`modeCart`, `keyCart`, `viewCart`; on the map `c` still posts a runner, and `pointer` spells a two-screen pointer `Cart on the dashboard screen (1) or the market screen (2).`): `↑↓` pick a line, `enter` opens a quantity for it (`setCartQty`: an order is placed again through `PlaceSell`; a buy is returned down to it, or added to with `Buy` where you stand or through the lieutenant who runs its city, #174, refused elsewhere), `←→` and `1-3` turn an order's dial (`PlaceSell` at the same units), `x` removes the line (`CancelSell`, or `Return` whole), `esc` closes from either step and `shift+tab` returns the quantity step to the line list; the footer is `modeBindings` gated by `cartHasLines` and `cartOnSell`; an empty cart says `Nothing in the cart.`.
**A return is the exact inverse of a buy**: `Purchase.Prior` records the supplier price the buy found, `World.Bought(city, product)` is what today's receipts hold, and `World.Return(city, product, qty)` takes the last buy first, refunds what `Buy` charged for the units (`Cost` less what it would charge for those kept, so a part is proportional and the whole exact), walks the supplier price back to `Prior` scaled by what is kept (read off the price, never the pressure), takes the units out of the stash (refused with `ErrReturnGone` when they are no longer there, e.g. on the road, and the modal and the status bar both say so), `BoughtToday` down and the receipt off `w.Today.Buys` (a returned buy is never reported: it never happened); `ErrNothingBought` once the day has ended and the scratch is gone.
`w.Buy` keeps its signature and behaviour and no harness policy returns anything, so no pinned number moved (`game/cart_test.go`, `ui/cart_test.go`; `TestModalsFit` opens the cart and the dialogs with a full cart through `fillCart`, and `TestTablesAreConsistent` reads the CART table, a dial column now taking a bare `quiet` / `aggr.` as it did `normal`).
**The dialogs and the cart show the price delta and the margin** (#138, `ui/market.go` `priceFacts`, `facts`, `priceLine`; `ui/dialogs.go` `dialogRows`, `priceRows`): the product step of both dialogs carries the market table's `Δ` and spark after the price (a buy's `product price street margin Δ Nd stash`, the margin signed over the supplier and `-` where the supplier does not sell it; a sale's `product price Δ Nd stash demand/day`; a column the modal has no room for is dropped from the right, never wrapped), the quantity and dial steps carry one `Subtle` line under the quantity (`price      $38 · +6% today · range 30d $30 – $45` for a sale, `supplier   $21 · street $38 · margin +82%` for a buy, and `shock      ×1.40, 3 days more` on a row of its own while one runs, as the pane names it), the cart's table has a `Δ` column after `price` for the sell lines (a buy's `-`, as its dial and heat are) and the cart modal carries the same line for the selected line under its quantity; every number comes off one `priceFacts` (the market table's `Δ` and spark cells, the market pane's `range 30d`, `margin +82% over supplier` and shock rows and the dashboard pane's `price` and `supplier` rows read it too; in the buy dialog the facts are read against the chosen connect's price, `factsAt` through `connectFacts`, #72, so the row and the line are what this buy pays and makes, `-` and `not from them` where the connect does not deal in it), so the dialog, the cart and the pane cannot disagree (`TestDialogShowsTheDelta`, `TestDialogPriceMatchesPane` in `ui/delta_test.go`).
**Restock fills the cart for the next days** (#356, `ui/restock.go`, `World.RestockPlan`, `World.StockLevels`): `R restock` on the market opens a one-field dialog (`modeRestock`, an `amountDialog` on the city a buy there would be in, refused where the buy is, `buyPointer`, `cannotOpen`) whose number is days of demand (blank is 2, up to 30) and whose body is the plan it would buy, redrawn as the number changes: a `product level have buy cost` table, the `total`, and the `after` row the buy dialog has.
The plan is `World.RestockPlan(city, days, keep)`: one line a product in ladder order, sized to the `StockLevels` level (days times `World.Demand`, the demand the corners you work there serve, cut to the stash's share by the market's demand where the levels together would overfill it) less what is stashed there and on the road to it, from the cheapest connect with units left after the lines before it, cut to the room and to the dirty cash over `keep` (the supply contracts' float, `market.Sim.Float`, through `Session.RestockPlan`) after the lines before it; a product nobody sells today or with nothing to buy has no line, and the plan changes nothing.
`StockLevels` is the stocked player's contract sizing moved out of `harness.Stocked` (`docs/supply-contracts.md`), so the policy and the screen share one sizing: `TestRestockMatchesStocked` compares the plan with what the stocked player's contracts would buy on the same world, product by product, and the stocked policy's run is byte-for-byte what it was.
Nothing is bought until `enter`, which buys the plan by hand, one `Session.Buy` a line, so the lines land in the cart as buys, each editable or returned there until the day ends (`Restocked 5 lines in Eastside for $28,261, in the cart until the day ends.`; a line refused on the way is named in the alarm); an empty plan is the dialog's error line (`Nothing to buy: the stash in Eastside is full.`).
`TestRestockFillsTheCart` pins it, `TestRestockPlan` the plan, `TestMaxBuyNeverRefused` that `World.MaxBuy` (the quantity field's max, `maxBuyFrom`) is never refused, and `TestErrNoRoomIsTyped` the room refusal, a `game.RoomError` (`Free`, `City`) that matches `ErrNoRoom`, from the buy, the cut and the cook alike.

**The day's preview is the END THE DAY? modal** (#353, `ui/daypreview.go`, `engine.Session.Preview`).
`enter` opens it, and `y` or `enter` there ends the day as before (`TestEnterDoesNotEndDay`); `n` still ends it at once, with no preview.
The body is `previewLines`, in this order:

- **What needs you.** The morning's alerts in the engine's order, each worded by `alertOf`, so the idle crew (`unposted`) and the corners nobody works (`idle_corner`) are among them. The selected one is marked `▸`: it is the dashboard's ALERTS cursor (`Model.alertCursor`) over the same list. `[ ] alert` picks one and `o open alert` goes where it is answered (`openAlert`, the alert's `Act`, #352), leaving the day unended. A pointer to a screen is never broken across a wrapped line (`wrapWhole`).
- **The cart.** The cart in a sentence (`endDayLine`), then each city's sales tonight (`Eastside: ~75 units sold, ~$3,006, +2.3 heat.`).
- **Tonight's money.** It is drawn as the report's cash flow (`moneyLines`), in its categories (`previewTable`, `tonight ~ dirty clean total`): `Now` (the piles as they stand), a row for each category the night is expected to move, and `Closing`, the projected piles.
- **What it cannot know.** `Estimates before the dice: robberies, the police, tomorrow's prices, audits, skims, the rivals and the crew's nights are not in them.` (`engine.PreviewUnknown`, worded by `unknownWords`).

The body scrolls (`scrollModal`); any key other than a scroll key goes back.
The lines are tonight's, and what the day has already paid (the cart's buys) is in `Now`.
So the morning's report and the preview meet at the closing: on a night the dice leave alone, they agree to the dollar (`TestPreviewAgreesWithAQuietNight`, `docs/engine.md`).
`TestEndDayShowsThePreview` pins the order, the closing and the scroll, `TestPreviewJumpsToAnAlert` the jump, and `TestModalsFit` opens it with a full cart.
Whether `n` should show the preview when something needs you is left open, as the issue left it.
