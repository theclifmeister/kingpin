# Diplomacy is the table

**Diplomacy is the table** (#32, `rivals.toml [diplomacy]` and `[deal.*]`, `game/diplomacy.go`, `sim/rivals/diplomacy.go`, `ui/diplomacy.go`): deals are state on the rival (`Rival.Trust` 0-100, `Rival.Deals`, `Rival.Betrayed`), offers are on the world (`World.Offers`), and everything is proposed, answered, kept and broken inside the rival's step. Three kinds ship: a **truce** (`Terms.Days`; no pushes, no undercutting, no tips, and it ends with `DealEnded` at the close of its last night), **tribute** (`Terms.PerDay`, dirty cash the rival step moves each night, `TributePaid`; it leaves every corner of yours alone until you stop paying; **the sum is a cut of the street the rival is in**, #162: `rivals.Sim.TributeBase(w)`, beside `Income`, is what the corners the player works at home move in a day in the products the rival deals in, the port's product (`NoSupply` at home) left out as `Income` leaves it out of the take, so a cut of it and the take are in one unit; `Favour` reads the terms as `PerDay / TributeBase`, `Sim.cut` / `Cut` turn a cut into a sum (two figures, never under `tribute_min`) for the opportunist's demand, the propose dialog and the diplomat, the propose dialog's tribute page says `Your street: $37K a day on your corners here in what they sell.` and the rivals pane on a tribute offered or holding reads `cut  ~10% of your street` over the same sentence (`tributeBaseLine`, `tributeRows`), and `World.StreetValue`, which counted every product, is gone; at equal ground the middle cut is `cut / margin`, 0.10 / 0.30 of the take, so `[deal.tribute]`'s bands did not move, and a fixed band could not have held: before designer unlocks the two bases agree, after it the old one was 3–13x the take by seed) and a **split** (`Terms.Corners`, the ids on the player's side of the line: the rival neither claims nor pushes on them, `Post` refuses a free corner past the line, and `World.SplitLines` are the three lines the UI offers). A deal holds on ticks before `Until` (`Deal.Live`), `World.Deal(kind)` asks about the coming tick, and `World.AtPeace` is a truce or a tribute. The joint shipment (`DealShipment`) is listed and refused with `ErrNoRoutes` until #30 lands; `joint_trust` is in the table for it. Player actions queue intent: `Propose(kind, terms)` (one a day, checked for shape; `Withdraw`), `Accept(id)` (moves the offer to `World.Accepted` scratch, sealed the next morning on exactly the offered terms) and `Decline(id)`; `Abandon` records the corner in `World.Abandoned` scratch so the step can notice a split corner walked off. The rival's answer is `rivals.Sim.Chance`: the kind's `base`, the terms' `Favour` (-1 the hardest ask to 1 the easiest: a short truce, a fat tribute, a modest line; a split past `split_fair + split_trust x trust` or over a rival corner is refused outright) times `terms`, `accept_trust` at full trust, the personality's `deal_bias`, `accept_war` at a full war, and `fear_deal` from `reputation.toml [effects]`; zero for `distrust_days` after a betrayal (`Sim.Distrusted`) or on a night the enforcers go in. The dialog shows that number and the dice use it. The rival offers too (`offer`, one on the table at a time, `offer_days` to answer, `personality.offer_chance` per day its situation calls for one): an expansionist that cannot pay its muscle `low_cash_days` asks for a truce, an opportunist with `upper_hand` times the guard on the front line demands tribute at the middle cut, a defensive one asks for the long truce once the war is loud, a chaotic one with a front line asks for the short one. Every night a deal holds earns `trust_kept` (times `Scale(respect, respect_trust)`) and, for a truce or a split, `respect.deal_kept` in the reputation sim, which reads `w.Rival.Deals` after the rival step has ended what ran out; a strike costs `force.X.trust`. **Betrayal** is a push or a hit under any deal (every deal breaks), a missed tribute or a split corner abandoned: trust to `betrayal_floor`, `Rival.Betrayed` stamped, one `RivalTippedPolice` that night whatever else happens, `DealBroken{By: "you"}`; a chaotic rival breaks deals at `personality.betrayal` per deal-night (`DealBroken{By: "rival"}`, its trust untouched), the others never. The crackdown ignores deals. `betrayal_spread` is in the table for Phase 4. Events: `DealOffered`, `DealAccepted`, `DealRefused`, `DealBroken` have headlines; `DealEnded` and `TributePaid` are report-only. Schema 8 seeds trust on a pre-#32 save (`Rivals.MigrateDiplomacy`, after #30's 6 -> 7 city step). The rival lives in the home city, so the split lines, the street a tribute is a cut of and the front line an opportunist weighs are all `w.Home()`'s. The rivals screen (8) is the panel (#87): `RIVALS · Eastside`, the leader line, the trust and war bars on one line, DEALS and OFFERS as `table()`s (`kind terms days who` / `kind terms answer by`) with the offer cursor and tonight's proposal under DEALS; the pane is the selected offer or, with none, the deal that holds or the mood line (`dealTitle`, `dealDoes`, `dealBreaks`, `moodLine`), then RULES (`dealRules`, wrapped whole) and LIFETIME (`rivalsDetails`); the empty states are `No deals. Press d to propose one.` and `Nothing on the table.`; `d` opens the two-page propose dialog (kind, then three terms with odds), `y` / `x` answer the selected offer; the dashboard's RIVALS panel carries trust and whatever holds or waits. `harness.Diplomat` and `diplomacy_test.go` pin the acceptance: a truce holds (no push, take, tip or undercut) and the rival is back the day after it lapses, the diplomat holds more ground than the passive player against an expansionist and runs cooler than a hit war, a defensive rival never breaks a deal over the horizon and a chaotic one does, offers lapse and seal as offered, a betrayal floors trust and nothing is accepted for `distrust_days`, the corner invariants hold through a split, and trust, deals and offers survive a save. The rival's offer roll is one more draw from the tick RNG on the days its situation calls for one, so pinned-seed tests that read a single day can shift when the rival's mood does (`TestHeldDemandIsServed` reads the run's peak).
