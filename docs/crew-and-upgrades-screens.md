# Crew and upgrades in the grammar

**Crew and upgrades in the grammar** (#86, `ui/crew.go`, `ui/upgrades.go`, `crew_test.go`, `upgrades_test.go`).
The crew screen's MAIN is `CREW · 3 of 10 on the payroll`, the pay line in the dial convention (`pay  stingy  [fair]  generous   $395/day`, `payRow`), the one warning line when there is one (`crewWarning`: the tell that somebody is talking, else a skim on record; MAIN truncates it, the pane's CREW section carries it whole) and the two tables, ON THE PAYROLL (name, role, skill, loyalty, wage, carry, post, hired; where MAIN is too narrow for the post to read whole, 64 columns beside the pane at 100, `carry` and then `hired` are dropped, the pane carrying both) and `LOOKING FOR WORK · new faces in 2 days` (fee instead of post and hired); nothing under them. `h` hires the face under the cursor and leaves the cursor in LOOKING FOR WORK on the face after it, so `h h h` hires down the list; with nobody left looking it lands on the last hire (`hireSelected`, #445, `TestHireKeepsTheCursorOnTheCandidates`; before, it jumped to the hire on the payroll and a second `h` did nothing). A fire keeps the cursor on its row of ON THE PAYROLL, the next member up under it, or the last member's row where the last was fired (`confirmFire`, #500: it stayed on the index, which after the last member was the first face LOOKING FOR WORK, and a tester hired the wrong person twice, fired both and lost 24 loyalty all round; `TestCrewCursorAfterHireAndFire`).
**Crew trouble is an alert** (#345): the loyalty bar is not the only place a line shows.

Pay them, or pay them off on the crew screen (4).` on the dashboard's ALERTS (the stop's words are the same sentence, `Deshawn is 4 loyalty from walking`, #522: `Fish 5 from skimming` read as a count; a member under a line reads `Wally at 29 loyalty, under the 30 line`, the roster's figure) (`crewLineAlert`; amber for the skim, red for the walk and a lieutenant's `turning`), and a fast-forward stops on it once a member a line (`engine.AlertCrewLine`, `docs/engine.md`).
Skimming suspected is an alert while the warning line is up, and stops once as it starts; the informant line has no alert, by design (`docs/snitching.md`).

The upgrades screen (#86, #120) is one branch at a time: MAIN is `UPGRADES · 9 of 56 owned · dirty $438K · clean $65K`, the branch tabs in the market's city-tab convention (`branchTabs`, `tabLine`: `Operations  [ Security ]  Legal  Crew  Laundering  Logistics  Street`, the shown branch bracketed and `Selected`; where the long names do not fit, as beside the pane at 100 columns, `branchShort` shortens them, `Ops  [ Security ]  Legal  Crew  Launder  Road  Street`, the way the title bar's tabs shorten), a blank, the shown branch as one `table()` (`node cost status`: the mark gutter `✓ ○ ·` as the node's state, the name indented one cell a level under the prerequisite it hangs from, the cost through `money()` (#237: a lump sum is `cost`, `kMoney`, on every table), and the status `owned` / `available` / `needs Burner phones` / `$3,000 short` through `upgradeStatus`, a clean-cash node's starting `clean · `, since a cash column holds only a figure; the node's name gives way to the width before the status does, #507), a blank and the legend `✓ owned  ○ available  · locked`.
`branchRows` lays a branch out as a tree: a node's parent is the deepest of its prerequisites in the branch (the later in the file between two as deep, so `cutouts` hangs from `ghosts`; a prerequisite in another branch, `safehouse` over `stash`, is the status column's business, not the layout's), the roots are the nodes with none, and children follow their parent in file order (`TestBranchReadsAsATree`), so the cursor walks a branch top to bottom and no branch is taller than eleven rows, which fits 80x24 with no paging.
`Model.branch` is the branch shown, an index into `content.Branches` and a view cursor like `Model.city` (never saved; `startRun` puts it back on Operations); `Model.upgradeCursor` is one node cursor a branch (`nodeCursor`, `upgradeSelected`), so a branch left and come back to is on the node it was on (`TestBranchCursorSticks`), while the screen is up: coming to it from another screen opens it on Operations' first node (`switchScreen`, #500: a branch remembered from the visit before turned `→ u y` into Burner phones, bought by accident; `TestPickersOpenFresh`); `←→` turn the branch round the end as the market's arrows turn the city (`upgradeMove`, the `←→ branch` binding, the tree's own), `↑↓` walk the branch's nodes (the tree is a `listScreens` screen since #120, the map alone listing `↑↓←→`), `u`/`enter` buy.
The pane (`upgradesDetails`) opens with the tree's cost string and the pool and where the node stands (`$12K dirty · needs Burner phones`, `available`, `owned`, `$3K short`; prerequisites too long for the line go on `needs` rows), then the description, the effects one per line (`effectWords`, one sentence a name of the vocabulary, a multiplier written by `times` with no trailing zero: `route risk ×0.8`, `wages ×0.9`, `+2 crew`; `TestEffectWordsCoverTheVocabulary` sets every field of `content.UpgradeEffects` alone through reflection and wants one sentence each), the clean-cash and fall-guy notes when they apply and `u  buy it for $12K dirty` when it can be bought, then the BRANCH section (`branchSection`, titled with the branch: what it is for in one line, `branchFor`, and `owned  3 of 11`).

**Veterans and the captain on the screen** (#346, `ui/captain.go`, `docs/crew-life.md`, `docs/lieutenants.md`): once anybody on the payroll has shown a trait, ON THE PAYROLL gains a `trait` column after `carry` (`traitCell`: green for a strength, amber for a flaw; the ladder beside the pane drops it after `age`), and the pane reads `trait  steady · served 150 days` with what it does under it (`takes a bad night at half the loss`, the trait's `says`), `trait  after 12d of service` for one who has not yet, and `captain  Eastside · $10,000 a night` for a captain (`veteranLines`), so a 150-day veteran reads differently from a new hire.

**The cursor stays on the person** (#536, `Model.syncCrew`): the crew screen's two lists are one cursor, and overnight the pool turns over and members leave, so the row it sat on held somebody else (a tester's first `h` on day 5, the ▸ nowhere in sight, hired the third face). The cursor keeps who it is on, by list and id (`crewID`, `crewIDs`, `crewAt`): where the rows have changed since it was last read it finds them again, where they are gone it keeps the row (the next one in the same list), and a cursor set since (a hire, a fire, an alert's jump, `pinCrew`) is taken as it is. A hire leaves it on the next face in LOOKING FOR WORK (#445), a fire on the next member in ON THE PAYROLL (#500). `TestCrewCursorFollowsThePerson` (both lists, a face gone, `h` hiring who the ▸ is on), `TestCrewCursorAfterHireAndFire`.
