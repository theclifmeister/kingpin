# Crew and upgrades in the grammar

**Crew and upgrades in the grammar** (#86, `ui/crew.go`, `ui/upgrades.go`, `crew_test.go`, `upgrades_test.go`). The crew screen's MAIN is `CREW · 3 of 10 on the payroll`, the pay line in the dial convention (`pay  stingy  [fair]  generous   $395/day`, `payRow`), the one warning line when there is one (`crewWarning`: the tell that somebody is talking, else a skim on record; MAIN truncates it, the pane's CREW section carries it whole) and the two tables, ON THE PAYROLL (name, role, skill, loyalty, wage, carry, post, hired; where MAIN is too narrow for the post to read whole, 64 columns beside the pane at 100, `carry` and then `hired` are dropped, the pane carrying both) and `LOOKING FOR WORK · new faces in 2 days` (fee instead of post and hired); nothing under them. The pane (`crewDetails`) is the person: `enforcer · skill 70 · hired d0` (a candidate: `· fee $235`), the loyalty bar against `skims under 30 · walks at 15` (a lieutenant `turns under 25`), the wage at the dial with the other two under it, carries, post or runs, temper (the word once observed, `shows in 8 days` while they run a city, `shows on the job` before), `SNITCH`, then what the keys do with the numbers the confirmations use (`h  hire for $235`, `f  fire: the rest lose 5 loyalty`, `t  give them a city`, `$  pay off for $3,900: 70 → 95`, `i  ask around $2,500, names ~64%`; `TestCrewPaneNamesTheCosts` holds them to `hireSelected`, `investigateConfirm` and `payOffConfirm`), then CREW (`crewSection`: the warning whole, capacity where you stand, worked corners, idle runners and unposted enforcers with the pointer to the map, what the accountants add, who runs each city, the named snitch or tonight's questions, sloppiness); every pane line fits `paneTextW` (`TestCrewScreenInTheGrammar` walks every row of the rich fixture). The upgrades screen (#86, #120) is one branch at a time: MAIN is `UPGRADES · 9 of 56 owned · dirty $438K · clean $65K`, the branch tabs in the market's city-tab convention (`branchTabs`, `tabLine`: `Operations  [ Security ]  Legal  Crew  Laundering  Logistics  Street`, the shown branch bracketed and `Selected`; where the long names do not fit, as beside the pane at 100 columns, `branchShort` shortens them, `Ops  [ Security ]  Legal  Crew  Launder  Road  Street`, the way the title bar's tabs shorten), a blank, the shown branch as one `table()` (`node cost status`: the mark gutter `✓ ○ ·` as the node's state, the name indented one cell a level under the prerequisite it hangs from, the cost through `cash()`, and the status `owned` / `available` / `needs Burner phones` / `$3K short` through `upgradeStatus`, a clean-cash node's starting `clean · `, since a cash column holds only a figure; the status is the last text column, so it alone gives way to the width), a blank and the legend `✓ owned  ○ available  · locked`. `branchRows` lays a branch out as a tree: a node's parent is the deepest of its prerequisites in the branch (the later in the file between two as deep, so `cutouts` hangs from `ghosts`; a prerequisite in another branch, `safehouse` over `stash`, is the status column's business, not the layout's), the roots are the nodes with none, and children follow their parent in file order (`TestBranchReadsAsATree`), so the cursor walks a branch top to bottom and no branch is taller than eleven rows, which fits 80x24 with no paging. `Model.branch` is the branch shown, an index into `content.Branches` and a view cursor like `Model.city` (never saved; `startRun` puts it back on Operations); `Model.upgradeCursor` is one node cursor a branch (`nodeCursor`, `upgradeSelected`), so a branch left and come back to is on the node it was on (`TestBranchCursorSticks`); `←→` turn the branch round the end as the market's arrows turn the city (`upgradeMove`, the `←→ branch` binding, the tree's own), `↑↓` walk the branch's nodes (the tree is a `listScreens` screen since #120, the map alone listing `↑↓←→`), `u`/`enter` buy. The pane (`upgradesDetails`) opens with the tree's cost string and the pool and where the node stands (`$12K dirty · needs Burner phones`, `available`, `owned`, `$3K short`; prerequisites too long for the line go on `needs` rows), then the description, the effects one per line (`effectWords`, one sentence a name of the vocabulary, a multiplier written by `times` with no trailing zero: `route risk ×0.8`, `wages ×0.9`, `+2 crew`; `TestEffectWordsCoverTheVocabulary` sets every field of `content.UpgradeEffects` alone through reflection and wants one sentence each), the clean-cash and fall-guy notes when they apply and `u  buy it for $12K dirty` when it can be bought, then the BRANCH section (`branchSection`, titled with the branch: what it is for in one line, `branchFor`, and `owned  3 of 11`). The dashboard's fact is the count, `upgrades 9 of 56` (`ownedLine`, `ownedCount`). `TestUpgradesScreenInTheGrammar` walks every branch at 80x24 and 120x40 (the title, the tabs whole with the shown branch bracketed, every node reached by `↓` with its name, cost and status on the cursor's row, both pane sections and every line in `paneTextW`); `TestUpgradeCostReadsTheSame` still holds the tree and the pane to one string; `TestUpgradeArrowsTurnTheBranch` the arrows. Neither screen prints prose under its tables or a `theme.Panel`.
