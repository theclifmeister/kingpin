# The frame and the details pane

**The frame and the details pane** (#79, `ui/frame.go`, `ui/pane.go`). Every play-mode screen renders through `Model.frame`, three parts (#95): row 0 the title bar, rows 1..h-2 the body (`bodyHeight()` is `height - 2`), row h-1 the status bar. From `paneMinWidth` (100) columns the body is MAIN (`mainWidth()` = width less `paneWidth`, 36) beside the details pane; under that MAIN runs the width and the pane collapses to the one-line **details strip** on row h-2 (`strip`: the selection's title and first facts, `␣ more` at the right), which costs MAIN a row (`mainHeight()`; `bodyHeight()` is still the body a modal has). Each screen has a `details() []section` method (`dashboardDetails`, `marketDetails`, `journalDetails`, `crewDetails`, `mapDetails`, `upgradesDetails`, `ledgerDetails`, `rivalsDetails`): the first section is the selection (its name in caps, facts as `row(label, value)` lines, what the keys would do to it as `keyRow`s), then the further sections the screen needs (the dashboard's ALERTS, the market's ELSEWHERE and NOTES, the rivals' RULES), and `pane` adds KEYS last, the key table's list for the screen (`keysFor`, below) in cells of `keyCellW` (two a line in the pane, four in the overlay, a wide pair taking two cells), never cut; sections over the room are cut from the bottom of the lowest first, each ending in `…` (`trimSections`). The inspectors live there and nowhere in MAIN: the map's corner inspector (`cornerSection`: owner and since, `holds` for the rival's, size, heat, risk, robbery, undercut, push odds, demand per product as many to a line as the value column holds, runner, enforcer, then the `keyRow`s for the corner's state: move a runner / post an enforcer / abandon on a worked one, the drift warning and post a runner on a held one nobody works, `w push takes it ~N%, hit ~N%` on the rival's with enforcers on the payroll, post a runner to claim a free one and `g` to go there when it is elsewhere) or the route's detail (`routeSection`: the edge, `dial [slow]`, `days N · capacity N`, `fare $N/u · seized ~N%`, the target, what is on the road, `r`/`R`) by `onRoutes`, the upgrades node, the market's product detail or the contract's (`contractSections`) by `onBuyers`. The pane has no toggle (#111): `paneShown()` is `width >= paneMinWidth`, so it is open beside MAIN wherever it fits, and `space` (`openDetails`) does nothing there and is not listed; where the strip is, `space` opens the same sections as a modal overlay (`modeDetails`, `overlay`, which is one `modal` like every other and scrolls; since #87 it renders every section whole, KEYS last, and scrolls rather than trims, because at 80 columns it is the one place a player can read what the strip could not show; `esc`/`space` close it; `TestSpaceOpensTheOverlayUnder100`, `TestRivalsOverlayScrolls`, and `TestModalsFit` covers it, and `TestRendersAtCommonSizes` pins that no render at 100x30 or 120x40 lacks the pane). `panel` (`frame.go`) is the one bordered-panel helper, the title in the top border (`╭─ STREET · Eastside ───╮`); the dashboard (#83, `ui/dashboard.go`) has two layouts, every panel sized to what it holds so none carries dead space: from 100 columns STREET the width (sized to its lines, at most `streetMaxH` rows) over HEAT | CASH at 46 | 38 of 84, LAW | RIVALS the same, and CITIES (`dashboardWide`); under 100 STREET over HEAT | CASH | LAW at 28 | 23 | 29 of 80 (`dashboardNarrow`), the rival folded into LAW and the other city into the street. HEAT, CASH, LAW and RIVALS are four lines each (HEAT: the gauge, `23/100 · peak 23 · file 0/7`, the thresholds on one line where they fit and two a line where not, the reputation bars; CASH: the dirty pile with `over $X: heat` on its row once it is past the threshold plus what the fronts cover (`heat.Sim.Cover`), the clean cash with the day's wash, the peak, the other city's heat). STREET's lines under the table are facts (`fact`, `pack`): topics one a line where the room allows (the stash and the supplier, the corners in the map's vocabulary `corners 1 worked, 1 held of 10, 1 theirs` in `theme.CrewText`, the crew and the skim, who runs a city, the contracts, the upgrades, somebody talking, a patrol cap, tonight's strike), packed as tight as the width allows and the least important dropped (`priSupplier` first) where it does not, and last, never cut, the line on tonight's sales. A line with a long and a short form takes the first that fits (`firstFit`). The ALERTS are the pane's; MAIN carries an ALERTS panel, sized to its lines, only where no pane sits beside it and there is room. The pane's selection is the product under the cursor: price and change, supplier and margin, stock here and elsewhere, the road, demand on your corners, the order, and `s  sell ~N at <dial> = ~$X`, the sell dialog's expect row for the whole stash at the dial it would open with (`sellEstimate`; `TestDashboardEstimateMatchesDialog`). `dashboard_test.go` pins the shape: no run of more than two blank rows in any panel, STREET's last line the sales line, four lines with no cut number in HEAT, CASH and LAW at 80x24, and the corner line in Crew blue, never purple (`TestDashboardCornersAreYours` renders under TrueColor). The status bar (`viewFooter`) is the message (#109; no legend: the keys are listed in the pane's KEYS, `paneKeys()`, the key table's `keysFor(screen)`): the status at the left, one space in, and `? help` (`helpPair`, the table's) pinned at the right; the message wins: it is never dropped, `? help` goes when the two cannot share the row and the message is cut only when it alone does not fit (`TestStatusMessageAlwaysShows`, `TestStatusBarIsMessageAndHelp` at 60, 80 and 120 columns, `TestStatusBarIsTheMessage` over the rich fixture); inside a modal the bar is the modal's footer (`modalFooter`); and the message is coloured by `statusKind` (`statusBody` a confirmation, `statusWarning` a refusal, `statusBad` a danger), which every site sets through `say()`, `refuse()` or `alarm()` (#88, below).
