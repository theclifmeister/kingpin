# Animation

**Animation** (#152, from the investigation in #150; milestone 8): a **scene** is a short, skippable, deterministic animation drawn through the model's own `View` and driven by a `tea.Tick` that exists **only while a scene is on screen**. The one rule that changed: the UI redraws on a key, a resize, *and* a tick while `Model.scene != nil`; never a tick in play mode, never inside fast-forward's loop, never in the harness (it does not import `ui`), never in the test fixtures or the README's captures (they construct with `Options{Anim: false}`), and `Init` still returns nil (the start menu's loop starts on the first `WindowSizeMsg`, inside `Update`). `internal/ui/anim` is the package: it imports `theme` and nothing else of `ui` (the model imports it), and the effects are ports of TerminalTextEffects read through ttfx, Omarchy's screensaver, with MIT attribution in `internal/ui/anim/NOTICE` (a new effect goes in the NOTICE's list). The API: `anim.Scene` (`Frame(t, w, h) []string`, h lines of at most w cells, and `Done(t)`), both pure functions of t, so a frame asked for twice is the same frame; `anim.Canvas` (cells of a rune and a `lipgloss.Color`, `Lines()` rendering each row as runs of one colour through `theme.Fg`, a cell set outside it dropped, so a scene wider than its room is cut, never wrapped); `anim.Text` (`NewText(art)`, the block art or the words a scene resolves to, `Origin(w, h)` centred, `Cells()` in reading order); `anim.Seed(seed, day, name) *rand.Rand`, a scene's own stream from the run's seed, the day and the scene's name, **never `game.RNGFor`'s** (a scene reads the world and never writes it: `TestSceneNeverTouchesTheWorld` plays a run with animation on and off to day 10 and wants them gob-equal); `anim.Frame` (30 fps); `anim.Decrypt(text, accent, over, rng)`, the first effect (the text typed in as blocks, every cell churning through cipher glyphs in `theme.Dim` and settling left to right, a flash of `theme.Text` as it is discovered, the settled text in the accent; the original's three phases, timed to the length given rather than its frame counts); `anim.Player` (the scene on screen: `Scene`, `Accent`, `Idle`, `Rest`, `Next`; `Tick(now)` advances its clock off the time each tick fired, the first setting the start, so a frame is never read off the wall clock and a test drives it with times of its own; `Frame(w, h)`; `T()`, `Pass()`); and `anim.Scenes()`, the registry of `Named{Name, New(seed)}` that `TestScenesFit` walks (the title for now; #161 grows it). **The model** (`ui/scene.go`): `Model.scene *anim.Player`, `play(p)` puts a scene up (`sceneGen` counts scenes so a tick outstanding when one ended lands on the old generation and is dropped, `ticking` says one is on its way), `stop()` takes it off, `tick()` is the command every branch of `Update` ends with (the next frame's `tea.Tick` while a scene is up and none is on its way, else nil), `onFrame` is a `frameMsg{at, gen}` landing (the clock moves, an interstitial that has run out comes off), and `skip()` at the top of `handleKey` (after `ctrl+c`) ends an interstitial on any key and consumes it (an `Idle` player, the title's, plays on and the key falls through), so the mechanism is there for #154's card before the first interstitial exists. **The title's idle loop** is the proof: `anim.Kingpin` is the block art (six rows, fifty columns, a Go constant), `anim.Title(rng)` decrypts it in `theme.Money` over `anim.TitleLength` (1.5 s), and `titleLoop` (on every `WindowSizeMsg`) runs it behind the start menu's box as an `Idle` player resting `anim.TitleRest` (4 s) then restarting on `anim.Seed(0, pass, "title")`, the way Omarchy's screensaver loops effects over its logo; `viewStart` draws the frame (`titleArt`, at the terminal's width, six rows) above the box, which sits under it; the loop stops when a run starts or continues (`startRun`, `continueRun`, `quit` call `stop`) and under 80x24 or with animation off the menu draws exactly as it did. **The off switch**: `ui.Options{Anim bool}` through `ui.New(cfg, opts)` / `ui.NewSlot(cfg, n, opts)`; `cmd/kingpin -no-anim` and `KINGPIN_NO_ANIM` (any value) turn it off, read where the options are built in `cmd/kingpin`, never in `ui`; every test fixture (`newTestModel`, `richModel`, `richFixture`, `richModelSeeded`, the captures) passes `Anim: false`, and `newAnimModel` in `anim_test.go` is the one fixture that holds a scene. **Every scene**: any key skips it and is consumed; the world has already stepped and saved before the first frame (`stepDay` then `morning`); it draws inside the mode's own modal or body; it fits 80x24, 100x30 and 120x40 through `assertFits`; it reads in sixteen colours (the churn is Dim, the flash Text, the art gold: bright black, white and yellow); colours only through `theme` (`TestThemeIsTheOnlyStylist` greps `anim/` too). **The guards** (`ui/anim_test.go`, `anim/anim_test.go`): `TestInitStartsNothing` unchanged; `TestNoTickInPlayMode` (`Update` returns nil for every key of the table on every screen in `modePlay` with animation on, and for a resize and a key in every mode of the rich fixture with it off); `TestSceneStopsTicking` (the menu's resize and every frame return a tick, a key on the menu acts on the menu and issues no second tick while one is on its way, a run started returns nil from then on, a straggling tick included, an interstitial ends on any key, consumed, and on `Done`, and the command after is nil); `TestSceneNeverTouchesTheWorld`; `TestScenesFit` (every registered scene at t = 0, half and the end at the three sizes, and the title over the menu with the box on row 8 and the settled art in gold); `TestSceneIsDeterministic` (the same seed the same frames, another seed others); `TestTitleLoopIsTheMenuOtherwise` (under 80x24 or with animation off the menu's render is the old one, the loop stops and starts on a resize across the floor, continuing a slot stops it); and the package's own (`TestCanvasRuns`, `TestTextCentres`, `TestSeedIsItsOwn`, `TestDecryptResolves`, `TestPlayerLoops`, `TestScenesRegistry`).
