# Animation

**Animation** (#152, from the investigation in #150; milestone 8): a **scene** is a short, skippable, deterministic animation drawn through the model's own `View` and driven by a `tea.Tick` that exists **only while a scene is on screen**. The one rule that changed: the UI redraws on a key, a resize, *and* a tick while `Model.scene != nil`; never a tick in play mode, never inside fast-forward's loop, never in the harness (it does not import `ui`), never in the test fixtures or the README's captures (they construct with `Options{Anim: false}`), and `Init` still returns nil (the start menu's loop starts on the first `WindowSizeMsg`, inside `Update`). `internal/ui/anim` is the package: it imports `theme` and nothing else of `ui` (the model imports it), and the effects are ports of TerminalTextEffects read through ttfx, Omarchy's screensaver, with MIT attribution in `internal/ui/anim/NOTICE` (a new effect goes in the NOTICE's list). The API: `anim.Scene` (`Frame(t, w, h) []string`, h lines of at most w cells, and `Done(t)`), both pure functions of t, so a frame asked for twice is the same frame; `anim.Canvas` (cells of a rune and a `lipgloss.Color`, `Lines()` rendering each row as runs of one colour through `theme.Fg`, a cell set outside it dropped, so a scene wider than its room is cut, never wrapped); `anim.Text` (`NewText(art)`, the block art or the words a scene resolves to, `Origin(w, h)` centred, `Cells()` in reading order); `anim.Seed(seed, day, name) *rand.Rand`, a scene's own stream from the run's seed, the day and the scene's name, **never `game.RNGFor`'s** (a scene reads the world and never writes it: `TestSceneNeverTouchesTheWorld` plays a run with animation on and off to day 10 and wants them gob-equal); `anim.Frame` (30 fps); `anim.Decrypt(text, accent, over, rng)`, the first effect (the text typed in as blocks, every cell churning through cipher glyphs in `theme.Dim` and settling left to right, a flash of `theme.Text` as it is discovered, the settled text in the accent; the original's three phases, timed to the length given rather than its frame counts); `anim.Player` (the scene on screen: `Scene`, `Accent`, `Idle`, `Rest`, `Next`; `Tick(now)` advances its clock off the time each tick fired, the first setting the start, so a frame is never read off the wall clock and a test drives it with times of its own; `Frame(w, h)`; `T()`, `Pass()`); and `anim.Scenes()`, the registry of `Named{Name, New(seed)}` that `TestScenesFit` walks (the title for now; #161 grows it). **The model** (`ui/scene.go`): `Model.scene *anim.Player`, `play(p)` puts a scene up (`sceneGen` counts scenes so a tick outstanding when one ended lands on the old generation and is dropped, `ticking` says one is on its way), `stop()` takes it off, `tick()` is the command every branch of `Update` ends with (the next frame's `tea.Tick` while a scene is up and none is on its way, else nil), `onFrame` is a `frameMsg{at, gen}` landing (the clock moves, an interstitial that has run out comes off), and `skip()` at the top of `handleKey` (after `ctrl+c`) ends an interstitial on any key and consumes it (an `Idle` player, the title's, plays on and the key falls through), so the mechanism is there for #154's card before the first interstitial exists. **The title's idle loop** is the proof: `anim.Kingpin` is the block art (six rows, fifty columns, a Go constant), `anim.Title(rng)` decrypts it in `theme.Money` over `anim.TitleLength` (1.5 s), and `titleLoop` (on every `WindowSizeMsg`) runs it behind the start menu's box as an `Idle` player resting `anim.TitleRest` (4 s) then restarting on `anim.Seed(0, pass, "title")`, the way Omarchy's screensaver loops effects over its logo; `viewStart` draws the frame (`titleArt`, at the terminal's width, six rows) above the box, which sits under it; the loop stops when a run starts or continues (`startRun`, `continueRun`, `quit` call `stop`) and under 80x24 or with animation off the menu draws exactly as it did. **The off switch**: `ui.Options{Anim bool}` through `ui.New(cfg, opts)` / `ui.NewSlot(cfg, n, opts)`; `cmd/kingpin -no-anim` and `KINGPIN_NO_ANIM` (any value) turn it off, read where the options are built in `cmd/kingpin`, never in `ui`; every test fixture (`newTestModel`, `richModel`, `richFixture`, `richModelSeeded`, the captures) passes `Anim: false`, and `newAnimModel` in `anim_test.go` is the one fixture that holds a scene. **Every scene**: any key skips it and is consumed; the world has already stepped and saved before the first frame (`stepDay` then `morning`); it draws inside the mode's own modal or body; it fits 80x24, 100x30 and 120x40 through `assertFits`; it reads in sixteen colours (the churn is Dim, the flash Text, the art gold: bright black, white and yellow); colours only through `theme` (`TestThemeIsTheOnlyStylist` greps `anim/` too). **The guards** (`ui/anim_test.go`, `anim/anim_test.go`): `TestInitStartsNothing` unchanged; `TestNoTickInPlayMode` (`Update` returns nil for every key of the table on every screen in `modePlay` with animation on, and for a resize and a key in every mode of the rich fixture with it off); `TestSceneStopsTicking` (the menu's resize and every frame return a tick, a key on the menu acts on the menu and issues no second tick while one is on its way, a run started returns nil from then on, a straggling tick included, an interstitial ends on any key, consumed, and on `Done`, and the command after is nil); `TestSceneNeverTouchesTheWorld`; `TestScenesFit` (every registered scene at t = 0, half and the end at the three sizes, and the title over the menu with the box on row 8 and the settled art in gold); `TestSceneIsDeterministic` (the same seed the same frames, another seed others); `TestTitleLoopIsTheMenuOtherwise` (under 80x24 or with animation off the menu's render is the old one, the loop stops and starts on a resize across the floor, continuing a slot stops it); and the package's own (`TestCanvasRuns`, `TestTextCentres`, `TestSeedIsItsOwn`, `TestDecryptResolves`, `TestPlayerLoops`, `TestScenesRegistry`). No pinned number moved: t1–t4 $84,930 / $605,294 / $17,269,047 / $82,302,054 before and after.

**The effect set** (#153, `anim/effects.go` and one file an effect, each ≤ 300 lines, each credited in `anim/NOTICE` beside the ttfx and TTE files it is read from): the effects a scene draws from, ported as **pure frame functions**, not the engine: no per-character motion objects or waypoints, a function of `(t, w, h, seed, text)` that fills a `Canvas`, every die thrown at construction off the rng the scene passes and `Frame` reading what they decided. One shape for all of them, Decrypt's: `anim.Maker` is `func(text Text, accent lipgloss.Color, over time.Duration, rng *rand.Rand) Scene`, every effect resolves the text in the accent at `over` and holds it after (the frame at `t >= over` is the still text, whatever a short length left unfinished), and `anim.Effects` is the table by name, `Effect{Name, New Maker, Needs, Dice}`, `anim.Names()` its names sorted. **`Needs{Text bool; MinW, MinH int}`** is what an effect needs of a scene, `Needs.Met(text, w, h)` the check, and **`anim.Still(text, accent)`** the fallback where a scene lacks it: the text at once, `Done` from t = 0, so a `Player` given one is over on its first tick. **`anim.Reverse(maker)`** plays an effect from its end (`Frame(over − t)`), so `Reverse(PourFrom(Up))` is the text falling off the bottom of the canvas (#156's cash figures). **The nine that resolve a text** (colour is decoration on a shape whose motion is in the characters): `decrypt` (#152's); `print` (`print.go`: a head writes the lines one at a time on the bottom line of the text's box, a cell typed in as `█▓▒░` in `theme.Text` before its character in the accent, and between lines the page feeds up a row and the head, `█`, returns to the next line's first column with in-out-quad easing; a column the head passes is a unit, a return a third of a unit a cell, the units sharing nine tenths of the length); `wipe` (`wipe.go`: the text revealed a group at a time, the diagonals from the top-left corner by default, `WipeFrom(Right|Down|Left|Up)` the columns or the rows, the newest group in `theme.Text` for two frames; eased *out*, `OutSine`, where TTE eases in and out over a hundred steps, because a curve that starts slow reveals nothing for the first several frames of a short wipe); **`curtain`** (in `wipe.go`, wipe's colour-only mode: no text, every cell of the canvas filled with `█` in the colour as the front passes, top to bottom, eased in like a thing that falls, and held: #156's red bars, `CurtainFrom` for another direction, `Reverse(Curtain)` the bars lifting; it is in the table with `Needs.Text` false and so out of the title's cycle and of the resolve test); `slide` (`slide.go`: the characters slide in from an edge a row at a time, the far end of each row released first so the row arrives together, `theme.Text` while moving and the accent once landed; one travel time, two fifths of the length, eased out, the releases spread over the rest; `SlideFrom(Right|Left|Down|Up)`, `Up` the counter rolling in from below for #159); `pour` and `rain` (`pour.go`, one `fall` engine: the characters fall from the edge's own row into place, the bottom row first so the text stacks; `pour` in order, every other row reversed (TTE's zigzag), each in `theme.Text` with a trail of `▒░` in `theme.Dim` behind it, eased in, `PourFrom` for another edge; `rain` in a random order a row at a time, each a raindrop `o . , * |` in `theme.Dim` at a speed of its own, flashing `theme.Text` as it lands; a drop shows only once it has moved, so a fall reversed ends on an empty canvas); `beams` (`beams.go`: beams of light along every row (`▂▁_`) and two columns in five (`▌▍▎▏`) of the text's box and a margin round it, a head in `theme.Text` fading through `theme.Logistics` to `theme.Dim`, leaving the characters they cross faintly lit in `theme.Dim`, over the first half; then Omarchy's About sheen, one band leaning `/` across the text left to right, `█▓` in `theme.Text` on the cells under it, the text behind it resting in the accent, over the second); `burn` (`burn.go`: the text stands in `theme.Dim` and burns from the bottom up, a flame front with a jitter a column and a cell over seven tenths of the length, each cell for a quarter of it through TTE's `' . ▖ ▙ █ ▜ ▀ ▝ .` in `theme.Text`, then `theme.Warn`, then `theme.Heat`, a wisp of smoke above it in `theme.Dim`, and is left in the accent; TTE spreads the fire along a random spanning tree, the port along the front); `vhstape` (`vhstape.go`: the text on screen and the tape bad: two streams of glitches back to back over the first fifty-five hundredths, a line or a band of three shoved left or right for two to six frames with its colour rolling `theme.Text, Heat, Market, Crew` (TTE's white, red, green, blue) and snow `# * . :` at its torn ends, whole frames of snow one in fifteen; then snow over everything to three quarters; then the lines redrawn one at a time from the bottom in the accent and held); `matrix` (`matrix.go`: digital rain over the whole canvas, three streams a column of TTE's glyphs (digits, punctuation, half-width katakana), a head in `theme.Text` over a body in `theme.Market` and a tail in `theme.Dim`, over the first half; every column filled to the bottom by seven tenths; then each cell resolving at a moment of its own to the text in the accent or to nothing; the streams are hashed off one seed and the column, so the same seed rains the same at any width, and the frame at 120x40 is 4,800 cells, which is why `Canvas.Lines` caches the open and close sequence of a colour per colour and colour profile (`styleOf`, behaviour for behaviour what `theme.Fg(c).Render` wraps a single tabless line in) rather than rendering a lipgloss style a run). **The easing table** (`easing.go`, TTE's `utils/easing.py`): `Linear` and in, out and in-out for sine, quad, cubic, expo, elastic and bounce, `type Easing func(p float64) float64`, `TestEasing` against the standard curves' known values. **The title cycles the set**: `anim.TitlePass(seed, pass, prev, pinned)` picks a pass's effect off `Seed(seed, pass, "title")` from `anim.TitleEffects()` (the table less the curtain) less the pass before's, so no effect plays twice running, then runs it on the same stream; `titleLoop` keeps `Model.titleEffect` for the next pass's `Next`; `Options.Effect` pins a name for every pass, read in `cmd/kingpin` from **`KINGPIN_ANIM_EFFECT=name`** (an unknown name is refused with the list, the curtain too), and `Scenes()`' `title` is pass 0 on the seed. The game's loop (seed 0) opens `burn, vhstape, wipe, beams, matrix, …`. **The guards** (`anim/effects_test.go`, over the title's 6×50 art at `TitleLength` and a 1×20 line at 0.5 s): `TestEffectsAreDeterministic` (the same seed the same frames twice; another seed other frames exactly for the effects whose `Dice` is set: print, wipe, slide and pour throw none), `TestEffectsResolve` (every effect's last frame, and every frame after, is `Still`'s, all in the accent; the curtain's the colour over the whole canvas; `Done` at the length), `TestEffectsFit` (every frame at 80x24, 120x40 and 10x3 the canvas's height and within its width: a canvas too small cuts), `TestEffectsReadInSixteenColours` (under `termenv.ANSI` no truecolor sequence, and every frame's uncoloured text differs from the previous frame's over the first half of the run for every effect that resolves a text; the curtain, colour by design, must have moved by the half), `TestStillReverseNeeds`, `TestTitleCyclesTheSet`, and `BenchmarkEffects` (a frame at 120x40 over the art: matrix ~106 µs, beams ~29 µs, the rest 10–21 µs on an M-series Mac; the budget is a millisecond); in `ui`, `TestTitleLoopCyclesTheEffects` (eight passes of the menu's loop play at least three effects, never one twice running, each settling the art in gold, and `Effect: "matrix"` plays matrix every pass) beside #152's guards, `TestSceneIsDeterministic` now reading the pass's pick as well as its dice. No pinned number moved.
