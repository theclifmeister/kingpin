# The endings, the exit plans and the run summary

Each ending well is also a plan the player can see and pin before it opens (#347, `docs/ambitions.md`): the bar reads full exactly when the condition below holds.

**A run ends nine ways** (#49, §7; `game/exit.go`, `content/endings.toml`, `ui/summary.go`, `ui/exit.go`; the three the run had, #195's `retired`, and five this issue added).
`Ending.Cause` is the kind, one of `content.Causes` (`CauseIndicted` ..
`CauseVanished`; no second `Kind` field, the coordinator's ruling), `Ending.Who` the name in it where there is one, and **every writer goes through `World.End(cause, day, who)`**, which stamps `Stats.Score` as the world stands and returns the `Ending` the owner assigns to `w.Over`.
Each ending is **detected by the sim that owns its condition, in its step, as a threshold on state it already reads, with no dice**, written as the heat sim writes the indictment; `TestSimsWriteOnlyTheirOwnState` lists `Over` for the four owners (heat, crew, rivals, laundering) instead of sharing it, and a zero in the owner's file boxes the detector (`harness.NoEndings` boxes all four), so a run on the file before the endings is the run it was.

**The exit plans.**
The offshore account (#195) is the first: what is in `World.Offshore` survives every ending, and only that (no `offshore` node, no `offshore_share`: #118 took the id and #195 made the account a number the player sets).
The new identity is the second: `identity`, a Legal-branch node beside `retainer` and `fallguy` (`upgrades.toml`: $4M clean, requires `retainer`, `identities = 1`, `Effects.Identities` summed, `TestFoldEffects`), enables `World.Vanish(fx)` (`ErrNoIdentity` without one; `CanVanish`) and turns the end into an exit: the heat sim runs the plans **in order where the indictment is written** (`heat.Sim.exit`: the fall guy takes the first, once each, wiping the file, dropping every city's heat to `heat.toml` `fall_heat` (50) and costing `fall_cash` (half) of the dirty cash and of the clean, `heat.Sim.takeFall`; then the identity makes the cause `vanished`; then the end), and since the run is over either way the identity is spent once.
`TestExitPlansRunInOrder`: on one seed and tree the aggressive trader is arrested on day 18; with the identity it vanishes on day 18; with a fall guy it is arrested on day 25 after the fall; with both it vanishes on day 25 (17 and 23 before #475: each arrest now waits a night behind its warrant, the aggressive trader selling through it); the account reads the same through all four.
`harness.BuyUpgrades` never buys the identity (an exit is a decision a policy makes, not the cheapest node left in Legal), so the boss's and the distributor's runs are the runs they were; a test that wants it owns it (`Own`).

**The reign** (#227): Kingpin is a crown you take, not a trapdoor that closes the run.
The rivals sim's detector stamps `World.Reign` (the day the reign began, sticky; 0 while none, the run before) the morning its condition first holds and emits one `events.ReignBegan{City, Crews, Homage}` (a `rivals` headline, `ReignBegan` in `headlines.toml`; the report's TIER section opens with `The city is yours: every crew in Eastside is gone or paying. Take the crown when you are ready (walk away on the dashboard), or reign.`; the dashboard's ALERTS carry `The city is yours: day 31 of the reign, 4 crews paying $41,200 a night. Take the crown or play on.`, keyed once, so a fast-forward stops the morning it begins and not again; `stopEvent` names `ReignBegan` too).
While it holds and you play on, every morning's TIER opens with `REIGN: day 31 of the reign · 4 crews paying homage · $41,200 a night` (`news.reignLine`: the crews are the homage deals live, `World.HomageDeals`, the money what the tick's `TributePaid{ToYou}` paid tonight; `every crew gone` with none paying) and the STREET facts' corners line carries `· reign d31` (`World.ReignDay`, the first morning day 1; on the corners' fact because the tier's is the first the panel drops for room).
The morning the condition stops holding (a homage ends and the faction sets up again, or the share falls) the stamp zeroes, `events.ReignBroken{Why}` (report-only: `The reign is over for now: a crew set up again.` / `the city slipped under the share`) opens the TIER section, the fast-forward stops, and it can begin again `dominant_days` on.

**The score** (#195's ruling): `Stats.Score = Offshore / (1 + Stats.Bodies)`, `World.Score()`, stamped by `End` the morning the run ends.
The pile left behind is printed, never scored; days are shown, never scored; so lying low k more days before an exit can never raise it (`TestRetireeRetires`, k = 0/10/30/60: 765,314 on seed 1 at every k) and the bodies divide it (`TestScoreIsTheAccount`).

**A decision per ending** (#478, the playtest's question whether each exit is a real choice; ruled on the issue, respecting #413: nothing pushes toward one ending).
- **The score is unchanged.** Every ending scores the account (#195), so no ending is worth more than another: the ending decides the story and the unlock. The tester's $48K vanish against a $755K retirement was the size of the account, not a vanish penalty. Each walk-away row now says what it scores.
- **Vanish is confirmed** as the exit that needs no terms, priced in the upgrade tree. Its plan gains the lawyer on call it was missing (`docs/ambitions.md`). Carrying the clean cash into the score was not taken: it would push players toward one ending.
- **Taken out is kept**, as losing a war you could not hold, and the war condition stays: dropping it would make taken out a trapdoor. A war you declared now counts as open, and the declaration and the rivals pane warn while you have fewer than `taken_out_muscle` enforcers (`docs/rival.md`; `TestADeclaredWarIsOpen`, `TestWarWarnsShortOfMuscle`). Measured with `cmd/balance`, 20 runs x 300 days, before and after: `war`, `warlord`, `saboteur`, `reckless`, `tipster`, `crewed`, `managed` and `quiet` end exactly as before (only `warlord` and `reckless` declare a war, and neither lost its last corner to that faction short of muscle), so no frequency or pin moved.
- **Kingpin: the share stays.** The playtest's hidden clock was factions yet to arrive, which block `Dominant()`; the closed crown row now counts them apart (`2 crews yet to arrive`), beside #472's per-faction lines in the plan and its corners held against the share.
- **Retire: an opt-in sweep** removes the manual transfer chore (`docs/laundering.md`): off by default, no balance change.
- **Businessman is confirmed** (#398).

**Going straight takes a real business** (#493, ruled on the issue, respecting #413: the rule describes the ending, nothing pushes toward it). A playtest went straight on day 77 on one Laundromat level (+$420 a day), one goodwill purchase and thirty nights laid low: a night laid low sells ~$0, and any front income out-earned it, so "the fronts out-earn the street" read true on $39K of front earnings against $1.1M of street. Of the issue's three options the smallest sound one is to measure the street, not last night: a night now counts only while the fronts out-earn **the more of the night's street and its average night over the run** (`World.StreetAverage(day)`: `Stats.TotalRevenue`, the street, the buyers and the exports, over the nights played; the laundering sim's `legit` reads it at the tick's day, `Session.AmbitionTerms` at the morning's, so the plan's `income` step and the count read one number). Not counting a lie-low night alone was not taken: a night that simply sells nothing is the same $0, and the ending would still open on any income. A run's peak night was not taken either: one lucky night would close it for good. The average falls as the nights pass without dealing, so a front business the size of the street you ran opens it, and one Laundromat level after $1.1M of street would need ~2,600 nights. A run that never sold reads a street of $0, as before (the harness's businessman scenario and `TestGoingStraightIsClaimed` are unchanged). The plan's step reads `$420/day against the street's $14,286 a night` (`stepWords`; it said `against $0 last night`). Guards: `TestLyingLowIsNotGoingStraight` (`sim/laundering`: the playtest's thirty nights after $1.1M of street count none; the same fronts in a run that never sold open it), `TestLieLowStreetIsZero` (`ui`). Measured with `cmd/balance`, 20 runs x 400 days: `boss` ends as before, a businessman on 4 of 20 (its levels out-earn its average night by then; the output is identical line for line); `cartel` a businessman on 8 of 20 where it was 13 (the runs whose streak ran against a slow night now wait for the fronts to beat the average night; the median score of the runs ended $11.4M to $13.0M), and nothing else ends differently. The engine's policies reach it on the business, as the ending means.

**Going straight reads the street's last nights** (#529, the owner's option (b), respecting #413: this is whether the ending is reachable and legible, not a push toward it). Against the run's average night (#493), a front business could not get there within the run a player plays: the average falls only as the run gets longer (a tester's fronts at $1,981 a day faced a $16.5K street falling ~0.7% a night laid low, ~280 nights before the first counted), and no policy went straight in 300 days. The street is now the average night over the last `laundering.toml [businessman] street_window` (60) nights (`World.StreetOver(day, window)`, `laundering.Sim.Street`), the more of it and tonight's street as before, so stopping dealing counts: each night given up takes a window's share off it. The laundering sim keeps the nights (`LaunderingState.Street`, what `Stats.TotalRevenue` rose by each night, the street, the buyers and the exports, and `StreetMark`, the total it was read against; `recordStreet`, before `legit`, no dice), from the first night anything sold, so a run that never deals keeps none. Nights of the window not recorded (a young run, or a save from before, which records from its first night loaded) read the run's nights before the record at their average, so a save from before reads #493's number and falls from there, and a run's first nights read the nights before it as nothing. **#493's guard stays**: the window is twice `legit_days`, so thirty nights laid low still leave at least half the window's street in it and the fronts' own income has to beat that; lying low alone never counts (`TestLyingLowIsNotGoingStraight`). The walk-away row says what the fronts are measured against: `the fronts out-earn the street's average night over the last 60 and goodwill tops pressure at home, 30 nights running`, short `the fronts' $1,981/day under the street's $16,550 a night` (`streetWords`, `straightShort`); help's WORDS has `street night` (`a night's dealing; going straight: fronts over its last 60`) beside `the street`. Guards: `TestStreetIsTheLastNights` (`sim/laundering`: a $10,000 street reads $10,000, falls a sixtieth a night given up, a save from before reads the run's average), `TestStoppingDealingGoesStraight` (`harness`: the boss to day 150, then `StopDealing`, which recalls the crew, lies low and keeps home's goodwill over its pressure; every seed whose fronts earn and that nothing else ends goes straight between day 180, the streak, and day 255, the window plus the streak plus 15; 6 of 6 on days 235–238 over ten seeds, the other four losing money on their fronts or betrayed; over twenty since #528 and #531 moved the boss's runs, 10 of 10 on days 235–239, where ten read only 4 earning). Measured, 20 runs x 300 days: `territory`, `boss`, `diplomat`, `laundered` and `retiree` end as before; `cartel -days 500` (10 runs) ends a businessman on 0 where it was 5 (its dealing grows with the run, so its last sixty nights are more than its average night; it never stops dealing, and the ending is the business, not the calendar).

**The crown waits on you, not on clocks** (#495, ruled on the issue, respecting #413). A playtest held all ten corners at home by day 52 and waited: a run-out faction's `gone in Nd` restarted every time it claimed a corner (`gone in 59d` again), and a sixth and a seventh faction, drawn to Bayport by the take, pushed the reign's start from day 263 to past 391. Three rulings, each a run that never meets its state being the run before:
- **A claim it does not keep is not somewhere to stand.** A run-out faction that claims a corner and loses it again inside `rivals.toml [factions] settle_days` (30) picks its landless clock up from the first rout (`RivalState.Spell`, `RunOut`); one that keeps a corner `settle_days` has settled and its next rout starts a new clock (`docs/rival.md`). 0 is the rule before, every claim restarting it.
- **No new faction is drawn while the crown waits only on clocks** (`rivals.Sim.CrownClock`): while you hold more than `kingpin_share` of home's corners and no faction still standing holds a corner (bar a run-out one on a claim it has not kept), the money draws no scouts. A faction already on its way still comes and a seat in the wings still moves in on its day; each is already on the rivals screen with its day.
