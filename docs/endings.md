# The endings, the exit plans and the run summary

Each ending well is also a plan the player can see and pin before it opens (#347, `docs/ambitions.md`): the bar reads full exactly when the condition below holds.

**A run ends nine ways** (#49, §7; `game/exit.go`, `content/endings.toml`, `ui/summary.go`, `ui/exit.go`; the three the run had, #195's `retired`, and five this issue added).
`Ending.Cause` is the kind, one of `content.Causes` (`CauseIndicted` ..
`CauseVanished`; no second `Kind` field, the coordinator's ruling), `Ending.Who` the name in it where there is one, and **every writer goes through `World.End(cause, day, who)`**, which stamps `Stats.Score` as the world stands and returns the `Ending` the owner assigns to `w.Over`.
Each ending is **detected by the sim that owns its condition, in its step, as a threshold on state it already reads, with no dice**, written as the heat sim writes the indictment; `TestSimsWriteOnlyTheirOwnState` lists `Over` for the four owners (heat, crew, rivals, laundering) instead of sharing it, and a zero in the owner's file boxes the detector (`harness.NoEndings` boxes all four), so a run on the file before the endings is the run it was.

**The exit plans.**
The offshore account (#195) is the first: what is in `World.Offshore` survives every ending, and only that (no `offshore` node, no `offshore_share`: #118 took the id and #195 made the account a number the player sets).
The new identity is the second: `identity`, a Legal-branch node beside `retainer` and `fallguy` (`upgrades.toml`: $4M clean, requires `retainer`, `identities = 1`, `Effects.Identities` summed, `TestFoldEffects`), enables `World.Vanish(fx)` (`ErrNoIdentity` without one; `CanVanish`) and turns the end into an exit: the heat sim runs the plans **in order where the indictment is written** (`heat.Sim.exit`: the fall guy takes the first, once each, wiping the file, dropping every city's heat to `heat.toml` `fall_heat` (50) and costing `fall_cash` (half) of the dirty cash and of the clean, `heat.Sim.takeFall`; then the identity makes the cause `vanished`; then the end), and since the run is over either way the identity is spent once.
`TestExitPlansRunInOrder`: on one seed and tree the aggressive trader is arrested on day 17; with the identity it vanishes on day 17; with a fall guy it is arrested on day 23 after the fall; with both it vanishes on day 23; the account reads the same through all four.
`harness.BuyUpgrades` never buys the identity (an exit is a decision a policy makes, not the cheapest node left in Legal), so the boss's and the distributor's runs are the runs they were; a test that wants it owns it (`Own`).

**The reign** (#227): Kingpin is a crown you take, not a trapdoor that closes the run.
The rivals sim's detector stamps `World.Reign` (the day the reign began, sticky; 0 while none, the run before) the morning its condition first holds and emits one `events.ReignBegan{City, Crews, Homage}` (a `rivals` headline, `ReignBegan` in `headlines.toml`; the report's TIER section opens with `The city is yours: every crew in Eastside is gone or paying. Take the crown when you are ready (walk away on the dashboard), or reign.`; the dashboard's ALERTS carry `The city is yours: day 31 of the reign, 4 crews paying $41,200 a night. Take the crown or play on.`, keyed once, so a fast-forward stops the morning it begins and not again; `stopEvent` names `ReignBegan` too).
While it holds and you play on, every morning's TIER opens with `REIGN: day 31 of the reign · 4 crews paying homage · $41,200 a night` (`news.reignLine`: the crews are the homage deals live, `World.HomageDeals`, the money what the tick's `TributePaid{ToYou}` paid tonight; `every crew gone` with none paying) and the STREET facts' corners line carries `· reign d31` (`World.ReignDay`, the first morning day 1; on the corners' fact because the tier's is the first the panel drops for room).
The morning the condition stops holding (a homage ends and the faction sets up again, or the share falls) the stamp zeroes, `events.ReignBroken{Why}` (report-only: `The reign is over for now: a crew set up again.` / `the city slipped under the share`) opens the TIER section, the fast-forward stops, and it can begin again `dominant_days` on.
No day cap, nothing ends a run for playing on (#27).
**The crown** is the walk-away dialog's third row, `Take the crown` (`ui/exit.go`, `World.CanCrown`: `day 31 of the reign` while it holds, `the city is not yours` greyed out and refused otherwise, the digit `3` selects it); its confirmation (`TAKE THE CROWN?`, `y crown`) ends the run `kingpin` through `World.Crown` and `End` exactly as `Retire` and `Vanish` do, on the day it is taken.
The summary's `reached` row then reads `Kingpin on day N` (the reign's first morning, `reachedLine`) and the epilogue names the reign lived (`.Reign`, `31 days`, `content.Epilogue`: `The city was yours 31 days before you took the crown, and the crown lasted 4 years.`, the years the function of heat and pressure they were).
The score is unchanged, `Offshore / (1 + Bodies)`, so playing the reign on is allowed and never rewarded (`TestCrownIsTheScoreAsItStands`: the kingpin scenario crowned 0, 10 and 30 days into the reign scores the same and the summary's day is the crown's).
Guards: `TestNoEndingIsTheOldRun` holds (the boss never reaches `Dominant()` by day 200), `TestEveryEndingIsReachable`'s kingpin row is the reign beginning on day 15 and the crown taken the same morning (`harness.Crowned(Idle)`), `TestReignBreaks` (`sim/rivals`: the stamp `dominant_days` on, the crown open, a homage ending zeroes it and closes the crown, a new deal begins it again, the share falling breaks it, the sim never writes `Over`), `TestFastForwardStopsOnTheReign` (`ui`: the stop, the report, the dashboard, the second fast-forward running past it, the crown from the dialog, the summary; the row refused with no reign), `TestModalsFit` (`walk away: crown?`).
`TestSeedDigest` moved on day 1 by shape alone (`World.Reign` in the walk; with the field skipped the digest is the list before it on all sixty days); `TestSimsWriteOnlyTheirOwnState` lists `Reign` under the rivals sim; no band or seed-pinned number moved.

**The score** (#195's ruling): `Stats.Score = Offshore / (1 + Stats.Bodies)`, `World.Score()`, stamped by `End` the morning the run ends.
The pile left behind is printed, never scored; days are shown, never scored; so lying low k more days before an exit can never raise it (`TestRetireeRetires`, k = 0/10/30/60: 765,314 on seed 1 at every k) and the bodies divide it (`TestScoreIsTheAccount`).
`cmd/balance` prints `endings: map[cause: runs]; N of M ended, scoring $X (median)` (the median over the runs that ended; a run still going on the horizon has no score), documented in `docs/harness.md`.

**The summary** (`ui/summary.go`, `viewOver`) replaces the game-over table: the one modal titled `<TITLE> · DAY N`, the ending's title in `theme.Good` for a run won (`endings.toml won`) or `theme.Bad` for one lost with the day and the tier reached, the **epilogue** (a `text/template` from `endings.toml` over `content.Epilogue`: `.Days`, `.City`, `.Here`, `.Offshore`, `.Left`, `.Bodies`, `.Fronts`, `.Corners`, `.Name`, `.Leader`, `.DA`, `.Chief`, `.Pages`, `.Years`, `.Hot`; the kingpin's is a function of heat and pressure at home the morning it ends and nothing else: `[kingpin] reign_years` × (1 − (heat + pressure) / 200), rounded, a year at least, `KingpinConfig.Reign`, and `.Hot` picks who finished it), **THE STORY** (the top `[summary] lines` headlines of the run by the `[summary.weight]` of their source, the paper's notoriety in the reputation sim's sense, the latest first among equals, shown in the order they happened as `day N  text`), **THE MONEY** (the account marked `· the score`, the pile left behind in dirty, clean and units, peak wealth and revenue, the wash and the seizures and the losses, the levels), **THE PEOPLE** (the bodies and #46's fallen spelled `Name (role · day N)`, the betrayals: informants, defectors, lieutenants who walked, deals broken by them and by you; the best of the crew: the first of the fallen, else the member whose tenure × loyalty is highest), **THE CITY** (the three reputation bars on one row, the ground and the busts, the law with the chief's temper as you know it, #45) and the **SCORE** line with the account, the bodies, the days and, since #50, where the run ranks among your own (`· 3rd of 12 runs`, `rankLine`, `docs/profile.md`; the first line carries the daily's date and what the run unlocked in gold, `unlockedLine`); `Model.finish` is every path to `modeOver` and files the run in the profile once.
It scrolls at 80x24 (`↓ more`) and stands whole at 120x40 for every cause on a run with the fallen, the levels and the betrayals to print (`TestSummaryReadsTheRun`); `TestRendersAtCommonSizes` and `TestNoKeyHintsOutsideTheLegend` walk `rich game over <cause>` for every cause; `TestEndedSaveReloadsToTheSummary` saves a run ended by each cause and continues it straight to the summary with the epilogue rendered; `TestEveryEndingHasAnEpilogue` (`content`) walks the causes against the file, which `validate` checks at load (one row a cause, a title, an epilogue that parses, a scene line, `[summary] lines` and `[kingpin] reign_years` at least one).
`Ending`, `score` and `walk away` are help WORDS.

**Walking away** (`ui/exit.go`, `w` on the dashboard since #245, `modeExit`): one modal with two pages that asks twice.
The first lists the three ways out with their terms and whether each is open (`Retire`: `retire_cash` offshore and `retire_days` quiet, with what is short; `Vanish`: a new identity; `Take the crown`: the reign, #227 above), the cursor on the first open one; `enter` on an open one turns to the confirmation (a closed one is refused with what is short), `shift+tab` goes back, `y` ends the run there and then (any other key declines, as every confirmation does, #241) through `laundering.Sim.Retire` or `World.Vanish`, saves, and opens `modeOver` on the ending's scene as a morning would (`confirmExit`); `esc` closes.
`TestWalkAwayAsksTwice`, `TestModalsFit` (`walk away`, `walk away: retire?`, `walk away: vanish?`); the day does not step (`TestWalkAwayAsksTwice`).
Its first page's `a ambitions` opens the ambitions panel (#347, `docs/ambitions.md`).

**Rulings and why.**
`kingpin_share` is not in the issue: with the incident table on (the tool's default), #44's `rival_leader_killed` empties a three-faction table over an idle player in about a year, and a quiet trader standing on one corner read kingpin on 8 of 50 seeds by day 300 (the harness's `TestEveryIncidentFires` ran the idle player into it on eight seeds); a city nobody holds has no kingpin, so the reign needs more than half of home's corners, which the tipster never holds (its seed 7 read kingpin at 5 of 10 before the share) and the boss holds on one seed in fifty by day 211 with the weather on, the mechanic and not an accident.
`betray_corners` is not in the issue either: the boss's seed 6 collapsed to one corner with an unpaid lieutenant on it, and a flip over one corner is a leak, not a betrayal.
`taken_out` counts the enforcers on the payroll, laid up or not (muscle you have), because the UI's rich fixture, a hand-built war with one head of muscle, was taken out on a seed in twenty.
The kingpin's epilogue is deterministic in the heat and the pressure (`Reign`), never the dice.
`GameOver` is emitted by every owner and the clock breaks the order on it, so the news sim never sees it: report-only by construction.
