# The keys are one table

**The keys are one table** (#80, `ui/keys.go`, the tables in `ui/bindings.go` and `ReadmeKeys` with the README splices in `ui/readme.go` since #275, the epic's "Key legend" rule; #109 made the listing the screen's): `bindings` lists every key play mode accepts, once, as a `binding{key, label, help, keys, screens, modes, global, quiet, when, do}`: the key as the pane draws it (`↑↓`, `[ ]`, `␣`, `pgup pgdn`), a label of one or two lowercase words that is the same everywhere the key appears (`end day`, `post runner`, `send enforcers`, `fund city`, `route dial`, `go to <city>`, filled in by `labelOf`), a help sentence of at most 45 characters (the help modal's column), the raw `tea.KeyMsg` strings it answers to, `screens`, the only thing that lists it (the pane's KEYS shows a binding on exactly the screens it `names`; nil lists it nowhere), `global` for one that works on every screen, listed or not (#109: a key is listed where it is used and works wherever it makes sense, so every global names the screens it belongs on: `b buy`, `s sell`, `x cancel order` the dashboard and the market, `l lie low` the dashboard, `p pay dial` the crew, `d launder dial` the ledger, `g go to <city>` the dashboard and the map, `r report` the dashboard and the journal, `[ ] city` the market and the map (`[ ] faction` is the rivals screen's own, shadowing it there, #239), `↑↓ pick` the six list screens (`listScreens`: the map and the tree list their own `↑↓←→`), and `n end day` and `? help` every screen (`everywhere`), as does `␣ more`, which is live only under `paneMinWidth` (`when: stripShown`, #111), so `keysFor` lists it at 80 columns and not at 100; `c cart` is the dashboard's and the market's own, not global; a screen's own binding for the same key wins there and the global one is not listed: `b` buys a front on the ledger and bails a member on the crew screen (#46), `r` turns a route on the map, `x` and `d` answer a buyer on the market while the cursor is on the buyers, `when`; `d` on the map buys the block under the cursor, #194, `docs/property.md`), `quiet` for the frame's keys (`enter`, `1-9`, `tab`, `ctrl+s`, `N`, `q`) that help and the README list and the pane leaves out, and `do`, what it does.
The table's order is the pane's: `n end day`, the cursor keys, the screen's actions, the keys that work everywhere, `? help`.
`keyPlay` dispatches through `lookup` and nothing else: a letter pressed on a screen that does not take it is refused with `pointer`, one sentence per binding that does in the one spelling `<Verb> on the <screen> (<digit>)` (`Hire on the crew screen (4).`), and every pointer in prose is written with `screenPointer(screen)`; the arrows, the paging keys and a key the screen lists but that is not live now are silent.
`keysFor(screen)` is what the pane's KEYS renders (`paneKeys`): every live binding that names the screen, the quiet ones left out and none that an earlier binding for the same key shadows there, which is the rule `lookup` dispatches by, so a key is listed exactly where pressing it does what the label says (the status bar lists nothing, #109 above); `modeKeys(mode)` is each modal's footer from the second table, `modeBindings` (`modalFooter` reads it; pages through `modalStep`), `helpLines` is the help modal (the whole table grouped by screen: GLOBAL, every `global` binding once whatever screens list it, then each screen's own, a binding once under the first screen it names, then WORDS; #89 above) and `ReadmeKeys` the README's table, which `go run ./cmd/keys -w` writes between the `<!-- keys:begin -->` / `<!-- keys:end -->` markers.
No other text names a key: not a title, a table cell, a status or a modal body, bar the dashboard's `tutorialLine` (`No sales queued. Press s to sell, n to end the day.`, its two keys in `theme.Key`), and the pane's `keyRow`s.
`keys_test.go` pins it: `TestEveryKeyIsInTheTable` (the keys the old switch handled are the table's, no more, and a letter pressed where it does nothing is pointed where it does), `TestLegendMatchesTable` (the pane's KEYS is exactly `keysFor(screen)`, `n end day` first, `? help` last, `q` in neither, and nothing listed that does not `names` the screen), `TestGlobalsAreListedWhereUsed` (each global names the screens above and is taken on every screen), `TestHelpMatchesKeys`, `TestReadmeMatchesKeys` (the README is stale until `cmd/keys -w` runs) and `TestNoKeyHintsOutsideTheLegend` (a grep over every render of the rich fixture at 80x24 and 120x40: no `(4, i)`, `(map, 5)`, `press x`, `(f)` or `a accept · x decline`, and no `(digit)` but the tail of `on the <screen> (<digit>)`).

**Keys act on the shown cursor and wear the region's label** (#239): the map's `r route dial` and `R route target` refuse on a corner (`Pick a route: the dial is on the routes under the grid, not a corner.`) rather than turning the route the hidden routes cursor last sat on, and stay live on the map rather than gated by `mapOnRoutes` because the global `r report` is theirs to shadow there; `←→` on the routes turns the city, as the market's does; the market's `% cut` (`t` until #241) and `o cook` are live and listed on the product table alone (`onProducts`, `onProductsWithChemist`), silent on the buyers and the connects; a buyer's offer on the market and a faction's on rivals are answered with the same pair, `a accept` / `x decline` (`y` is a confirmation's yes and nothing else); and the market's `x decline` on the buyers shadows the global `x cancel order` there, one key with the region's meaning as the pane's label says, accepted rather than re-lettered.
`TestMapKeysActOnTheShownCursor` pins it.

**The new-run dialog and the summary's keys** (#50, `docs/profile.md`): `modeNewRun` lists `↑↓ pick`, `1-6 choose` and `enter next` on the picker (`enter start` with the cursor on the daily), the number field's row with `enter next` (or `enter start` while the hard DA is locked) on the seed, `←→ toggle` and `enter start` on the hard DA (`newRunNext`, `newRunStart`), `⇧tab back` past the first page and `esc close` (to the menu); the summary (`modeOver`) lists `n new run` (the same character, a fresh seed), `esc close` (to the menu, the ended save kept) and `q quit`.
`character`, `daily` and `profile` are help WORDS (39 now).

**The walk-away key** (#49, on the dashboard since #245): `w walk away` names the dashboard and nothing else, beside `l lie low` and `F fast-forward`, the other run-level keys (the map's `w send enforcers` is that screen's own), opens the two-page `modeExit` dialog (`↑↓ pick`, `1-9 choose`, `enter next` on the first page, `y retire`, `y vanish` or `y crown` (#227) on the confirmation by the row, `⇧tab back`, `esc close`), and `ending`, `score`, `walk away` and `reign` (#227) are help WORDS; `docs/endings.md`.
**The alert keys** (#352, `docs/ui.md`): `[ ] alert` and `o open alert` name the dashboard, live while there is an alert (`hasAlerts`); the dashboard's `[ ]` shadows the global `[ ] city` there as the rivals screen's `[ ] faction` does, and `o` is the market's cook and the ledger's reserve too, each screen's own; the report lists `o open alert` in its footer while it opens on a fast-forward stopped on an alert (`stoppedOnAlert`).
**The war order** (#229): `w` on the rivals screen is `declare war` while none is on and `call off war` while one is (two bindings on one key by `when`, `atWar` / `notAtWar`, each asked once in `modeConfirm`), the map's `w send enforcers` and the dashboard's `w walk away` being those screens' own; `war` is a help WORD; `docs/rival.md`.

**The restock key** (#356): `R restock` names the market and nothing else (the map's `R route target` is that screen's own), opens `modeRestock` (`docs/cart.md`), whose footer is the number field's row, `enter buy` and `esc close`.
