# The rival

**The rival** (`internal/sim/rivals`, `World.Rival()`, tuning in `rivals.toml`) is the faction at home: since #43 one of three to six (`World.Rivals`, the table, its own paragraph at the end of this file), the first of them, with a leader, a personality (`expansionist / defensive / opportunist / chaotic`) and a starting trust in the player from the seed. Every reader that means "the rival" reads `w.Rival()`; every sim method that reads a faction's books, odds or table takes the `*game.RivalState` (`Odds(w, r, force)`, `Income(w, r)`, `Chance(w, r, d)`, `Stale(r, day)`, ...). **It has an id and one writer** (#144, groundwork for #43): `RivalState.ID` is the faction id, seeded with `game.FactionRival` (`"rival"`) and read through `RivalState.Faction()`, which resolves the zero id of a save from before ids to it, so no migration and no schema bump (`TestOldSaveNamesTheFaction`; `game.Load` stamps the id onto an old save's rival corners on the way in). `Corner.Faction` names the faction holding a corner while `Owner` is `OwnerRival` (`OwnerRival` keeps meaning "a rival", the id says which) and is `""` otherwise: every hand-over to the rival stamps it (`rivals.Sim.take`, the lieutenant's walk) and every hand-over off it clears it (a strike taken, an abandoned corner, a crackdown, a raid, `World.Post`, `World.Abandon`, the territory sim's drift); `TestRivalInvariants` pins it every day of three policies. Every event that names the rival (`Rival`, the leader's name, for the headline) carries `Faction` beside it, twenty-two of them: `CrewDefected`, `CornerLost` (the faction that lost a corner to a crackdown, `""` for the player), `RivalMovedIn`, `CornerTaken`, `RivalEyeing`, `RivalOutbid`, `RivalPushed`, `CornerStruck`, `RivalTippedPolice`, `RivalUndercut`, `DealOffered`, `DealAccepted`, `DealRefused`, `DealBroken`, `DealEnded`, `TributePaid`, `LieutenantWalked`, `RivalAbandoned`, `RivalBoosted`, `PoliceTipped`, `RivalRaided`, `RivalMusclePoached` (`TestRivalInvariants` reads them off every event by reflection). `TestRivalStateHasOneWriter` (`sim/rivals/writers_test.go`) is the source grep that no sim but `rivals` assigns into `w.Rival` or takes its address: a defector is a lead on the crew sim's own queue (`CrewState.Leads`, written fresh every crew step, read by the rivals sim next step, the day it always was; `Rival.Leads` is gone), and the one exception, listed so the list can only shrink, is the lieutenant's walk booking its flip (`Flips`, `LastFlip`, `Observed`) the night the corners change hands (`docs/lieutenants.md` says why). It moves in on `arrive_day` on a corner away from you, earns off its corners, claims free ones by personality up to `max_share` of home's corners (`rivals.Sim.MaxCorners`: 0.6 of ten is six), pushes on the corners of yours it borders (`Corner.Borders`, `World.Contested`; odds from its muscle on the front line against `rivals.Sim.Guard`: an enforcer, you, a runner, plus the tree's `guard_bonus`, the front line, a body on every held corner, so the push odds the map shows fall on a bare corner and a guarded one alike and a defector's lead is no longer walked onto unopposed but pushes at those odds; and the push chance is `rivals.Sim.PushPace`, fear's cut times the tree's `rival_push_mul`, held ground; both fold through `rivals.Sim.Effects` where they are read, #119, and the strike odds and the claim pace read neither), undercuts you there (`Corner.Squeeze` cuts the corner's `Share`, plus a small street-price drag) and tips off the police while it holds a grudge. `SendEnforcers(corner, force)` is the player's answer, on the `events.Force` dial `warn / push / hit`: `rivals.Sim.Odds` is what the picker shows and what the dice use, heat scales with the corner, and every strike or push feeds `Rival.War`; past `crackdown_threshold` the police clear both sides' front line. A corner won by force is held with nobody on it, so post a runner before it drifts. `harness.Warlike` and `cmd/balance -policy war -force F -rival P` measure it; `rivals_test.go` in the harness pins the acceptance criteria (a passive player loses corners to an expansionist within 60 days, a hit war reaches the patrol line faster than aggressive selling, ownership / cash / muscle / squeeze invariants). The expansionist is the hard draw: it keeps pushing past its cap at half pace, so a player who never guards or fights loses ground for as long as they let it. **Its pace is data** (#60, `rivals.toml [pace]`, folded in from #69, which keeps the tell): its claim chance is scaled by the player's share of home's corners between `claim_scale_min` (0.25, nothing held) and `claim_scale_max` (1.5, all of it; `rivals.Sim.ClaimScale`), cut by fear (`reputation.toml rival_claim_cut`, `rivals.Sim.ClaimPace`, read the way `PushPace` reads `fear_push_cut`), and after any claim it waits `claim_cooldown` (12) days before it claims again unless the player's enforcers have been in within those days (`Rival.LastStruck`; `rivals.Sim.Rested`), its arrival not counting as a claim; for `arrive_grace` (5) days after it arrives it never sets up on a corner the player has ever held (`Corner.Yours`, stamped by the territory sim). The cooldown roll is still made, so the seed's dice do not move, and while it waits it pushes at `push_past_cap` as it does at its cap, because a slower spread must not be a longer fight at full pace (with pushes unpaced the expansionist took eight of the delegated player's corners on seed 2 where the flat pace took four). Against an expansionist a player holding half the city sees a median of 3 rival corners at day 30 (6 before) and 9 at day 120 (9 before); `cmd/balance` prints the medians and `-pace off` is the flat pace. **The claim is telegraphed** (#69, the tell): the day the claim roll succeeds the sim picks the corner (`pickFree`, as before) and *eyes* it (`Rival.Eyeing`, `Rival.EyeingDay`, `RivalEyeing{Corner}`, headline and report line, the reason named only once `Observed`), stamping `LastClaim` that day so the cooldown runs from the choice and the cadence #60 set is unchanged, and on the next step (`rivals.Sim.resolveEyeing`) sets up on it, `claim_cost` spent, unless the corner is held by then (a `Post` of you, a runner or an enforcer, the crew step's delegation counting since it runs between; an enforcer alone holds it, and the rival never walks onto held ground without a push), in which case the claim fails, nothing is spent, `Grudge` rises by `outbid_grudge` and `RivalOutbid{Corner}` is emitted, or dropped silently where it can no longer act (a split sealed overnight covers it, its share reached, routed). No new dice: the roll is the one made before, resolved a day later, so a pinned seed that reads a single day can shift (the claim lands a day later). The map marks the eyed corner `?` in `theme.RivalText` with `theirs tomorrow` in its cell, the inspector reads `they set up here tomorrow` with `c post a runner to keep them off`, the dashboard's RIVALS line (LAW's last line at 80 columns) and the rivals screen's leader line carry `eyeing Riverside` (`eyeingWord`; the tell outranks the temper, the count and the name where the line is short), and fast-forward stops on `RivalEyeing`, the one thing it exists to give the player a day for. `harness.Outbidder` / `harness.Outbid` is the scripted player that posts a runner on the eyed corner the morning of the tell; `TestTellIsAnswerable` pins that every claim then fails on every seed with the rival's cash unspent and the grudge risen, and `sim/rivals` pins the tell, the outbid's exact cash and the stale tells. **The price war is the third answer, money** (#68, `rivals.toml [pricewar]`, `game/territory.go`, `sim/market/pricewar.go`, `ui/rivals.go`'s `modeUndercut`): `World.Undercut(corner, dial)` queues a price war on a rival corner for tonight in the per-day scratch `World.Today.Undercuts` (`CancelUndercut`, `Undercutting`; `u` on the map opens the picker, the three dials with the share, the units, the price and the rival's loss, `stop` calls it off; the cell is marked `$` in `theme.MarketText` and the inspector carries `u  undercut: takes ~N% at normal, no heat`, `undercut  normal · takes ~N/day` while one is queued and `squeezed  -N% by you, 3 days` the morning after), legal only on a rival corner next door to a corner you work (`World.NextDoor`: the demand of your worked corners bordering it, capped at `NextDoorMax` standard corners, the mirror of `Contested`; `ErrNotNextDoor`) and refused under a truce or a tribute (`ErrAtPeace`) and on the rival's side of a split's line, the way `offLimits` keeps its undercutting off yours (`World.CanUndercut` is the one check, asked again at night). **The market resolves it inside tonight's orders** at home (`resolveAt` → `share` → `undercut`; a fresh order or a standing one): each undercut corner adds `Steal(w, c, dial)` = `steal × the dial's impact × NextDoor` of its full demand for the product (`Corner.Full`, the share with no squeeze on it) as a pool on top of your own corners' capacity, served at the dial's price less `price_cut`, an order too small for both pools shared pro rata (so a price war always bites when you sell at all, and the bite is units sold for less: the crewed player is bag-limited), with no order for a product nothing moving for it, and the undercut units weigh `glut` each in the order's price impact over your own corners' demand, so a long price war crashes the product it is fought with. The units are part of the night's `PlayerSold` (`Undercut`, `UndercutRevenue`), so heat, the law, notoriety and a lieutenant's or standing cut count them as the sales they are; `PlayerUndercut{Corner, Product, Units, Share, Revenue, Dial}` is the report-only breakdown per corner (one SALES line each). The rival's corners at home start every market step unsqueezed and `closeWar` writes the share of each undercut corner's trade taken at the opening prices onto `Corner.Squeeze`, which `rivals.Sim.Income` reads as less income there without knowing who did it; the rivals sim's own `undercut` zeroes and rewrites the squeeze on every corner that is not its own and leaves its own to the market. **The rival answers by temper** (`rivals.Sim.pricewar`, before its claims): a squeezed corner is a day starved (`Corner.Starved`, `StarvedDay`; a rest of more than `pricewar_days` forgets the count, so a war fought every other day, which is how a player at the lie-low line fights it, still reaches the line, later), every undercut day adds `war` (2) to `Rival.War` and no `City.Pressure`, and a corner starved `pricewar_days` (5) days gets its answer: a defensive or an expansionist rival pushes on the corner doing the cutting (`cutter`: your biggest worked corner next door) at `push_chance × pricewar_push` (2.0) through the usual push odds (`RivalPushed` / `CornerTaken` marked `Pricewar`), an opportunist gives the corner up (`RivalAbandoned{Reason: "pricewar"}`, the corner free and unsqueezed, a headline, and fast-forward stops on it: a corner to post on), a chaotic one rolls between the two, and the answer, when it comes, holds a grudge if `grudge` says (a grudge every undercut day made the price war hotter than a hit war: fourteen calls in 120 days). No fear from any of it; notoriety through the units as any sale. No new dice on a corner nobody undercut, and the rival corners' squeeze is zero in a run with none, so a run that never undercuts is byte-for-byte the old run (`TestNoUndercutIsTheOldNight`, `TestNoUndercutIsTheOldRun`; no pinned number moved). `harness.Pricewar` (`cmd/balance -policy pricewar -undercut quiet|normal|aggressive`) is `territory` that undercuts the biggest rival corner next door every day it can instead of striking; `harness/pricewar_test.go` pins the invariants (squeeze in 0..1, a rival corner squeezed only by a war the report names, the units never over `Steal` of the corner's demand, your own corners' demand the corners' whatever is squeezed, nothing under a truce or a tribute), the rival's answers (opportunists give corners up, expansionists push back, a defensive rival never gives one up), the margin cost (median net worth at day 120 $783k against `crewed`'s $833k; `territory`'s at the same corners is $714k, the ground an opportunist gives up being worth more than the discount costs), that it is the quieter answer against a defensive rival (heat from the fight 12 against the hit war's 49 over 120 days, the war at its loudest 16 against 41; both lie low at the same line, so the gauge reads the same), determinism and the save. **The rival's money is priced in the ladder's unit** (#139, `rivals.toml [rivals]`, `rivals.Sim.CornerDay`): a corner-day is what a standard corner at home earns it in a day, `margin` (0.3) of the street value the corner moves in the products the street there sells (`rivals.Sim.Standard`; the port's product, `no_supply` at home, comes by the road the rival does not run, so designer is out of its take and out of `CornerIncome`, and its take is heroin-and-meth-sized, $11k–$35k a day at day 120, not the $300k–$800k designer made it), and its wage (`muscle_wage` 0.9 a corner's guard, a head earning that over the personality's `muscle_per_corner`: `Sim.Wage`, `Wages`), its hires (`muscle_fee` 0.5, `Sim.Fee`), its claims (`claim_cost` 1.0, `Sim.ClaimCost`) and the chest it arrives with (`start_cash` 80) are so many corner-days, so they climb the ladder with the take. **Its muscle is what the take pays for**: the wages come out of the chest, what the day's take did not cover of them is owed (`Rival.Arrears`, a running account a surplus day pays down; zero value the pre-#139 state, no schema bump), and at a full wage owed a head walks, one a day, never one of the `start_muscle` it came with; it recruits up to `muscle_per_corner` per corner plus one (`Sim.Want`) and no further than the take pays for (`Sim.Afford`), while the chest covers a fee and a claim besides, and the claim roll is made before the chest is asked, so a rival that cannot pay for a corner today rolls all the same and no seed's dice move when its money binds. A rival left alone (`territory`, five seeds) holds a chest of 17 (defensive), 24 (expansionist), 27 (opportunist) and 18 (chaotic) days of its wage bill at day 120 and never runs it dry, its take a tenth over its wages on three defensive corners and a third over on eight expansionist ones; a price war fought in earnest (every corner undercut nightly with a stash that fills the pool, `pricewarFixture`) takes a third of a defensive rival's take and two of its seven heads within the month, dice-free, while the harness's `pricewar` at normal (one corner, the nights the bag-limited player sells) takes one to four points of it, under the head that is a tenth of the take, and its muscle per corner held reads level with `territory`'s (`TestPricewarStarvesTheMuscle`, `TestRivalEconomyBinds`; `harness.RivalBooks` reads the take and the bill, `cmd/balance` prints both with the chest at the pace days). **The books are the fourth answer, beside the enforcers, the table and the price war** (#70, `rivals.toml [books]`, `[boost]`, `[tip]`, `[poach]`; `sim/rivals/books.go`; `game/rivals.go` for `Scout`, `Tip`, `BuyOff` and `Known`, `Boost` beside `SendEnforcers` in `game/territory.go`; `ui/books.go`; `harness.Saboteur`). The rival is a machine with a weak point since #139 (its muscle is what the take pays for, its chest what a hire or a claim needs), and four moves make it legible and let the player drain it, each queued as intent and resolved in the rival's step, each confirmed first in the UI with the numbers the dice use (`modeConfirmScout`, `modeConfirmBoost`, `modeConfirmTip`, `modeConfirmBuyOff`, the last a `numberField` for the heads). **Scouting** (`World.Scout(cost)`, `World.Today.Scouting` scratch, `i` on the rivals screen; `i` on the crew screen still investigates and `pointer` names both) buys a look at the books at `scout_cost` with `rivals.Sim.ScoutOdds` (base, the best enforcer's skill, `scout_learn` per empty night in `Rival.Scouted`: the shape of `crew.Sim.InvestigateOdds`, so the two read the same), resolved at the end of the step off `Tick.Sub("books")`: a read stamps `Rival.Known` (`game.Known{Day, Cash, Income, Muscle, Wages}`, one snapshot with one day, nothing else writes it) with the night's closing numbers, and it goes **stale** after `stale_days` (`Sim.Stale`); a second scout the same night is refused (`ErrScouting`, the investigation's rule) rather than charged. the strike picker's rows 4–6, `boost warn/push/hit`, in one picker with one `rivals.Sim.Odds` and a `for` column, `the corner` or `~$13K`) sends the enforcers for the corner's **till**, not the ground: at the force's odds on the strike's roll (one roll either way, on the home stream) it takes `take` (1.0) of the corner's day of takings (`Sim.BoostTake`: `take x trade`, the street value the corner moved, a price war's squeeze off, which is its income over `margin`; at push or hit under a live deal it is a betrayal through `rivals.Sim.crossed` as a strike is, at warn it is not; it is fear, not respect (`reputation.toml [fear] boost`), and `law.toml [pressure] boost` (3) at home. **Tipping** (`World.Tip(corner)`, `World.Today.Tipoff` scratch, `t` on the map with a rival corner under the cursor; a raid is not a rout, so a rival raided off its last corner sets up again at its own pace rather than regrouping); a tip costs `tip.trust`, a raid holds `tip.grudge` (a grudge a *tip* made an expansionist push at full pace past its cap and take the saboteur's corners faster than the passive player's), under a truce or a tribute a tip is a **betrayal** of every deal (`rivals.Sim.breakAll`, the truce binds the rival to no tips so it binds you; determinism, the save with every move queued, and `TestNoBooksIsTheOldRun` (the territory player's rival keeps heat 0 and unread books: no pinned number moved, t1–t4 $84,930 / $605,294 / $17.27M / $82.3M before and after). `sim/rivals/books_test.go` pins the arithmetic: the boost's exact take, heat, war and toll, a failed boost's hurt, the betrayal at push and not at warn, the raid at the line with the cooldown and the heads away, the rival's heat needing a tip to start, the buy-off's price and refund and the grudge on failure, and the scout's snapshot, stale days and empty nights. **The table** (#43, `rivals.toml [factions]`, `sim/rivals/factions.go`, `game/factions.go`, `harness/factions_test.go`, `sim/rivals/factions_test.go`).
