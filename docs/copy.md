# Copy, colour and status

**Copy, colour and status** (#88, `ui/copy_test.go`).
Since #233 the sims' multipliers are also said in words where the player reads them: the dashboard pane's YOUR NAME section, the rivals screen's `your name:` line, the pool's `asked to work for you`, the report's opening lines under TIER and the paper's `{{.Title}}` (`docs/reputation.md`); a number in words is the number the dice use, never a rounding of its own.
One voice: terse, second person, present tense, sentence case, real plurals (every count through `plural`, no `(s)` in a string of `internal/ui` or `sim/news`; `TestNoPluralHacks` greps the sources and every render of the rich fixture for `(s)`, `->`, ` ·  ` and a count two spaces after a caps title), `→` for a change, `×` for a multiplier (`×1.2`, `(×1.4)`), days as `3d` in tables and strips and `3 days` in prose.
**A card's chips** (#358, `docs/dilemmas.md`) are the one voice at its tersest: a lowercase noun then a signed move (`dirty −$12K`, `crew loyalty +8`, `respect +5`), `−` (U+2212) and `+` as signs, money through `format.Cash`, a crossed line as `before → after: over the sting line`, a grudge as what it means (`grudge +20: Big Sal's crew remember`), a corner and a favour as deeds (`give up Rail Yard`, `you'll owe a favour`), joined with ` · `; a hidden card says `costs you something`, a choice that moves nothing `changes nothing`.
**Status**: every site sets the kind through one of three helpers, `say` (a confirmation, `Body`), `refuse` (a refusal, `Warning`, ended with a full stop where the site left it off, so `Can't hire: ` + a game error reads as a sentence) and `alarm` (a danger, `Bad`); a refusal starts `Can't …` or `Nothing to …` (the key table's pointers, `Hire on the crew screen (4).`, are refusals too), a dialog error goes through `dialogError` (sentence case, a full stop: `Enter a whole number above zero.`, `Can't afford or hold any.`), and the morning after the tell has shown twice the status bar carries `Somebody is talking. Investigate on the crew screen (4).` in red over the save line (`TestStatusKinds`).
**Empty states** are sentences in `Subtle`, sentence case, a full stop, every one through `emptyState` (#240: its parts alternate prose and key, so a line with no key is `emptyState(text)` and the dashboard's `tutorialLine` is one with two; the ledger's PAYOFFS line names no key, `$` being in KEYS beside it), the key named the legend's way (`Nobody on the payroll. Pick a face below and press h.`, `No fronts yet. …press b to buy one.`, `No deals. Press d to propose one.`, `Nobody right now.`, `Nothing on the table.`, `Nobody is asking. Offers come here and lapse in a few days.`); the rival's and the paper's `Yet.` lines stay, they are the voice (`TestEmptyStates`).
**Status vocabularies**, lowercase in tables, and **one meaning per word** (#238): the table below lists each state word once with the one thing it means and where it is read; `TestStateWords` reads it (no word twice) and pins the words on the screens.
