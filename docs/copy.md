# Copy, colour and status

**Copy, colour and status** (#88, `ui/copy_test.go`). One voice: terse, second person, present tense, sentence case, real plurals (every count through `plural`, no `(s)` in a string of `internal/ui` or `sim/news`; `TestNoPluralHacks` greps the sources and every render of the rich fixture for `(s)`, `->`, ` ·  ` and a count two spaces after a caps title), `→` for a change, `×` for a multiplier (`×1.2`, `(×1.4)`), days as `3d` in tables and strips and `3 days` in prose. **Status**: every site sets the kind through one of three helpers, `say` (a confirmation, `Body`), `refuse` (a refusal, `Warning`, ended with a full stop where the site left it off, so `Can't hire: ` + a game error reads as a sentence) and `alarm` (a danger, `Bad`); a refusal starts `Can't …` or `Nothing to …` (the key table's pointers, `Hire on the crew screen (4).`, are refusals too), a dialog error goes through `dialogError` (sentence case, a full stop: `Enter a whole number above zero.`, `Can't afford or hold any.`), and the morning after the tell has shown twice the status bar carries `Somebody is talking. Investigate on the crew screen (4).` in red over the save line (`TestStatusKinds`). **Empty states** are sentences in `Subtle`, sentence case, a full stop, the key named the legend's way through `emptyState` (`Nobody on the payroll. Pick a face below and press h.`, `No fronts yet. …press b to buy one.`, `No deals. Press d to propose one.`, `Nobody right now.`, `Nothing on the table.`, `Nobody is asking. Offers come here and lapse in a few days.`); the rival's and the paper's `Yet.` lines stay, they are the voice (`TestEmptyStates`). **Status vocabularies**, lowercase in tables: fronts `open` / `opens tomorrow` / `audit, back in 14d` / `shut, back in 2d` (`frontStatus`); crew `idle` / `unposted` / `no city` / `no front` / `<corner>` / `runs <city>` / `1 front` / `3 fronts` (`post`); corners `free` / `theirs` / `nobody, 3d left` / `you` / `<name>`, with `⚔ push tonight` on the rival's corner the enforcers go against. **Colour**: `theme` is the only package that builds a `lipgloss.Style` (`TestThemeIsTheOnlyStylist`; a view file takes `theme.Fg(colour)`, `theme.Heading(colour)`, `theme.SourceText(name)` and `theme.Plain` for its layout sizing), one meaning each: `MarketText`/`Good` the market and good, `LawText`/`Bad` the law and danger, `RivalText` theirs only, `CrewText` yours (the player's corner count is never purple), `RoadText` the road, `NewsText` the paper, `Warning` a warning line and the reputation and dilemma accent; `Selected` is the cursor row and the current tab and nothing else; every dial (sell, pay, launder, the fund dialog's city) is drawn by `dialCells` as `quiet  [normal]  aggressive` with the chosen notch bracketed in `theme.Dial(on)` (`DialOn`), the route's pane row one bracketed notch, `[slow]`, red at fast. `game.Deal.String()` is the report's words (`a split: 2 corners your side of the line`, `tribute of $1,200 a day`), `World.Describe` names the corners (`a split: yours Rail Yard`) and `World.Side` is the corners alone, so the rivals table and the propose dialog's `live:` note read `yours Rail Yard` without a second `a split:`.
