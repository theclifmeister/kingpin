# Dilemma cards

**Dilemma cards** (#15, `internal/content/dilemmas.toml`, `game/dilemmas.go`, `sim/news/dilemmas.go`, `ui/card.go`) interrupt the day with a choice.
The news sim, last in the order, deals at most one a day from the day's RNG: none for `min_gap` days after the last, then a chance rising each day to certainty at `max_gap` (one roll a day whatever happens, so the stream is fixed for the seed), then a weighted pick over the eligible cards with ones already drawn thinned by `1/(1+drawn)` and `once` cards retired.
`[card.trigger]` fields (`content.CardTrigger`) must all hold, and the ones that name somebody fill the slots (`game.CardSlots`, `Slots` being the save slots' list: the least loyal member under `loyalty_below`, the most loyal over `loyalty_above` or of `role`, the biggest worked corner for `corners` (the corner the named member is posted to first, when the card names one, #421: "Pep took twenties on The Projects" was dealt with Pep on The Docks, `TestCardNamesTheMembersCorner`), the pair for `contested`, the first front) deterministically, no dice; `game.Eligible` is that check (in `game/dilemmas.go` beside `FoldEffects` since #144: a query over `World` and content with no sim behind it, which the news sim, the market's buyers and the progression's tiers share, so no sim imports another, `TestSimsNeverImportEachOther`) and `TestTriggersHold` in `news/dilemmas_test.go` tables it.
A slot is filled only by the trigger fields that name it: `Name` and `Role` by `role`, `loyalty_below` or `loyalty_above`; `Corner` by `corners` or `contested`; `Theirs` by `contested`; `Front` by `fronts`; `City`, `Rival`, `Product` and `Amount` for every card.
**`Rival` is the faction the card is about** (#385, `Card.Faction`, `CardSlots.Faction`): the one on the corner a contested card borders, else the standing faction holding most corners in the card's city, else most anywhere (`game.cardFaction`); a gone faction (absorbed, scattered, fragmented, its corners still drifting) is never it, and a contested card borders only a standing faction's corner. The `rival`, `personality` and `war_min` triggers read that faction and fail when no standing faction holds a corner; `war`, `grudge`, `rival_muscle` and `rival_cash` land on it, and its preview and chips name it. With nobody standing the slot names the rival at home, as before, and `Card.Faction` "" (a save on a card from before) is the rival at home. Before, every card named and hit `World.Rival()`: a seed-41 replay dealt "Uncle Roy is in hospital" thirteen days after the incident killed him, and "Push now" warred on a dead faction while Lena held the corner. The duel is the old run (the seed digest moved on day 5 only because the day's card carries the new field; the run's world is byte-for-byte over 200 days); on the full table `managed`, `boss` and `war` are byte-for-byte and `tipster` moved (30 runs, 200 days: 29 indicted against 28, 2 scattered against 3, `dominant on` 120 days against 197) (`TestCardIsAboutAStandingFaction`).
An empty slot is no template error, so it rendered as a hole (#349: the funeral card, on `crew_min` alone, opened "'s brother was shot"; it now also sets `loyalty_above = 1`, the most loyal member's brother); `news.New` refuses a card whose title, text, labels, outcomes or follow-up use a slot its trigger never fills (`checkSlots` in `news/dilemmas.go`, walking the parsed templates, so a slot inside an `if` counts), and `TestEverySlotIsFilledByItsTrigger` pins the shipped deck passing and each misplaced slot refused.
The card is rendered when drawn (`game.Card`, text, choices and effects included) into `World.Dilemmas.Pending`, so a save on a card restores it without the content and `World.Choose(i)` resolves it: effects apply at once in sorted key order through one table, `game.effects` (#274: a `map[string]func(w *World, c *Card, v float64)`, the one place that knows what every key does; `EffectKeys` is its keys sorted, `KnownEffect` a lookup in it and `applyEffect` one key through it, so the list of keys and what applies them cannot drift). `Choose` then stamps the high-water marks (`World.stampPeaks`, the clock's nightly stamp), so a card that pays out in the morning raises `Stats.PeakCash` and `PeakClean` the day it lands and the CASH panel's peak never reads below the pile (#439, `TestChooseStampsThePeaks`).
Every key of the choice is checked before any applies, so a key it does not know is an error that leaves the world untouched and the card still pending (`TestUnknownEffectChangesNothing`; it used to apply the keys sorted before the bad one); `news.New` refuses a deck with one, so a typo fails at start-up, the outcome goes in the journal with source `dilemma`, and `Dilemmas.Answered` (per-day scratch) carries the optional follow-up headline for the news sim to print the next morning.
Cash keys clamp at zero, heat/loyalty/war at 0..100; `*_amount` keys are multiples of the card's `Amount` (`game.CardSum`: `amount_share` of dirty cash, at least `amount`, rounded to two figures, at most `amount_max`) so a card can name a sum and then take it; `fear`, `respect`, `notoriety` move `Player.Reputation` at once, clamped to 0..100, and the reputation sim applies its sum cap that evening as it does to every source.

## Stakes (#342)

A playtest's furnace cost millions and a card game $1.9M: the sum had no top, so a personal card grew with the bag and past a few million every choice was only a balance change.
Every card declares `stakes`, and `content` validation refuses one without (`TestEveryCardDeclaresItsStakes`): `personal` is a fixed range, `amount` up to a required `amount_max` (a personal card that names no sum, `cops_daughter`, needs none), and `business` keeps the share of the bag with an optional `amount_max`.
The sum is `min(amount_max, nice(max(amount, amount_share x dirty)))`, capped after the rounding so the cap is never passed; `TestPersonalStakesAreCapped` renders every card against $100M dirty and holds each at its cap.
**A card is what you can pay** (#384): a card with a choice that pays its whole sum out of one pile (`clean_amount` or `dirty_amount` of -1 or less; `game.payingPile`, the smaller pile when both) names no more than that pile holds, rounded down to two figures and never under `amount`, and is not drawn at all while the pile is under its `amount`. Before, the effect's clamp at zero charged what the pile held while the text and the outcome named the full sum (`permit` on day 37 of a seed-41 replay: "$4,100, in clean money", charged $2,550), and `cousins_books` paid 70% of a $1,000 wash in clean off $300 dirty. The text, the preview, the outcome and the charge are one sum (`TestCardSumIsWhatThePileCanPay`).
The pass: the furnace (`mother_calls`) $250 to $5K; the card game $10K to $100K and on $20K in hand (it was $500 on $3K; the real cost is being seen, which is its `notoriety`); wages, bail, the gun, the funeral, the witness's rent, the church roof and the car capped at what the thing costs; the stash, the shipment, the bulk buyer and the cards that name no sum stay uncapped business; the bribes and the car wash are business with a cap (`lose_a_file` and `detective_lean` $100K, `sergeant` and `permit` $50K, `cousins_books` $250K).
The **rich band** closes the file: seven cards gated on `peak_cash_min = 500000` whose choices move ground, people and favours, not a bigger number (`TestRichCardsMoveMoreThanCash`: every card gated on the peak or weighted up past the rich tier has a choice with a key that is not cash): selling a corner to buy peace (`peace_for_a_corner`), the councilman's favour (`councilman`, `once`) and its collection (`favour_due`), an insult at a wedding (`the_wedding`: the grudge and the member's loyalty), a lieutenant who wants a say (`a_say`), the mayor's gala (`the_gala`) and the grandmothers' petition (`the_petition`).
Two keys joined `game.effects` for it: `corner` (negative only) gives the card's corner to the street through `World.Abandon`, a corner lost since the draw being nothing to give; `owes` runs up `World.Dilemmas.Owes`, the favours owed, which the trigger field `owes_min` reads, so the councilman's favour comes due as a later card.
Both write only state a card already wrote or the deck owns: `Abandon` is the player's own action and `Owes` is the deck's; the issue's `goodwill`/`pressure` keys were left out, those being the law sim's (`TestSimsWriteOnlyTheirOwnState` names them its own, and goodwill reaches it only as `Today.Funded`), so the councilman buys heat instead.
`news.New` refuses a `corner` on a card whose trigger names no corner, a positive one, and one on the first choice, the one `enter` takes: ground is given up on purpose (`TestDeckRefusesAStrayCorner`).
From `rich_tier` (4, Distribution) a card is drawn at its `weight_rich`, or at `rich_rest` (0.2) times its weight if it sets none; the band is at 4 and the cards that only cost money at 0.1, so a rich player's cards are mostly the band (`TestRichDeckLeansToTheBand`: 0.60 of a rich year's cards, 0.12 with the lean off).
`harness.Run` plays with the deck boxed, so no money band moves; `RunWith` chooser runs deal the new cards and the capped sums once the peak passes $500K.
`Owes` is a zero field on `DilemmaState` (no schema bump) and moved `TestSeedDigest` on day 1 by shape alone.

## What a choice costs (#358)

**Every card shows what each choice costs before you choose** (#358, `game/preview.go`, `engine/card.go`).
Every effect is deterministic, so nothing is uncertain at the moment of choosing; the uncertainty is downstream (a grudge becomes a war, heat trips a rung, loyalty crosses the skim line).
`Card.Preview(w, i)` applies choice `i` through `Card.apply`, the path `Choose` takes, on a copy of the world (`World.clone`, the save's gob encoding), and returns what moved as `game.Change`s (`Key`, `Member`, `From`, `To`) over `game.Gauges`: dirty and clean cash, the heat where you stand, each member's loyalty, the war, grudge, muscle and cash of the faction the card is about (#385), the units you hold, the corners you hold, the favours you owe (`Dilemmas.Owes`), and the three reputation axes.
The clamps land as they will, so the preview is the outcome; the reputation sim's cap on the axes' sum is applied that evening, so it is a note, not a number.
`engine.ChoiceChips(cfg, rules, w, c)` puts each choice's changes in words, one `Chip` (`Text`, `Tone`: `gain`, `cost`, `line`, `note`) a thing: `dirty −$12K`, `heat +7`, `Vee's loyalty +8` (the named member) or `crew loyalty +8` (everyone, `up to +8` where a clamp made them differ), `war with Big Sal's crew +5`, `their muscle −1`, `their cash −$2,500`, `stock −120`, `respect +5`, `give up Rail Yard` (the card's corner, the `corner` key) and `you'll owe a favour` (the `owes` key, in the `line` tone: the favour comes due as a later card).
Where a move crosses a line the world already knows, the chip says so in words and never in dice, in the `line` tone: `heat 53 → 59: over the sting line` (the highest rung of `Heat.Ladder` crossed; `back under` going down), `Vee's loyalty 32 → 27: under the skim line` (`skim_threshold`; `over` going up), `grudge +20: Big Sal's crew remember`. A crew loyalty chip takes its tone from its text: one member down and the rest up reads `crew loyalty down to −23` and is a cost, never a gain (#384, `TestMixedCrewLoyaltyIsACost`).
The rival's figures are deltas only: the view leaves its grudge, chest and muscle out, and the card does not leak them.
A choice that pushes the axes past `total` gets `more than the street can hold: it evens out tonight`; one that moves nothing says `changes nothing`.
**`hide = true`** on a card (`CardConfig.Hide`, carried on `game.Card.Hide`) is for a card whose drama is the unknown: every choice shows `costs you something` (`engine.Hidden`) and no figure.
Use it sparingly: the turncoat, who might be a plant, is the one card that has it.
The TUI draws the chips under each label, indented to the label's text, in the theme's tones (a gain `Good`, a cost `Bad`, a line `Warning`, a note `Subtle`), joined with ` · ` and folded to the modal's width (`cardChoices`, `foldChips`); they are worked out once a card (`cardChips`), the modal scrolls, and the cursor keeps its whole choice in view, label first.
The card's scene reserves the chips' rows, so the box is its finished size from the first frame.
The engine's view carries the same chips (`CardView.Choices[].Preview`, view 4) and the web client draws them under each button.
`TestPreviewIsTheOutcome` (harness) holds every card in the deck, the rich band's included, on four worlds two runs reached, two of them broke and hot, and one with a $5M bag, the card naming a corner you hold, to `Preview(i)` being the change `Choose(i)` makes, gauge for gauge, and a corner given up and a favour owed among what moved; `TestPreviewNeverWritesTheWorld` digests the world around a preview of every key; `TestChoiceChipsSayTheLines` (engine) pins the words, the lines, the corner and the favour, `hide` and the view; `TestCardShowsWhatEachChoiceDoes` (ui) fits the longest card with its chips at 80x24, 100x30 and 120x40.

## Showing and testing

The UI shows the card in `modeCard` before the report (`showCard`): the card is dealt, its title decrypting and its prose wiping in, any key skipping to the finished card (#154, `docs/animation.md`); `1-3`/`enter` decide, the outcome shows, a second `enter` opens the report, and `enter` never ends the day; `continueRun` reopens a pending card, dealt again.
The deck's convention is that the last choice is what doing nothing gets, and that is `harness.Decline`; `harness.Run`/`RunFrom` play with the deck boxed (the invariants they pin belong to the other sims), `RunWith(..., Chooser)` deals it and answers before the policy acts.
`harness/dilemmas_test.go` pins determinism (same seed, same choices, same cards on the same days), a save on a card replaying identically, and every card in the deck being dealt and rendering without a hole; `news/dilemmas_test.go` pins the deck's shape, the pacing and the unknown-key refusal.
The zero value of `DilemmaState` is the pre-#15 state, so no schema bump.
