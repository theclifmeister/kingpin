# The connects are the supply side

**The connects are the supply side** (#72, `internal/content/suppliers.toml`, `content.SuppliersConfig`, `game/suppliers.go`, `sim/market/suppliers.go`, `ui/suppliers.go`, `harness.Leveraged`). A connect is a named person in a city you buy from: `World.Suppliers []Supplier`, seeded by the market sim (`market.Sim.Seed`, one draw from the day-0 RNG after the law) as every `[[supplier]]` that is not in the pool, in file order (the street connect in each city, Cass at home and Marisol in Bayport, and the wholesaler, the Dutchman, flagged `wholesale`), plus one of the `pool = true` rows by seed (Ricky Two-Tone and Silva at home, Auntie Mai in Bayport: a narrower range, a bigger book, a door that opens at `unlock_cash` **and** `unlock_rel`, the street connect in their city vouching, `Supplier.Locked`; `Unlocked{Gate: "connect"}` the morning it opens, #148). The static part is copied in (`Products`, nil for everything the city's supplier sells; `Lot`; `SmallLot`, the premium on a buy under the lot, 1 for none; `Temper`; `CreditDays`, `CreditRatio`; `UnlockCash`, `UnlockRel`; `Wholesale`), the market sim **stamps** the rest each morning from the band the relationship sits in (`Price` per product, `Cap`, `Limit`, `Band`, and since #47 `Quality` per product, the file's `quality` table for the connect or `[quality] default`, `Supplier.QualityOf`: what a buy from them blends into the lot, `docs/quality.md`; every connect in the file sells at the default today; `stampAll` at seed, `stampBand` and `stampPrice` in the step), and the relationship is the state: `Rel` 0..100, `Debt`, `DebtDue`, `Extended`, `FrozenUntil` (`Frozen(day)`), `Warned`, `Opened`, `Late`, `BoughtToday` (the day's book against `Cap`, cleared by the market sim, never the clock), `Bought`, `LastBought`, `Lots`. **Bands** (`[suppliers] band`, 20: five over 0..100, the third neutral; `content.SupplierTuning.Band`, `Neutral`, `Ratio`, `CapacityMul`, `CreditMul`): every temper starts in the neutral band (`[temper.*] rel`: patient 55, sharp 45, connected 50), where the price is the middle of the connect's `ratio` band (0.55 for the street connects, `[0.52, 0.58]`; 0.385 for the wholesaler, `[0.365, 0.405]`: **today's numbers, so a fresh run that never takes credit and never buys below a lot prices as it did**, `TestConnectsSeedAtTodaysPrice`) and nothing is scaled; the top band is the bottom of the ratio band, capacity ×1.5 and credit ×2, the floor the top of it, capacity ×0.5, no credit, and under `freeze_rel` (20) they stop taking calls (`SupplierFrozen{Why: "floor"}`, `freeze_days`, re-armed while Rel stays under). **Buying**: `World.Buy(supplier, product, qty, credit, pressure)` names the connect (`ErrNoSupplier`, `ErrElsewhere` from another city unless a lieutenant runs it, #174, `docs/lieutenants.md`: then the same path at the contract markup, `World.BuyMarkup`, the receipt carrying `Lieutenant`; `ErrNotSupplied`, `ErrSupplierLocked`, `ErrSupplierFrozen`, `ErrSupplierCapacity` past `Left()`, `ErrNoCredit`, `ErrCreditLimit`) and goes through `world.buy`, the one path, with `FillSupply(supplier, ...)` (a supply contract buys for cash from the cheapest available connect in its city for its shortfall, the premium counted, `market.Sim.cheapest`, never credit; short of every connect it is `SupplyShort{Why: "supplier"}`) and never `Restock` (the road's, above): the unit price is `Price[product]` × `SmallLot` under the lot × `CreditRatio` on credit (`Supplier.Quote`; `World.Quote` with the markup a buy through a lieutenant pays), a cash buy comes off dirty cash, a credit buy goes on the book (`Debt`; `DebtDue = day + CreditDays` when the book was clear, kept when it was not; `Stats.Credit`), the receipt carries `Supplier`, `Credit`, `SmallLot`, the pressure nudges the connect's price and `World.SupplierPrice(city, product)` (the best available connect's, else the cheapest that deals in it, else what the market stamped) refreshes `ProductMarket.SupplierPrice`, which is the supplier column, `NetWorth` and the harness's read; the cart's `Return` walks that connect's price back and `ReturnCredit` takes a credit receipt off the book (`World.Booked`). **The market sim's step** opens with `book` (yesterday's receipts as `SupplierBought` and `CreditTaken`, report-only; a bust that took stock in the city yesterday, `HeatState.Busts`, the heat sim's record kept a month like the seizures, costs `seize_rel` a lot lost up to `seize_rel`, a seizure on the road out of the city yesterday costs the wholesaler the same, `TestBustsSeizuresAndQuietMoveRel`; left alone `quiet_days` the relationship fades toward the temper's start by `quiet_decay` of the gap; a debt due is **collected**, dirty then clean, `DebtPaid` and `rel_paid` in full, short `DebtLate` and `rel_late` and the temper: `patient` extends once (`Extended`) and freezes the second time, `sharp` freezes `freeze_days` and adds `late_fee`, `connected` sends somebody (`SupplierCollected`: the best enforcer loses `collect_loyalty` and, failing a nerve roll off `Tick.Sub("suppliers")`, `collect_hurt` skill and their corner; with no enforcer, product from the stash in their city against the debt), what is left due again in `extend_days`; never a game over, `TestDebtNeverEndsTheRun`), then `supply`, then `credit` (`rel_per_lot` per lot on the day's book, so a contract's morning lot counts as the hand's, `TestSupplyMatchesTheHand`; the count cleared and the band stamped), and step 5 of the city loop stamps every connect's price for the product from the new street price (a city with no connect reads the file's `supplier_ratio` fold as before, `BaseRatio`), and closes with `warn`: a connect at the top band tips you off the day before a shock or a slump on something they sell in their city (`SupplierWarned`; `peek` reads tomorrow's market rolls off the seed in the order `Step` will make them, no draw and no stream moved, right unless a seizure tonight displaces the roll, `TestWarningsAreRightTheDayBefore`). The headlines (`DebtLate`, `SupplierFrozen`, `SupplierWarned`, `SupplierCollected`, source `market`, and the connect's `UnlockedConnect`, source `unlock`) pick their template off `Tick.Sub("suppliers")` as the buyers' do, so a run that never takes credit is the run it was. **Balance**: `rel_per_lot` 0.05 with the street lots at 50 is a slow build (the crewed player reaches band 3 in a week and the top band in six; tier 1 does not move: t1 84,930 → 84,930, t2 582k → 597k, t3 14.5M → 17.3M, t4 76.3M → 84M, `TestMoneyCurve`), the street connects' capacity is 1,500 a day (600 capped the crewed player at tier 2 and cost `upgraded` its margin over `crewed`), the wholesaler's 6,000 (the road's lots at tier 4, `TestDistributorBeatsLaundered` and `TestLogisticsBranchMovesMoreForLess` hold), and a bust's cost is by the lot because the always-aggressive trader, stung every few days, would otherwise freeze Cass out at the floor and sit out the indictment the difficulty tests pin (`TestLawyerThinsTheFile`, `TestSecondFallGuyTakesTheSecondFall`). The harness's `restock` buys by hand from the cheapest connect that is not the wholesaler (`harness.retail`: the lots are the road's, and a scripted player buying them by hand for the corners in Bayport measured a second road; `buyHere` quotes the premium and, for `leveraged`, the book first). **Migration** (10 → 11, `market.Sim.Migrate`): an old save gets the street connect in each city and the wholesaler at today's price (the street connect's the market's supplier price as saved, pressure and all, the wholesaler's that ×0.7 with the ticket folded), no pool connect, `TestMigrateSuppliers` and `TestSaveMigratesTheSuppliers`. **UI**: the market screen's SUPPLIERS block under BUYERS for the shown city (`suppliersLines`: name, their price for the product under the cursor, the lot, what they have left, the relationship as a bar, or as a number where MAIN is too narrow for the row whole, and what needs saying: frozen, waiting for a word, what you owe, a tip, the credit; its own cursor, `supplierCursor` / `onSuppliers`, reached off the bottom of the buyers and left off its top) with the connect's sections in the pane (`supplierSections`: temper, rel and band, the price now and at the next band, the lot and its premium, the day, credit and debt, status, `b` to buy from them where you stand, or through the lieutenant who runs their city at the markup, #174, RULES); the buy dialog (`dialog.pick`, `paged`, `supplier`, `credit`) opens on a **connect step** before the product only where more than one connect where you stand is dealing (`connectTable`, `connectBlurb`; with one it is skipped and the product step's footer lists no `⇧tab back`; `modalStep` counts the page, `buyAt`, `buyList`, `buyNext`), the title names the connect, the quantity step's max is the cash way (`maxBuy`; the book's `maxBuyBy(id, true)` is what a blank quantity means once the pay notch is on credit) and says what the book covers and the premium under the lot, and the last step has the `pay  [cash]  credit` notch under `repeat` (`payNames`; `c` turns it, listed only where the connect gives credit and the line is `once`, `buyPay`); the dashboard's supplier fact reads the best available connect where you stand (`supplierLine`: `Cass sells at ~55% of street`), the street carries `owe $X, due tomorrow` (`debtLine`) and the ALERTS a debt due tomorrow (`debtAlerts`, the last morning to raise the cash: the market collects at the top of the day it is due, so the morning it is due it is paid, or late and re-dated, and a fast-forward stops on the alert once), the ledger's route pane names the connect the road buys from (`buysFromRow`), the cart marks a credit line `credit` (returned off the book, never added to), the report's MONEY carries the credit taken, the payment or the late one and what the temper did, SALES the freeze, the tip and the door, CREW the collection, and help's WORDS has `connect` and `credit`. `TestSuppliersInTheGrammar`, `TestBuyDialogConnectStep` and `TestBuyRefusalsNameTheConnect` pin the UI;
