# Cities

**Cities** (#30). `World.Cities` (keyed by id, `CityOrder` fixing the sequence, home first) holds what is per city: a `City` has its own `Market` (per-product `ProductMarket`, priced by `[city.market]` multipliers in `city.toml` on the ladder in `market.toml`, so coke is cheap in Bayport and weed is dear), its own `Corners`, its own `Heat` (0..100) and its static `HeatMul` and `Wholesale` flag. `World.Home()` is the first city (where a run starts and the only city the rival fights over: the rivals sim reads `w.Home().Corners`), `World.Here()` the one the player is in (`Player.Location`). Stock is a stash per city, the **street** plus the **stash houses** there (#73, the paragraph at the end): `Player.Stash[city][product]` is the street, what you carry there and what the runners posted there hold, `World.Houses` the rest, and the accessors read and write both (`w.Stock(city, id)` and the copy `w.StashOf(city)` are the street and the houses together, `w.Street` and `w.StreetOf` the street alone; written only through `w.AddStock`, which puts what arrives away into the emptiest house there with room and onto the street when they are full or there are none, `w.TakeStock`, which draws the street first and then the houses in the order bought, is clamped and returns what it took, so a bust's or a robbery's "lost" is the call's result, `w.TakeStreet`, `w.TakeFromHouse` and `w.MoveStock`; `w.SetStock` is the tests' and the migration's and sets the street, #144), and moves between cities only on the road, which the routes run; `World.Capacity(city)` is what the supplier there will sell into: `StreetCapacity(city)`, the carry limit if the player is there, plus runners posted on corners there, plus idle runners wherever the player is, plus the houses' capacity there, and `Free(city)` is what is left of it. `Buy` is from a connect where the player stands (#72, below), into the stash there; `Travel(city)` moves the player at once, recalls `You` from their corner (it drifts unless a runner takes it; the confirmation names it) and leaves the stock, and is for what only you can do there (stand on a corner, hire, ask around, sign a deal); `Post(corner, You)` is refused outside the player's city (`ErrElsewhere`), runners go anywhere. Sell orders are per city (`OrderKey(city, product)`, `w.Order`), resolved by the market against that city's worked corners, so a `PlayerSold` in a city the player is not in is the runners there (`TestSalesElsewhereAreRunnersOnly`). `Corner.City` says which city a corner is in; ids are unique across cities (content refuses otherwise) so `w.Corner(id)`, `w.PostOf(id)`, `Held()`, `Worked()` and `RivalHeld()` search every city, `WorkedIn`, `HeldShare` and `Demand` take a city, and `Borders` never crosses one. `PriceMove`, `PriceShock`, `PlayerSold`, `HeatChanged` and `Enforcement` carry the `City`. `SchemaVersion` is 12 (7 was the cities, 8 the rival's trust, 9 the chief and the DA, 10 the fall guys' count, 11 the connects, 12 the stash houses): `Load` reads a pre-7 save's single-city fields (`Market`, `Territory.Corners`, `Player.Stock`, `Heat.Value`) off the stream a second time into an unexported `v6` record and `World.MigrateCities` wraps them into the home city (`logistics.Sim.Migrate` then lays out every other city in the config with every product the run has unlocked, and `territory.Sim.Migrate` runs again to seed corners for cities that have none), so an old save is a two-city world whose home city plays on identically (`TestSaveMigratesTheOneCity`, `TestOldSaveIsMigrated`).
