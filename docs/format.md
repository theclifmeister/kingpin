# Cash formatting

**Cash formatting** (#82): `internal/format` is the one place a number is written, for the UI and the report alike: `format.Cash` compacts totals (`$45K`, `$1.2M`, `$3.4B`) for the title bar, dashboard, report and run summary; `format.Money` keeps exact figures for itemised amounts (fees, wages, a purchase); `format.Price` is per-unit (cents under $1,000); `format.Arrow` is `→` for a change (`14 → 17`, never `->`; the loyalty, heat and quality changes in `ui/life.go`, `crew.go`, `map.go` and `quality.go` pass it as an argument too, #275), `format.Pct(frac, prec)` is a fraction as a percent to `prec` decimals (`Pct(0.25, 0)` is `25%`, `Pct(0.045, 1)` `4.5%`: the `%.0f%%` of `frac*100` every site wrote by hand, #275), `format.Times(x, prec)` a multiplier to `prec` decimals (`×1.25`) and `format.TimesSig(x, sig)` one to `sig` significant figures (`×1.5`, the old `×%.3g`), each site keeping the precision it had so no rendered text moved (a percent already scaled, `pctText`'s and the odds bands' `~30–45%` in `ui/intel.go`, and `ui/reputation.go`'s `math.Round` ones, which round half away from zero where `%.0f` rounds half to even, stay as they were; `ui/upgrades.go`'s `times` trims trailing zeros and is its own), `format.Plural` is `1 corner` / `3 corners` (`format.Plurals` the bare plural, with the irregulars the game counts: `city` / `cities`, `person` / `people`, `box` / `boxes`, a two-word noun on its last word) and `format.A` is a noun with its article (`a runner`, `an enforcer`). `internal/ui/format.go` keeps `cash()`, `money()`, `price()` and `plural()` as thin aliases, and `sim/news` (`news.go` and the `report_<sim>.go` files, #275) builds the report's money and arrow lines with the package (no `$%d` anywhere in it). **A refusal for want of cash** (#275) is one type, `game.ShortError{Need, Have, Pool}`, written `need $1,234, only have $1,000 dirty` (`Pool` `dirty`, `clean`, or empty for a cost `spend` takes from both) with `format.Money`, since the UI prints it after `Can't ...: `; `World.payDirty` / `payClean` are the check-then-deduct every single-pool purchase does (hire, bail, a house, a front, a level, a bribe, a checkpoint, a fund, a backing, the reserve, a deed, an asset, a buy-off, a cut, a cook, the road's `Restock`), `Investigate`, `PayOff` and `Scout` return the error when `spend` refuses, and a site that checks before it deducts (`Upgrade`, a cash buy through `buy`) returns it bare; `ErrNoDirtyCash` / `ErrNoCleanCash` still come first where a site had them, and no `$%d` is left in `internal/game`. **Tables** are one component, `ui/table.go`: `table(cols, rows, cursor, width)` draws declared columns (`col{title, kind, width}`, kinds `text, int, cash, money, price, pct, days, bar, dial`) with one number format per column, text left and numbers right, the header in `Subtle`, the cursor's row as `▸` + `Selected` across the row, and the last text column giving way to the width; a cell may be wrapped in `styled`, `approx` (`~40`) or `signed` (`+8%`), a bar column takes a `gauge` or a `spark`, a dial column an `order`, a days column a `day` (`d0`), and a row may start with a `mark` for its gutter. A column exists only if every row has it, so a note is a titled status column or leaves the table. `tableHook` lets `TestTablesAreConsistent` run `checkTable` over every table the rich fixture renders (`richFixture` in `ui_test.go`, shared with `TestRendersAtCommonSizes`). The lists still aligned by hand stay off `table()` because it would move rendered bytes (#275): every one is headerless where `table()` always draws a header, and most join their cells with one space where it joins with two: the bribe picker (`%-26s %-14s`, a numbered row selected whole), BUYERS and SUPPLIERS (`fit` to 22 and 14 with a Gold `▸` on the name alone, and SUPPLIERS' long and short forms picked whole), the diplomacy terms (`proposeLine`), the map's routes (an edge drawn between two padded cities, its terms falling away by width), the dashboard's city rows (no gutter, the right edge trimmed), the game-over figures (`%-14s %*s`), the summary's `factLines` and headlines, the journal's day column and the help's key column.

**One header per quantity, one kind per header** (#237). The same header is the same kind on every screen and in every modal, and a quantity has one header wherever it is tabled; the table below is the vocabulary, and `TestTablesAreConsistent` checks every column the rich fixture renders against it (a header rendered under two kinds, or under a kind other than the one listed here, or not listed here at all, fails; `Nd` stands for the spark column's `5d`, `30d`).

| kind | headers | the rule |
|---|---|---|
| text | `as read` `asset` `block` `books` `city` `connect` `corner` `fact` `faction` `for` `force` `from` `front` `guard` `heat` `holding` `house` `kind` `known` `lands` `line` `most` `muscle` `name` `node` `place` `product` `reports` `risk` `role` `runs` `since` `source` `spy` `stance` `status` `subject` `target` `temper` `terms` `way out` `what` `where` `who` `with` | `city` is a place, on every table (never `home`); `where` is a posting, the cell or the cot a crew member is at (never `post`); `holding` is a stash house's `40/100`; `heat` is a text on every table (the cart's `+0.5`, a house's `×0.6`, the strike's `+3`), never a plain int; `terms` is what a deal or a way out asks; `lands` is the strike picker's odds, not a place |
| int | `age` `carry` `corners` `demand/day` `floor` `keep` `left` `lot` `lvl` `qual` `quality` `road` `room` `sells/day` `skill` `stash` `there` `units` `units/day` `war` | `stash` is units stored in a city, on the market, the dashboard and every product picker (never `stock` or `have`); `room` is a house's capacity (never `holds`); `age` is years and the crew's alone |
| cash | `credit` `they lose` | a compact total: a connect's book, what an undercut costs them a day |
| money | `cash` `cost` `earns/day` `fee` `lifetime` `rent/day` `today` `upkeep/day` `wage` `washes/day` | an itemised amount: `cost` is every lump sum to pay, a front, a house, an asset, a deed, an upgrade (never `price`, never `kCash`); a per-day sum is money like the others on its row |
| price | `buy` `cook` `price` `street` `supplier` | a unit price: `price` is the one under the cursor (never `price/unit`, `$/unit`), `supplier` and `street` the other side of the buy, `cook` and `buy` the lab's two |
| pct | `audit` `margin` `off` `right` `sure` `takes` `Δ` | `Δ` is the day's price change, `off` an undercut's cut, `takes` a share won |
| days | `answer by` `days` `hired` `learnt` `read` `under` | `3d` or `d12` from `kDays`, never prose (`days` is a term's days left; `learnt` and `read` a fact's and the books' age, `age` being years) |
| bar | `loyalty` `rel` `trust` `Nd` | a gauge with its number, or the spark: `trust` is a bar on the FACTIONS table as it is in the pane |
| dial | `dial` `order` | an order at a dial, or a dial's name |
