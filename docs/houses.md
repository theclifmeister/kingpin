# Stash houses are where you keep it, and which one the raid finds

**Stash houses are where you keep it, and which one the raid finds** (#73, `internal/content/houses.toml`, `content.HousesConfig`, `game/houses.go`, `World.Houses`, `harness.Stashed`, `ui/houses.go`). A house is a place in a city on a block (`House{ID, City, Corner, Capacity, Price, Rent, Stock, Guard, Known, Bought, Unpaid, Robbed, Raided}`; the corner's heat is the raid's weight and its risk the robbers'), rented for its `price` in dirty cash through `BuyHouse(offer)` (`HouseOffer`, `Locked` at `unlock_cash` like a front's) and kept for `rent` in clean cash a day, which the **territory sim** pays house by house in the order bought after its corners (`houseStep`; the day of the lease is in the price; `RentPaid` report-only, `Stats.Rent`), a day it cannot pay counting against the house (`Unpaid`, a paid day clearing it) until at `[houses] rent_days` (5) running the landlord throws you out with everything in it (`HouseLost`, `World.LoseHouse`, `Stats.HousesLost`): the rent is the second thing after the retainer that a street operation with no fronts cannot pay, which is the pull toward laundering. **Buying is putting it away**: everything that arrives in a city (a hand buy, a contract's morning lot, a shipment landing, the road's lots, a card) goes into the emptiest house there with room, the next once that is full, and onto the street when the houses are full or there are none, and everything that leaves (a sale, a shipment, a handoff, a collection) comes off the street first, then the houses in the order bought, so nothing depends on iteration order; `Move(city, from, to, product, units)` (`game.Street` is the street) is free and instant within a city, refused past a house's room, and records the units in the per-day scratch `World.Today.Moved`, which the heat sim counts as exposure at `[houses] move_heat` (0.3 of a unit sold on a standard corner, `heat.Sim.MoveHeat`, `StockMoved` report-only) and never as a page (#27: a drive is not dealing), so Move is for spreading and for leaving a house the police know about; `Drop(id)` walks away from a lease with whatever is in it. The harness's `restock` fills the *street's* bag (`StreetCapacity`): a house is where the bag is kept, not a bigger bag, so a policy with houses holds what the same policy without them holds and keeps it off the street. **The raid hits one place, not the operation** (`heat.Sim.place`): a sting or a raid in the city whose police answer takes its `stock_loss` from the fullest house there the police `Known` about, else, with houses there holding anything, from one place rolled off `Tick.Sub("houses")` over every place holding stock, each house weighted by the heat of its block and the street by a standard corner's (heat 1), so a cheap empty house never shields the street (`TestDecoyHouseNeverShieldsTheStreet`), else from the street as it always was, with no roll; a raid while an informant is on the payroll goes to the fullest house, which becomes `Known` (`HouseCompromised{Why: "informant"}`), and takes the whole of it and nothing from the others (`Enforcement.Stash`, `House`, `HouseName`; `HouseRaided{Whole}`; the snitching paragraph). `Known` is set by that informant, by a robbery (`HouseRobbed`, word gets out: `Why: "robbery"`) and by a bust that took anything from the house (`Why: "bust"`), and never unset but by dropping the house, so a known house is the raid's target until it is dropped (the dashboard's alert and fast-forward's stop; `TestRobberyMakesTheHouseKnown`). **The robbery** is the territory sim's, per house per day at `robbery_chance x Risk x house_risk` (0.25: a house is behind a door) times the tree's `robbery_mul`, off `Tick.Sub("houses:"+city)`, taking `robbery_stock` of that house alone, and a **guard** (`World.Guard(house, id)`, an enforcer inside rather than on a corner; `GuardOf`; `Recall` clears it, so a `Post` takes them off the house and a `Guard` off the corner: one enforcer, one job) cuts it exactly as `enforcer_cut` cuts a corner's (`TestGuardIsWorthItsWage`: a house on the Docks robbed 17 times unguarded against 5 with a skill-90 enforcer inside over 200 days and five seeds); the corner robbery takes from the street alone, what the runner was carrying. **The dice rule**: a run with no house draws nothing the old run did not (the house robbery and the raid's roll are on the houses' side streams, the news picks the houses' headlines off `houses:news`, and the raid with one candidate rolls nothing), so a `crewed`, `laundered` or `distributor` run replays byte-for-byte as before #73 and no pinned number moved. **Schema 12** (`game.MigrateHouses`, the 11 -> 12 step): a fresh run starts with no house, and an old save's pile in each city goes into a rent-free starter house there (`game.StarterHouse+city`, on the block the player stands on, sized at the carry limit or the pile) so it plays on as it did. **UI**: the ledger's STASH table under the one cursor between FRONTS and LOGISTICS (`house city block stock rent/day guard status`, the guard and then the block dropped where MAIN is too narrow; the status `known` / `rent unpaid 2d` / `quiet`), the pane's house section (`houseSection`: the block's heat and risk, what it holds, the rent, the guard, the robbery odds, the warning on a known house, `m` / `e` / `x`), the buy picker's first page is the kind, `front` or `house` (`modeFront` has two pages since #73: `frontStep`, `frontKind`, `keyFront`;
