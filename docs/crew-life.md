# Crew life: kin, ageing, arrests and getting shot

**Crew life** (#46, `crew.toml [life]`, `[life.force]`, `[life.personality]`, `[role.driver]` and every role's `bail`; `sim/crew/life.go`; `ui/life.go`) gives the stat blocks lives.
Everything rolls off **`Tick.Sub("life")`** and never the home stream: `crew.Sim.life` is called at the top of the crew step after the day's hires, firings and pay-offs are reported and before the skim, in a fixed order (the kin's loyalty for what you did today, the cells and the cots, last night's sweep, tonight's shooting, the birthdays, the kin faces), and `crew.Sim.Step` returns from it at once when the table is boxed, so **a run with `[life]` zeroed is byte-for-byte the run before the feature** (`harness.NoLife`, `cmd/balance -life off`; `TestNoLifeIsTheOldRun` plays the managed player, who hires nobody, on the money curve's twenty seeds to day 200 with the life table on and under the box and finds the same run, the hiring pool set aside, and crewed and boss runs under the box with none of #46's events, stats or fields; until #276 it pinned main's pre-#46 t1–t4, $84,930 / $605,294 / $16,650,659 / $83,811,847, under the box, a copy of the money curve).
The pool rotation (`Candidates = nil` every `pool_days`) now happens before `life` and the refill after, so a kin recommended tonight is a face in the morning's pool; no draw moved.
**Ages.**
`CrewMember.Age` is years, `age_min..age_max` (19–44) at generation, the one draw `generate`, `chemist` and `driver` make off the life stream (`crew.Sim.age`; the four ways onto the crew share `pickName` and `roll`, #275, which draws skill, loyalty, greed and nerve in that order, skill in `crew.toml [crew]` `skill_min..skill_max` (15..85) and greed and nerve in `greed_min..greed_max` and `nerve_min..nerve_max` (5..95), and differ only in the pool, the fallback name, the fee and the dice the age is drawn on), so the faces the home stream draws are the faces it always drew (`TestAgesAreOffTheLifeStream`); a save from before it is **schema 13 → 14**, `crew.Sim.MigrateAges` giving every member and candidate an age off `Tick{Day, Seed}.Sub("ages")`, never the home stream (`TestOldSaveIsMigrated` reads them).
`Kin []int`, `Growth`, `JailedUntil`, `WoundedUntil`, `Bailed`, `Crew.Fallen`, `Crew.BailedToday` (per-day scratch the clock clears with `PaidOffToday`), `Heat.Sweep`, `Shipment.Driver`, `RouteSetting.Driver` and the seven `Stats` (`Bodies`, `Fallen`, `Arrests`, `Bails`, `BailCash`, `Wounded`, `Retired`) are zero values, the pre-#46 state.
**Ageing** (`TestAgeingAndRetirement`): a member is a year older every `year_days` (30) on the payroll, counted from the day they signed (`crew.Sim.Birthday`; candidates do not age, the pool rotates); skill grows `skill_growth` (0.2) a day times the pay dial's wage multiplier (0.6 / 1 / 1.5) up to `skill_cap` (80), the fraction kept in `Growth` and no dice, so a skill-30 runner on generous pay reaches the cap inside the horizon and `Units` and `Wage` stay what they signed at (the world never needs the tuning to price them); past `nerve_age` (38) every birthday takes `nerve_loss` (4); on the birthday at `retire_age` (50) they retire (`CrewRetired{Age, Kin, Sour}`, `Stats.Retired`, recalled from the corner, a lieutenant's standing orders dropped): one at or over `retire_loyal` (60) recommends a kin, a face in the pool at the discount (`Kin` names them), one under the informant line (`[informant] loyalty`, 20) is `Sour` and talks on the way out, **one informant page** the heat sim files off the event (`informant_evidence`, the crew step is before heat's) whatever was sold: the informant exception applied once, named in `docs/corners.md`'s list.
The roster tags one whose next birthday is the farewell RETIRING (`crew.Sim.Retiring`).
**Kin** (`TestKinRememberEachOther`): a hire's cousin, partner or friend turns up in the pool with `kin_chance` (0.35) the night they sign (`crew.Sim.kinFace`: `generate` on the life stream, so the same roles, names and skills a candidate gets, the fee at `kin_discount` (0.5) off, `Kin` linked both ways; `KinLooking` is report-only), one **extra face** beside the pool's `candidates` faces exactly as #47's chemist and #46's driver are (`crew.extra`, `refill` counts the faces without them), so the home stream's faces are untouched; the relation word (cousin / partner / friend) is `(a+b) % 3` of the pair's ids, nothing saved (`ui.relation`).
Kin remember: firing one costs each kin on the payroll `kin_loyalty` (10) beyond `fire_loyalty`, unless the fired one was an informant (the crew do not hold that against you and neither do they), one who walks or defects costs it too, a pay-off lifts it, and one shot dead on your corner costs `kin_body_loyalty` (25) and gives `kin_body_nerve` (15).
**Arrests** (`TestArrestsAndBail`, `TestJailedMembersWorkNothing`): the heat sim stamps every sting and raid on `Heat.Sweep{Day, City, Level, Corners, Crew, Units}` (the held corners in the city with a crew id on them and the crew who stood there when the police came, never `You`; `Enforcement.Corners` carries the corners for the report) and, the crew step being before heat's, the crew sim reads yesterday's the next morning (the way the heat sim reads the law's night, #42) and rolls `arrest_chance` (0.25) for each of them (a runner pulled off the corner overnight is still on the list: the police came while they stood there); a raid that took stock (`Units > 0`) is the lab's too and the best chemist rolls the same; the driver of a shipment seized today goes with it, no dice (logistics steps before crew, `ShipmentSeized.Driver`).
Jailed: `w.Recall`, `JailedUntil = day + jail_days` (10), `CrewArrested{Corner | Route, Days, Bail}`, `Stats.Arrests`; a member in a cell or laid up **works nothing**: `Post` and `Guard` refuse them (`ErrJailed`, `ErrWounded`), the lieutenant's `delegate` skips them, `CrewState.Role` counts only the members at work (`Working`: neither field set; `OnPayroll` counts them all), so a jailed enforcer deters, protects and strikes nothing and `rivals.Sim.Strength` leaves them out (a boost's `Hurt` lands on the least nervy enforcer at work), while the wage runs on (the family eats) and the roster slot stays taken, `Chemist()` and `Fixer()` pick the best at work (both `CrewState.Best(role)`, #275; `CrewState.Strongest(role)` is the best of a role on the books, at work or not, which the best-enforcer odds read: the investigation and the scout; the connect's collector keeps its own loop, a cross-sim write `TestSimsWriteOnlyTheirOwnState` lists by its line), and the harness's `staff` hires around them.
**Bail** (`World.Bail(id, cost)`, `crew.Sim.BailCost`, the role's `bail`: runner $1,500, enforcer $2,500, driver $2,000, accountant $4,000, fixer $5,000, chemist $6,000, lieutenant $10,000): **clean cash only**, the whole of it (a bondsman takes a cheque; `TestBailAndTheDriver`), once (`ErrBailed`), `JailedUntil = day+1` and `Bailed`, `Stats.Bails` / `BailCash`, `Crew.BailedToday` for the report's `CrewBailed` line, and `docs/laundering.md`'s list of clean-money sinks gains it; the morning they walk (`CrewReleased{Bailed}`) they gain `bail_loyalty` (15).
**The bondsman** (#230, `upgrades.toml` `bondsman`, `auto_bail`, `docs/upgrades.md`): with the node owned, `crew.Sim.jail` pays the bail from clean cash the night the arrest lands when the account covers it, the same numbers and the same `Bailed` morning as a hand bail, `CrewArrested.Sprung` on the arrest's line and `CrewBailed{Who: "the lawyer"}` on the money; short of the cash they sit and `CrewArrested.Short` says the lawyer could not cover it.
The arrest still happened: the heat, the sweep's odds and the informant roll on an unbailed release are untouched, and a run that never owns the node is byte-for-byte the run before (`TestNoBondsmanIsTheOldRun`, `TestBondsmanBailsTheMorningOf`).
Unbailed, on the day the cell opens they come back at `jail_loyalty` (10) under the informant line and **roll the turn at once** at `release_turn` (0.35, × the tree's `informant_chance_mul`): the DA had them for ten days and your dealing made the witness (#27 bends for something you did, the fourth bend beside the informant, the backfire and the offshore lots).
**Shot** (`TestShotOnTheCorner`, `TestWarHasBodies`): your enforcers going in (`CornerStruck`: one of the enforcers at work, picked off the life stream) and the enforcer guarding a corner the rival pushed and did not take (`RivalPushed` with `Corner.Enforcer` set; an unguarded push shoots nobody) roll a death at `kill_chance` (0.04) and then a wound at `wound_chance` (0.15), both times the multiplier: `[life.force]` by the strike's force (warn 0.5, push 1.5, hit 2.5) and `[life.personality]` by the rival's on a push (expansionist 0.6, defensive 0.4, opportunist 0.6, chaotic 1.0), so a hit strike is 10% a death and 37.5% a wound and an expansionist's push 2.4% / 9%; and the rival's muscle dies the same way on its side (`crew.Sim.theirs`, `CrewShot{Theirs}`), a body the count takes and the paper prints while the rival's headcount stays the rivals sim's (`Muscle` is written nowhere here: `TestRivalStateHasOneWriter`).
A wound is `WoundedUntil = day + wound_days` (12), off the corner, `CrewShot{Days}`, `Stats.Wounded`, `CrewRecovered` (report-only) the morning they are back; a death is `CrewShot{Dead}`, off the roster onto `Crew.Fallen` (`Fallen{ID, Name, Role, Age, Day, Corner}`), `Stats.Bodies` and `Stats.Fallen`; **`Stats.Bodies` counts both sides and never decreases** (#49's divisor), and every death is a headline about you and pressure in its city: the reputation sim reads `CrewShot{Dead}` for `reputation.toml [notoriety] body` (5) and the law sim for `law.toml [pressure] body` (10) in the corner's city (home for one with no corner), the same tick, the way an overdose is read (#47); `TestWarHasBodies` pins the hit war over `territory` at day 120, the count monotone, and the morning of the first death higher on both than the same seed with the two knobs zeroed and equal on every morning before it.
**The driver** (`[role.driver]`, `game.RoleDriver`; `TestDriverComesLookingOnceARouteHasRun`, `TestDriverCutsSeizures`) comes looking once a route has run (`crew.DriversWanted`: `Stats.Shipments > 0`, announced as an `Unlocked{Gate: "role"}` like the fixer), one extra face off `Tick.Sub("driver")` with a name from `names.toml`'s `drivers`, the chemist's pattern.
`World.SetRouteDriver(route, id)` puts a driver on a route (`RouteSetting.Driver`, persistent like the dial; one route a driver, `unseat`; 0 takes them off; `Fire` takes them off; `ErrNotDriver` for anyone else), `World.RouteDriver(route, day)` is the one riding today (on the payroll and fit), `DrivenRoute(id)` the route they drive.
The logistics sim stamps `Shipment.Driver` on every shipment the route sends while they are fit (`ShipmentSent.Driver`), and `logistics.Sim.Cut` compounds the driver's cut with a bought checkpoint's, `1 − (1 − deal)(1 − driver_cut × skill/100)` (`driver_cut` 0.5: a skill-60 driver takes 30% off the day's risk; `Sim.DriverCut`), the route's driver for the odds the map shows and a new shipment's, the one it left with for a shipment on the road (`riding`), so the odds the player reads are the ones the dice use and a run with no driver rolls the road it always did; a seized one carries `Driver` and `DriverName` and the crew sim jails them that night.

**Spies** (#45, `docs/intel.md`, `sim/crew/spy.go`) are the crew sim's: `World.PlantSpy(faction, member)` sends a member under that night (`CrewMember.Undercover`, `UndercoverDay`; a lieutenant running a city, one in a cell or laid up, or one already under is refused), and a spy works nothing for you: `Working()` and `Fit()` are false, so `Role` counts them out, `Post` and `Guard` refuse `ErrUndercover`, the lieutenant's delegation and the route's driver skip them, the wage runs on.
Every `spy_days` under they file a report on `Tick.Sub("intel")` (the faction's muscle, its next move and its fattest till, at `spy_accuracy` scaled by skill) and roll the faction's `spy_found` by temper: found, `turn_share` come home turned (an informant, the silent event: the snitching path) and the rest are shot through this sim's own `fall` (off the roster onto `Crew.Fallen`, `Stats.Bodies` and `Fallen`; no `CrewShot`, so no notoriety or pressure: the body is theirs to hide); `SpyFound` names each with `Dead` or `Why`.
