# World is a plain struct

**World is a plain struct** (`internal/game/world.go`; its parts split out by #275: `crew_state.go` the crew and the roles, `rival_state.go` a faction and the orders against one, `report.go` `DayReport`, `Ending` and `Stats`, `stash.go` the stock accessors) that sims mutate directly.
Player actions (`Buy`, `PlaceSell`, `CancelSell`, `Travel`, `SetRoute`, `SetRouteTarget`, `SetLieLow`, `Hire`, `Fire`, `SetPay` (like `SetRoute` and `SetLaunderDial`, it refuses a run that is over and a position off the dial, `ErrBadDial`, #281), `Investigate`, `PayOff`, `BuyFront`, `Invest`, `BuyAsset` (#48, `assets.go`), `SetLaunderDial`, `Assign`, `Unassign` in `actions.go` and its area files (#275: `actions_trade.go` the orders, supply contracts and returns, `actions_routes.go` the routes and the driver, `actions_crew.go` the crew, `actions_fronts.go` the fronts and the launder dial; the `Err*` values stay in `actions.go`); `Cut` and `CookOrder` in `stash.go` (#47: the cut applies at once, the cook queues on `Crew.Cooks` for the crew sim); `Fund`, `Back`, `Reserve` in `law.go`; `Retire`, `Vanish` and `End` in `exit.go` (#49: every ending is written through `End`, which stamps `Stats.Score`; `World.LegitDays` is the laundering sim's count toward the businessman ending, zero the run before); `World.Start` (#50, `profile.go`: the character, the hard DA and the daily the run began as, stamped by `sim.NewWorldWith`, read by the summary and the profile and by no sim, zero every run before, `docs/profile.md`); `Post`, `Recall`, `Abandon`, `SendEnforcers`, `CallOff` in `territory.go`; `Propose`, `Withdraw`, `Accept`, `Decline` in `diplomacy.go`) only queue intent in `World.Today` (#144: one struct of the per-day scratch, `Orders`, `Buys`, `LieLow`, `Strike`, `Investigation`, `UpgradesToday`, `Proposal`, `Accepted`, `Abandoned`, `Funded`, `Deliveries`, `Undercuts`, `Moved`, `HousesBought`, `Scouting`, `Tipoff`, `Poach`, `Backed` (#193), `Invested` (#192), `Reserved` (#195) and `AssetsBought` (#48), the names they had on `World` before, so a save written since reads the same at each level; the clock zeroes it as a unit, `ClearToday`, keeping only #113's contract receipts in `Buys`; a save from before #144 loads with `Today` zero, which is what its clock left after the day it was saved on, and no schema bump) (or, for a buy and a trip, apply at once and are reported; the route dial and target are persistent settings like the pay dial); the market sim resolves sell orders during `EndDay`, the crew sim reports hires and fires, the territory sim reports claims, the rival sim resolves the strike and answers the proposal and seals the accepted offers, the laundering sim reports front purchases, the logistics sim runs the routes and moves the shipments, the law sim turns the day's funding into goodwill, and the morning `DayReport` is what the UI shows, its `Flow` the night's cash by category and pile, and `World.Flows` the last nights of it (#351, `game/cashflow.go`, `docs/market-and-journal.md`).

**The factions** (#43): `World.Rivals []*RivalState` is the table in id order, `Rivals[0]` the rival at home that `w.Rival()` returns (a world built by hand with none gets one on demand), `w.Faction(id)` a seat by id (`""` the rival at home), `FactionIndex`, `FactionName`, `CityOf`, `RivalHeldBy`, `HeldByIn`, `StrongestFaction`, `Contested` (any border with another side), `ContestedBy`, `Stance` and `Dominant()` beside them (`game/factions.go`).
`Corner.Faction` names the faction on a rival corner and `Corner.FactionID()` resolves a blank to the rival at home (a save from before ids, a world built by hand).
`SchemaVersion` is 15: the 14 -> 15 step seats a save's one rival first (`World.SeatRival`, the `v14` shape read off the stream a second time) and `rivals.Sim.MigrateFactions` seeds the table beside it off the seed's factions stream (`docs/saves.md`).
The rivals sim is the one writer of every faction (`TestRivalStateHasOneWriter` greps `w.Rival()`, `w.Rivals` and `w.Faction(...)`; the lieutenant's walk through `w.Faction(to.ID)` stays the exception); the crew sim reads `CrewPoached` and `RivalLeaderArrested` off the tick and the market sim reads `Fragmented`.

**The file** (#45): `World.Intel []Fact` is what the player knows (`game/intel.go`, `docs/intel.md`), written by the sims that own each truth through `World.Learn` (`LearnBooks`, `Unlearn`, `Expose`) and read by the panels through `game.Known(w)`; `PayCop` and `PlantSpy` queue `Today.Cop` and `Today.Spy`; `CrewMember.Undercover` is a spy's faction.
`World.Trade(c)` and `World.Fattest(faction)` are the corner-trade queries the rivals sim and the crew sim share.

**The sims' test fixture** (#276): `internal/gametest` is the one-city world and the step the sims' unit tests share, imported by tests only (`TestOnlyTestsImportGametest` parses every non-test file in the module; gametest itself imports `game` and `events` and nothing else, so a sim's test that stands on it reaches no other sim).
`OneCity(seed, cash, products...)` is `game.NewWorld` over one city, `test` named Testville, capacity 100, dealing `gametest.A` ($10, demand 5) when no product is given; `City(seed, id, name, cash, products...)` names the city (territory and rivals stand on the file's home id, reputation's is Test), and `gametest.Weed` is the $20, demand-60 product those three deal; both products carry the supplier's 0.55 (#273).
`TickOn(w, day, evs...)` is the day's tick as the clock builds it (`RNGFor(w.Seed, day)`, `Seed` set for `Tick.Sub`) with `evs` pre-emitted as if by the sims before; `Tick(w, evs...)` is the next day's; `Step(w, s, evs...)` steps `s` on it and moves `w.Day` on, clearing nothing (heat's helper calls `w.ClearToday` itself, crew's zeroes the day's hires).
`StepUnseeded` is `Step` on a tick with `Seed` zero: the crew, laundering, rivals and territory tests were written on such a tick and every side stream they roll comes from seed 0, so the zero is kept and named rather than moving their dice.
Each package keeps its own `world`/`step` wrapper for what differs (crew boxes life and seeds the pool, laundering sets the peak and the dial, rivals and territory seat a crew, heat builds `sim.NewWorld` and posts the player, logistics the config's cities); the fixture worlds are byte-identical to the literals they replaced.

**The shared helpers** (#275): every find-by-id lookup (`w.Front`, `w.House`, `w.GuardOf`, `w.Asset`, `w.Contract`, `w.Offer`, `w.Supplier`, `w.Corner`, `w.PostOf`, `Crew.Member`, and in `content` every table's `Product`, `Upgrade`, `Route`, `City`, `Corner`, `Card`, `Front`, `Asset`, `ByEffect`, `Buyer`, `Character`, `House`, `Incident`, `Supplier`, `Ending`) is one unexported generic `find(s, match)` per package (`game/world.go`, `content/load.go`: content imports nothing of game's), each keeping its signature and its nil for none.
`w.SortedProducts()` is the ladder sorted by id, the fixed order the market, territory, the connects' peek and the heat ladder walk the products in (the map's demand line keeps the ladder's order: its stable sort by demand breaks a tie by where it starts, #275).
`CrewState.Best(role)` is the most skilled member of a role at work (`Chemist()` and `Fixer()` wrap it) and `CrewState.Strongest(role)` the most skilled on the books, at work or not (the investigation's and the scout's best enforcer, and the UI's confirmations that name his skill, `investigateConfirm` and `scoutConfirm`; the connect's collector keeps its loop, a write the guard lists by its line); the first on the roster wins a tie in both.
`game.Rand` is the one interface the sims pass their dice down as (`IntN`, `Float64`: `*rand.Rand` from `Tick.RNG` or a `Sub`).
A clamp is Go's built-in `max(lo, min(hi, v))` inline, bar `game.clamp` (0..100, the cards and the incidents).
