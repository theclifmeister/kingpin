# World is a plain struct

**World is a plain struct** (`internal/game/world.go`) that sims mutate directly. Player actions (`Buy`, `PlaceSell`, `CancelSell`, `Travel`, `SetRoute`, `SetRouteTarget`, `SetLieLow`, `Hire`, `Fire`, `SetPay`, `Investigate`, `PayOff`, `BuyFront`, `Invest`, `BuyAsset` (#48, `assets.go`), `SetLaunderDial`, `Assign`, `Unassign` in `actions.go`; `Cut` and `CookOrder` in `world.go` (#47: the cut applies at once, the cook queues on `Crew.Cooks` for the crew sim); `Fund`, `Back`, `Reserve` in `law.go`; `Retire`, `Vanish` and `End` in `exit.go` (#49: every ending is written through `End`, which stamps `Stats.Score`; `World.LegitDays` is the laundering sim's count toward the businessman ending, zero the run before); `Post`, `Recall`, `Abandon`, `SendEnforcers`, `CallOff` in `territory.go`; `Propose`, `Withdraw`, `Accept`, `Decline` in `diplomacy.go`) only queue intent in `World.Today` (#144: one struct of the per-day scratch, `Orders`, `Buys`, `LieLow`, `Strike`, `Investigation`, `UpgradesToday`, `Proposal`, `Accepted`, `Abandoned`, `Funded`, `Deliveries`, `Undercuts`, `Moved`, `HousesBought`, `Scouting`, `Tipoff`, `Poach`, `Backed` (#193), `Invested` (#192), `Reserved` (#195) and `AssetsBought` (#48), the names they had on `World` before, so a save written since reads the same at each level; the clock zeroes it as a unit, `ClearToday`, keeping only #113's contract receipts in `Buys`; a save from before #144 loads with `Today` zero, which is what its clock left after the day it was saved on, and no schema bump) (or, for a buy and a trip, apply at once and are reported; the route dial and target are persistent settings like the pay dial); the market sim resolves sell orders during `EndDay`, the crew sim reports hires and fires, the territory sim reports claims, the rival sim resolves the strike and answers the proposal and seals the accepted offers, the laundering sim reports front purchases, the logistics sim runs the routes and moves the shipments, the law sim turns the day's funding into goodwill, and the morning `DayReport` is what the UI shows.

**The factions** (#43): `World.Rivals []*RivalState` is the table in id order, `Rivals[0]` the rival at home that `w.Rival()` returns (a world built by hand with none gets one on demand), `w.Faction(id)` a seat by id (`""` the rival at home), `FactionIndex`, `FactionName`, `CityOf`, `RivalHeldBy`, `HeldByIn`, `StrongestFaction`, `Contested` (any border with another side), `ContestedBy`, `Stance` and `Dominant()` beside them (`game/factions.go`). `Corner.Faction` names the faction on a rival corner and `Corner.FactionID()` resolves a blank to the rival at home (a save from before ids, a world built by hand). `SchemaVersion` is 15: the 14 -> 15 step seats a save's one rival first (`World.SeatRival`, the `v14` shape read off the stream a second time) and `rivals.Sim.MigrateFactions` seeds the table beside it off the seed's factions stream (`docs/saves.md`). The rivals sim is the one writer of every faction (`TestRivalStateHasOneWriter` greps `w.Rival()`, `w.Rivals` and `w.Faction(...)`; the lieutenant's walk through `w.Faction(to.ID)` stays the exception); the crew sim reads `CrewPoached` and `RivalLeaderArrested` off the tick and the market sim reads `Fragmented`.

**The file** (#45): `World.Intel []Fact` is what the player knows (`game/intel.go`, `docs/intel.md`), written by the sims that own each truth through `World.Learn` (`LearnBooks`, `Unlearn`, `Expose`) and read by the panels through `game.Known(w)`; `PayCop` and `PlantSpy` queue `Today.Cop` and `Today.Spy`; `CrewMember.Undercover` is a spy's faction. `World.Trade(c)` and `World.Fattest(faction)` are the corner-trade queries the rivals sim and the crew sim share.
