# World is a plain struct

**World is a plain struct** (`internal/game/world.go`) that sims mutate directly. Player actions (`Buy`, `PlaceSell`, `CancelSell`, `Travel`, `SetRoute`, `SetRouteTarget`, `SetLieLow`, `Hire`, `Fire`, `SetPay`, `Investigate`, `PayOff`, `BuyFront`, `Invest`, `SetLaunderDial`, `Assign`, `Unassign` in `actions.go`; `Cut` and `CookOrder` in `world.go` (#47: the cut applies at once, the cook queues on `Crew.Cooks` for the crew sim); `Fund` in `law.go`; `Post`, `Recall`, `Abandon`, `SendEnforcers`, `CallOff` in `territory.go`; `Propose`, `Withdraw`, `Accept`, `Decline` in `diplomacy.go`) only queue intent in `World.Today` (#144: one struct of the per-day scratch, `Orders`, `Buys`, `LieLow`, `Strike`, `Investigation`, `UpgradesToday`, `Proposal`, `Accepted`, `Abandoned`, `Funded`, `Deliveries`, `Undercuts`, `Moved`, `HousesBought`, `Scouting`, `Tipoff`, `Poach`, `Backed` (#193) and `Invested` (#192), the names they had on `World` before, so a save written since reads the same at each level; the clock zeroes it as a unit, `ClearToday`, keeping only #113's contract receipts in `Buys`; a save from before #144 loads with `Today` zero, which is what its clock left after the day it was saved on, and no schema bump) (or, for a buy and a trip, apply at once and are reported; the route dial and target are persistent settings like the pay dial); the market sim resolves sell orders during `EndDay`, the crew sim reports hires and fires, the territory sim reports claims, the rival sim resolves the strike and answers the proposal and seals the accepted offers, the laundering sim reports front purchases, the logistics sim runs the routes and moves the shipments, the law sim turns the day's funding into goodwill, and the morning `DayReport` is what the UI shows.
