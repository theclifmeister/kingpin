# World incidents are the weather

**World incidents are the weather** (#44, `internal/sim/world`, `internal/content/incidents.toml`, `game/incidents.go`, `World.Incidents`). The world changes without you: a port strike, a hurricane, a snowstorm, a harvest glut, a new synthetic, a celebrity overdose, a documentary, a journalist's profile, a snap election, a chief who resigns, federal attention (the starter eleven; `rival_leader_killed` waits for #43's fragmentation and is not in the key set or the table). The **world sim** steps **first** in the order (`world -> market -> …`, `sim.Default`), so an incident lands on the world before anything reads it and every sim reacts the same day: the market prices the shock, the road finds the route shut, the law seats the chief. It deals the way the news sim deals the deck (`docs/dilemmas.md`): at most one a day, none for `[incidents] min_gap` days after the last, then a chance rising each day to certainty at `max_gap`, **one roll a day whatever happens**, then a weighted pick over the eligible rows. Its dice are `Tick.Sub("incidents")` and nothing else (the news sim picks the headline's template off `Tick.Sub("incidents:news")`), so **a run with the table boxed (`cfg.Incidents.Table = nil`) is byte-for-byte the run before the sim existed**: `TestIncidentDiceAndGaps` (`world/world_test.go`) reads the home stream after the step, `TestNoIncidentsIsTheOldRun` (`harness/incidents_test.go`) plays the boss boxed through `Run` and `RunWith` and finds no event, no record, no closure, and `TestSeedDigest` pins the boxed world for sixty days (its digest moved on day 1 by shape alone, five zero fields, and on day 25 by the wording of one headline that now names the chief; no number moved). **The harness runs with it boxed** (`harness.Run`, `RunFrom`, `RunWith`; `harness.Play(cfg, w, days, policy, Options{Incidents: true, Cards: pick})` is the general form, `cmd/balance -incidents on|off`, on by default so the tool plays the game as played and off for a pinned number): incidents are weather, not difficulty, and the money curve and the difficulty tests keep their numbers (`TestIncidentsAreWeather` logs the medians with and without and holds the tier-1 and tier-2 bands and the aggressive/quiet ordering with the table on: managed at day 30 $85,772 either way, crewed at day 70 $591,954 with against $605,294 without, -2.2%; `cmd/balance -runs 20` reads the four checkpoints at $86,091 / $591,954 / $15,177,970 / $80,978,047 with the table on against the pinned $84,930 / $605,294 / $18,031,607 / $85,056,915 off, +1.4% / -2.2% / -15.8% / -4.8%, the boss paying most because it runs the boat route the strikes and the hurricanes shut; every band holds).

**A row** (`content.IncidentConfig`, `[[incident]]`): `weight` (0 reads as 1), `min_day`, `min_gap` (its own cooldown, days since it last fired, `World.Incidents.LastOf`), `once` (`Count`), `route_mode` (`boat | truck | car`: the routes a `route_closed` shuts, every one of that mode with both ends on the map, the first by name the `{{.Route}}` slot), `names` (the pool `{{.Name}}` is drawn from, `names.toml`'s `celebrities` and `reporters`, `NamesConfig.Pool`), `report` (the morning report's line, a template over the slots), `[incident.trigger]` (`content.CardTrigger`, the deck's vocabulary through `game.Eligible`; #44 added `city`, which must exist and is the city the row lands in, and `da_stance`; a row with no `city` lands at **home**, since the world does not follow the player around; the issue's `route_mode` is the row's own field above and `rivals_at_war` is `war_min`), and `[incident.effects]`, the **fixed key set** (`content.IncidentEffects`, a struct: an unknown key in `incidents.toml` fails at start-up through `decodeBytes`'s undecoded check, `TestIncidentsRefuseUnknownKey`): `route_closed = N` (nights), `market_shock = { product, mul, days }` (the price target × mul in the row's city), `demand_shift = { product, mul, days }` (the demand × mul, the slump's shape), `pressure`, `heat` (deltas on the city, 0..100), `notoriety` (on you), `heat_decay = { mul, days }` (the heat sim's decay × mul: federal attention), `chief_replaced = true`, `election_called = N`. A row about a product the city has not listed waits until it has (`listed`). `content.IncidentsConfig.validate` checks ids unique, a name and a report, the pacing, the city, the product, the mode, the pool, that a closure has a mode and that a row does something. The headline is `headlines.toml`'s under **`Incident` + the id in camel case** (`IncidentConfig.Key`: `IncidentPortStrike`), with variants like every other event's, and `Incident` itself for a row without one (it names the row); `TestEveryEmittedEventHasTemplate` requires both.

**Effects write world state only, never into a sim**: `World.ApplyIncident(day, effects, target)` (`game/incidents.go`) is the one function that knows the keys, the way `applyEffect` is for a card's, and the sim's own writes are `World.Incidents` alone (`TestSimsWriteOnlyTheirOwnState`'s row `"world": {"Incidents"}`). A closure sets `RouteSetting.ClosedUntil = day + N` (`RouteSetting.Closed(day)` is `day < ClosedUntil`, asked with the tick's day; `World.RouteClosed(id)` asks for tonight's, `w.Day+1`, for the UI) on every route of the mode, keeping the later date if one is already shut; the **logistics sim** reads it (`docs/logistics.md`): `move` holds every shipment on a shut route where it is, `Arrives` one later for every night shut, no interception roll, and `run` skips the route, so a shut route moves nothing and refuses new shipments, and on reopening what was on it lands with its days left intact and the shortfall that opened goes the first morning it can (`TestStockIsConservedAcrossShipments`'s closure case: sent day 1, shut for ticks 3-5, lands three days late; a target raised on day 3 sends on day 6). A shock writes the market's own `ShockFactor`, `ShockDays` (`days + 1`: the market steps next and takes one off before it prices, so the shock holds for `days` ticks counting this one and the pane reads `days` the morning after, as the market's own shocks do) and `ShockSlump` on the city's product; the market's random shock for that product waits while one runs. `pressure` and `heat` clamp 0..100 on the row's city (`Heat.Peak` follows); `notoriety` clamps on the axis and the reputation sim's sum cap applies that evening as to every source; `heat_decay` sets `HeatState.FederalUntil = day + days` and `FederalDecay`, and the **heat sim** multiplies its decay by it on every tick before that day (one line in `Step`, beside lying low). `World.RecordIncident` books the pacing (`Last`), `Count`, `LastOf` and `Fired` (`game.Incident{Day, ID, Name, City, Route, Product, Person, Days}`, the run summary's); the zero `IncidentState` is the pre-#44 state and every new field's zero is the old run, so **no schema bump** (`SchemaVersion` stays 12).

**The two effects that need another sim's dice ride the event.** `chief_replaced` and `election_called` cannot be written as `Chief.Since` or `DA.ElectedDay` without the law's own term (another sim's tuning), so the `events.Incident` carries `NewChief` and `Election` and the **law sim**, later in the same tick, honours them: a `NewChief` is `replaceChief(w, t, "resigned")` on `Tick.Sub("law")` at once (`ChiefReplaced{Why: "resigned"}`, the report's `Chief X resigned; Y takes over`, unless the term already replaced them this tick), and `Election` sets `LawState.SnapElection = t.Day + N`, the one new field on the law's state, which `law.Sim.NextElection` returns when it comes before the term's end and the election clears when it is held (`ElectedDay = t.Day`, the term reset); an actor whose clock is stopped (a term of 0, `harness.Appoint`) is held through both. `TestSnapElectionAndResignation` holds it: the vote is `election_called` days after the call, the term resets, the chief changes the same morning and the headline names the one who left, because the event carries `Chief` and `DA` **as they were when it fired** and the news sim reads those over the morning's (`d.Chief = ev.Chief`).

**Named headlines.** `news.data` gained `Route`, `Days`, `DA`, `Chief`, `Leader` and `Faction` (`Big Sal's crew`), the last four filled on **every** line from the world (`base`), so any template in `headlines.toml` can read like a paper (`Chief {{.Chief}} hails 'major blow' to {{.City}} drug trade as DA {{.DA}} promises a crackdown`): the `{{.City}}` syntax, `format.A`'s article rewrite and `TestArticlesAgreeWithTheValue` (its vowel and consonant rows carry the new slots) cover them, and three existing lines were reworded in place to name the DA or the chief (`EnforcementRaid`'s third, `HeatWarning`'s second, `PressureShiftedUp`'s third) rather than added, so no list grew and no home-stream draw moved. The incident's slots are the event's: `{{.City}}` where it landed, `{{.Route}}`, `{{.Product}}` (the shock's, by name), `{{.Name}}` who it named, `{{.Days}}` its **first** timed effect's in a fixed order (the closure, the price shock, the demand shift, the federal window, the snap election; `game.IncidentDays`), so a row's text knows which it names. The journal entry has **source `world`** (first in `journalSources`, its own colour `theme.World`, tan, "the weather: an incident nobody caused", through `theme.Source`; `f` filters to it), and the **report opens with it**: `DayReport.Incident`, the `INCIDENT` section before `TIER` in `reportLines`, in `theme.Fg(theme.World)` (`TestIncidentInTheGrammar`; `TestIncidentOpensTheReport` in the harness holds that the section is there exactly on the mornings one fired and reads clean). The routes screen's pane adds a `closed  N nights to go` row while a route is shut (tonight and the nights after it).

**The table** (`incidents.toml`, `[incidents] min_gap = 8, max_gap = 26`, so one every two to three weeks): `port_strike` (boat routes shut 6, coke ×1.6 for 10 days in Bayport), `hurricane` (boat routes shut 4; the issue's "inland truck risk up" would need a `route_risk` key and state and is left out), `snowstorm` (car routes shut 2; the issue said "slow", and a slow key would need `RouteSetting` state of its own, so a short closure stands in), `harvest_glut` (coke ×0.6 for 12 days in Bayport), `new_synthetic` (pills demand ×0.5 for 60 days at home, once; the issue's "designer offered early" is left out on purpose: a product's listing is the market's peak-cash gate and the ladder rule of #60, and designer for a tier-2 crew was the indictment shape it removed), `celebrity_overdose` (pressure +15, names a celebrity), `documentary` (pressure +10, notoriety +10, once, `peak_cash_min` $100k), `journalist_profile` (notoriety +15, names a reporter, `peak_cash_min` $250k), `election_called` (7 days), `chief_resigns`, `federal_attention` (decay ×0.5 for 20 days, `peak_cash_min` $200k). The dice are fixed for the seed and only a row's trigger reads the player (as the deck's do): `TestIncidentStreamIsTheSeeds` sees the same stream for the idle and the hiding player over 200 days on three seeds and replays a run saved on the morning of one identically; `TestEveryIncidentFires` deals every row across 200 seeds × 300 days to a rich idle player and finds no hole in any headline; `TestIncidentPacing` and `TestIncidentEffectsLand` (`world/world_test.go`) pin the gaps, the once and the per-row cooldown, and where each key lands (the named city and no other, the routes of the mode and no other, the market's fields, the federal window, the record). `cmd/balance -trace` prints `day N incident id in city route R product P Nd` and every run prints `incidents: N per run, M of 11 in the table seen: …`.

**Adding an incident**: a `[[incident]]` row in `incidents.toml` with a `report`, an `Incident<CamelId>` list in `headlines.toml`, and nothing else; a new effect key is a field on `content.IncidentEffects`, a case in `World.ApplyIncident` and a line here. An effect that needs another sim's dice or tuning rides the event, as the two law effects do. Incidents the player can cause or buy off, tier-5 incidents and anything that ends a run are out of scope (no day cap, no scripted death, #27).
