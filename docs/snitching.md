# Snitching

**Snitching** (#13, `crew.toml [informant]`, `heat.toml informant_*`) is the crew sim and the heat sim talking through the world. A member under both the loyalty and the nerve line rolls daily to turn (`CrewMember.Informant`, the hidden flag; the roster never shows it, `Stats.Informants` counts them); the crew sim emits `CrewTurnedInformant`, which has no headline on purpose and which the heat sim consumes only to start its clock (`Heat.LeakDay`). From then on, every `informant_days`, the heat sim adds `informant_evidence` pages to `Heat.Evidence` and resets `Heat.EvidenceDay` whatever was sold and whatever the lawyer's `evidence_cut` says (a witness is not a bust), so an informant is the one exception to #27's rule and the reason the retainer's decay is worth having; it adds `informant_heat` that is deliberately left out of the `HeatChanged` reasons. The tells are therefore in the report: a "the DA's file on you grows" line under a heat delta with no sting or raid, and a delta the dial does not add up to. `Heat.Leaks` counts them, resets to zero once nobody on the payroll is talking, and at two the dashboard and crew screen hint "Somebody is talking." A raid while one is on the payroll goes to the fullest house in the city, which the informant knows (`HouseCompromised{Why: "informant"}`, `House.Known`), and takes the whole of it and nothing from the others (`Enforcement.Stash`, `House` and `HouseName`, `HouseRaided{Whole}`; #73); with no house it is the whole street, as it always was. The player's answers: `World.Investigate(cost)` queues a night's questions (`World.Investigation`, per-day scratch, paid up front from dirty then clean cash) that the crew sim resolves with `crew.Sim.InvestigateOdds` (base, plus the best enforcer's skill, plus `investigate_learn` per empty night in `Crew.Investigated`; the UI shows the same number), naming an informant into `Crew.Exposed` (the roster marks them SNITCH) or costing everyone `investigate_loyalty`; `World.PayOff(id, cost, loyalty)` buys loyalty at once, not silence; and `Fire`, which is the only thing that stops an informant, and which the crew do not hold against you when it was one (`CrewFired.Informant`). At the quit threshold a member walks as before unless the rival holds ground, in which case they defect (`CrewDefected`, naming the rival and its `Faction`; `Crew.Leads`, the crew sim's own queue since #144, started afresh every crew step so it holds last night's and only last night's; `Stats.Defections`): the rival sim, next step, reads the queue, adds them to its muscle and walks onto the corner they ran if nobody stands there (`CornerTaken.Handed`), or pushes at the usual odds if somebody does (the crew sim writes nothing into `w.Rival`: `TestRivalStateHasOneWriter`, `TestDefectionQueuesALeadForTheRival`); a corner in another city is no lead at all (the rival lives at home: the crew sim hands over none and the rival sim refuses one, `TestDefectionElsewhereHandsNoCorner`). `harness.Plant` puts an informant on the payroll from day 0, `harness.Vigilant` is the crewed player who investigates once the tell has shown twice and fires whoever is named, and `snitch_test.go` pins the acceptance: the always-quiet player with a plant is indicted within `harness.SnitchDays`, the vigilant one survives, a skill-90 enforcer names the informant within three nights on every seed and a skill-10 one takes materially longer, a defection is a rival corner gain the next day. An audit (#29) is the other way in: the laundering sim, stepping after heat, turns the least loyal accountant under the same loyalty line with no dice (`laundering.Sim.flip`, `TestAuditFlipsTheAccountant`), and because heat never sees that tick's event it stamps `LeakDay` every day nobody is talking, so the clock runs from the audit either way. **Your own tip is the other bend in #27** (#70): a tip to the police on a rival corner (`PoliceTipped`) files a page with `heat.toml tip_evidence` (0.2) chance, rolled off `Tick.Sub("books")`, whatever was sold: talking to police is talking to police, and the rule bends only because *you* did something, as the informant's pages are somebody on your payroll doing something. None of the new world fields needed a schema bump: their zero values are the pre-#13 state.
