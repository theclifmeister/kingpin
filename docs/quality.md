# Quality is the last dial

**Quality is the last dial** (#47, `market.toml [quality]` and the per-product `cut_max` / `cut_cost` / `cook_cost`, `crew.toml [role.chemist]`, `suppliers.toml` `quality` per connect, `law.toml [pressure] overdose`, `reputation.toml [notoriety] overdose`, `names.toml chemists`; `game/world.go` the lots, `Cut`, `CookOrder`, `MigrateLots`; `sim/market/quality.go` the price, the corners' memory and the overdoses; `sim/crew` the chemist and the cook; `ui/quality.go` the two dialogs; `harness/quality.go` `Cutter` and `Cook`). **Stock has a quality**, 0..100, one number per product per city (`Player.Quality[city][product]`, the street and the houses there as one lot: the raid and the robbery take units, so a quality per house would split nothing; `World.Quality(city, product)`, `World.Lot`, `game.Lot{Units, Quality}`), kept only by the accessors (`TestStashHasNoWriters` greps `Player.Quality` as it does `Player.Stash`): `AddStock(city, product, units, quality)` blends what comes in by units (a lot with nothing in it takes the incoming figure; a quality of zero or under reads as the default, so a card's windfall is street product), every take (`TakeStock`, `TakeStreet`, `TakeFromHouse`) and every move within a city leave the mean where it is, a house-first landing and a street-first draw hold the one number (`TestLotQuality`), `SetQuality` is the tests' and the migration's, and a `Shipment` carries `Quality` from the stash it left (`World.Send`) to the one it lands in. The **default** (`[quality] default`, 50) is what the connects sell at (`Supplier.Quality[product]`, stamped by the market sim from `suppliers.toml`'s `quality` table per connect, missing is the default; every connect in the file sells at it today, so the pinned rows stand, and the table is the content pass's dial), what a lot with no figure reads (an empty stash, a stash set by hand) and where the price multiplier is 1; the market sim stamps it onto the world (`World.BaseQuality`) at seed and every morning as it does `Markup`, and `game.StreetQuality` (50) is what a world built by hand reads before it has. **The cut** (`World.Cut(city, product, ratio, most, cost, bonus, chemist)`, instant, `actions.go`'s errors `ErrBadRatio`, `ErrNothingToCut`) adds `round(units x ratio)` units at nothing, so the lot's quality falls by the same ratio (the pure weight is conserved: `units x (1 + ratio)` at `quality / (1 + ratio)`), costs `cut_cost` a unit added in dirty cash, is refused past the product's `cut_max` (1.0: doubled), past the room the city has (the units have to be held) and past the till, and goes on the day's scratch (`Today.Cuts`, `CutRecord`) for the report (`StockCut`, report-only; `Stats.Cut`, `CutCost`); a chemist's hand puts `bonus` points back, never over what it was (`TestCut`). **The price pays the quality** (`market.Sim.QualityMul`, `content.QualityTuning.Mul`): linear from `low_mul` (0.7) at 0 to 1 at the default and from there to `high_mul` (1.25) at 100, on every street sale (`resolveAt`: the average, the undercut units' price too; `PlayerSold.Quality` and `QualityMul`, the report's `, quality 33 x0.83` where it left a mark) and on a buyer's premium (`deliver`: a contract delivers at the stash's quality and the premium pays the multiplier, `ContractDelivered.Quality`); the rival sells at the default and has no lots. **The corners remember** (`Corner.Repeat`, 0..1, the market sim's field on the territory's corner, `TestSimsWriteOnlyTheirOwnState` lists `Cities.Corners.Repeat` as the market's; `Corner.Repeats()` reads a zero as all of them, the pre-#47 corner): the share of a corner's customers who come back, folded into `Corner.Share` and so into `World.HeldShare` and `World.Demand`, the one served-demand number the market, the heat sim, the UI and the harness read (the ruling was `market.Sim.Demand`; folding it a step lower keeps the dial preview and the heat sim's `attempted` honest, and a corner at 1 multiplies by nothing, so the number is what it was). Every worked corner in a city sold the same mix tonight (an order is the city's), so the day's quality there is one units-weighted number (`soldQuality`): under `repeat_floor` (40) every worked corner there loses `repeat_loss` (0.0075), sold better it wins `repeat_gain` (0.005) back, never under `repeat_min` (0.2) or over 1, and a corner sold nothing (a quiet day, nobody on it) holds what it remembers, so a rest day is no cure and good product is (the crewed player sells every other day, and a recovery on the days off made a half cut free: `market.Sim.repeat`). The market stamps `repeat_start` (1.0) onto a corner reading zero at seed and every morning, so a corner laid out by a later step starts with all its customers. **Overdoses** (`market.Sim.overdoses`, `events.Overdose{City, Corner, CornerName, Product, Quality}`, `Stats.Overdoses`): a night a hard product (`od_products`, the law's list said again in the market's own file) sells under `od_quality` (30) off your own corners rolls once per `od_units` (20) sold there, a part of one in proportion, at `od_chance` (0.15), off `Tick.Sub("overdose")`, and every hit names one of the corners worked there by share; the **law sim** adds `[pressure] overdose` (6) in the city, the **reputation sim** `[notoriety] overdose` (3), the **news sim** a headline naming the corner under source `overdose` (off `overdose:news`; a source neither the law nor notoriety counts, so it is not counted twice) and a LAW line in the report, and the heat sim never reads it: an overdose is pressure and news, never a page (#27; `TestOverdosesArePressureNeverEvidence` greps the heat sim for the word and runs the cut-everything player against the same run with `od_chance` zeroed: pressure and notoriety up, a sting or raid on a quiet day adding nothing). **The chemist** (`[role.chemist]`, `game.RoleChemist`, `CrewState.Chemist()` the best by skill) is who makes quality. One comes looking for work once `unlock_product` (meth) is on the ladder (`crew.Sim.ChemistsWanted`, announced as `Unlocked{Gate: "role", ID: "chemist"}` the way the accountant is): the pool carries **one chemist beside its usual faces**, not in place of one, generated off `Tick.Sub("chemist")` with a name from `names.toml`'s `chemists` (a list of their own, so the crew names roll as they did), and `refill` counts the faces without them, so the pool the home stream draws is the pool it always drew and no policy hires a different runner for it (the #42 lane's fixer displaced one; this is why the chemist does not). Their quality is `quality_base + quality_per_skill x skill` (30 + 0.6 x skill: 39 at 15, 81 at 85, `crew.Sim.ChemistQuality`), what a cook lands at; a cut with one on the payroll keeps `cut_bonus x skill` points of what it would have lost (0.2: a skill-80 chemist keeps 16, so a half cut of the default lands back at 49 and a full one at 41, over the floor: cutting with a good chemist is nearly free, `CutBonus`); a cook is for at most `batch_per_skill x skill` units an order (2.0, `Batch`) and takes `cook_days` (3, `CookDays`). **The cook** (`World.CookOrder(city, product, units, cost, days, quality, batch, chemist)`, `game.Cook` on `CrewState.Cooks`, `ErrNoChemist`, `ErrNotCooked`, `ErrBatch`, `ErrCooking`): precursors cost the product's `cook_cost` a unit (meth 300 against 440 from a street connect, designer 900 against 1,375; a product with none is bought, never cooked), dirty, paid on the order, one order a product a city a day, refused past the room with what is already on its way counted (`CrewState.Cooking`); the lot's quality is the chemist's the day it was ordered, so firing them drops the next cook's, not this one's (`TestCookBeatsBuying`: with two on the payroll the best cooks, without the best the next does, with none nothing), and the crew sim lands it the morning it is ready (`crew.Sim.land`: `AddStock` at that quality, `Cooked` report-only, `CookOrdered` report-only the morning after the order, so the money lines read the day it was paid; `Stats.Cooked`, `CookCost`). Designer cooks at home: the port's product reaches home by the road or by a chemist. The lab raid that jails them is #46's; nothing here is left for it but the role. **The old run** (`TestNoCutIsTheOldRun`): the crewed player over 120 days sells every unit at the default with the multiplier at 1, no corner's `Repeat` moves, nothing overdoses, no chemist is hired, and its net worth every day and its stats are what the same run makes with the `[quality]` table in its box (`market.Sim.boxed`: a zero default stamps, moves and rolls nothing); every pinned tier row stands (t1 $84,930, t2 $605,294 as before; t3 and t4 the boss's under #193). **Schema 13** (`World.MigrateLots(quality, repeat)`, the 12 -> 13 step through `market.Sim.MigrateLots`): a save from before carried units alone, so every lot holding anything, every shipment on the road and every connect's product gets the default and every corner `repeat_start`, prices as saved and never restamped (`TestSaveMigratesTheSuppliers`); the old save plays on as it did. **The greed curve** (`harness.Cutter(cfg, ratio, policy)`, `cmd/balance -cut R`): the wrapper plays the policy, returns `ratio / (1 + ratio)` of what it bought today to the connect (`World.Return`, the exact inverse of the buy, so the saving is the returned units' cost) and cuts the lot to put the same units back at nothing, so the bag holds what the policy meant and its order stands; measured on the managed player over five seeds, `-cut 0.5` is $105,695 at day 30 against $83,801 uncut and $282,573 at day 120 against $353,569 (`TestGreedCurve`: a third off the bag today, the corners' customers tomorrow; the crewed player reads +22% at 30 and -10% at 200), and `-cut 1.0` draws the overdoses (about forty a run for the managed player over 200 days). **The cook policy** (`harness.Cook`, `-policy cook`) is laundered with a chemist: it hires the first chemist looking (the least skilled runner making room on a full roster, as the delegated player does for a lieutenant) and every morning cooks meth and designer toward the demand's share of the bag instead of buying them, the rest of the ladder bought as laundered buys it; $2.16M at day 120 and $5.25M at 200 against laundered's $0.92M and $1.49M, well under distributor's $7.2M and $37.9M (the road's lots are the tier-3 engine, the chemist is a home-town one; the tier-4 band is the boss's). `TestQualityInvariants` walks a cutting crewed run, a full-cut managed one and the cook every morning (quality in 0..100, `Repeat` in `repeat_min..1`, no lot under the connects' figure that no cut or chemist's lot has touched), `TestQualityIsDeterministicAndSaves` replays the cutting cook from its seed and from a save with cut lots, a cook on its way and the corners' memory. The output's `quality:` line prints the units cut in and their cost, the units cooked and theirs, the overdoses, the runs ending with a chemist, the mean quality sold and the median repeat business of the held corners at the end. **UI**: the market table's `qual` column after `stash` (the lot's figure, blank with nothing held, `Warning` under the default; dropped where MAIN is too narrow for the table whole, where the pane carries a `quality` row for a lot that is not at the default and a `cooking` row for a lot on its way; a connect's figure under it where it is not the default, and in the connect's pane section and the buy dialog's supplier line), the **cut dialog** (`t` on the market, `modeCut`, `labDialog`: the products the stash where you stand holds with their quality, the most and the cost a unit, then the percent added through `numberField`, blank the most the product, the room and the till allow, with the preview `After 150 units at quality 33, sells at x0.83, for $50`; a lie-low day and an empty stash are refused in the status bar), the **cook dialog** (`o` on the market, `modeCook`, listed and live only with a chemist on the payroll, `hasChemist`: the products a chemist cooks with the cook and the buy price a unit, then the units, blank a batch, with the cost against a connect's), both for where you stand (`TestCutDialog`, `TestCookDialog`, `TestModalsFit` opens all four pages), the map's `repeat` row on a corner that has lost customers (`repeatText`, a row the corner never sold under the floor does without, so the pane reads as it did), the crew screen's chemist (`the lab` / `cooking` in the post column, `cooks at quality 78, 160 a batch` and `cuts keep 16 points` in the pane for the best, `second to the best chemist` for another), the report's SALES cut line, CREW cook lines and LAW overdose line, and help's WORDS (`quality`, `cut`, `cook`, `repeat`, `overdose`).
