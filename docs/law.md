# The law has actors

**The law has actors** (#41, `internal/sim/law`, `World.Law`, `City.Pressure`, `City.Goodwill`, tuning in `law.toml`, names in `names.toml`).
A **police chief** (`Chief{Name, Personality, Since, Observed}`, `corrupt | zealous | lazy`) and a **DA** (`DA{Name, Stance, ElectedDay}`, `law_and_order | moderate | reform`) are drawn from the day-0 RNG after the crew and the rival (`law.Sim.Seed`; schema 8 -> 9 seeds them on an old save from the current day, `law.Sim.Migrate`).
They are tables the heat sim multiplies *its own* tuning by, read off `w.Law` through `content.LawConfig` and never the other way: the chief's `cooldown` scales `cooldown_days` for stings and raids (the patrol keeps its cadence under every chief: it fires as often as its cap lifts, and one that came back sooner would never lift, which is what stops managed play paying at all), `cap` what a patrol lets through and `decay` the daily decay (`heat.Sim.CooldownDays`, `PatrolCap`, `Decay`); the DA's `evidence_arrest` scales the pages an indictment needs (`heat.Sim.EvidenceArrest`, over the retainer's) and `sting` the sting line (`heat.Sim.Threshold`). #27 holds throughout: the law moves thresholds, cooldowns and decay and never adds a page (`TestQuietDayRuleHoldsUnderEveryLaw` runs the rich hider under every chief and DA).
**Pressure** is per city, 0-100: the law sim, stepping after heat (so the police answer on *yesterday's* pressure, the way reputation reads yesterday's journal) and before laundering, adds `[pressure]` sources (`CornerStruck`, `RivalPushed`, a flip and a crackdown at home; hard product, `hard_products`, per `hard_units` sold in the city; yesterday's headlines about you, `headline_sources` as notoriety's, where you are; a front whose growth made the paper yesterday, `front_grew` = 5 off `Front.Grew == t.Day-1`, where you are, #192), fades it toward `baseline` by `decay` of the gap, takes `goodwill_cut x Goodwill/100` off it, and reports a band crossed (`PressureShifted`, headlines `PressureShiftedUp/Down`).
What pressure does, each sim reads off `w.Law`'s city through `[effects]` in its own config: the heat sim cuts the patrol, raid and arrest thresholds of the city whose police answer by `pressure_threshold_cut` at 100 and what a patrol there lets through by `pressure_cap_cut` (the sting line is the DA's alone: it is where a case starts), the rival sim raises its tip chance by `pressure_tip` (`rivals.Sim.TipPace`, the home city's).
**Goodwill** is bought with `World.Fund(city, amount)`, clean cash only (`ErrNoCleanCash`, "the clean pile is empty", whatever the dirty pile and whatever the fronts have washed before, #474; a sum at or below zero is `ErrBadAmount`; `Reserve(amount)` beside it, #195, is the same rule for the offshore account, `docs/laundering.md`), queued in `World.Today.Funded` scratch and converted by the law sim at `goodwill_cash` a point (`CityFunded`, report-only), capped at 100 and fading by `goodwill_decay` a day; it is what 4.9's legitimate ending will read. A front standing in a city whose role carries `goodwill_day` (the restaurant, 0.25: the chief eats there, #344, `docs/laundering.md`) adds it there every day in `fund`, before the fade, with no cash and no event (the law sim folds the tree for that one word, `game.FoldEffectsIn`).
**Terms**: the chief serves `chief_term` days and is replaced on schedule (`ChiefReplaced{Why: "term"}`), the report naming their personality only once `Observed` (`observe_days` in office, or any patrol, sting or raid); **elections** every `term_days`: the law-and-order share of the vote is `0.5 + (mean pressure - 50)/100 x election_swing` (`law.Sim.LawAndOrderShare`), a moderate takes `moderate` of every vote whatever the mood, one draw from `Tick.Sub("law")` decides it, a win on the sitting DA's ticket re-elects them by name (`DAElected{Incumbent}`, headline `DAReElected`), and a law-and-order DA elected with the mean over `replace_pressure` replaces the chief at once (`ChiefReplaced{Why: "da"}`, `ChiefReplacedDA`).
A term of 0 stops the clock (`harness.Appoint` uses it to hold a forced chief or DA for a run).
The world's incidents (#44, `docs/incidents.md`) reach the law through the `Incident` event the same tick: a chief who resigned is replaced at once on the law's dice (`ChiefReplaced{Why: "resigned"}`, `Chief X resigned; Y takes over`), and a snap election sets `LawState.SnapElection`, the day `NextElection` returns when it comes before the term's end and the election clears when it is held, the term reset from it (`TestSnapElectionAndResignation`); a term of 0 holds the actor through both.
The dashboard's LAW panel (four lines: the chief and what they are like once observed with the days left, the DA and their ticket with the election countdown, the pressure bar where you are in heat's red with the goodwill, the other city's pressure; at 80 columns the days and the countdown go, `law-and-order` reads `law-order` and the last line is the rival's) sits beside RIVALS, the heat gauge's marks are `heat.Sim.ThresholdsIn(w, here)` so the ladder the player reads is the one the dice use, `f` on the ledger opens the fund dialog (`ui/law.go`, `modeFund`: left/right turn the city, an amount, blank fills goodwill to 100 from what is in hand less tonight's clean upkeep (#458), with the upkeep warning when the amount leaves less), and the report has a LAW section (elections and new chiefs first).
`harness/law_test.go` pins the acceptance: the aggressive trader is indicted sooner under a zealous chief than a lazy one and the hot crewed player sooner under a law-and-order DA than a reformer, pressure and goodwill stay in 0-100 under every policy, a hit war is louder than holding ground, the `funded` player ends quieter than `laundered` and gave only what its fronts washed, elections at pressure 80 return law-and-order more often than at 20 over 50 seeds and fire on schedule with a headline naming the winner, and the law survives a save.
The sting line is also where an investigation opens (#343, `docs/corners.md`): the DA who moves the line moves the night the police name an operation, and the law never names one itself; a favour called in on the night one lands stands it down like the sting (`heat.Sim.Due` reads `sting` that morning).
With investigations on in the file, the hot crewed player of `TestChiefAndDATable` lies low at the sting line itself (`HotCrewedLine`, 55, which `TooHot` moves with the DA): lying low at 50 its mornings fall on the nights the named hits land, so it misses them and files nothing, and it was indicted on 4 (law and order) and 1 (reform) of 20 seeds, both medians the horizon; riding the line, law and order indicts on day 31 against the reformer's 36 (45 against 54 with the blind sting).
The DA's two levers are unchanged and both reach the investigation: the line it opens at and the thinner file.
The harness policies that "pay attention" (`harness.TooHot`) lie low at the same fraction of the sting line as `heat.toml` prints it, and `RedMargin` lower once the file is within two pages of an indictment: the law moves the ladder the dashboard shows, and a scripted player calibrated to a fixed 55 would otherwise be stung on every seed that draws a zealous chief.

**The dial stays, and the arrest gets a night's warning** (#475, a ruling from the third playtest, #457).
The playtest found the sell dial binary: `normal` and `aggressive` indicted on every seed (`normal` median day 59, 40/40), and a reckless run arrested at dawn one night after its heat crossed the line, with nothing that said "tomorrow".
Measured with `cmd/balance`, 20 seeds x 300 days, incidents on: `normal`, `aggressive` and `quiet` are the naive traders, selling everything and never lying low; `managed`, the same dial lying low at 50, is 0/20 indicted at $666K; `surgeon`, which answers the named investigations, 2/20; lying low only at the sting line (55) 12/20, because the investigation is already open by then.
Not reacting is what kills a player, and so is reacting only at the sting line. **The dial and the evidence ramp stay**: the ramp is #343's guard (`TestSurgeonBeatsManaged`), and the README's first-day paragraph now says the normal dial needs lie-low days.
**The arrest was the real gap.** In `reckless`'s heat arrests the heat the morning before read 50-87 and one night added 15-45, so no morning alert could promise "tomorrow"; and a night a named hit or a task force landed set the ladder aside (`resp = nil`) and skipped the arrest rung whole, which is how heat could read 100 one morning and the arrest come the next.
**The warrant** (`heat.toml [heat] warrant_days` 1, `HeatState.WarrantDay`, `heat.Sim.warrant` in `ladder.go`, `WarrantDue`, `ArrestLine`; `events.WarrantSigned{City, Heat, Line, Due}`, `WarrantLapsed{City, Signed, Heat, Line}`, both report-only): the arrest line met in the city whose police answer signs a warrant instead of arresting, the night's one response on an ordinary night.
`warrant_days` nights later it is **served**, the arrest rung fired as it always was (the fall guy and the identity still take it), on any sale attempted anywhere or on the heat still at the line; otherwise it **lapses** and the line met again signs another.
It is read **before** the task force and the investigation blocks, so a warrant due tonight is served whatever else lands, and the line met on a task-force or hit night signs one beside them (they were announced, and still come).
