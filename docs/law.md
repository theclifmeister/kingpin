# The law has actors

**The law has actors** (#41, `internal/sim/law`, `World.Law`, `City.Pressure`, `City.Goodwill`, tuning in `law.toml`, names in `names.toml`). A **police chief** (`Chief{Name, Personality, Since, Observed}`, `corrupt | zealous | lazy`) and a **DA** (`DA{Name, Stance, ElectedDay}`, `law_and_order | moderate | reform`) are drawn from the day-0 RNG after the crew and the rival (`law.Sim.Seed`; schema 8 -> 9 seeds them on an old save from the current day, `law.Sim.Migrate`). They are tables the heat sim multiplies *its own* tuning by, read off `w.Law` through `content.LawConfig` and never the other way: the chief's `cooldown` scales `cooldown_days` for stings and raids (the patrol keeps its cadence under every chief: it fires as often as its cap lifts, and one that came back sooner would never lift, which is what stops managed play paying at all), `cap` what a patrol lets through and `decay` the daily decay (`heat.Sim.CooldownDays`, `PatrolCap`, `Decay`); the DA's `evidence_arrest` scales the pages an indictment needs (`heat.Sim.EvidenceArrest`, over the retainer's) and `sting` the sting line (`heat.Sim.Threshold`). #27 holds throughout: the law moves thresholds, cooldowns and decay and never adds a page (`TestQuietDayRuleHoldsUnderEveryLaw` runs the rich hider under every chief and DA). **Pressure** is per city, 0-100: the law sim, stepping after heat (so the police answer on *yesterday's* pressure, the way reputation reads yesterday's journal) and before laundering, adds `[pressure]` sources (`CornerStruck`, `RivalPushed`, a flip and a crackdown at home; hard product, `hard_products`, per `hard_units` sold in the city; yesterday's headlines about you, `headline_sources` as notoriety's, where you are; a front whose growth made the paper yesterday, `front_grew` = 5 off `Front.Grew == t.Day-1`, where you are, #192), fades it toward `baseline` by `decay` of the gap, takes `goodwill_cut x Goodwill/100` off it, and reports a band crossed (`PressureShifted`, headlines `PressureShiftedUp/Down`). What pressure does, each sim reads off `w.Law`'s city through `[effects]` in its own config: the heat sim cuts the patrol, raid and arrest thresholds of the city whose police answer by `pressure_threshold_cut` at 100 and what a patrol there lets through by `pressure_cap_cut` (the sting line is the DA's alone: it is where a case starts), the rival sim raises its tip chance by `pressure_tip` (`rivals.Sim.TipPace`, the home city's). **Goodwill** is bought with `World.Fund(city, amount)`, clean cash only (`ErrNoCleanCash`, whatever the dirty pile), queued in `World.Today.Funded` scratch and converted by the law sim at `goodwill_cash` a point (`CityFunded`, report-only), capped at 100 and fading by `goodwill_decay` a day; it is what 4.9's legitimate ending will read. **Terms**: the chief serves `chief_term` days and is replaced on schedule (`ChiefReplaced{Why: "term"}`), the report naming their personality only once `Observed` (`observe_days` in office, or any patrol, sting or raid); **elections** every `term_days`: the law-and-order share of the vote is `0.5 + (mean pressure - 50)/100 x election_swing` (`law.Sim.LawAndOrderShare`), a moderate takes `moderate` of every vote whatever the mood, one draw from `Tick.Sub("law")` decides it, a win on the sitting DA's ticket re-elects them by name (`DAElected{Incumbent}`, headline `DAReElected`), and a law-and-order DA elected with the mean over `replace_pressure` replaces the chief at once (`ChiefReplaced{Why: "da"}`, `ChiefReplacedDA`). A term of 0 stops the clock (`harness.Appoint` uses it to hold a forced chief or DA for a run). The world's incidents (#44, `docs/incidents.md`) reach the law through the `Incident` event the same tick: a chief who resigned is replaced at once on the law's dice (`ChiefReplaced{Why: "resigned"}`, `Chief X resigned; Y takes over`), and a snap election sets `LawState.SnapElection`, the day `NextElection` returns when it comes before the term's end and the election clears when it is held, the term reset from it (`TestSnapElectionAndResignation`); a term of 0 holds the actor through both. The dashboard's LAW panel (four lines: the chief and what they are like once observed with the days left, the DA and their ticket with the election countdown, the pressure bar where you are in heat's red with the goodwill, the other city's pressure; at 80 columns the days and the countdown go, `law-and-order` reads `law-order` and the last line is the rival's) sits beside RIVALS, the heat gauge's marks are `heat.Sim.ThresholdsIn(w, here)` so the ladder the player reads is the one the dice use, `f` on the ledger opens the fund dialog (`ui/law.go`, `modeFund`: left/right turn the city, an amount, blank fills goodwill to 100 from what is in hand), and the report has a LAW section (elections and new chiefs first). `harness/law_test.go` pins the acceptance: the aggressive trader is indicted sooner under a zealous chief than a lazy one and the hot crewed player sooner under a law-and-order DA than a reformer, pressure and goodwill stay in 0-100 under every policy, a hit war is louder than holding ground, the `funded` player ends quieter than `laundered` and gave only what its fronts washed, elections at pressure 80 return law-and-order more often than at 20 over 50 seeds and fire on schedule with a headline naming the winner, and the law survives a save. The harness policies that "pay attention" (`harness.TooHot`) lie low at the same fraction of the sting line as `heat.toml` prints it, and `RedMargin` lower once the file is within two pages of an indictment: the law moves the ladder the dashboard shows, and a scripted player calibrated to a fixed 55 would otherwise be stung on every seed that draws a zealous chief.

**Campaigns** (#193, `law.toml [campaign]`, `World.Back`, `City.Campaign`, `DA.Backed`, `LawState.CampaignOpen`): the politician half of §4.8. Every `term_days` the city elects a DA; from `open_days` (30) before the vote to the day of it the two tickets the share separates, `reform` and `law_and_order` (`game.Tickets`; the moderate takes `moderate` of every vote whatever the mood, so money cannot move them), take **clean cash**: `World.Back(city, ticket, amount)` in `game/law.go` beside `Fund` (clean only, `ErrNoCleanCash`; `ErrCampaignClosed` outside the window, which the law sim stamps on `w.Law.CampaignOpen` at the end of every step for the day to come, `law.Sim.CampaignOpen(w, day)`, so `Back` refuses without the law's tuning; `ErrNoTicket` for any other ticket), queued in `World.Today.Backed`, folded by the law sim into the city's `Campaign{Ticket, Cash, Hedged}` after the goodwill and reported (`CampaignBacked`, report-only: the amount, the campaign's total and the points it moves; `Stats.Backed`, `Stats.Campaigns` counts a city's campaign the night it first holds money). Money on the other ticket sets `Hedged`: the cash still counts and buys nothing. A campaign is public: `pressure` (0.5) a day in every city whose campaign holds money, added with the day's sources before the fade. **At the count** the share moves *before the one draw* from `Tick.Sub("law")`, so a run with no money on the table rolls the same dice as before the feature: `law.Sim.Swing(w)` is the mean over the cities (as pressure is) of each city's `± min(swing_max, Cash / cash / 100)` (`content.CampaignTuning.Swing`: `cash` $200,000 buys a point, one hundredth, of that city's vote; `swing_max` 0.15; `Fill()` $3M is what buys a city the whole swing), positive toward law and order, a hedged campaign nothing; `share = clamp01(LawAndOrderShare(mean) + swing)`, the moderate's cut and the draw as before, so money moves the vote and never decides it. The campaigns are then spent (`City.Campaign` zeroed): a city whose ticket won makes the winner **`DA.Backed`** (set on every election: an incumbent re-elected without your money is not), `DAElected{Swing, Backed}` with the headline `DABought` in place of `DAElected`/`DAReElected` and `Stats.CampaignsWon`; a city whose ticket lost gets `loser_pressure` (10) at once (before the same step's fade) and, if the winner is law-and-order and `loser_chief` is on, a **zealous chief** at once (`ChiefReplaced{Why: "campaign"}`, headline `ChiefReplacedCampaign`, `replaceChief` with the personality forced and the name still drawn from the side stream; one replacement an election, so the `replace_pressure` rule then stands down), and `CampaignLost{City, Ticket, Cash, Winner, Pressure, Chief}` with a headline; a hedged campaign is `CampaignHedged` and nothing else. What a backed DA is worth: the heat sim reads `DA.Backed` through `law.toml [effects] backed_sting` (1.10) on the sting line, on top of the DA's own `sting`, and nothing else on the ladder (`TestLadderUnderTheLaw`); #42's `da_price` reads `backed_da_price_mul` (0.5). Nothing here touches the file: #27 holds. **UI**: the fund dialog (`f` on the ledger, `modeFund`) is two pages while a campaign is open (`Model.hasPages`, `campaignOpen`): the first as before (the city dial, the goodwill amount; `enter next`, and `tab` once the amount reads), the second `FUND · city · campaign` (`viewCampaign`: the ticket as a dial on `←→`, what the campaign holds today's money included (`World.Campaigning`), an amount whose blank is *nothing* and whose `m` is `maxBack`: the clean cash left after the goodwill up to what fills the swing; `enter give` gives both, `⇧tab back`, `esc` closes from either); with the window shut the dialog is the one page it was and `tab` is silent on it. The dashboard's LAW panel shows `backing reform $250K` (or `backing both` in red) after the pressure while a campaign here holds money, the bar giving way to the number for it; an ALERT names the race while the window is open, you have clean cash and this city's campaign has none of it; the report's LAW section names the money in and, at the count, what it bought; the run's summary counts `behind the DA tickets`. **Harness**: `funded` and `boss` back the reform ticket in every city the day its campaign opens (`harness.backReform`: `FundShare` of the clean cash in hand up to `Fill()`, once a city an election), and the boss now pays the town as `funded` does, in every city (`payTown`); `cmd/balance` prints `campaigns: N backed, N won, $N put behind a ticket per run`. Pinned: `TestCampaigns` (law_test.go: the window, the money in and the pressure it adds, the swing's cap and mean, over 50 seeds at pressure 80 full reform campaigns return law-and-order less often than none and still return it, the backed winner, the backed loser under a moderate and under law and order with the zealous chief, the hedge), `TestBack` (game), `TestNoCampaignIsTheOldRun`, `TestCampaignSurvivesSave`, `TestCampaignSizing` (the boss puts $1M-$10M a city into a tier-4 election, $3M the fill, and ends with goodwill over 50; `funded` under $500k a campaign at tier 3), `TestCampaignKeys` (ui). Sizing at HEAD: `boss` 10 runs, 200 days: 40 campaigns backed, 6 won, $5.5M a run, goodwill 97 at the end; `funded`: $342k a run, 22 of 40 won. **The bought law** (#42, `law.toml [bribes]` and `[effects]`, `World.Bribe`, `World.BuyCheckpoint`, the law's `ChiefBought`, `ChiefShare`, `DABought`, `Leads`, `LeadDay`, `Backfired`, `Filed`, `Cold` on `LawState`, `RouteSetting.Bought`, `crew.toml [role.fixer]`): money's third use after product and fronts, in **dirty cash** (cash in a bag;
