# Events are the cross-sim channel

**Events are the cross-sim channel.** Sims never call each other, and no package under `internal/sim/` imports another (`TestSimsNeverImportEachOther` in `sim/sim_test.go` parses every non-test file's imports, #144; a query two sims share, `game.Eligible`, lives in `game`). A sim emits typed events (`internal/events/events.go`, each with a stable `Kind()`) via `Tick.Emit`; later sims in the same tick read `Tick.Events()` to react (territory, crew and heat consume the market's `PlayerSold`, the crew sim for a lieutenant's cut and the heat sim for their temper; crew applies the rival sim's `CornerStruck` toll to enforcers by nerve; heat adds the heat carried on `CornerStruck`, `RivalTippedPolice`, a crackdown `WarEscalated` and the logistics sim's `ShipmentSeized`; the law sim counts the violence, the hard product on `PlayerSold` and an `Enforcement` that shows the chief's hand; news consumes everything). After the tick, the bus delivers events to the UI (`Model.onEvent`) for the alerts and the journal. Events are not saved: a sim that must act on last night's event next step (the rivals sim on a defector) reads a queue the emitting sim keeps on its own state (`Crew.Leads`, #144), never another sim's. Every event that names the rival by name carries a `Faction` id beside it (`docs/rival.md`). Adding an event kind means adding a headline template in `internal/content/headlines.toml` and listing it in `TestEveryEmittedEventHasTemplate`, unless it is report-only bookkeeping like `PriceMove`, `CrewPaid`, `RivalUndercut`, `WholesaleBought`, `SupplyBought`, `SupplyShort`, `StandingShort`, `SupplierBought`, `CreditTaken`, `DebtPaid`, `ShipmentSent`, `ShipmentArrived`, `LieutenantActed`, `CityFunded`, `PlayerUndercut`, `StockMoved`, `RentPaid`, `HouseCompromised`, `RivalScouted`, `PoliceTipped`, `StockCut`, `CookOrdered` or `Cooked` (#47), or deliberately silent like `CrewTurnedInformant` and `LieutenantFlipped`.
