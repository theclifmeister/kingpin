# Progression tiers are a name for what has opened

**Progression tiers are a name for what has opened** (#147, `internal/content/progression.toml`, `content.ProgressionConfig`, `game/progression.go`, `sim/news/progression.go`). The file is the ladder of §6.1, one `[[tier]]` a stage in order (`corner`, `crew`, `territory`, `distribution`; tier 5 arrives with #48): `name`, `blurb` (one line), `checkpoint` (the day the harness reads the money curve at: 30, 70, 120, 200), `opens` (short lines on what the stage opens, the interstitial's and the summary's, #149), `next` (free text on what the next stage takes) and `[tier.enter]`, the trigger that must hold to enter it, a `content.CardTrigger` like a card's or a buyer's, which gained `peak_cash_min` (`Stats.PeakCash`, the high-water mark every unlock reads; `cash_min` is today's pile) and `cities_held` (cities with a held corner, `World.CitiesHeld`, the lieutenant gate's count), both checked in `game.Eligible` (#144) and tabled in `TestTriggersHold`; decode refuses a checkpoint not past the tier before, a trigger on the first tier (it is day 0) or none on a later one, and an unknown key (`TestProgressionReadsAsALadder`). **A tier is read, never written by a sim**: nothing gates on it, the gates stay in their own files and a tier's trigger *describes* them: tier 2 `crew_min = 1` (the first hire), tier 3 `crew_min = 1, peak_cash_min = 25000` (the laundromat's line, the pool connect's, the last buyer's), tier 4 `crew_min = 1, peak_cash_min = 500000` (the wholesaler's and designer's). Every line past the first keeps the crew in it, so the ladder is strict by description and a tier is never skipped: the lone trader who never hires stays a Corner trader whatever the pile (`managed`, the money curve's tier-1 row at $85k, never leaves tier 1; `TestTiersAreOrdered` pins it beside `idle`), and the crewed player enters Territory with its laundromat and Distribution with its wholesaler. `World.Progression.Reached` is `map[int]int`, the tier's number (1 the first) to the day it was entered, sticky: a tier once reached stays reached whatever the pile does after; `World.Tier()` is the highest reached (1 while none is stamped: the first tier is day 0 and never stamped), `ReachedOn(n)` the day (0 for tier 1, -1 for one never reached), `TierName(cfg.Progression)` the file's name for the UI, `Reach(n, day)` the stamp. **The news sim stamps it at the top of its step** (`news.Sim.tier`, before the money loop): for every tier past the highest reached, in order, the first whose trigger holds this morning is stamped `t.Day` and emitted as `events.TierReached{Day, Tier, Name}`, **one a morning at most**, so a night that crosses two lines (the boss's wholesaler morning also opens designer) enters them on consecutive mornings and each has one thing to say; no dice (the headline, source `news`, the journal's `flavour`, picks its template off `Tick.Sub("progression")`, so the home stream never moves and a run before #147 replays byte-for-byte: no pinned number moved), and the reputation and law sims do not count it (`headline_sources` unchanged). The tier-3 morning is the first the news sim reads the laundromat's line off `Stats.PeakCash`, which the clock stamps *after* the sims step, so it is the morning after the ledger first reads `open to you`; the tier-4 morning is the wholesaler's `Unlocked{Gate: "connect"}` (#148), which reads the same stamp in the same tick (`TestTiersAreOrdered` pins both against the gates on five seeds of `boss`, and the order). The zero value is the pre-#147 world, so no schema bump: a save from before it loads with nothing reached and catches up the same way, one tier a morning from its first, the highest last, and is otherwise the same world (`TestProgressionIsDeterministicAndSaves`: a save on the morning a tier is entered reloads with it reached and never emits it again; `TestTiersOneAMorning`: a world past every line on day 0 enters them on days 1, 2 and 3). The report's TIER section is first (`DayReport.Tier`, `tierLines`: `TERRITORY, tier 3 of 4. <blurb>`, `Opens:` one line a thing, `Next:`), the dashboard's STREET facts carry `tier Territory` after the corners (`priTier`, the first fact dropped where the width has no room), the summary's `reached` row under `days survived` reads `Distribution on day 59` (`reachedLine`; the name alone for a fresh run) and help's WORDS has `tier` (`TestTierIsNamed` walks the three at 80x24 and 120x40 on the rich fixture, which is at tier 4). `harness.TierDays` is `cfg.Progression.Checkpoints()` (the same four numbers, so no pinned number moved), the money curve's rows read their days from it (`tierDay`), and `cmd/balance` prints `tiers: 2 Crew d2 · 3 Territory d12 · 4 Distribution d66 (median day entered)` per policy, `never` where half the runs never got there, so the tuning of a line is a number in the output. The per-gate announcements are #148's; tier 5's condition and row are #48's.
