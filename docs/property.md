# Property is the block a corner is on, bought with clean cash

**Property is the block a corner is on, bought with clean cash** (#194, #191's third proposal, §4.6 "clean cash buys property"; `city.toml [deed]`, `content.DeedTuning`, `game.Deed`, `Corner.Deed`, `World.BuyDeed`, `ui/deeds.go`). A deed is clean cash turned into ground: `Corner.Deed *Deed{Bought, Price}` (nil is the corner as it was, whoever holds it: no schema bump, a save from before loads with none) is bought through `World.BuyDeed(corner, price)` in `game/territory.go` beside `Post`, **clean cash only** (`ErrNoCleanCash`, `Fund`'s rule; the shortfall named otherwise), one deed a block (`ErrDeeded`), none where the file puts none on sale (`ErrNoDeeds`, a price of 0), and the corner need not be yours: a deed on a rival's block is legal, pays rent and cuts their defence of it, the businessman's way to a corner. The money goes at once (`Stats.Deeds`, `Stats.DeedCash`, `Today.DeedsBought` for the territory sim's report), `NetWorth` counts a deed at cost, and the deed stays on the block through every hand-over, both ways. **The price** is `territory.Sim.DeedPrice(w, c)` = `days` (90) × `World.CornerTrade(c)`, the street value the corner moves in a day at today's prices (every product the city sells, `Demand × Full(id) × Price`, summed over the ladder as unlocked: the shape of the rival's `trade`), so a rich block costs more and a block bought at tier 4 costs what tier 4's prices say ($400k–$1M a hub corner, $1M–$2M a home corner in the boss's runs; the rich fixture's Rail Yard with designer unlocked $5.2M); there is no property market: the price moves with demand at purchase only. **The rent** is `territory.Sim.DeedRent(d)` = `rent` (0.002) of the price a day, clean, paid by the territory sim at the top of its step (`deedStep`, before the corners and the houses) from the night of the purchase, deed by deed in city order (`Stats.DeedRent`, `DeedRent` report-only: `Rent from 3 blocks +$4,812 clean` in the report's MONEY), so a deed pays itself back in 500 days: cover, not income (#192's levels are the income). Everything else a deed does is read off the corner by the sim that owns the number, each copying `cfg.City.Deed` at construction and folding it where it reads the file's number, so the map's odds are the dice's; **no dice**: nothing here rolls, and a run that never buys a deed is byte-for-byte the run before (`TestNoDeedIsTheOldRun`: the laundered and the crewed players hashed daily on the file and under `harness.NoDeeds`, 120 days, three seeds; `cmd/balance -deeds off` prints 8b22ab1's trace to the dollar on boss, distributor, delegated and retiree over three seeds).

- **Robbery** (`sim/territory`): `robbery_mul` (0.5) on the robbery chance of the corner (`robberyChance`, the deed's factor beside the tree's `robbery_mul`, before the enforcer's cut) and of a house on that block (`houseRobberyChance`), no other corner's (`territory.TestDeedsPullTheirWay`).
- **The rival's push** (`sim/rivals`): `push_mul` (0.5) on the chance of a push at a corner of yours whose block is yours (`Sim.DeedMul(c)`, folded into the push loop's `chance` and into the price war's push through `PushPaceOn(w, c)` = `PushPace(w) × DeedMul(c)`), and on **their defence** of a corner of theirs whose block is yours: `Sim.OddsOn(w, r, c, force)` = `flip × attack / (attack + Defence(w, r) × DeedMul(c))`, what the strike and the boost roll on and what the strike picker, the boost confirmation and the inspector's `w` row show (`Odds(w, r, force)` is `OddsOn` with no corner: the faction's odds, the harness's boss and the map's route lines read it). `push_mul` is never zero (`DeedTuning.validate`, `content.TestDeedTuning`): a deed slows the rival and never stops it, the Street branch's rule (`rivals.TestDeedsPullTheirWay`: the pace and the odds move on the deeded corner and no other; `TestDeedSlowsTheRivalNeverStopsIt`: the passive player with the deed to every block it holds, next to an expansionist under `OneFaction`, loses a deeded corner by day 60 on 6 seeds of ten, never on all ten, 7 corners lost against 12 bare).
- **The raid** (`sim/heat`): `raid_mul` (0.5) on a house's weight in the raid's roll over the places holding stock (`Sim.RaidWeight(w, h)` = the block's heat × `raid_mul` where the block is yours; #73's `place`): a house on a deeded block is not known from the street. The roll is the same roll; only the weights move (`heat.TestDeedsPullTheirWay`).
- **Pressure** (`sim/law`): `pressure` (0.5) a day per deed held in a city, added with the day's sources before the fade, over what goodwill covers: deeds are public record (`law.TestDeedsPullTheirWay`).
- **The paper**: `DeedBought` is report-only bookkeeping; the purchase that brings a city's deeds to `headline_deeds` (4) or past it is `DeedsBought`, a headline under the laundering source (a story about your money, as `FrontGrew` is: notoriety through the reputation sim's `headline_sources` and pressure where you are through the law's, the morning after), picked off `Tick.Sub("deeds:news")` so a run with no deed draws the old run's headlines; the forfeiture's `DeedSeized` is the same source.
- **The forfeiture** (`sim/law`, at the end of its step, after the election): a threshold, not a die. When the deeds held cost more than `forfeit_ratio` (0.5) × `Stats.Laundered` (`law.Sim.DeedLimit(w)`, `Forfeits(w)`; `World.DeedValue()` is what the deeds held cost, a seized one leaving it) the money has no story: the DA seizes the newest deed (`World.NewestDeed`, `World.SeizeDeed`: no refund, `Stats.DeedsSeized`, the one write outside the law's own state, listed in `TestSimsWriteOnlyTheirOwnState`'s row), one a night until the rest are under the line, emits `DeedSeized{Corner, Price, Spent, Washed, Limit}` (a headline: `DA Ortega seizes the block Rail Yard is on: 'the money has no story'`) and stamps `Law.Forfeited` with the day; the heat sim, which steps before the law and so reads the law's night the next morning as it reads `Backfired` (#42), files `forfeit_evidence` (2) pages where you are, once, whatever was sold (`heat.TestDeedsPullTheirWay`): #27's spirit, buying it was something you did, the list of bends in `docs/corners.md` gaining the forfeiture's pages. `Stats.Laundered` is the wash alone: a run with no fronts and a deed off a card's clean cash loses it the first night (`law.TestDeedsPullTheirWay`), and the levels' income (#192) does not explain a deed. The pages live in the deed table with the rest of the deed's numbers (`heat.Sim.ForfeitEvidence` reads them off its own copy, as `backfire_evidence` lives in the law's file and the heat sim reads it): one table, one place to tune the fantasy.
