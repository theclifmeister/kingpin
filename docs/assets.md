# Assets are the supply side bought with clean money

**Assets are the supply side bought with clean money** (#48, `internal/content/assets.toml`, `content.AssetsConfig`, `game/assets.go`, `World.Assets`, `harness.Cartel`, `harness.Reckless`).
An asset is to the supply side what a front is to the money side: bought once through one action, owned as state, read by the sim that owns the number it moves through its own copy of the file, as the fronts and the tree are.
Five rows, one effect each (`AssetEffects`: `supplier`, `port`, `airstrip`, `lab`, `tunnel`; decode refuses a second row with the same effect, an unknown effect, a city that does not exist, a cost that is not positive, a floor off the scale or an effect's numbers out of range): `id`, `name`, `city`, `unlock_cash` (peak **clean** cash, `Stats.PeakClean`: the high-water mark the clock stamps beside `PeakCash` after the sims step, so the wash is the clock, whatever the street earns), `cost` (clean), `upkeep` (clean, a day), `heat_floor`, `pressure` (a day, in its city) and the effect's own numbers: `own_ratio` (the supplier), `capacity_mul` (the port), `lab_mul`, `lab_quality`, `lab_cost_mul` (the lab).
`[assets] upkeep_freeze_days` (3) is a front's rule for unpaid upkeep.
The file: the tunnel ($8M peak clean, $1.5M, $2k a day, floor 3), the Dutchman's book ($10M, $3M, $8k, floor 5, pressure 0.5 in Bayport, `own_ratio` 0.1), the lab ($14M, $4M, $12k, floor 8, pressure 1 at home, `lab_mul` 8, `lab_quality` 90, `lab_cost_mul` 0.5), the port ($20M, $2.5M, $5k, floor 6, pressure 1 in Bayport, `capacity_mul` 2), the airstrip ($28M, $4M, $8k, floor 8, pressure 1 at home).
The ladder of lines is the clock (#191: the wash caps clean income at a few hundred thousand a day and the levels earn about as much again): the boss's peak clean is $9.6M at the tier-4 checkpoint (day 200, the median of ten seeds; $3.4M at 120, $18M at 250, $25M at 300), so the first door opens about day 190 and the cartel buys the last of them in the last weeks before day 300; the costs are what the boss's levels forgo (a level pays 1% a day, #192), which is why they are small: see the ruling below.

**The state** (`game/assets.go`): `World.Assets []Asset` is what is owned, in the order bought (`ID`, `Name`, `Effect`, `City`, `Cost`, `Upkeep`, `Bought`, `FrozenUntil`), `World.AssetsLost` what the task force took or the police found (`Lost` the day, `Why` `seized` or `found`), for the record and the summary; both zero in a run before the feature, so **no schema bump** (`SchemaVersion` stays 14; the issue asked for one; `Stats.PeakClean` is zero in an old save and catches up the first `EndDay`; the plane and the tunnel are content, `World.Routes` is keyed by id, and a save from before has no entry for them, which is off).
`w.Asset(id)`, `HasAsset`, `AssetLive(id)` (owned and not idle tonight: the test every effect reads), `AssetLost(id)`.
**`BuyAsset(o AssetOffer)`** (`AssetOffer{ID, Name, Effect, City, Cost, Upkeep, UnlockCash, HeatFloor}`, priced by `laundering.Sim.AssetOffers` / `AssetOffer` / `BuyAsset`, cheapest first, as `Offers` prices the fronts): clean cash only (`ErrNoCleanCash`, Fund's rule, or a `ShortError`, `need $X, only have $Y clean`, #275), refused under the line (`Locked`: `Stats.PeakClean < UnlockCash`), owned (`ErrAssetOwned`) or gone for good (`ErrAssetGone`: the tunnel the police found; one the task force seized is for sale again at the price); it takes the price, appends the asset, stamps `Stats.Assets` and `AssetCash` and queues `Today.AssetsBought` for the laundering sim's report; the asset **stands from tonight** (the sims read it at `EndDay`).
**`NetWorth` counts an asset at cost** while it stands, nothing once lost.
`LoseAsset(id, day, why)` moves it to the lost record (`Stats.AssetsLost`); the **laundering sim is its one caller**, since the assets are clean money and its to write (`TestSimsWriteOnlyTheirOwnState` lists `Assets` and `AssetsLost` on the laundering row): the heat sim names what the task force takes (`AssetSeized`) and the logistics sim what the police found (`TunnelFound`), and the laundering sim, stepping after both, books it the same tick.

**The laundering sim's books** (`laundering.Sim.assetsStep`, after the wash and the levels' income, before the reserve): the seizures and the tunnel come off the books; a purchase made yesterday is reported (`AssetBought`, a `laundering` headline off `Tick.Sub("assets:news")`, the report's MONEY line `Bought X -$Y clean. It stands from today, $Z/day clean to keep.`); every asset's upkeep is billed in clean cash (`Stats.AssetUpkeep`, `Sim.AssetUpkeep` the ledger's line), and one the pile cannot cover stands idle for `upkeep_freeze_days` (`FrozenUntil`, `AssetFrozen` report-only, the MONEY line `X stands idle for N days: $Z upkeep unpaid. Wash something.`), its effect off until it reopens, billed again the day it does.
The door opens on peak clean cash the way a front's does (`announce`, `Laundering.Offered["asset:"+id]`, `Unlocked{Gate: "asset"}` once, the report's UNLOCKED line `X is for sale on the ledger screen (7), clean cash: $Y.`, the `UnlockedAsset` headline under the `unlock` source, the fast-forward's stop `X is for sale`; `TestAssetDoorsOpenOnce`, `TestAssetsAreCleanMoney`).

**The effects**, each in the sim that owns the number: the **supplier** (`market.Sim.owned` / `Owned`): the wholesale connect in its city, while the asset stands, is stamped `Supplier.Owned` each morning (`stampBand`), sells at `own_ratio` of street flat, whatever the band, the contact, the respect and the ticket (`supplierRatio`), without limit (`Cap = game.OwnedCap`, `Left()` reads it), and a buy nudges nothing (`buy` and `Restock` skip the pressure term on an owned book); `TestOwnedBookAndTheCartelWar`.
The **port** (`logistics.Sim.port`): every boat route through its city (the file's one boat sails out of Bayport, the port city, so the issue's "into" reads either way) carries `capacity_mul` the capacity (`Sim.capacity`, after the tree) and clears customs for nothing (`cut` reads 1: the customs agent's envelope is redundant); `TestAssetsOnTheRoad`.
The **airstrip** and the **tunnel** open a route: `routes.toml` gained `asset` on a `[[route]]` (`RouteConfig.Asset`, validated against the assets file), and `logistics.Sim.Open(w, r)` is `r.Asset == "" || w.AssetLive(r.Asset)`: a route that is not open is not on the map, the ledger or the road (`RoutesOpen(w, city)` is what the UI lists, `run` skips it), so a run without the asset is the run before the route existed.
The plane (`airstrip`, mode `plane`, 1 day, 600 units, $25 a unit, risk 0.05) is the fastest and the priciest and its risk is **only the task force's**: `dayRisk` reads the file's number while the feds watch the skies (`HeatState.WatchUntil`, stamped by the heat sim when a task force comes, for its cooldown, fired or stopped by a favour: the favour cancels the bust, not the watch, #281; `logistics.Watched(w, day)`) and zero otherwise, so the routes screen's pane reads `watched  the feds, N nights to go` or `nobody: the sky is clear`.
The tunnel (mode `tunnel`, 4 days, 300 units, $0 a unit, risk 0.003) is cheap, slow and nearly never seen, and **found once**: a seizure on it is `TunnelFound` (the logistics sim's, a `heat` headline, the report's SHIPMENTS line `THE TUNNEL IS FOUND: N units taken in it, and it is shut for good.`), the laundering sim takes the asset off the books as `found` and `BuyAsset` refuses it for the rest of the run; `TestTunnelFound`.
The **lab** (`crew.Sim.Lab(w, city)`, the row while it stands in that city): a cook there is `lab_mul` the best chemist's batch (`BatchIn`), lands at `lab_quality` where that is over the chemist's (`QualityIn`), and the precursors cost `lab_cost_mul` of the product's `cook_cost` (`CookCostIn`); the cook dialog and the harness read those three, and a cook anywhere else, or with the lab idle, reads the chemist alone (`TestTheLabMultipliesTheCook`).
The **law sim** adds each standing asset's `pressure` in its city every day (its own copy of the file, beside the campaign's line).

**The task force** is the heat sim's fifth rung (`content.TaskForce`, between `Raid` and `Arrest` in `Levels`, so `Rank(Arrest)` is now 5; `heat.toml [[response]] taskforce`: threshold 88, `stock_loss` 0.5, `cash_loss` 0.3, `heat_drop` 30, `evidence` 2, and its own `cooldown_days` 30, a field every rung may carry: `CooldownDays` returns it flat, no chief shortens the feds, `TestLadderUnderTheLaw`).
**It forms only against an asset owned or dirty cash over `[heat] taskforce_cash`** ($25M; `Sim.TaskForceEligible`): the ladder loop skips the rung otherwise, so a player with neither plays the four rungs they always did and nothing about their run moves (`TestTaskForceNeverMeetsTierThree`: every money-curve row's policy to its checkpoint and the aggressive and quiet traders to the horizon, no `TaskForceFormed`, none fired; `TestNoAssetIsTheOldRun`: the boss to day 200 on the file and with the assets boxed, `harness.NoAssets`, the same net worth, piles, heat, file and stats every day; `TestSeedDigest` re-pinned for the shape, no number moved).
Its line drops with the city's pressure as the police's do, and again under an extradition treaty (`HeatState.LineUntil` / `LineMul`, an incident's window, read for tonight's tick).
**Announced a day ahead**: the morning the hottest city crosses the line with the rung off cooldown, `TaskForceFormed{City, Assets}` goes out (a `heat` headline; the report's HEAT line `A TASK FORCE has formed. It comes tomorrow night and it will take an asset with it. Lie low: what they find on a quiet night is not a case.`), `HeatState.TaskForceDay` is stamped (`Sim.TaskForceForming(w)` the morning after, the dashboard's alert `A task force formed this morning. It comes tonight: lie low.`, the ledger's warning line, a fast-forward stop) and the announcement is the day's one response.
**It comes the next night whatever the heat**: the day's one response, `fire` at the file's numbers (no node softens the feds), a page only on a day you dealt (#27 holds: `Evidence` 0 on a quiet night), the sweep for the crew sim's arrests and the chemist's (`life.go` reads `Rank(level) >= Rank(Raid)`), the crew sim's danger (`taskforce` beside the sting and the raid), `Stats.TaskForces`, `WatchUntil = day + cooldown`, and **one asset seized** (`Sim.seize`: the one in the city it came to if any, else the costliest owned; `AssetSeized{City, Asset, Name, Cost}`, a `heat` headline, the HEAT line `they took X: $Y of yours, gone.`; nothing to take on the pile alone).
`TestTaskForceFormsAgainstAnAssetOrThePile`, `TestTaskForceNamesOneAsset`, `TestTaskForceLineAndTheFloor` (`sim/heat/taskforce_test.go`).
The **heat floor**: `Sim.Floor` is the higher of the fear floor and the highest `heat_floor` among the assets standing (`AssetFloor`), and decay works on what is above it, so an owned asset is federal attention a city never cools out of.
The UI reads the ladder through `heat.Sim.Ladder(w, city)`, `ThresholdsIn` without the task force's rung while it cannot form, so the dashboard's gauge marks the line only once an asset is owned or the pile is over the line, and its threshold text lays five rungs out three and two where they fit and the four it always had where they do not (`thresholdLines`); the level's word in the report and the stop line is `TASK FORCE` / `the task force in Eastside` (`enforcementLine`, `stopEvent`).

**The numbers and the ruling** (ten seeds, incidents boxed, median net worth on day 300, main at 8b22ab1 with #43's table of factions, plus this): boss $187.0M, **cartel $181.3M** (-3.0%; -8.8% to 0% a seed, seed 6 the table wiping both before an asset is bought), corrupt $91.7M, distributor $97.7M; `TestMoneyCurve`'s twenty-seed tier-5 median is $179.0M; on the duel before #43 the same read boss $218.5M, cartel $201.6M (-7.7%; -3.5% to -11.8% a seed), corrupt $94.3M, distributor $97.4M.
The band the issue asks for is $500M–$5B.
The tier-5 row in `TestMoneyCurve` is enforced at $100M–$1B (#205: the band the operation earns; the issue's $500M–$5B was three times what any policy reads, and #223 is the demand-side lever that would move it), and what is pinned beside it is an ordering: `TestCartelBeatsCorruptAndDistributor` (the issue's acceptance) and `TestCartelIsWithinFifteenPercentOfBoss` (the stocked/routine pattern, on the median and on every seed; `cartel > boss` holds on no seed but the one the table wipes and is not pinned).
Why: the tier-4 operation is demand- and wash-bound, not supply-bound (revenue ~$0.94M a day off two cities' corners, the wash $0.5M and the levels $0.6M a day, net worth growing ~$1.2M a day, the dirty pile at the cover line), so cheaper lots, a bigger boat, a plane and a tunnel move nothing while demand is the bind and the lab's quality is worth about +$8M per 100 days; against the boss the assets lose because a level pays 1% a day by #192's design and clean cash in an asset forgoes it.
The floors were 8–30 in the first cut and cost the managed cartel a sale day in three (decay works on what is above the floor, and the boss lies low at 40), so they are 3–8; the costs came down from $5M–$25M to $1.5M–$4M for the same reason, the lines staying where the clock puts them.
Per-asset income was ruled a design fork and not added.
