# Supply contracts are the buy side of the routine

**Supply contracts are the buy side of the routine** (#113, `World.Supply`, `game.SupplyContract`, `market.Sim.Plan`, `ui/dialogs.go`, `harness.Stocked`).
A contract is *keep the stash in this city at N units of this product*, `World.Supply map[string]SupplyContract` keyed like `Orders` (`SupplyKey`; `SupplyContract{City, Product, Units, Since}`), set by `SetSupply(city, product, units)` (refused with `ErrNotSupplied` where the supplier does not sell the product; allowed where the player holds no capacity, filling as far as `Free(city)`) and dropped by `ClearSupply`; persistent like a route target, nothing per-day, zero value the pre-contract world, no schema bump.
**The market sim fills them at the top of its step**, before the orders resolve and with no dice: `Sim.Plan(w)` lays out one `SupplyPlan` per contract standing (`World.StandingSupply`: yours, else the lieutenant's for the product in a city they run, #174, `docs/lieutenants.md`; `SupplyPlan.Lieutenant` names them, and theirs are skipped on a lie-low day) in `CityOrder` then ladder order, each the shortfall (`Sim.Shortfall`: the level less the stash less what is `Bound` there) cut to the stash's room and the cash over the contracts' float after the contracts before it have had theirs (**short, by margin**, #470: where that leaves a contract short of `cash` or `room`, the morning is laid out again in `World.ByMargin` order, by what a dollar brings back, `World.Margin(city, product, true)`, the street price over the supplier price, where the cash ran short, by what a unit earns, the street price less it, where only the room did, the city and ladder order breaking a tie; the markup scales every product's supplier price alike, so the order is the hand's and the restock's too; a morning with enough of both is the plan it always was, so a run whose contracts never run short is byte-for-byte the run before), and `supply` buys the plan through `World.FillSupply`, **the same path as `World.Buy`** (`world.buy`: the price pressure and `BoughtToday` move exactly as for a buy by hand, `TestFillSupplyIsBuysPath`, `TestSupplyMatchesTheHand`) at the **contract markup** (`market.toml [supply] markup`, 1.05: the supplier's price for a standing order, the penalty the routine costs so buying by hand keeps a point; `Sim.Markup`, `Sim.SupplyPrice`), emitting `SupplyBought` and, for a contract that could not reach its level, `SupplyShort{Why: "cash" | "room" | "supplier"}` (both report-only, both carrying `Lieutenant` where the contract is theirs; the report's SALES section carries one line per contract of yours that bought and one per contract short, `nobody there sells it today` for `supplier`, and for `cash` `Supply contract out of cash: 40 Coke short in Bayport.` with, where the wash took dirty cash the night before (the last of `World.Flows`), `  the wash took $12,000 last night and left the till $50,000` under it, each whole at 80 columns (#459: the old line, `…, there was no cash over the float for the rest`, was cut off, and the contracts' float is 0: the laundering float is what the wash leaves for them, `TestStarvedRoutineSaysWhy`), MONEY the receipt; a lieutenant's is the CREW line's).
**Held by the road, said** (#503, a playtest's Eastside contract counted a route's heroin on the road and stopped buying: two weeks of stockouts with nothing on the dashboard): the shortfall still counts what is `Bound` for the city (a contract that ignored the road would buy what the road then lands on top of, and every route-and-contract run would move), so the market sim says it instead: after the buys, a contract of yours held under its level by stock on the road (`World.Road(city, product)`, the units on the road it counts, at most what the level lacks) emits `SupplyShort{Why: events.SupplyRoad, Short}` (report-only; SALES reads `Supply contract holding: 60 Heroin on the road to Eastside.`, whole at 80 columns (`TestStarvedRoutineSaysWhy`); a fast-forward never stops on it, the shortfall reading covered), and the pane's contract rows read `holding: 60 on the road` (`TestContractHeldByTheRoadSaysSo`, `TestLandedAndHoldingAreSaid`).
**The wash keeps the contracts' morning** (#496, `docs/laundering.md`): the laundering sim's line is the till or `World.SupplyOutlay()`, what the contracts standing are expected to spend in the morning, where that is more, so a big front no longer washes the dirty cash a contract over the till needs.
**The contracts' float is their own**: `[supply] float` (0 in the file) folded through `World.Float` like the wash's (`Sim.Float`, `Sim.Budget`); the issue asked for the laundering float, but a run starts with $500 against a $50,000 float, and a contract *is* the street's restock, the thing the wash and the road hold the float back for.
The contract keeps nothing back for tomorrow's wages either, as the hand does not: the market sim cannot price the payroll without the crew's tuning.
**By margin, not by the ladder** (#470): a playtest's contracts filled weed first and left the meth, the best earner, unbought whenever the room or the cash ran short. The first attempt ranked by the margin a unit whatever ran short, and a stocked run went broke on day 21 (seed 12): with $600 in the till the coke's margin a unit bought five units where the weed bought thirty and sold them, so short of cash the rank is a dollar's return. Measured with `cmd/balance -policy stocked -runs 30 -days 200`, net worth medians before/after: day 30 $220k/$236k, day 70 $492k/$508k, day 120 $684k/$698k, day 200 $955k/$975k, no run ended; `routine` and `crewed` set no contract and did not move, and `delegated` (the lieutenant's contracts) moved by a few dollars ($1,277,899 to $1,277,990 of cut over 30 runs).
**Lost corners and no scale-down** (#470): a contract is a level, not a daily order, so it buys back only what left the stash (sold, driven off by a route, seized); a corner lost cuts what sells and so what it buys the next morning, and the cost of a level set for more corners is the stash held over, the room and the cash in it, not a daily overbuy (the playtest's "contract short … the stash" every day was the room: levels together over the stash). The contract does not pause itself (a pause would move every contract policy's numbers); the cart flags it instead: its modal carries every contract of yours as a `keep` line and, where a level is over what the product is expected to sell in its city tonight (`keepExpected`: the market's `Capacity` at the dial of the order standing there, normal with none, zero with no corner worked), the Warning line `Contracts exceed tonight's expected sales: Heroin keeps 200 against ~40 in Bayport. A contract buys back only what leaves the stash; the rest sits there. Lower a level on its keep line.` (`keepOver`). What the routes out of the city are expected to ship of it tonight counts as leaving (#503, `keepShipped`: each open route on its dial, its shortfall at the far end up to its capacity; the warning adds `(40 by the road)` and the keep line `, ~40 ship`): a playtest's `Coke keeps 40 against ~5 in Bayport` was a contract feeding a route.
**Lying low still runs them**: a lie-low day skips the lieutenant's contracts, never yours, and the wages run too; the `l lie low` help reads `lieutenants stop too; wages, contracts run` (#497; it read `no sales, heat fades; …`, and the 44-character cap left room for one or the other) and the status `Lying low today: no sales, heat fades faster; wages and contracts still run.` (`TestLieLowSaysWhatStillRuns`).
**The receipts**: a contract's buy is a `Purchase{Contract: true, Day}` in `w.Today.Buys`, and the clock keeps the morning's contract receipts through the day (`clock.supplied`: with `Prior` cleared, since the market reset the supplier price after the buy) and drops them the next, so `w.Today.Buys` is nil as before in a run with no contract; `World.Bought` / `Return` are the hand's, `SuppliedIn` / `ReturnSupplied` the contract's (the last receipt first, at the price paid, the supplier price left alone), `SuppliedToday` the morning's units and cost; the news sim skips yesterday's contract receipts in its money loop and counts this morning's, so `CashBefore` holds.
**A sell order may count on the morning's contract** (`PlaceSell` allows the stash plus `World.SupplyDue`, the standing contract's shortfall, the lieutenant's too; the UI's and the harness's expected amount is `Sim.Due`, the plan's), because the contract fills before the orders resolve; without it a stocked player's orders run a day behind the stash and it sells half of what the hand sells (measured: $431k against `crewed`'s $580k at day 70; #114's standing orders want the same rule).
**UI**: the buy dialog's last step, after the quantity, is `repeat  [once]  keep at` (`dialog.repeat`, `repeatOnce` / `repeatKeep`, drawn by `dialCells` and turned by `stepRepeat`, the helper the sell dialog's standing orders mirror; `←→` and `1-2`, `enter buy` / `enter keep at` in the footer by `buyOnce` / `buyKeep`; a product with a contract opens at once since #500 (it opened on its level at keep at, and a buy for a buyer replaced the contract), its quantity step saying `Kept at 200 by contract; this buys once and leaves it.`; turned to keep at, the quantity step says `Editing the contract (keep 200); pick once to buy just once.` and the last step `Kept at 200 since day 16; this replaces it.`, both in `theme.Warning` (`editingRows`, #443); a refusal on the last step returns to the quantity step, `quantityAgain`), the market's product table has a `keep` column beside `order` (the level, `-` with none), the pane's product section has `contract  keep at 120` / `bought 40 today for $1,900` / `brings N in the morning at $P` and `x  clear the contract` (`contractRows`, the dashboard's too), `x` on a product with a contract and no order clears it (with an order it cancels the order first, as before), the dashboard's STREET facts carry `supply 2 contracts · $3,400 this morning` (`supplyLine`, `priSupply`), the cart shows the morning's buys as lines marked `contract` (`cartLine.contract`, kept apart from the hand's, `x` and a smaller quantity return them through `ReturnSupplied`, never added to: a larger one is refused with `A contract's line only goes back: raise its level on the buy dialog.`; the line is the morning's buy, not the contract, so under it the cart says `The contract keeps 200; it brought 120 this morning. Its level is set on the buy dialog, at keep at.` (`contractLevelNote`, #444, `TestCartContractLineNamesTheLevel`: a playtest read the 120 as a raise to 200 that had not taken; since #470 it reads `Its level is its keep line below.` and the refusal `raise its level on its keep line`)), **every contract of yours is a `keep` line in the cart modal** (#470, `cartModalLines`, `cartLine.keep`: in city and ladder order after the day's lines, in any city, so a Bayport contract is edited from Eastside; its quantity is the level, `keep at` on the quantity step with `~N sell tonight` beside it, `enter` sets it through `SetSupply`, `x` clears it through `ClearSupply`; the lines are the modal's alone, `totals`, `cartSummary`, the pane's CART and the END THE DAY? sentence count the day's buys and orders, not the levels; `TestCartEditsContractsInEveryCity`; the buy dialog is still bound to where a buy can be, `buyPointer`), `totals` counts them in `buys` / `spent` and again as `contracts` / `supplied`, and the END THE DAY summary reads `Buying 3 lines for $12K (2 by contract)`; the sell dialog's quantity max is `sellable` (the stash plus `Sim.Due`) and says `N stashed and M the contract buys before tonight's sales.`
**When it buys, said** (#467): the contract fills in the market sim's step at the end of the day, before the night's orders resolve, which the report calls the morning's buy; a playtest read the buy dialog's `the same each morning` as tomorrow's, so the dialog says when: the once line `Bought now, once. Keep at tops it up every night, before the sales.`, the contract row `keep 200 here, topped up nightly before the sales at $21 (×1.05)`, the status `Keeping 200 Weed in Eastside: topped up at the end of each day, before the night's sales, at ×1.05 the supplier's price.` (`TestKeepAtSaysWhenItBuys`). A contract set today counts for tonight's orders as an old one does, standing orders included (`TestSameDayContractSells`); where `Sim.Due` is 0 with the level unmet, the sell dialog's product step says why rather than `You have none of that here.`: `…, and the contract brings none tonight: the stash there is full` / `nobody there has any left today` / `it buys for cash, and $0 dirty does not cover one` (`contractBringsNone`, the plan's reasons read off the world, since `Plan` is not on `engine.MarketRules`). The front ends' check stays `Sim.Due` against the game's `SupplyDue`: the game allows an order the plan cannot fill (it sells what is there), the dialog refuses it, new and edited orders alike.
**Harness**: `harness.Stocked` (`cmd/balance -policy stocked`) is `crewed` that never buys by hand: each morning it sets a contract for every product the supplier where it stands sells at `harness.StockDays` (1) of the demand the corners it works there serve, cut to the bag's share by demand where the levels together would overfill it (the way `restock` shares the bag; `World.StockLevels` since #356, the market screen's restock sharing the sizing, `docs/cart.md`), and orders the stash plus what the plan brings; `TestStockedIsWithinFifteenPercentOfCrewed` pins it at day 70 (median $555k against $590k, 6.0% under since #470's fill by margin, 7.6% before it ($545k): the markup and the routine's bluntness; the line was 10% and the gap 9.3% until #139, whose rival, its first corner paying two heads rather than the four its old chest bought, pushes less in the first month, and the crewed player's median seed kept a corner more of it than the routine), `TestSupplyIsDeterministicAndSaves` the replay and the save, `TestSupplyKeepsTheLevel` the level every morning with cash and room, what the float leaves short of cash and `Free(city)` short of room, `TestSupplyPlan` the plan's order (the ladder with cash and room, the margin a unit short of room, a dollar's return short of cash), and `TestSupplyContractInTheGrammar` the UI.
A run with no contract is byte-for-byte the pre-contract run: `Plan` returns nil and the clock's scratch stays nil, so no pinned number moved.
Setting a contract in days of demand, as a route target can be (#356 part 4), is not built: the restock (`docs/cart.md`) fills the stash to days of demand by hand, and a contract kept in days is left for its own issue.
**Presets** (#357, `docs/presets.md`): *preserve cash* and *go dark* clear every contract of yours through `ClearSupply` (there is no pause: a saved preset keeps the levels to set again), and a saved preset sets its contracts back through `SetSupply`, before its standing orders so they may count on them; the review shows each contract's morning buy before and after as `Plan` lays it out.
