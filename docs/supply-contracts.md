# Supply contracts are the buy side of the routine

**Supply contracts are the buy side of the routine** (#113, `World.Supply`, `game.SupplyContract`, `market.Sim.Plan`, `ui/dialogs.go`, `harness.Stocked`). A contract is *keep the stash in this city at N units of this product*, `World.Supply map[string]SupplyContract` keyed like `Orders` (`SupplyKey`; `SupplyContract{City, Product, Units, Since}`), set by `SetSupply(city, product, units)` (refused with `ErrNotSupplied` where the supplier does not sell the product; allowed where the player holds no capacity, filling as far as `Free(city)`) and dropped by `ClearSupply`; persistent like a route target, nothing per-day, zero value the pre-contract world, no schema bump. **The market sim fills them at the top of its step**, before the orders resolve and with no dice: `Sim.Plan(w)` lays out one `SupplyPlan` per contract standing (`World.StandingSupply`: yours, else the lieutenant's for the product in a city they run, #174, `docs/lieutenants.md`; `SupplyPlan.Lieutenant` names them, and theirs are skipped on a lie-low day) in `CityOrder` then ladder order, each the shortfall (`Sim.Shortfall`: the level less the stash less what is `Bound` there) cut to the stash's room and the cash over the contracts' float after the contracts before it have had theirs, and `supply` buys the plan through `World.FillSupply`, **the same path as `World.Buy`** (`world.buy`: the price pressure and `BoughtToday` move exactly as for a buy by hand, `TestFillSupplyIsBuysPath`, `TestSupplyMatchesTheHand`) at the **contract markup** (`market.toml [supply] markup`, 1.05: the supplier's price for a standing order, the penalty the routine costs so buying by hand keeps a point; `Sim.Markup`, `Sim.SupplyPrice`), emitting `SupplyBought` and, for a contract that could not reach its level, `SupplyShort{Why: "cash" | "room" | "supplier"}` (both report-only, both carrying `Lieutenant` where the contract is theirs; the report's SALES section carries one line per contract of yours that bought and one per contract short, `nobody there sells it today` for `supplier`, MONEY the receipt; a lieutenant's is the CREW line's). **The contracts' float is their own**: `[supply] float` (0 in the file) folded through `World.Float` like the wash's (`Sim.Float`, `Sim.Budget`); the issue asked for the laundering float, but a run starts with $500 against a $50,000 float, and a contract *is* the street's restock, the thing the wash and the road hold the float back for. The contract keeps nothing back for tomorrow's wages either, as the hand does not: the market sim cannot price the payroll without the crew's tuning. **The receipts**: a contract's buy is a `Purchase{Contract: true, Day}` in `w.Today.Buys`, and the clock keeps the morning's contract receipts through the day (`clock.supplied`: with `Prior` cleared, since the market reset the supplier price after the buy) and drops them the next, so `w.Today.Buys` is nil as before in a run with no contract; `World.Bought` / `Return` are the hand's, `SuppliedIn` / `ReturnSupplied` the contract's (the last receipt first, at the price paid, the supplier price left alone), `SuppliedToday` the morning's units and cost; the news sim skips yesterday's contract receipts in its money loop and counts this morning's, so `CashBefore` holds. **A sell order may count on the morning's contract** (`PlaceSell` allows the stash plus `World.SupplyDue`, the standing contract's shortfall, the lieutenant's too; the UI's and the harness's expected amount is `Sim.Due`, the plan's), because the contract fills before the orders resolve; without it a stocked player's orders run a day behind the stash and it sells half of what the hand sells (measured: $431k against `crewed`'s $580k at day 70; #114's standing orders want the same rule). **UI**: the buy dialog's last step, after the quantity, is `repeat  [once]  keep at` (`dialog.repeat`, `repeatOnce` / `repeatKeep`, drawn by `dialCells` and turned by `stepRepeat`, the helper the sell dialog's standing orders mirror; `←→` and `1-2`, `enter buy` / `enter keep at` in the footer by `buyOnce` / `buyKeep`; a product with a contract opens on its level at keep at; a refusal on the last step returns to the quantity step, `quantityAgain`), the market's product table has a `keep` column beside `order` (the level, `-` with none), the pane's product section has `contract  keep at 120` / `bought 40 today for $1,900` / `brings N in the morning at $P` and `x  clear the contract` (`contractRows`, the dashboard's too), `x` on a product with a contract and no order clears it (with an order it cancels the order first, as before), the dashboard's STREET facts carry `supply 2 contracts · $3,400 this morning` (`supplyLine`, `priSupply`), the cart shows the morning's buys as lines marked `contract` (`cartLine.contract`, kept apart from the hand's, `x` and a smaller quantity return them through `ReturnSupplied`, never added to), `totals` counts them in `buys` / `spent` and again as `contracts` / `supplied`, and the END THE DAY summary reads `Buying 3 lines for $12K (2 by contract)`; the sell dialog's quantity max is `sellable` (the stash plus `Sim.Due`) and says `N stashed and M the contract brings in the morning`. **Harness**: `harness.Stocked` (`cmd/balance -policy stocked`) is `crewed` that never buys by hand: each morning it sets a contract for every product the supplier where it stands sells at `harness.StockDays` (1) of the demand the corners it works there serve, cut to the bag's share by demand where the levels together would overfill it (the way `restock` shares the bag), and orders the stash plus what the plan brings; `TestStockedIsWithinFifteenPercentOfCrewed` pins it at day 70 (median $542k against $605k, 10.5% under: the markup and the routine's bluntness; the line was 10% and the gap 9.3% until #139, whose rival, its first corner paying two heads rather than the four its old chest bought, pushes less in the first month, and the crewed player's median seed kept a corner more of it than the routine), `TestSupplyIsDeterministicAndSaves` the replay and the save, `TestSupplyKeepsTheLevel` the level every morning with cash and room, what the float leaves short of cash and `Free(city)` short of room, `TestSupplyPlan` the plan's order, and `TestSupplyContractInTheGrammar` the UI. A run with no contract is byte-for-byte the pre-contract run: `Plan` returns nil and the clock's scratch stays nil, so no pinned number moved.
