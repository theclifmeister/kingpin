# Standing orders are the sell side of the routine

**Standing orders are the sell side of the routine** (#114, `World.Standing`, `market.Sim.standing`, `ui/dialogs.go`, `harness.Routine`).
A standing order is *sell N units of this product in this city at this dial every night until I cancel it*, `World.Standing map[string]SellOrder` keyed like `Orders`, set by `PlaceStanding(city, product, qty, dial)` with `PlaceSell`'s checks (a city, a product, a quantity above zero and no more than the stash plus `SupplyDue`: a standing order for a contract's level sells what the contract brings every night) and dropped by `CancelStanding` (`YourStanding` reads it); persistent like a supply contract, never cleared by the clock or by lying low, zero value the pre-standing world, no schema bump.
**Sized for the road, kept at the most** (#503): `PlaceStanding` allows the stash, the contract's `SupplyDue` and `World.Landing(city, product)`, what lands there tonight after the sales (the shipments due by tomorrow's day on a route not shut, and the chemist's batches ready by then), which the order sells from the night after (a playtest's cartel and vanish runs could not size an order for goods arriving by road, and raising one meant raising `keep` in the cart first); `PlaceSell` does not (tonight's order sells before the landing). A quantity of `game.AllUnits` (-1, the wire's too) is **the whole stash every night**: stored as `SellOrder.All` with `Qty` the stash it was set on (zero value a number, no schema bump), the market sim's `standing` sells whatever the stash holds and emits `StandingShort{All: true}` only when it holds nothing (`Standing order for all the Weed: nothing stashed, nothing sold.`); a playtest's "blank = max" froze at 16, the report said `only 15 stashed` every night and `F` stopped for it. The sell dialog's blank at standing places it (`all of the stash at normal nightly`), a number up to `standable` (sellable and the landing) is allowed on the quantity step with `50 land tonight after the sales: a standing order may be sized for them (up to 60).`, and once over what sells tonight is refused on the last step (`pick standing to count them from tomorrow night`); the order column reads `all norm. ↻`, the pane `standing  all norm.`, and a preset re-places an all order at `AllUnits` (`TestStandingSizedForWhatLands`, `TestStandingForAllSellsTheStash`, `TestStandingSizedForTheRoad`). No policy sets either, so no number moved.
**Route-landed stock with no order** (#503, a playtest's 917 designer shipped to Eastside sat unsold with nothing flagging it): the engine's `landed` alert (`engine.AlertLanded`: `City`, `Product`, `Count` the units stashed; keyed by the city and the product, so `F` stops once; the act the market turned to the city with the product under the cursor) fires for a product a route on its dial keeps in a city (a units or days target) while the stash there holds some and no order stands for it, yours today, your standing one or the lieutenant's: `120 Weed landed in Eastside by the road, and no order sells it: s sells it, standing if it should go nightly on the market screen (2).` (`TestLandedAlert`, `TestLandedAndHoldingAreSaid`; the web client words it).
**`World.StandingOrder(city, product)` is yours first, then the lieutenant's** (`DelegatedOrder`): a standing order you set wins in a delegated city, as your fresh order does.
**The market sim resolves it where you placed no order that day** (`Sim.standing`), through the same path as a fresh order (`resolveAt`: the fill, the dial, `Heat.SellCap`, `HeldShare`, the price impact) for **at most what the stash holds** (the hand could place no more, and `Wanted` is what was attempted, so the heat is the hand's; with nothing stashed nothing is attempted, as no order could be placed), with **`[standing] cut`** (`market.toml`, 0.05; `Sim.Cut`) of the take to the crew, off dirty cash at once and into `Stats.Cuts` as the lieutenant's cut is; `PlayerSold` carries `Standing` (it stood rather than being placed today, yours or the lieutenant's), `Delegated` (the lieutenant's) and `Cut`, the heat sim's reason line reads `standing order moved N`, the report's SALES line `(normal, standing, cut $63)` and MONEY `The crew's cut on the standing orders -$X`.
No dice: a run where a standing order is placed once and left sells exactly what the same order placed by hand every day sells, less the cut (`TestStandingSellsLikeTheHand`: same units and heat night for night on one seed over 30 days, the cash short by exactly the cuts), and a run with none is byte-for-byte the old run.
A standing order that finds **less in the stash than it is for** emits `StandingShort{Units, Stock}` (report-only, in SALES: `Standing order for 120 Weed: only 30 stashed.` / `nothing stashed, nothing sold. Restock, or cancel it.`), which is #116's stop condition.
A standing order **too small for the stash** says so (#418): one of yours (not the lieutenant's) that sold every unit it was for with at least as many again left in the stash adds a SALES line under its own, `  the standing order sold all 5 with 20 more in the stash: raise it on the sell dialog or the cart` (`TestSmallStandingOrderIsFlagged`); a playtest's 5 Heroin a night stood seventy days beside a stash of 25 and a contract keeping 30. A standing order reaches the contract's level on a thin day (`PlaceStanding` allows the stash and the morning's `SupplyDue`, `TestStandingReachesTheContractLevel`), and re-issuing it through `s` replaces its quantity: the dialog reopens on the order's units and the first digit typed replaces them (#426, `TestStandingOrderReissued`).
**The cut is off the take, not the profit**: at the crewed player's ~40% margin a cut of the take weighs about two and a half times what it reads, and from day 0 the early game has no free cash flow for one to come out of (any cut stalls the $500 bag's exponential phase on some seeds), so 0.10 measured 77–82% of `crewed` at day 70 and 0.05 is the number that lands the routine at 88% (measured, ratio of medians over 20 seeds, Routine standing from $20k: 0.10 → 77%, 0.05 → 88%, 0.04 → 91%, 0.03 → 94%; standing from day 0: 0.10 → 78%, 0.05 → 83%, and 38%–108% seed by seed).
**UI**: the sell dialog has a fourth step after the dial, `repeat  [once]  standing` (`sellRepeatNames`, the buy's `repeatKeep` notch under the name `repeatStanding`, turned by `stepRepeat`, `←→` and `1-2`; `enter sell` / `enter sell nightly` in the footer by `sellOnce` / `sellStanding`; `shift+tab` back to the dial keeps the dial and the quantity; a product with a standing order opens on its units at standing (a kept product opened at keep at until #500; a buy now opens at once), and its quantity step says so in `theme.Warning`, `Editing the standing order (120 normal); pick once to sell tonight only.` (`editingRows`, #443: a playtest typed a one-off 150 there and replaced the order), with the repeat step's `Standing at 120 normal now; this replaces it.` in Warning too (`TestSellOverAStandingOrderSaysItEdits`); a refusal returns to the quantity step, and since #467 a quantity over `sellable` is refused on the quantity step itself, new or edited alike, the field set to what there is (`checkQuantity`, `TestSellQuantityRefusedWhereTyped`: a playtest's edit to 20 over 3 stashed walked the dial and the repeat to be refused), the `order` column of the dashboard and the market reads `120 aggr.` for the day's order, `120 aggr. ↻` for your standing one (`order.standing`, Gold) and `120 aggr. (lt)` for the lieutenant's (`CrewText`), the pane's product section has `standing  120 aggr. · cut 5%` and `x  cancel the standing order` (`standingRows`, the dial short so the row fits the pane; the dashboard's `order` row goes when the standing one stands in for it), `x` cancels the day's order first, then the standing one, then clears the contract (`cancelSelected`), the STREET's last line reads `Standing orders sell tonight; the crew keep 5%.` where only standing orders stand, the cart lists a standing order as a line marked `standing` where you placed no order of the day over it and never on a lie-low day (`cartLine.standing`, `totals.standing`; its quantity and dial edit through `PlaceStanding`, `placeLine`, and `x` is `CancelStanding`; the expected take is after the cut and for at most `sellable`), `cartSummary` and the END THE DAY? modal read `selling 2 lines (1 standing)`, help's WORDS gains `standing`, and the modal's scroll mark is never cut: a footer too long for `↓ more` (a number field's) gives way to it.
**Harness**: `harness.Routine` (`cmd/balance -policy routine`) is `crewed` that sells by hand until its peak cash clears `harness.RoutineCash` ($20k: nobody automates on day one with a $500 bag, and the policy models the player the feature is for) and from then on keeps a standing order per product for the stash at normal (`stand`: set where none stands or the stash has outgrown the one that does, never lowered); `TestRoutineIsWithinFifteenPercentOfCrewed` pins it at day 70 between 85% and 100% (median $513k against $580k, 88%), `TestStandingYieldsToTheHand` the fresh order winning its night and the standing one back the next, `TestStandingNeverOutsellsTheStash` on every day of every policy that no order sells more than the morning's stash plus the contract's buy, a standing one tries no more than the stash, its cut is the file's share and a fresh or delegated order's is nothing, nothing sells lying low and the clock never touches a standing order, `TestStandingIsDeterministicAndSaves` the replay and the save, `TestPlaceStanding` / `TestStandingOrderIsYoursFirst` / `TestClockKeepsTheStandingOrders` the actions, and `TestStandingOrderInTheGrammar` the UI.
**Presets** (#357, `docs/presets.md`): a preset turns every standing order of yours at once, re-placing each at its quantity and the preset's dial through `PlaceStanding` (quiet trading, push) or ending them all (go dark), after a review that says which orders move and what each is expected to take and heat by `orderEstimate`; an order the stash no longer covers is refused as `PlaceStanding` refuses it and listed, and the lieutenant's are never touched.
