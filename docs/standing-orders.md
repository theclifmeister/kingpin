# Standing orders are the sell side of the routine

**Standing orders are the sell side of the routine** (#114, `World.Standing`, `market.Sim.standing`, `ui/dialogs.go`, `harness.Routine`). A standing order is *sell N units of this product in this city at this dial every night until I cancel it*, `World.Standing map[string]SellOrder` keyed like `Orders`, set by `PlaceStanding(city, product, qty, dial)` with `PlaceSell`'s checks (a city, a product, a quantity above zero and no more than the stash plus `SupplyDue`: a standing order for a contract's level sells what the contract brings every night) and dropped by `CancelStanding` (`YourStanding` reads it); persistent like a supply contract, never cleared by the clock or by lying low, zero value the pre-standing world, no schema bump. **`World.StandingOrder(city, product)` is yours first, then the lieutenant's** (`DelegatedOrder`): a standing order you set wins in a delegated city, as your fresh order does. **The market sim resolves it where you placed no order that day** (`Sim.standing`), through the same path as a fresh order (`resolveAt`: the fill, the dial, `Heat.SellCap`, `HeldShare`, the price impact) for **at most what the stash holds** (the hand could place no more, and `Wanted` is what was attempted, so the heat is the hand's; with nothing stashed nothing is attempted, as no order could be placed), with **`[standing] cut`** (`market.toml`, 0.05; `Sim.Cut`) of the take to the crew, off dirty cash at once and into `Stats.Cuts` as the lieutenant's cut is; `PlayerSold` carries `Standing` (it stood rather than being placed today, yours or the lieutenant's), `Delegated` (the lieutenant's) and `Cut`, the heat sim's reason line reads `standing order moved N`, the report's SALES line `(normal, standing, cut $63)` and MONEY `The crew's cut on the standing orders -$X`. No dice: a run where a standing order is placed once and left sells exactly what the same order placed by hand every day sells, less the cut (`TestStandingSellsLikeTheHand`: same units and heat night for night on one seed over 30 days, the cash short by exactly the cuts), and a run with none is byte-for-byte the old run. A standing order that finds **less in the stash than it is for** emits `StandingShort{Units, Stock}` (report-only, in SALES: `Standing order for 120 Weed: only 30 stashed.` / `nothing stashed, nothing sold. Restock, or cancel it.`), which is #116's stop condition. **The cut is off the take, not the profit**: at the crewed player's ~40% margin a cut of the take weighs about two and a half times what it reads, and from day 0 the early game has no free cash flow for one to come out of (any cut stalls the $500 bag's exponential phase on some seeds), so 0.10 measured 77–82% of `crewed` at day 70 and 0.05 is the number that lands the routine at 88% (measured, ratio of medians over 20 seeds, Routine standing from $20k: 0.10 → 77%, 0.05 → 88%, 0.04 → 91%, 0.03 → 94%; standing from day 0: 0.10 → 78%, 0.05 → 83%, and 38%–108% seed by seed). **UI**: the sell dialog has a fourth step after the dial, `repeat  [once]  standing` (`sellRepeatNames`, the buy's `repeatKeep` notch under the name `repeatStanding`, turned by `stepRepeat`, `←→` and `1-2`; `enter sell` / `enter sell nightly` in the footer by `sellOnce` / `sellStanding`; `shift+tab` back to the dial keeps the dial and the quantity; a product with a standing order opens on its units at standing, as a kept product opens at keep at; a refusal returns to the quantity step), the `order` column of the dashboard and the market reads `120 aggr.` for the day's order, `120 aggr. ↻` for your standing one (`order.standing`, Gold) and `120 aggr. (lt)` for the lieutenant's (`CrewText`), the pane's product section has `standing  120 aggr. · cut 5%` and `x  cancel the standing order` (`standingRows`, the dial short so the row fits the pane; the dashboard's `order` row goes when the standing one stands in for it), `x` cancels the day's order first, then the standing one, then clears the contract (`cancelSelected`), the STREET's last line reads `Standing orders sell tonight; the crew keep 5%.` where only standing orders stand, the cart lists a standing order as a line marked `standing` where you placed no order of the day over it and never on a lie-low day (`cartLine.standing`, `totals.standing`; its quantity and dial edit through `PlaceStanding`, `placeLine`, and `x` is `CancelStanding`; the expected take is after the cut and for at most `sellable`), `cartSummary` and the END THE DAY?
