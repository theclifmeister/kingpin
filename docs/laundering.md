# Fronts are the laundering

**Fronts are the laundering.** `World.Fronts` (bought with dirty cash through `BuyFront`, priced by `laundering.Sim.Offers` from `internal/content/laundering.toml`, gated by `unlock_cash` like products) each wash `min(throughput x dial.mul, dirty cash over the float)` from dirty to clean every day, pay `upkeep` in clean cash (unpaid, the front shuts for `upkeep_freeze_days`) and roll an audit at `audit_risk x dial.risk`; an audit freezes the front for `audit_freeze_days`, seizes `audit_seize` of what it washed that day and emits `FrontAudited`. `World.Laundering.Dial` (`careful | normal | greedy`, `events.Launder`) is a persistent setting like the pay dial. The wash never takes dirty cash below `float`, so fronts can never starve the street of restock money (that is what "broke" means). **The laundering sim folds the tree** at the top of its step (#118, `laundering.New` takes it): `wash_mul` on every front's throughput, `audit_risk_mul` on `audit_risk x dial.risk`, `audit_seize_mul` on what an audit takes, `upkeep_mul` on a front's upkeep (`Sim.FrontUpkeep`, the ledger's), `audit_freeze_cut` off `audit_freeze_days` (`Sim.AuditFreezeDays`, never under a day) and `float_mul` on the float; the folded float is **one number on the world**, `World.Float(tree, base)` = `base x FloatMul` (the lowest owned wins), which the wash (`laundering.Sim.Float`, `Washable`) and the road (`logistics.Sim.Budget`) read, each passing its own copy of the tree and the file's float, so the two cannot disagree about where the street's restock money starts and a world owning no float node reads the file exactly (#113's supply contracts fold their own `[supply] float` through the same function: a contract is the street's restock, so the laundering float is what is left *for* it, not a line it keeps); the public reads (`Throughput`, `AuditRisk`, `Capacity`, `Upkeep`, `AnyAuditRisk`) fold for themselves so the ledger prints what the dice use. `TestLaunderingNodesPullTheirWay` (`sim/laundering`) probes every number the branch can move with each node owned alone and checks it moves the ones it names and no other; `TestLaunderingInvariantsUnderTheBranch` (harness) runs the laundered player with the whole branch and pins the pools, the folded capacity, the frozen fronts and that the wash and the road keep to the same halved float. Laundering steps *after* heat, so heat reads yesterday's audit off the front's `Audited`/`AuditDial` record the next morning: `audit_heat` in `heat.toml` every time, plus `audit_evidence` only if the front was being run greedy (#27: a case is built from what you do). Accountants (`[role.accountant]` in `crew.toml`, only in the hiring pool once a front is owned) add `accountant_throughput` to every front and cut audit risk by `accountant_risk_cut`, both scaled by skill; a skimming accountant takes from the previous day's wash, in clean cash (`CrewSkimmed.FromWash`). `NetWorth` counts clean cash, stock at supplier price and fronts at cost.
