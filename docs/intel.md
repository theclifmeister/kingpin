# Intel: what you know against what is true

**Intel** (#45, `game/intel.go`, `World.Intel`, `content/intel.toml`, `ui/intel.go`, the intel screen on tab `9`) puts a layer between the player and the world. The dashboard used to read the truth: the rival's temper once observed, its muscle, the chief's temper, a route's risk. Since #45 those panels read a **file of facts** with a confidence, and the truth stays with the sims. **Intel is a layer the UI reads, not a new truth**: `World.Intel []Fact` is written by the sims that own each truth through their own rule (the rivals sim a faction's temper and muscle and the books, the law sim the chief, the heat sim the police's next move, the logistics sim a road's risk; the crew sim what a spy sends out; the rivals sim what a faction feeds you), read by the panels through `game.Known(w)` (a `Knowledge` with `Fact`, `Facts`, `Personality`, `Chief`, `Muscle` (a band), `MuscleWord`, `Books`, `Response`, `Risk`, `Move`, `Stash`, every one answering `game.Unknown` (`?`), zero or false where the file holds nothing alive), and never off `Rival.Personality`, `Rival.Muscle`, `Law.Chief.Personality` or `Route.Risk` (`TestPanelsReadTheFile` type-checks `internal/ui` from source with `go/types` and fails on a selection of those, of `Fact.Planted`, or a call of the logistics sim's `Risk`/`DayRisk` or the rivals sim's `Odds`/`OddsOn`/`PushOdds`/`Defence`; `demo.go`, which builds a fixture by hand, is the one file exempt). The harness policies keep reading the world: they tune the sims, they are not players; `informed` is the one exception, and it is the value of the file in numbers. There is **no intel sim**: nothing steps the file, and each writer is the sim whose truth it is, in its own step.

**A fact** is `Fact{Subject, Kind, Value, Number, Confidence, Day, Source, Stale, Forget, Planted}`, keyed by `(Subject, Kind)`, latest wins (`World.Learn`, which also drops every fact dead on the day it files; `Unlearn` drops one key). A subject is a faction id, a city id (the police there), a route id or `game.SubjectChief`; the kinds are `personality` (a faction's temper, or the chief's under `chief`), `muscle` (`Value` a count or a band `4–6`, `Number` its middle; `Fact.Band`), the books' `cash`, `income`, `wages` (#70), `move` (the corner a faction moves on next), `stash` (the corner its till is fattest on), `response` (the police's next rung in a city: `Value` the level, `Number` the first night it can fire) and `risk` (a route's risk a day in transit). **Facts age on read**: `Confidence` is what the fact was filed at, `Now(day)` is that less `Stale` for every day since, clamped to 0..1, and `Alive(day)` is `Now` at or over `Forget` (and over zero); `Stale` and `Forget` are stamped on the fact by its writer from `intel.toml`'s `stale_rate` (0.05) and `forget` (0.2), so nothing steps the file, a save reads the same on any build and a fact filed at 1 lives sixteen days, one at 0.6 eight; a temper (a faction's, the observed chief's) is filed with `Stale` 0 and never fades, and the law sim `Unlearn`s the chief's when the chief is replaced (`replaceChief`). **Sources** are `seen` (observation), `books` (the scout), `cop`, `spy` and `contact` ("a contact says"); a **planted** fact carries the faction that fed it in `Planted`, which the sims read to make it bite and the screen never does (`Fact.Lure`, `World.Lure`), and once it has bitten `World.Expose` makes its `Source` the faction's id, so the screen reads `fed by Sal`.

**Observation is free and slow, and rolls no dice**: every observation fact is written off events the tick already emitted, so **a run that pays no cop, plants no spy and takes no lure is byte-for-byte the run before the feature** (`TestNoIntelIsTheOldRun`: t1–t4 read main's $84,930 / $544,231 / $16,399,563 / $90,250,452 (dff4a0a, after #194) to the dollar with the feed on, the crewed player under `harness.NoIntel` (the feed boxed) is the same run day by day and stat for stat, the file aside; `TestSeedDigest` moved on day 1 by shape alone, and with the new fields set aside on both sides the digest is dff4a0a's on all sixty days; `TestNoUnlockIsTheOldRun`'s print folds the report-only `IntelGained` out the way it folds a headline's article). The rules: the **rivals sim** (`sim/rivals/intel.go`, `observe`, at the end of its step) files the muscle you met within a band the night your enforcers or a faction's met, off `CornerStruck`, `RivalBoosted`, `RivalPushed` and a `CornerTaken` from you (`observe_band` 3 heads wide, `game.MuscleBand`: 4–6; `observe_confidence` 0.6, `Source: seen`; a count from the scout or a spy stands over a band while it lives, so the saboteur's books read as #70 left them until they fade), and the temper of every faction with `Observed` set (#12's flag stays the rivals sim's own; the fact is filed once at 1, the lieutenant's walk's observation a step late); the **scout** (#70, `books.go`) files the four books facts through `World.LearnBooks` at 1 in place of the `Rival.Known` snapshot, which is gone (`game.Books{Day, Cash, Income, Muscle, Wages}` is the read as `Known(w).Books(faction)` folds it back, its day the oldest fact's; `RivalScouted` carries the numbers for the report; `rivals.Sim.Stale(w, r, day)` reads the file; `Rival.Scouted`, the empty-nights counter the odds read, stays the sim's state); the **law sim** files the chief's temper at 1 once `Chief.Observed` (`intelChief`, after the observation block; `TestChiefFact`); the **logistics sim** files a route's risk a day at 1 on a seizure (`learn`, off its own `ShipmentSeized`; `TestLureAndTheRiskFact`), the one thing that shows you what a road is, and never on a staged one. What the panels then show: the rivals screen's FACTIONS table and leader line read `muscle 4–6` or `muscle ?` (`muscleWord`), the dashboard's RIVALS line `muscle 4–6 (2d)` where it fits, the strike picker's `lands` column a band `~30–45%` over the muscle band (`rivals.Sim.OddsOnAt`, `DefenceAt`, `PushOddsAt`: `OddsOn` with the muscle given, #194's deed cut folded, so the dice's shape is read on what you know), `?` and a line that the odds read blind while the muscle is unknown, the map's inspector the push odds the same way and its `pusher` by the known muscle (the corner count where none), the LAW panel, the bribe dialog and the summary the chief's word or `new`, and every `seized ~N%` on the map, the route pane, the target dialog and the checkpoint dialog `seized ?` on a road you have never lost a shipment on and nobody has told you about, else the known base risk folded the dice's way (`logistics.Sim.RiskFrom(w, r, ship, base)`; `riskWord`). Trust and the corner count are not facts: the bar and the map show them already, and a kind that is on screen is not intel (the issue listed them; dropped with the reason); factions run no road, so "a rival's shipment" has no truth to be a fact of, and the till (`World.Fattest`, the corner of theirs moving the most `World.Trade`, the query moved into `game` because the rivals sim's boost and the crew sim's spy both read it) is the stash the issue meant.

**A cop** (`World.PayCop(amount)`, `$ pay cop` on the intel screen, `modePayCop`: one page, a money `numberField` whose blank is `cop_price`, the accuracy the amount buys, `enter pay`): dirty cash, gone at once (`Stats.CopsPaid`, `CopCash`; `ErrCopPaid` twice a day, `ErrNoDirtyCash`), queued in `World.Today.Cop`. Two sims read the envelope that night, each rolling on **`Tick.Sub("intel")`** at `content.IntelTuning.Accuracy(amount)` = `cop_accuracy` (0.75) at `cop_price` ($5,000) and less in proportion under it: the **heat sim** (`cop`, at the end of its step, after the ladder ran) files the police's next move where you stand, `heat.Sim.Next(w, city, day)`: the highest rung whose line the city's heat is at or over (the patrol under every line) and the first night it can fire, tomorrow or the day its cooldown lifts (the arrest has none); a wrong word is the rung beside the truth or one to `cop_slip` (3) nights out, never off the ladder (`TestCopIsRightAtTheAccuracy`: right within ± 0.05 of `cop_accuracy` over 500 nights, half the price half the accuracy, a night with no cop rolls nothing); the **law sim** (`intelChief`) files the chief's temper at the same accuracy, a wrong word one of the other tempers, fading, unless the chief is already observed (`TestChiefFact`: right within ± 0.05 over 400 seeds). A cop is not a bribe: no lead, no page, nothing the cold ends. The response fact is what the informed player plays on: the sting cannot fire before the night it names, whatever the heat.

**A spy** (`World.PlantSpy(faction, member)`, `p plant spy` on the intel screen, `modeSpy`: the faction (skipped with one on the ground) then the member, each a picker with what the file holds of the faction and each member's odds; `ErrNotSpy` for a lieutenant running a city, `ErrJailed`, `ErrWounded`, `ErrUndercover`, `ErrSpying` twice a night, `ErrNoRival` for a faction gone) queues `World.Today.Spy` and the **crew sim** (`sim/crew/spy.go`, `spies`, after `life` and before the skim) sends them under that night: `CrewMember.Undercover` the faction id, `UndercoverDay` the day, recalled from their corner, `SpyPlanted` (a headline). A spy **works nothing for you**: `Working()` and `Fit()` are false (`CrewState.Role` and the enforcers' strength, the lieutenant's delegation and the route's driver leave them out; `Post` and `Guard` refuse `ErrUndercover`; `CrewState.Spies()` lists them), the wage runs on, and they stay a member (the skim's roll on the home stream is the roll it was). Every `spy_days` (5) nights under they file a **report** on `Tick.Sub("intel")` (`Stats.Reports`): the faction's muscle (`spy_accuracy` 0.8 at `spy_skill` 80, `content.IntelTuning.SpyOdds` scaling by skill up to 0.98: a skill-90 spy reads 0.9, a skill-10 one 0.1; a wrong count is off by one to `spy_slip` (3) heads), the corner it moves on next (`nextMove`: the tell while the corner is free, else the first corner of yours it borders with no enforcer, else the first it borders; wrong, another of yours) and its fattest till (`World.Fattest`; wrong, another of its corners), each filed at the spy's odds as `Source: spy`. Each report rolls `[intel.spy_found]` by the faction's temper (expansionist 0.10, defensive 0.15, opportunist 0.10, chaotic 0.20): found, `turn_share` (0.5) of them come home **turned** (`Undercover` cleared, `Informant` set, `CrewTurnedInformant` emitted silently so the heat sim starts its clock, #13; the paper says only that they were made and sent home: `SpyFound` with `Why: "made"`) and the rest are **shot** (`SpyFound{Dead}`, headline `SpyShot`; the crew sim's own `fall`, off the roster onto `Crew.Fallen`, `Stats.Bodies` and `Fallen`; no `CrewShot`, so no notoriety or pressure: the body is theirs to hide); `Stats.Spies`, `SpiesFound`, `SpiesShot`. A faction gone (#43) sends its spy home with nothing (`Why: "gone"`, report-only). `TestSpies` pins the plant, the cadence, the three facts, a skill-90 spy right at least `spy_accuracy` of the time over 400 reports against a skill-10 one under it by 0.3, the shooting, the turning, the faction gone and that with nobody under the intel stream is never drawn. There is no recall: a spy comes home found or when the faction is gone (the issue asked for no key; one is a line to add).

**Bad intel** (`rivals.Sim.feed`, after `observe`): a faction alive that trusts you under `feed_trust` (25) rolls `feed_chance` (0.05) a night on `Tick.Sub("intel")` (the roll is made only under the line, so a run nobody distrusts draws nothing) and plants a lie at `feed_confidence` (0.9) as `Source: contact` with itself in `Planted`: a **road** claiming `feed_risk` (0.01) a day, always one with its dial off and nothing on it (and open: a road an asset opens, #48, is no road until it does; the rivals sim copies the route ids, names and assets at `New`), never the road you run, so a policy that never turns a lured road on is not touched (`TestNoIntelIsTheOldRun`'s bands); or one of its own corners as where its **till** is. The lie replaces a true fact on the same key (latest wins: a contact's word on a road you have lost a shipment on is what the panel now reads). **It bites**: the logistics sim, moving the road, takes the first shipment on a lured route whatever the dice (the roll is made all the same, so the road's dice do not move), emitting the same `ShipmentSeized` a real seizure emits (the stats, the record, the report, the driver's cell all read it), files no risk off a staged seizure, `Expose`s the lie, counts `Stats.Bitten` and emits `IntelFalse{Subject, FactKind, Name, Rival, Faction}` (headline `IntelFalseRoute`); a boost on the corner a lie named finds the till empty (`rivals.Sim.lured` in `boost`: the strike's roll is made, `Taken` is false, the heat and the war land, the trust goes), the lie is exposed and `IntelFalseStash` is the headline. `TestTheFeed` pins the plant's shape, the line, the bite and that the feed moves none of the rival's dice; `TestLureAndTheRiskFact` that a lured road is seized on twenty seeds, the fact names the faction, the second shipment rolls the road as it was, and a lure on a road you are not on moves nothing. Fast-forward stops on `SpyFound` and `IntelFalse`.

**The screen** (`ui/intel.go`, `screenIntel`, tab `9`; the frame's digits are `1-9` and the title bar's short names read `Mkt` for the market since the ninth tab, so nine short tabs and the news count fit 120 columns): `INTEL · N facts` over one `table()` of the live facts, newest first (`subject` a leader in the faction's colour, `Chief X`, `<city> police` or a road; `fact` in words: `expansionist`, `4–6 heads`, `$48K chest`, `$12K/day take`, `moves on Riverside`, `till on Docks`, `sting from d34`, `seized ~2%/day`; `sure` as a percent; `learnt` in days (#237: `age` is years, the crew's); `source` `seen`, `the books`, `a cop`, `your spy`, `a contact says` in the warning colour, `fed by Sal` in red once it bit) under one cursor (`intelCursor`, `↑↓`), then SPIES (who is under, with whom, their skill, the days under and `next in 3d · ~75% right`) and `Tonight` lines for a plant queued and a cop paid; the empty state names `$` and `p`. The pane is the fact under the cursor (its words, `sure ██████░░ 75%`, `learnt day 4 · 3 days ago`, the source, `fades −5%/day · gone at 20%`, and the story: what it means and how far to trust it), the two `keyRow`s with the price and the cadence, then SPIES and KEYS; the details overlay carries it whole under 100 columns; `TestIntelScreenFits` renders thirty facts of every kind and source at 80x24, 100x30 and 120x40 at the top, the middle and the bottom of the file, `TestIntelKeys` the keys, the dialogs, the jump and the panels. `i intel` on the map with a rival corner under the cursor (`mapOnRivalCorner`) and on the dashboard jumps here to the subject's first fact, the faction's or the chief's (`jumpIntel`), with a word that nothing is known yet where the file is empty; `i` on the crew screen still investigates and on the rivals screen scouts (the ledger's invest is `u` since #241: `i` is information), and the pointer names all four. The report has an INTEL section (`DayReport.Intel`, `intelLine`: `The cop says: the next thing coming in Eastside is a sting (75% sure).`, `Seen: Sal has 4–6 heads (60% sure).`, the spy's night, a lie that bit) after LAW; `IntelGained{Subject, FactKind, Value, Confidence, Source, Name}` is the report-only event every writer emits, the news picking its intel templates off `Tick.Sub("intel:news")`. Help's WORDS gains `intel` and `spy`. The theme's accent is `theme.Intel` (sky), one meaning: what you know.

**Schema 16**: `Rival.Known` is gone (the books are facts), so `game.MigrateBooks` (the 15 -> 16 step; `Load` reads the old field off the stream a second time as `v15`, on every faction and on a pre-15 save's one `Rival`) files a read snapshot as the four facts on the day it was read, fading from there as a fresh read would (`BooksStale`, `BooksForget` are the file's rates as the step was written); every other field (`World.Intel`, `Today.Cop` and `Spy`, `CrewMember.Undercover` and `UndercoverDay`, `DayReport.Intel`, eight `Stats`) is a zero value, the pre-#45 state. `TestSaveFilesTheBooksRead` pins the fold and a round trip; `TestIntelIsDeterministicAndSaves` a run with a cop paid, a spy reporting and a lie that bit, twice the same and replaying exactly after a save on a morning with the spy under and the lie live.

**Harness** (`docs/harness.md`): `informed` (`harness.Informed`, `cmd/balance -policy informed`) is `diplomat` that pays a cop `cop_price` whenever the heat where it stands is over `InformedHeat` (50) and the file holds no cop's word on the police there that still speaks of a night to come (`InformedMargin` 3× the price in hand), and lies low whatever the margin on a day the word says the police can move at the sting rung or above (`from <= w.Day+1`), so a sting that comes finds it not dealing and adds no page (#27). `TestInformedOutlivesDiplomat`: on twelve seeds lying low at `InformedTableHeat` (55, over the sting line, so the diplomat sells into stings) the diplomat is indicted on day 83 (median) and the informed player on day 134, holding $557,480 at day 200 against $415,442, having paid 182 cops. `harness.NoIntel` boxes the feed (`-feed` has no flag: the observation facts roll nothing and nothing reads them). `TestIntelInvariants` walks a spymaster (the informed player with a spy under and every lured road turned on) over five seeds with the feed at 1: every fact's confidence in 0..1 on every day, a fact gone once under its forget line and no sooner, a spy never at work, on a corner or a route, a road lie at `feed_risk` and every bite naming its author. `cmd/balance` prints `intel: 15.2 cops paid for $75833 per run, 0.0 spies planted (0 found, 0 of them shot; 0 reports), 0.0 lies fed (0 bit) per run; 4.4 facts held at the end` for any run that paid, planted or was fed, and for `informed` always. No knife-edge test moved: the observation facts write no dice and the feed rolls on its own stream, so the seed-pinned mechanism tests run as they did.
