# UI

**UI** (`internal/ui`, the grammar of epic #78, finished by #79–#89 and #95) is one Bubble Tea `Model` with a `mode` state machine (start menu, play, the dilemma card, the buy/sell dialog, the route target dialog, the fund dialog, the report, help, the details overlay, the confirmations for a new run, a slot's deletion, a firing, the end of the day, an upgrade, the questions, a pay-off and a trip, the post, strike, undercut, front and assign pickers, the propose dialog and game over; `modeCount` is the sentinel `TestModalsFit` walks) and a `screen` tab (dashboard, market, journal, crew, map, upgrades, ledger, rivals). Every play-mode screen is drawn through the same parts, each fixed by its own paragraph below: the three-part **frame** (`frame.go`, #79 and #95: row 0 the title bar, rows 1..h-2 the body, row h-1 the status bar; there is no ticker, the journal is the news and the title bar counts the unread), the **details pane** (`pane.go`: each screen's `details() []section`, the selection first with its facts as `row`s and what the keys would do to it as `keyRow`s, the screen's further sections, KEYS last; beside MAIN from `paneMinWidth`, 100 columns, with no toggle, the one-line strip on row h-2 under that, where `space` opens it whole as the `modeDetails` overlay), the **key table** (`keys.go`, #80 and #109: `bindings` is the one place a key is bound, and the pane's KEYS, the modal footers, help and the README are rendered from it, a key listed only on the screens it names; `keyPlay` dispatches through `lookup` and nothing else), the **modal** (`modal.go`, #81: one box for every dialog, picker, confirmation, the card, the report and help, `min(width-4, 76)` wide, scrolling), the **tables** (`table.go`, #82: `table(cols, rows, cursor, width)`, one number format per column) and `internal/format` for every number. Where things live: state in MAIN, the inspectors and the prose in the pane (no view file draws an inspector, a hint line or rules under its table), and no key hint anywhere but the pane's KEYS, the pane's `keyRow`s, a modal's footer, the empty states through `emptyState` and the dashboard's `tutorialLine` (`TestNoKeyHintsOutsideTheLegend`); a pointer to another screen is always `screenPointer`'s `on the <screen> (<digit>)`. `Model.city` is the one view cursor for the per-city screens, the city the market and map are turned to (`shown()`); it follows the player when they travel, `[`/`]` cycle it on any screen and left/right cycle it on the market (`cycleCity`), the map's cursor is an index into the shown city's corners, and `actionCity()` is what `s` and `x` act on: the shown city on the market and map, where the player is everywhere else. Key handling: `handleKey` dispatches by mode, `keyDialog` owns the buy/sell flow (product -> quantity -> dial; a buy is where you are, single units held to the stash), `keyTarget` the route target dialog (`routes.go`, `modeTarget`: product -> units or days of demand on `←→` -> the number, the current target prefilled, blank clears, #115), and `keyPlay` the play screen through the table, where a screen's own bindings win over the globals (`h`/`f`/`t`/`i`/`$` hire, fire, assign, investigate and pay off on the crew screen, `t` there giving the selected lieutenant a city through the `modeAssign` picker; `c`/`e`/`a`/`w`/`u` post a runner / an enforcer / abandon / send the enforcers / undercut on the map (`map.go`, `rivals.go`; the pickers are `modePost`, `modeStrike` and `modeUndercut`, #68; posting yourself on the other city's map explains that you have to go there; `u` is the tree's buy key on the upgrades screen and the map's own here) and `r`/`R` there turn the selected route's dial (`cycleRoute`: off, slow, normal, fast) and open its target dialog (`openTarget`); `f` on the journal turns the source filter (`journal.go`, `cycleFilter`, #122); `u` buys the selected node on the upgrades screen (`upgrades.go`; one branch at a time under tabs, `modeConfirmUpgrade` asks first) and `enter` there is the buy key; `b` on the ledger buys a front through the picker (`ledger.go`, `modeFront`), `f` there funds a city (`law.go`, `modeFund`) and `enter` buys the selected offer or turns the selected route's dial (`ledgerActable`); `d` on the rivals screen opens the propose dialog (`diplomacy.go`, `modePropose`: kind, then terms, `shift+tab` goes back a page) and `y`/`x` answer the selected offer; `a`/`x`/`d` on the market with the cursor on the buyers accept, decline and deliver) and the globals work wherever nothing shadows them (`b` buys from the supplier where you stand, `s` sells in the shown city, `x` cancels an order, `l` lies low, `p` and `d` cycle the pay and launder dials, `g` asks before travelling (`modeConfirmTravel`, naming the corner you leave), `r` reopens the report, `space` opens the details overlay under 100 columns, `?` help; the frame's `1-8`, `tab`, `ctrl+s`, `N` and `q` are quiet). `n` ends the day at once; `enter` on the play screen opens a confirmation (`modeConfirmEnd`) and only a second `enter` or `y` ends it. `F` is fast-forward (#116, `ui/fast.go`, listed on the dashboard and global): `modeConfirmFast` takes a cap in a `numberField` (blank is `fastDays`, 7; `fastDaysMax`, 30) and `y`/`enter` runs `fastForward`, which ends days in a loop inside the one `Update` (no timer or command; never a scene mid-loop, the stopping morning plays its own: the bust's on an enforcement past a patrol, #155), each through `stepDay` as `endDay` does (`endDay` is `stepDay` then `morning`, the over/card/report opening), and stops before the report of the first morning `stopReason` names: a card pending, an alert whose `key` was not in `alerts()` the morning before (`alerts()` is the dashboard's ALERTS list, one source for both: somebody talking, a contract due tomorrow or today keyed per contract, heat where you are at or over the patrol line, dirty cash under the laundering float while a front or a route reads it, wages the dirty cash cannot pay, and the nearest gate ahead while it is within reach, keyed per gate, #148), then the first of the tick's events `stopEvent` names (`Enforcement` past a patrol, `CornerStruck`, `CornerTaken` from you, `CrewQuit`, `CrewDefected`, `LieutenantWalked`, `FrontAudited`, `ShipmentSeized`, `DealOffered`, `DealBroken`, `RivalAbandoned` (a corner given up to a price war: free, the tell's kind of stop), `ContractOffered`, `PressureShifted` and `ReputationShifted` up, `ChiefReplaced`, `DAElected`, `SupplyShort`, `StandingShort`, `Unlocked` (`unlockStop`: `the Laundromat is open to you`, `Heroin is on offer`, `the Dutchman deals with you`, `lieutenants want work`) and `RivalMovedIn`, #148), else the cap; the report then opens with `Stopped after 3 days: contract due today.` (`Model.fastStop`, UI state, never on the world, kept until the next day ends so `r` reopens it) and the status bar says `Ran 3 days.`; `TestFastForwardIsTheSameDays` pins that F for five days and `n` five times leave the same world, `TestFastForwardStopsOnACard` that a stop never skips a card. Inside dialogs, the card and the report `enter` confirms and closes and must never advance the day (`TestEnterDoesNotEndDay`, `TestCardBeforeReport`). Up/down move the cursor on every screen and left/right move within it, never between tabs (`tab` / `shift+tab` and the digits do that): the map is walked as a grid (`mapMove`, the cursor stays an index into the shown city's `Corners` so the pickers are unaffected), the upgrades tree turns to the next branch (`upgradeMove`), the market turns to the other city, dialogs move the dial, and the other screens ignore them. The map (#85) is one title line (`MAP · Eastside`, the city tabs, `N/N held · N worked · ~N/day free`; the rival's count is the corner inspector's), the grid, a blank, `ROUTES` and one line per route touching the shown city (`routeLines`: `name  from ─mode─····▶ to  dial  Nd · N units · $N/u · ~N%`, the terms shortened to `Nu` and then dropped where the width has no room for them, the pane having them). **The road shows where a shipment is** (#160, a still marker redrawn on key like every other cell: no tick, no scene, the road moves a step a day): the edge carries a **track** of `trackW` (5) cells between the mode and the arrowhead, `Bayport ─truck──▪───▶ Eastside`, and every shipment in flight on the route (`World.Shipments`, `Sent`, `Arrives`) is a `▪` in `theme.RoadText` on the cell `floor(elapsed / days × trackW)` (`trackCell`), clamped inside the track, so one landing today sits at the arrowhead's end, two on one cell sharing it with the count after the marker (`▪2`, kept inside the track; `Model.track` builds it plain, `markTrack` colours it, `modeEdge` is the edge without its arrowhead); the track is the last thing the width ladder drops, after the terms, so the marker is on the line wherever even the bare line fits (at every tested size), and the pane's route section carries `on the road  ▪ day 2 of 3 · 400 Weed` per shipment in the order sent (`shipmentLines`; the day is `elapsed + 1`, so the morning it left is day 1 and the morning it lands tonight is day 3 of 3; the units go on a row of their own where the value column has no room for both) whatever the width; the ledger's LOGISTICS row (`roadOn`, units per product with the soonest arrival) and the dashboard's `on the road` fact count units as before. `TestRouteMarkerMoves` sends a three-day shipment by hand and reads the track at `▪────`, `─▪───`, `───▪─` and empty once it lands, at 80 and 120 columns, and `checkMap` wants every route's line to carry the track with its markers or the bare edge, never a mix; the routes are always listed whole and the grid scrolls by row (`Model.mapTop`) to keep the corner under the cursor in view when MAIN is short. The chosen corner's name cell is `Selected` and stays so while the cursor is on the routes, whose row is then `▸` + `Selected`; the routes have their own cursor (`routeCursor`, `onRoutes`) that down off the bottom row of the grid reaches and up off the first route leaves, and while it is on the routes the pane shows the route instead of the corner inspector. The ledger (#87) is the till lines (`LEDGER`, dirty · clean · seized, the launder dial as `careful  [normal]  greedy`, the dirty-cash warning) over FRONTS, LOGISTICS and ON OFFER as `table()`s under one cursor (`ledgerCursor`, `ledgerRows`: fronts, then routes, then offers; `ledgerMove`), the LOGISTICS heading `LOGISTICS · shipped 60 units in 1 run · seized 0` and every route with its dial, targets, what is on the road, the week's lots and fares (`RouteSpend`) and what it has lost; a ledger taller than MAIN scrolls with the cursor (`ledgerScroll`, `ledgerWindow`) and never clamps; the pane is the selected front (`frontSection`), route (`ledgerRouteSection`) or offer (`offerSection`), then WASH (`washSection`). The market shows the other city's price and where else you hold the product; the dashboard's CITIES panel (`citiesLines`, one aligned line a city with no header, under LAW | RIVALS in the wide layout only, with two cities and a terminal tall enough to leave the street its lines, 100x30 is not; there and at 80 columns the street carries the stash elsewhere and the road as facts, `elsewhereFacts`) shows every city side by side with the value of its stash, corners held, who runs it, its heat and what is on the road to it, and CASH's last line names the other city's heat. Rival corners are purple on the map; the dashboard's RIVALS panel (wide only; at 80 columns LAW's last line is the leader and their corners) shows the leader, corner count and personality (once observed) on one line, the war and the trust as bars, and what holds or waits at the table. The title bar says when there is news you have not read: `Model.journalSeen` is the journal's length when the journal screen was last shown (set on entering it and on every render of it, clamped by `refreshJournal` so a new run starts at zero, set to the whole journal on continuing a save, never saved: a view cursor like `Model.city`), and the Journal tab reads `Journal 3` with the count in `theme.News` wherever the tab's name fits, the digits-only bar dropping the count with the name (`TestJournalUnreadCount`); the bar drops to short tab names, then the clean cash, then the city, then the digits as width runs out (the day, the dirty cash and the heat never go; the day is `dayLabel`, which rolls the number over while the morning's scene runs, #159); long lines on a screen should be `truncate`d, not left to wrap (the report and the help truncate their lines to the modal). The UI redraws only on a key or a resize (`Init` returns nil, `TestInitStartsNothing`). **Help** (#89, `viewHelp`, `helpLines`) is the key table in the modal: `GLOBAL`, then a group per screen with only its own keys (`helpGroups`, a binding once under the first screen it names), each row `key  label — help` (`helpRow`; a help sentence runs to `helpSentenceW`, 44 characters, at most, so no row is cut at 80 columns), then `WORDS` (`words`: the dial, the float, the target, the file, drift, undercut, keep at, standing, connect, credit, unlock, the pane and the strip, a line each) and the one line of voice; `TestHelpMatchesKeys` holds the rows to the bindings and `TestHelpScrollsToTheLastRow` the shape and that the modal scrolls to its end at 80x24. **The README is held to the code**: `go run ./cmd/keys -w` writes its key table from `ReadmeKeys` between the `keys` markers (`TestReadmeMatchesKeys`), and `go test ./internal/ui -run TestReadmeCaptures -update` renders its two captures, the dashboard at 80x24 and the map at 120x40, from the rich fixture on `captureSeed` (`richModelSeeded`; `Model.startRun(seed)` is what makes a run replayable, `newRun` calling it with `game.NewSeed()`) between the `capture:<name>` / `capture:end` markers, trailing blanks dropped, so a screen change fails `TestReadmeCaptures` until the README is regenerated (`SpliceReadme` / `ReadmeSection` are the one splice for both; `TestCaptureFixtureIsDeterministic` pins that the fixture on the seed renders the same twice). Layout must fit 80x24, 100x30 and 120x40 (`TestRendersAtCommonSizes`, whose `richFixture` walks every screen, dialog and picker with a route and a shipment in flight; its `assertFits` also fails on a modal wider than the terminal, which wraps its border, and its `assertFrame` pins the three-part frame); `richModel` in `ui_test.go` is the fixture with something behind every modal. Colours live in `internal/ui/theme`, one accent per sim.
