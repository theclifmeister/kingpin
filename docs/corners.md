# Corners are the demand

**Corners are the demand.** Each city's `Corners` (seeded from `internal/content/city.toml`, one `[[city]]` per city with its `[[city.corner]]`s) each carry a size, per-product `Taste`, a heat multiplier and a robbery `Risk`. `ProductMarket.Demand` is per *standard corner* in that city; what the player can sell there is `World.Demand(city, product)` = that times `HeldShare(city, product)` (the summed shares of corners there that are `Worked()`: owned and with a runner, or `game.You`, posted). A held corner with nobody on it drifts back to `none` after `drift_days` (`territory.Sim.DriftDays(w)`: the file's plus the tree's `drift_days_bonus`, the corner boys; zero in the file is never and stays never; the map's `nobody, 3d left` reads it); an enforcer posted on a corner cuts its robbery chance (`territory.Sim.RobberyChance(w, c)`: `robbery_chance` times the corner's risk times the tree's `robbery_mul`, the watchmen and the dogs, and then the enforcer's cut, so the cut comes off the folded number); a robbery takes from the street of the corner's city, what the runner there was carrying (#73: the houses have their own robbery, per house per day at `robbery_chance x Risk x house_risk`, the corner's `Risk` being the block's, rolled off `Tick.Sub("houses:"+city)` and cut by a `Guard` exactly as `enforcer_cut` cuts a corner's: `territory.Sim.HouseRobberyChance`). The territory sim folds the tree once at the top of its step (`Sim.Effects`, #119) and reads those two names and no other. `Fire` and crew quits `Recall` the member so a corner never points at someone who is gone. The heat sim weights every unit sold by the corner it moves on (`CornerWeight`: corner heat, and `crew_heat` for a runner's corner) and by the city's `HeatMul`, so where you sell matters as much as how much. Dirty cash over `dirty_cash_threshold`, past what the fronts cover (`dirty_cash_cover` times what they cost: `heat.Sim.Cover`), is heat too, where the player is, but only dealing builds a case: a sting or raid that fires on a day the player attempted no sale in that city (no `PlayerSold` there with `Wanted > 0`; lying low resolves none) still costs stock and cash and cools heat but adds no evidence, so a cash pile draws stings without being a countdown (#27, `TestRichHiderIsNeverIndicted`). Heat is per city and the case is personal: every city's heat takes its own sales, decays on its own and holds the fear floor; the war's heat lands at home, an informant's, an audit's and the cash pile's where the player is; and the *hottest* city's police answer at the thresholds (one response a day, the cooldown ladder and `Evidence` global), taking stock from one place there (#73: a house the police `Known`, else a roll over the houses and the street by the heat of the block, else the street). `Heat.Peak` is the hottest any city has been.
