# Corners are the demand

**Corners are the demand.**
Each city's `Corners` (seeded from `internal/content/city.toml`, one `[[city]]` per city with its `[[city.corner]]`s) each carry a size, per-product `Taste`, a heat multiplier and a robbery `Risk`.
`ProductMarket.Demand` is per *standard corner* in that city; what the player can sell there is `World.Demand(city, product)` = that times `HeldShare(city, product)` (the summed shares of corners there that are `Worked()`: owned and with a runner, or `game.You`, posted; a corner's `Share` is its size, its taste, less a rival's squeeze and, since #47, times its `Repeat`, the share of its customers bad product has not cost it, the market sim's field on the corner: `docs/quality.md`).
A held corner with nobody on it drifts back to `none` after `drift_days` (`territory.Sim.DriftDays(w)`: the file's plus the tree's `drift_days_bonus`, the corner boys; zero in the file is never and stays never; the map's `street in 3d` reads it); an enforcer posted on a corner cuts its robbery chance (`territory.Sim.RobberyChance(w, c)`: `robbery_chance` times the corner's risk times the tree's `robbery_mul`, the watchmen and the dogs, and then the enforcer's cut, so the cut comes off the folded number); a robbery takes from the street of the corner's city, what the runner there was carrying (#73: the houses have their own robbery, per house per day at `robbery_chance x Risk x house_risk`, the corner's `Risk` being the block's, rolled off `Tick.Sub("houses:"+city)` and cut by a `Guard` exactly as `enforcer_cut` cuts a corner's: `territory.Sim.HouseRobberyChance`).
The territory sim folds the tree once at the top of its step (`Sim.Effects`, #119) and reads those two names and no other.
`Fire` and crew quits `Recall` the member so a corner never points at someone who is gone.
The heat sim weights every unit sold by the corner it moves on (`CornerWeight`: corner heat, and `crew_heat` for a runner's corner) and by the city's `HeatMul`, so where you sell matters as much as how much.
Dirty cash over `dirty_cash_threshold`, past what the fronts cover (`dirty_cash_cover` times what they cost: `heat.Sim.Cover`), is heat too (the sum is `heat.Sim.ExposureLine`, the one line the pile is charged against and every screen warns at, #350), `dirty_cash_heat` a threshold multiple past it and at most `dirty_cash_heat_max` (35) a night (`heat.Sim.PileHeat`, #396), where the player is, but only dealing builds a case: a sting or raid that fires on a day the player attempted no sale in that city (no `PlayerSold` there with `Wanted > 0`; lying low resolves none) still costs stock and cash and cools heat but adds no evidence, so a cash pile draws stings without being a countdown (#27, `TestRichHiderIsNeverIndicted`).

**The pile's bound** (#396): unbounded, the rule charged a pile $40M past its cover 200 heat in one night, and heat 100 is the arrest, so an export landing past the cover was an arrest with its own warning (the playtest on `d2ec6d2`: three billionaires arrested with no evidence, 122 to 193 heat off the pile on the last night). `dirty_cash_heat_max` bounds a night's pile heat, so a pile far past its cover arrests in about five nights, about eleven lying low: time to wash it, buy cover or spend it. The bound is 35 and not lower because lying low more than doubles the decay: at 25 or under, lying low every night holds a pile of any size under 100 while the export lanes keep paying, a pardon (`TestPileHeatIsBounded` pins both: never more than the bound a night, and the arrest within thirty nights lying low). No harness policy meets it: over 37 policies, ten seeds and 400 days the pile's worst night is 17.4 (street-scale policies sit over the line most mornings at 2 to 5 a day, which is the drag the pile is for), so no band, digest or ending frequency moved. With the fronts covering a twentieth of what they do (`dirty_cash_cover` 0.5, a probe), the cartel was arrested on all ten seeds unbounded (day 242 on average) and on none bounded: it washes the spike.
The rule bends twice, both for something you did: an informant on the payroll files pages on their clock (`docs/snitching.md`), and **a bribe that backfires** (#42, `docs/law.md`) files `backfire_evidence` pages and `backfire_heat` the morning after whatever was sold, on a quiet day too (`TestBackfireOnAQuietDayIsEvidence`; the tip's `tip_evidence` page (#70) is the same shape), and a third time for clean cash moved offshore over the lot in a day (#195, `structure_evidence` a lot over `laundering.toml [offshore] lot`, read off `Laundering.Structured` the morning after, `TestStructuringFilesPages`).
Heat is per city and the case is personal: every city's heat takes its own sales, decays on its own and holds the fear floor; the war's heat lands at home, an informant's, an audit's and the cash pile's where the player is; and the *hottest* city's police answer at the thresholds (one response a day, the cooldown ladder and `Evidence` global), taking stock from one place there (#73: a house the police `Known`, else a roll over the houses and the street by the heat of the block, else the street).
`Heat.Peak` is the hottest any city has been.
The ladder's levels are constants (#144: `content.Patrol`, `Sting`, `Raid`, `TaskForce` (#48), `Arrest`, `content.Levels` in the order the thresholds climb and `content.Rank` a level's place on it, 1 to 5; `HeatConfig.validate` refuses a `[[response]]` the code does not know, a rung listed twice, a rung missing or thresholds out of order), so no copy of the ladder is a string literal: the heat sim dispatches on them, `Enforcement.Level` carries them, and the crew sim's danger, the law's observation, the news' lines, the dashboard's patrol alert, fast-forward's stop, the bust scene's rank (`bustRank`, `content.Rank` past a patrol) and the harness's sting line all compare against them.
**The task force** (#48, `docs/assets.md`) is the rung between the raid and the arrest (threshold 88, its own flat `cooldown_days` 30) that forms only against an asset owned or dirty cash over `[heat] taskforce_cash` ($25M) and is skipped otherwise (`heat.Sim.TaskForceEligible`), so every run without either plays the four rungs it always did; it is announced a day ahead (`TaskForceFormed`, `HeatState.TaskForceDay`), comes the next night whatever the heat at the file's numbers, files a page only on a day you dealt, seizes one asset (`AssetSeized`, booked by the laundering sim), and stamps `HeatState.WatchUntil` for the plane's risk.
**Targeted investigations** (#343, `heat.toml [investigation]`, on in the file; `sim/heat/investigation.go`): the police name an operation before the sting, so the answer to a warning is no longer always to stop the whole business.
The heat sim keeps a tally of the heat it already computes by the source it can put a name to, `HeatState.Trail` (keyed `game.LeadKey(city, kind, target)`): a street sale's heat split over the corners it moved on in `CornerWeight`'s proportions, a buyer's handoff by its product, a move by the house the stock went into (or came out of, to the street); every tally forgets `1/window_days` (7) of itself a night, and nothing rolls a die.
The night the hottest city's police would send the sting, they open `HeatState.Investigation{City, Kind, Target, Opened, Due}` on the biggest source there instead (`heat.Leads`, an argmax; a tie goes to the corner, then the product, then the house, then the id) and emit `InvestigationOpened`; with nothing to name (heat off the pile, the war, the informant) the sting is the blind one it always was, so the rich hider's stings are blind and file nothing (`TestRichHiderIsNeverIndicted` and `TestQuietDayRuleHoldsUnderEveryLaw` play with it on as well as off).
While it runs the sting rung is its (the patrol under it still answers, the raid, the task force and the arrest over it are untouched: heat saturates), and the rung's cooldown runs from the night it opened.
`lead_days` (2) nights later it lands on its target alone, as the night's one response (behind a task force it waits a night; a favour called in stands it down, `InvestigationClosed{Fell}`): a corner worked and sold on that night takes the sting's share of the street and the pile and its runner and enforcer are swept; a product loses its whole street stock in the city; a house is raided at the raid's share.
It files `evidence` (2) pages only if the target was in use that night (the corner sold on, the product sold or handed over, the house holding stock on a night you dealt in its city): only dealing builds a case (#27).
A hit that found anything goes out as the sting's `Enforcement` beside `InvestigationClosed{Hit}` and takes `closed_heat_drop` (4, each repeat less as every rung's) off the city: feed them one corner and keep the rest running.
A target suspended, moved or emptied in time is a miss: nothing taken, nothing filed and nothing cooled.
The numbers are the guard's: two pages, two nights and a drop of four are where a player who ignores every warning does no better than under the blind sting (`TestSurgeonBeatsManaged`: $144k against $148k at day 200; a drop of eight or a third night had it outlive the blind sting), while `harness.Surgeon`, who walks off the named corner and never lies low, ends the Distribution tier on $658k against managed's $516k (and on $157k, indicted, with the blind sting).
The rulings on the issue's open questions: the patrol's `SellCap` stays global (a follow-up); a product is named only off a buyer's handoffs, street sales naming their corners, so a city with one product names its corner; the route and the front the issue lists are not tallied yet, their hits being the logistics and laundering sims' to make (a follow-up).
**It ships on** (#343's second PR): `enabled = false` is the blind sting, the run before the feature byte for byte (`TestNoInvestigationIsTheOldRun`), and `cmd/balance -investigation off` plays it.
The gaps the switch exposed are the game's to close, not the tests'.
**The cop's word** names the night the hit lands (`heat.Sim.Next`): an investigation open in the city is the sting on its `Due` whatever the heat does meanwhile, and a sting line met with a source to name is `lead_days` past the night it opens, later again while one open elsewhere holds the rung (`TestNextKnowsTheInvestigation`); the `informed` player lies low the one night that matters.
**A lieutenant of any temper answers** (`crew.Sim.delegate`, `namedCorner`, `namedProduct`): the crew come off a corner an open investigation names and it stays held, unworked and never abandoned, until it closes; a named product gets no standing order and no supply contract in the city (`TestLieutenantAnswersAnInvestigation`); the tempers still differ in the dial, the heat, the skim and the guard.
**A captain answers too** (`crew.Sim.plan`, `docs/lieutenants.md`): whoever stands on a named corner in the captain's city comes off it and nobody is posted there until it closes, inside the plan the day's preview reads, so `CaptainNights` and the night agree (`TestCaptainAnswersAnInvestigation`); a named product is nothing for a captain, who places no orders and keeps no contracts.
**The POLICE section's sting rung** reads the named hit's pages (`heat.Sim.Rungs`: `evidence` less the lawyer's cut, the sting a dealer meets being the named one), so the panel and the file agree; `TestRungsAreWhatTheyTake` plays the hit through to the night it lands.

An owned asset's `heat_floor` is a floor under every city beside fear's (`Sim.Floor`, `AssetFloor`).
`heat.Sim.Ladder(w, city)` is the ladder the player faces, the rung listed only once it can form: the dashboard's gauge reads it.
**The sim's files** (#275): `heat.go` holds the `Sim`, its readers and `Step`, which reads as its phases in a fixed order over one working `day` (the sources `sales`, `war`, `contracts`, `moves`, `sloppy`; the pages `informants`, `envelopes`, `forfeiture`; `dirtyCash`; `audits`, `structuring`; then `cool`, `sellCap`, `respond`, `cold`, `indict`, `settle` and `cop`), the order being the reasons' and the dice's.
`sources.go` is what draws heat (`SaleHeat`, `ContractHeat`, `MoveHeat`, `CornerWeight`, `Sloppiness` and the phases that add it through `day.add`); `evidence.go` the file (`EvidenceArrest`, the pages every phase files through one `day.file(city, pages, why)`, which stamps `EvidenceDay` and writes "why (n)" unless the why is empty, as the audit's is, its line riding the audit's heat; the case going cold and a response's pages in `fire` stay by hand); `ladder.go` the decay, the night's response, `fire`, `seize`, `place` and `hottest`; `exit.go` `indict`, `exit` and `takeFall`; `intel.go` `Next`, `Due` and `cop`.
`internal/sim/heat/heat_test.go` (#144) is the sim at unit level, one world and one step at a time, in the shape of `laundering_test.go`: the ladder is the file (`TestLadderIsTheFile`), the law moves the ladder and nothing else under every chief and DA and the neutral fallback (`TestLadderUnderTheLaw`), sale heat follows volume, the dial, the city and the corner (`TestSaleHeatFollowsVolume`), the quiet-day rule at the rung, a sting and a raid on a quiet day, a day you sold there, a day you sold elsewhere and a handoff (`TestQuietDayRule`), the ladder fires highest first and one a day with the patrol's cap, the cooldown, the repeat's smaller drop, the fall guy and the arrest (`TestTheLadderFires`), the file's indictment, the retainer's cold pages and the lawyers' cut (`TestTheFile`), decay, lying low, the fear floor and the cap (`TestDecayAndTheFloor`), the informant's clock (`TestInformantClock`), the cash pile's line, the fronts' cover, the exposure line and quiet money (`TestCashPileLine`) and the raid's place (`TestRaidPlace`).
Note for a test at this level: the sim decays before it checks the ladder, so a city carries `(line + 1) / (1 - decay)` into the night to fire a rung, which reads over 100 for the arrest (the day's sales add before the decay and the cap).
**Crew life bends it twice more, both again for something you did** (#46, `docs/crew-life.md`): a member you left in a cell comes back under the informant line and rolls the turn at once (`release_turn`), and a sour retiree talks on the way out, one `informant_evidence` page the heat sim files off `CrewRetired{Sour}` the same tick, whatever was sold.

**The block a corner is on can be bought** (#194, `docs/property.md`): `Corner.Deed` is clean cash turned into ground, and this sim pays its rent at the top of its step and reads `robbery_mul` off it on the corner and on a house there.

**The tax** (#231, `city.toml [tax]`: `share` 0.5, `cut` 0.10, `min_held` 1; `territory.Sim.HoldsTheCity`, `TaxDue`, `taxStep`; `events.Taxed` (report-only); `Stats.Taxed`; `docs/tuning.md`): the demand-side lever #223 asked for, the one that rewards dominance rather than more selling.
Once the city is yours in the kingpin's sense (`World.Dominant`: every faction at the table arrived and gone or paying homage) and you hold more than `share` of a city's corners (`min_held` at least; its own number beside `rivals.toml [endings] kingpin_share`, so the ending's file is untouched), every corner there nobody holds (`OwnerNone`: not yours, not a faction's) is worked by independents who pay you for the right: `cut` of the corner's trade (`World.CornerTrade`, every product the city sells at its price and the corner's full share) a night, jittered `jitter` (0.1, a tenth) either way (`TaxTuning.Jittered`) on `Tick.Sub("tax:"+city)`, in dirty cash, summed per city (`Taxed{City, Corners, Amount}`, the report's MONEY line `The tax: 4 free corners +$9,300`), on `Stats.Taxed` (the ledger's `tax` line reads the night's due and the total, the summary's THE MONEY has a row) and in `cmd/balance`'s `tax:` line.
No heat and no evidence: nobody of yours moved a unit, and the tax is not a sale (`TestTaxStopsWhenTheShareFalls` pins the heat and the file still, the corner a faction set up on paying nothing, the share falling stopping it and `cut` at 0 boxing it).
It is dirty money, so the task force's `taskforce_cash` still bites and money at tier 5 is still not safety (`docs/assets.md`).
The territory sim pays it after every city's drift and robberies and before the houses, on the tax's own stream, so a run in which the city is never yours is byte-for-byte the run before (`TestNoTaxIsTheOldRun`: the boss, the laundered and the crewed players hashed daily to day 200 on the file and with `cut` at 0); `TestTaxPaysTheReign` runs the kingpin scenario thirty nights uncrowned and reads the four free corners paying every one, no page filed.
**The ruling** (the issue read the share alone): every crewed policy holds six of home's ten corners from day 6 or 7, before the rival, arrived on day 10 with one, has grown, for a week or three (8–24 days over the seeds; the boss in the hub too), and the share alone taxed those days, moved seven seed-pinned tests (`TestNoLifeIsTheOldRun` to the dollar among them) and flipped two orderings (`TestUpgradedBeatsCrewed`, `TestLogisticsBranchMovesMoreForLess`) on the compounding of a few thousand a night at tier 1; the table's read is what makes it dominance and not a land grab, and it is what the issue's own guards assumed (the sixteen policies of `TestNobodyIsDominantByAccident` never reach it).
`TestTaxAtTierFive` re-reads the tier-5 band with the tax on (the boss $172.7M and the cartel $167.1M on ten seeds at day 300, unchanged: neither dominates by then, so the lever pays the reign and not the harness); no pinned number moved bar `TestSeedDigest`'s shape (`Stats.Taxed` in the walk).

**The police's next move is a fact** (#45, `docs/intel.md`): `heat.Sim.Next(w, city, day)` is the truth a paid cop's word is measured against, the highest rung whose line the city's heat is at or over (the patrol under every line) and the first night it can fire, tomorrow or the day its cooldown lifts (the arrest has none).
The heat sim files it where the player stands at the end of its step, after the ladder ran, off `World.Today.Cop` at `content.IntelTuning.Accuracy(amount)` (`cop_accuracy` 0.75 at `cop_price` $5,000, less in proportion under it), a wrong word the rung beside the truth or one to `cop_slip` nights out, never off the ladder, rolled on `Tick.Sub("intel")`; a night with no cop files nothing and rolls nothing.
The response fact (`game.FactResponse`: the level in `Value`, the night in `Number`) is what `harness.Informed` lies low on: a sting cannot fire before the night it names, whatever the heat, and one that comes on a night you lie low adds no page (#27).
`TestCopIsRightAtTheAccuracy` pins the word right within ± 0.05 of `cop_accuracy` over 500 nights, `TestNextRung` the truth.

**A hand-over** (#281): every change of holder goes through `game.(*Corner).Hand(owner, faction, day)`: your strike taking a corner, a faction taking one (`rivals.Sim.take`), the price war's abandon, a crackdown, a raid your tip brought, a fragmenting faction's drift, the lieutenant's walk, the territory sim's drift and `World.Abandon`.
It starts again everything that belongs to the holder (`Runner`, `Enforcer`, `Idle`, `Squeeze`, `Since`, `Starved`, `StarvedDay`) and leaves `Robbed` alone: that is your stick-ups on the corner, not the holder's, and the territory sim forgets it once the corner has been off your street for the drift days.
Before #281 the sites disagreed: the rivals' reset `Starved` and not `Robbed`, the lieutenant's walk the reverse, and the drift and `Abandon` neither `Squeeze` nor `Starved`, so a corner you gave up kept the day's undercut share on a street corner nobody worked (the one pinned number that moved: `TestSeedDigest` on day 56, the boss abandoning eastside's sixth corner with a 0.15 squeeze on it; the day after reads as before, and every report and journal over 200 days on 24 runs is unchanged).
`TestHandResetsTheHolderNotTheStickUps`.

**Who writes a corner** (#274).
The corner is the territory sim's: it posts and drifts, counts `Idle`, `Robbed` and `Yours`, and hands a corner nobody worked back to the street.
A change of holder anywhere is `Corner.Hand` (#281): outside `internal/game` nothing assigns `Owner` or `Faction`, or a whole `Corner`, directly (`TestCornerOwnerIsHanded`, in `internal/sim/corners_test.go`; the one exception is `ui/demo.go`'s demo world, which paints a corner for the scenes and is never a hand-over in a run).
Every other write into a corner from a package that is not `game` or the territory sim is a row of `cornerWriters` in the same file, and `TestCornerWritersAreDeclared` fails on a write the table does not list and on a row the code no longer needs, so a new cross-sim write is a ruling before it lands.
The table today:

| Package | Writes | Why |
|---|---|---|
| `sim` (the assembler) | `Hand()`, `Since`, `Idle` | a character's day-0 corners (#232, `sim.applyStart`): a street corner handed to you, the one you stand on its clock restarted |
| `sim/crew` | `Hand()`, `Since` | the lieutenant's walk hands his corners to the faction he walks to or the street (#144); his claim stamps `Since` on a street corner he posts on |
| `sim/market` | `Repeat`, `Squeeze` | `Repeat` is its field on the corner (#47); `Squeeze` on the **rival's** corners is your price war (#68: zeroed in `openWar`, written in `closeWar`) |
| `sim/rivals` | `Hand()`, `Squeeze`, `Starved`, `StarvedDay` | `Squeeze` on **your** corners is the undercut (zeroed once a step, then the deepest faction's); `Starved`/`StarvedDay` the price war's count on its own corners; every hand-over a faction makes (take, the strike, a crackdown, the books, the war order, a fragmenting faction) |
| `ui` | `Owner`, `Runner`, `Enforcer` | the demo world (`ui/demo.go`), as above |

A World method that writes a corner (`Post`, `Recall`, `Abandon`, `BuyDeed`, `SeizeDeed`, the migrations) is `game`'s and is not listed at its callers.
The scan is type-checked (`golang.org/x/tools/go/packages` over the module, every field of a `game.Corner` however it was reached, `c := &city.Corners[i]` included), which the line grep of `TestSimsWriteOnlyTheirOwnState` could not see.
`Squeeze` is one field two sims write on disjoint corners, the rivals' on yours and the market's on the rival's; splitting it into two would change the save's shape and is left as a follow-up.
