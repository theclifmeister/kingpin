# Reputation is the player's face

**Reputation is the player's face** (#14, `internal/sim/reputation`, `Player.Reputation`, tuning in `reputation.toml`): three axes 0-100, `Fear`, `Respect`, `Notoriety`, that the reputation sim drifts from the day's events and fades toward `baseline` by `decay` of the gap a day. Sources: `CornerStruck` (taken or held) and `RivalPushed` are fear, `CrewPaid` at generous pay in full, `CrewPaidOff` and every night a truce or a split holds (`deal_kept`, read off `w.Rival.Deals`) are respect (a short payroll costs it), `PlayerSold` volume per `units`, a strike, and every headline about you are notoriety, where "about you" is a journal entry from yesterday whose source is in `headline_sources` (heat, crew, territory, laundering: market and flavour are the city's news, the rival's story is its own, and the strikes are counted directly). The sim steps after laundering and before news, so it reads yesterday's journal and the band it crosses today (`ReputationShifted`, a multiple of `band`, up or down, six headline keys `Reputation<Axis>Up/Down`) is today's news. The street has only so much attention: past `total` between the three every axis shrinks in proportion, so you cannot max all three (`TestReputationCannotMaxAllThree` feeds every source for the horizon and the sum holds at 200). The effects live in `[effects]` as what the axis does at 100, scaling linearly (`content.Scale` / `content.Cut`), and each is read by the sim that owns the number, from its own copy of `content.ReputationFX` and `w.Player.Reputation`, never through another sim: the rival's push *chance* (`rivals.Sim.PushPace`, fear), its claim chance (`rivals.Sim.ClaimPace`, `rival_claim_cut`, fear) and what it makes of a proposal (`fear_deal`) and a kept deal-night (`respect_trust`), the crew's daily loyalty loss (`crew.Sim.LoyaltyLoss`, respect) and a candidate's signing fee (`crew.Sim.HireFee`, notoriety; fixed when the candidate is generated, so the pool catches up as it rotates), the supplier ratio, per connect since #72 (`market.Sim.SupplierRatio(w, sup)`: the band the relationship sits in on the connect's ratio band, then `supplier_mul` and respect's `respect_supplier_cut` on top; the dashboard's supplier line reads the best available connect's), and in the heat sim a floor heat decays toward and is never taken under (`heat.Sim.Floor`, fear) plus the weight of a unit *you* move on your own corner (`heat.Sim.PersonalHeat`, notoriety; a runner's units are theirs, so a notorious boss gets off the corner). The dashboard draws the three as one line of bars under the heat gauge (purple, blue, orange; the 80-column HEAT panel has no room for them) and the game-over summary reports them; `cmd/balance` prints the end medians. **What your name buys is said** (#233, `ui/reputation.go`: `nameEffects`, `nameSection`, `nameLine`; `docs/frame-and-pane.md`, `docs/copy.md`): the dashboard's pane carries a YOUR NAME section, an axis a row with its effects in words under it (`fear 62` / `rivals set up on a free corner 25% less often, push you 37% less` / `heat never falls under 16`; `respect 40` / `the connect knocks 6% off, the crew lose loyalty 20% slower`; `notoriety 70` / `hire fees 35% off, every unit you move yourself draws 35% more heat`), one line an effect over zero, from `content.ReputationFX` scaled by the axis with the same `content.Cut` and `Scale` the sims read, so the numbers are the numbers the dice use (`TestEffectsInWordsAgreeWithTheSims` reads them against `rivals.Sim.ClaimPace` and `PushPace`, `market.Sim.SupplierRatio`, `crew.Sim.LoyaltyLoss` and `HireFee`, `heat.Sim.PersonalHeat` and `Floor`, and walks the dashboard, the rivals and the crew screens at the three sizes with the section on); the rivals screen carries one line of it under the leader's bars (`your name: fear 62: they set up 25% less, push 37% less · respect 40: a kept deal earns 20% more trust · notoriety 70`); a candidate in the pool at notoriety over 50 `asked to work for you: your name is in the paper`. **The paper names you**: `Title` in the headline context is `a dealer` with nobody on the payroll, `a crew` with somebody, `the boss of Eastside` while the city is yours in the kingpin's sense (`news.Sim.title`, `boss`: `World.Dominant` with more than `kingpin_share` of home's corners held, the tax's read, #231), and `headlines.toml [swagger]` are the boss's flavour lines (`Op-ed: Eastside has the boss of Eastside now, ...`), rolled at `flavour_chance` off `Tick.Sub("swagger")` only while the title is the boss's: the flavour on the home stream rolls as it always did, so no seed-pinned number moves and a run that never held the city has the paper it had. **The morning opens on you** (`news.swaggerLines`, under the reign's line in TIER): `2 crews paid homage last night: $5,000.` (before the reign, whose line carries it), `Vasquez's crew pushed on your front line twice and were held off, losing 1 head.` (`RivalPushed` is a push that did not take; the heads are `CrewShot{Theirs, Dead}` off a push), `Nobody set up on a free corner in Eastside: your name kept them out.` off `events.ClaimDeterred` (report-only), which the rivals sim emits where it made the claim roll and fear turned it: the one roll it always made, read either way, a roll under the full chance and over what `ClaimPace` leaves it (`TestClaimDeterredIsReadOffTheRoll`; `TestThePaperNamesYou` in `sim/news`). Nothing in `internal/content/*.toml` changed but `headlines.toml`; `TestSeedDigest` moved on day 56 by copy alone, the report's opening line the night the rival's push was held off (`World.Report` is in the walk): with the report set aside the digest is main's on all sixty days, verified on both trees, and no number moved.

`reputation_test.go` in the harness pins the acceptance: every axis in 0-100 and the sum under `total` on every day of every policy, a feared player carries more heat than a respected one at the same volume, no policy ends with all three above 70, each effect pulls its way, and the save round-trips (`TestSaveKeepsReputation`). The zero value is a nobody, which is what the pre-#14 world was, so no schema bump. Fear's floor and notoriety's personal heat are what moved the difficulty tests when they landed: the crewed player's late game sits a sale under the sting line, so the vigilant player in `snitch_test.go` lies low at 35, and `TestGreedyWashesMoreAndIsAuditedMore` reads its wash over five seeds rather than each.
