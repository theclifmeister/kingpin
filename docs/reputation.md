# Reputation is the player's face

**Reputation is the player's face** (#14, `internal/sim/reputation`, `Player.Reputation`, tuning in `reputation.toml`): three axes 0-100, `Fear`, `Respect`, `Notoriety`, that the reputation sim drifts from the day's events and fades toward `baseline` by `decay` of the gap a day. Sources: `CornerStruck` (taken or held) and `RivalPushed` are fear, `CrewPaid` at generous pay in full, `CrewPaidOff` and every night a truce or a split holds (`deal_kept`, read off `w.Rival.Deals`) are respect (a short payroll costs it), `PlayerSold` volume per `units`, a strike, and every headline about you are notoriety, where "about you" is a journal entry from yesterday whose source is in `headline_sources` (heat, crew, territory, laundering: market and flavour are the city's news, the rival's story is its own, and the strikes are counted directly). The sim steps after laundering and before news, so it reads yesterday's journal and the band it crosses today (`ReputationShifted`, a multiple of `band`, up or down, six headline keys `Reputation<Axis>Up/Down`) is today's news. The street has only so much attention: past `total` between the three every axis shrinks in proportion, so you cannot max all three (`TestReputationCannotMaxAllThree` feeds every source for the horizon and the sum holds at 200). The effects live in `[effects]` as what the axis does at 100, scaling linearly (`content.Scale` / `content.Cut`), and each is read by the sim that owns the number, from its own copy of `content.ReputationFX` and `w.Player.Reputation`, never through another sim: the rival's push *chance* (`rivals.Sim.PushPace`, fear), its claim chance (`rivals.Sim.ClaimPace`, `rival_claim_cut`, fear) and what it makes of a proposal (`fear_deal`) and a kept deal-night (`respect_trust`), the crew's daily loyalty loss (`crew.Sim.LoyaltyLoss`, respect) and a candidate's signing fee (`crew.Sim.HireFee`, notoriety; fixed when the candidate is generated, so the pool catches up as it rotates), the supplier ratio, per connect since #72 (`market.Sim.SupplierRatio(w, sup)`: the band the relationship sits in on the connect's ratio band, then `supplier_mul` and respect's `respect_supplier_cut` on top; the dashboard's supplier line reads the best available connect's), and in the heat sim a floor heat decays toward and is never taken under (`heat.Sim.Floor`, fear) plus the weight of a unit *you* move on your own corner (`heat.Sim.PersonalHeat`, notoriety; a runner's units are theirs, so a notorious boss gets off the corner). The dashboard draws the three as one line of bars under the heat gauge (purple, blue, orange; the 80-column HEAT panel has no room for them) and the game-over summary reports them; `cmd/balance` prints the end medians. `reputation_test.go` in the harness pins the acceptance: every axis in 0-100 and the sum under `total` on every day of every policy, a feared player carries more heat than a respected one at the same volume, no policy ends with all three above 70, each effect pulls its way, and the save round-trips (`TestSaveKeepsReputation`). The zero value is a nobody, which is what the pre-#14 world was, so no schema bump. Fear's floor and notoriety's personal heat are what moved the difficulty tests when they landed: the crewed player's late game sits a sale under the sting line, so the vigilant player in `snitch_test.go` lies low at 35, and `TestGreedyWashesMoreAndIsAuditedMore` reads its wash over five seeds rather than each.
