# The stage is the interstitial

**The stage is the interstitial** (#149, `ui/stage.go`, `modeStage`, `game.Progression.Seen`, `[tier] text` and `closing` in `progression.toml`). The morning a tier is entered the game stops and says so: one modal, before the card and the report (`showStage` is what `morning`, `continueRun` and the fast-forward's stop open on, and falls through to `showCard`), titled `STAGE 3 · TERRITORY`, the tier's `blurb` in gold, its `text` (three to six lines of the file, `content.StageTextMin`/`Max`, wrapped as one paragraph through `wrapLines`), `OPENED` with the file's `opens` lines and `NEXT` with the tier's `next` line or, on the last tier, the file's `closing` line (decode wants `text` on every tier past the first and `closing` on the last and no other; `TestProgressionReadsAsALadder`). The prose is the file's and never code's; the OPENED block reads the file alone, not the tick's events, because a save on the modal reopens it and the events are gone by then (#148's `Unlocked` lines are the report's). **Once per tier per run**: `Progression.Seen map[int]bool` beside `Reached` (nil is nothing seen, no schema bump), `World.StagePending()` the highest tier reached and not seen (never tier 1: it is day 0 and never stamped, so a fresh run opens on no stage), `World.SeeStage(n)` marks n and every tier under it seen, so a save from before the stage with three tiers reached is shown the highest once and not walked through them (`TestOldSaveSeesOneStage`). `enter`/`esc` close it (`keyStage`: `SeeStage`, a quiet `game.Save` so the morning's status, the tell in red, stays, then `showCard`) and never end the day (`TestEnterDoesNotEndDay` has the mode; `TestStageBeforeCard` pins stage → card → report); the footer is `enter esc close` from `modeBindings`; the day's save is made before the morning opens, so quitting on the modal reopens it and quitting after does not (`TestStageOnce`). Fast-forward stops on it first, before the card's reason: `Stopped after N days: a new stage.` (`stopReason`, `TestFastForwardStopsOnAStage`). The dashboard's tier fact reads `tier Territory · new` in `theme.NewsText` while the stage waits, the way the Journal tab counts the unread, and is worth a line then (`priStage`, over the counts; `priTier` otherwise). `TestStageFits` renders every tier's modal from the file's copy at 80x24 and 120x40 and wants it whole: no line cut, no scroll mark, `stageLines` within `modalRoom` (fifteen rows at 80x24: the blurb, four lines of text, four `opens` lines and a two-line `next` is the most the copy can be), the title, the blurb, every `opens` line and the `next` or `closing` line on the screen; `TestModalsFit` opens the mode from the rich fixture (whose tiers are seen, so the days a test ends from it open on the report as before; a test wanting the stage deletes tier 4 from `Seen`), and `richFixture` renders a tier-4 world with the stage pending and its modal at both sizes. `richModel`'s and the walker's mornings after a hire see the stage through the `endDay` and `closeMorning` helpers. The animation (#157) plays where the one-line hook in `showStage` says; nothing else of it is here. Help's WORDS `tier` line says the stage is shown once; the README's progression passages say so too.
