# Buyers are the market screen's reason to exist

**Buyers are the market screen's reason to exist** (#71, `internal/content/buyers.toml`, `content.BuyersConfig`, `game/buyers.go`, `sim/market/buyers.go`, `ui/buyers.go`). A buyer is a named person in a city who wants product off-corner on a deadline at a premium; the deck is `[[buyer]]` entries with a `city` (or `any`), a `product` (or `any`, narrowed by `min_price` to a tier), bands for `size` (in days of the demand your corners in that city serve for the product, never under one standard corner's, so a buyer scales with the operation and a cornerless player still gets an order), `days` and `premium`, a `penalty` (respect) and `penalty_cash` (of the undelivered units' street value), a `heat` multiplier per unit, `weight`, `once`, `unlock_cash` like products and an optional `[buyer.trigger]` in the shape of a dilemma card's (`content.CardTrigger`, checked through `game.Eligible`, so one trigger vocabulary and one checker and no `market → news` import, #144); `[buyers]` carries the pacing (`min_gap`/`max_gap`, exactly the dilemma deck's rule), `offer_days`, `blacklist_days`, `respect` for a full delivery, `notoriety_penalty` for a failure and `keep_days`. Every price in the file is a multiplier of the street price in the buyer's city on the day of delivery, never a dollar figure, so a rescale of the economy carries through. The **market sim** deals and resolves them (`market.Sim.deal`, `deliver`, `settle`), and every roll comes off `Tick.Sub("buyers")`, one a day whatever happens, so the deck never moves the home stream: a run with the deck boxed (`cfg.Buyers.Deck = nil`) is the same world as one with it dealt, contracts aside (`TestContractsAreDeterministicAndBoxable`), and the harness leaves the deck **on** in every run, like the rival's offers (an offer nobody answers touches nothing but itself; the buyers' headlines are source `buyers`, which neither notoriety nor the law counts, and pick their template off the same side stream so the news sim's dilemma roll is not moved), which is why no pinned number moved when it landed. One offer is on the table at a time. **State**: `World.Contracts []Contract` (`offered / accepted / delivered / failed / expired / declined`, with `City`, `Units`, `Delivered`, `Premium`, `Street` the price the day it was offered, `Since`, `Expires`, `Due`, `Paid`), `World.Buyers` (`NextID`, `LastOffer`, `Drawn`, `Blacklist`), and the per-day scratch `World.Deliveries` (contract id -> units) the clock clears; zero values are the pre-#71 state, so no schema bump. **Actions**: `AcceptContract(id)` / `DeclineContract(id)` apply at once while the offer is open (`Contract.Open`; `Accept`/`Decline` are the rival's, `Offer` too, hence `OfferContract`), `Deliver(id, units)` queues a handoff against a contract you hold, out of the stash in its city, refused with `ErrElsewhere` unless you stand there (a handoff is what only you can do somewhere; the stock got there by road or was bought there), `ErrLyingLow` on a lie-low day (`SetLieLow` clears the queue: everyone's day off) and `ErrContractDue` past the due day. The market sim hands over `min(queued, owed, stash)` at the city's opening street price times the premium **before** the street orders (a contract has first call on the stash), moves the units and the cash, and emits `ContractDelivered` (`Complete` and `Respect` on the last handoff; `HeatMul`, `Street`, `Signed`); after the price drift it `settle`s: `ContractAccepted` (report-only) for yesterday's acceptances, `ContractFailed` for a contract short at its due day (`TakeCash` collects `PenaltyCash` of the undelivered value, dirty then clean; `Respect` and `Notoriety` on the event; the buyer is blacklisted `blacklist_days`), `ContractExpired` (report-only) for a lapsed offer, and prunes what has been resolved `keep_days`. **Off-corner is the point**: a handoff consumes no `World.Demand`, takes no dial, is not capped by `Heat.SellCap`, needs no corner and pays no lieutenant's cut (it is a `ContractDelivered`, never a `PlayerSold`, so the crew sim never sees it). The **heat sim** consumes it in one block at the end of its event loops: `attempted[city]` exactly as a `PlayerSold` with `Wanted > 0` (#27: a sting on a delivery-only night files pages, one on a night an offer merely lapsed files none, `TestHandoffHeat`) and `heat.Sim.ContractHeat(w, city, product, units, mul)` = `sale_heat x SaleHeatMul x units x HeatMul x product heat / street_units x mul`, SaleHeat's formula less the fill, the corner weight, the dial and the lieutenant, kept beside it and previewed on the market screen; the **law sim** counts the units against `hard_units` in the city delivered in; the **reputation sim** adds `Respect` for a full delivery (the third respect source beside the payroll and a kept deal) and takes the penalty's respect and adds its notoriety on a failure. The **UI**: the market screen's BUYERS block sits under the product table for the shown city with its own cursor (`buyerCursor`, `onBuyers`), reached off the bottom of the table the way the map's routes are and left off its top; while it is live `a` accepts, `x` declines and `d` hands over everything deliverable from that city's stash (on the product table `x` still cancels an order and `d` still turns the launder dial), and the detail under the table is the contract's (pitch, what it pays today against the day it was asked, owed, the heat a handoff draws tonight). The dashboard gets one line (`contractsLine`: contracts held, due today/tomorrow, offers waiting) and an alert for a contract due today or tomorrow, so the screen keeps its reason to be visited; the report's SALES section carries the offer, the acceptance, the handoff and the failure. `harness.Dealer` (`cmd/balance -policy dealer`, in `harness/buyers.go`) is `crewed` that answers the offers where it stands (takes what it can cover from the stash plus one buy, counting the room tonight's street sales make; declines the rest and anything in the other city), buys the shortfall before the restock, keeps a contract's units out of the night's street orders (`reserve`) and hands over as much as keeps the city under the sting line by `DealerMargin` with the night's orders counted, everything on the due day; `harness.Welsher` is the dealer that never delivers its first contract. There is no `-contracts` flag: the deck is on in the harness as it is in the game. `harness/buyers_test.go` pins the acceptance; `TestDealerBeatsCrewed` reads day 120 over five seeds and the tier rows of `TestMoneyCurve` were left alone for #60 to re-measure.
