# Lieutenants run a city for you

**Lieutenants run a city for you** (#31, `crew.toml [lieutenant]`, `[lieutenant.personality.*]` and `[role.lieutenant]`; `sim/crew/lieutenant.go`; `ui/lieutenant.go`). A lieutenant is a crew member (`CrewMember.Personality`, fixed at generation from `violent / greedy / careful / steady` and hidden until `Observed`; `City`, `Assigned`) and everything they do is a policy over the actions the player has, run inside the crew step for each city that has one (`World.Assign(id, city)` / `Unassign`, one per city; `CrewState.Lieutenant(city)`): nothing new is simulated, only who decides. Candidates are lieutenants with `chance` only once the player holds corners in two cities (`crew.LieutenantsWanted`; the roll is only made then, so a one-city run draws the pool it always did), and an *assigned* lieutenant adds `[role.lieutenant].crew` slots to the roster (`crew.Sim.MaxCrew(w)`; `Hire` takes the cap from the caller), which is how a six-strong crew staffs a second city. Every night, after the roster has settled: the cut (`[role.lieutenant].cut` of their city's takings, `Stats.Cuts`) and a greedy one's `skim` on top, at any loyalty, come off dirty cash first (the skim is folded into the day's `CrewSkimmed`); under `flip` loyalty they turn informant with no dice (`LieutenantFlipped`, hidden like `CrewTurnedInformant`: the heat sim starts its clock on it and, while a lieutenant is talking, files `[lieutenant].evidence` pages a leak instead of `informant_evidence`); at `quit_threshold` one running a city **walks with it** (`crew.Sim.walk`: every corner held there bar the one you stand on goes to the rival if it holds ground in that city, else to the street, the stash there is emptied, `LieutenantWalked`, `Stats.Walked`); then delegation (`crew.Sim.delegate`, no dice): the crew come off a corner robbed twice since it was claimed (`Corner.Robbed`, counted by the territory sim and forgotten `drift_days` after the corner goes back to the street), idle runners go on the best corners there (a held one nobody works, else the biggest free one; `Post` refuses a split corner, so the next is tried, and a claim's `Since` is set to the tick so the territory sim reports it in the morning), idle enforcers guard the worked ones if the temper's `guard` says so, whatever is held with no runner is abandoned (never the player's own corner or one a live split covers), and every product in the stash gets a **standing order** at the temper's `dial` (`World.Delegated`, keyed like `Orders`, refreshed nightly and dropped on `Unassign`, `Fire` or a walk; `World.DelegatedOrder`; `World.StandingOrder` is the player's own standing order first, #114, then the lieutenant's). The market resolves the lieutenant's order only where the player placed no order of their own and no standing one (`PlayerSold.Standing` and `Delegated`), and every `PlayerSold` in a run city carries `Lieutenant` / `LieutenantName`, which the crew sim uses for the cut and the heat sim for the temper's `heat` multiplier (`heat.Sim.LieutenantHeat`, inside `SaleHeat` so the dial preview is honest). Lying low is everyone's day off. `LieutenantActed` is report-only bookkeeping for what they did and what it cost; the report names the temper once they have run a city for `reveal_days` (`Observed`). The zero values are the pre-#31 state, so no schema bump. Acceptance in `harness/lieutenant_test.go`: the delegated player never places an order at home once a lieutenant has it and home sells on standing orders (unassigned nightly, it sells nothing); on one seed and policy a violent lieutenant's city is hotter at day 60 than a careful one's, a greedy one keeps less of the same takings as a steady one and a careful one takes less; a lieutenant at loyalty 0 walks the next night with every corner unheld and the stash gone; the player's order for a product wins the day; a flip is dice-free and files thicker pages; lieutenants only come looking with two cities held; assignment, temper and standing orders survive a save and the run replays; and `delegated` at `steady` is within 20% of `distributor` at the horizon (`harness.Delegate` puts one on the payroll for a test). Balance: the lieutenant adds corners, not a heat budget or the rival's forbearance, so the tier-4 row is the boss's, not `delegated`'s (see `moneyCurve`); a violent one in a delegated run can raid the operation into an arrest, which is the temper working as designed under a policy too blunt to fire them (the boss reads the aggressive dial off their standing orders and fires them that morning).

**A city a lieutenant runs takes your buys, and they keep it stocked** (#174). *Buying through them*: `World.Buy(supplier, product, qty, credit, pressure)` is allowed from another city where `w.Crew.Lieutenant(city)` is assigned there (`World.CanBuyIn(city)`; `ErrElsewhere` otherwise, as before) and is the one path, `world.buy`, at the **contract markup** (`World.BuyMarkup(city)`: 1 where you stand, else `World.Markup`, which the market sim stamps at seed and at the top of every step from `market.toml [supply] markup`, `market.Sim.Markup`, ×1.05, the same number the contracts pay, so buying by hand where you stand keeps its point; zero reads as 1, so a save from before #174 buys through them at the connect's price on the day it is loaded and at the markup from the next morning): the connect's price pressure, `BoughtToday`, the relationship, the cap and the credit rules move exactly as for a buy by hand, a credit buy goes on the book as yours, the receipt carries `Purchase.Lieutenant` (the name; a contract's receipt carries it too where the contract is theirs), `World.Quote(sup, product, qty, credit)` (`Supplier.QuoteAt` at the markup) is what it charges, and the cart's `Return` refunds exactly what was charged, the markup included (`TestBuyThroughTheLieutenant`). *The lieutenant restocks by temper*: `delegate`'s last step (`crew.Sim.restock`, no dice) keeps a **delegated supply contract** for every product a connect in their city sells (`Supplier.Sells` and not `Locked`; never a `NoSupply` product) at `[lieutenant.personality.*] stock_days` of the demand the city's worked corners serve under the tree (`World.Demand` × the fold's `DemandMul`, the number the market serves an order against), shared out by demand where the levels together would overfill the stash (`World.Capacity`, the way `harness.Stocked`'s `contract` shares the bag), refreshed nightly (`World.DelegateSupply`, `World.DelegatedSupply` keyed like `Supply`, `World.DelegatedSupplied` mirroring `DelegatedOrder`, `World.StandingSupply` your own contract first as `StandingOrder` is, `World.SupplyDue` reading it, dropped by `DropStanding` on `Unassign`, `Fire`, a move or a walk); the market sim fills it through `Plan` and `FillSupply` at the markup, for cash, never over the contracts' float, and **never on a lie-low day** (`Plan` skips the lieutenant's contracts while `w.LieLow`; yours still fill), and the standing orders count on it (the stash plus what the contract brings, cut to the room, so the night sells what the morning buys: without it a city that sells out sells every other day). The stock days: violent 3, steady 2, greedy 1.5, careful 1. The contract fills before the night's sale and the dial sets what the night sells (`[dial.*] fill` of demand, ×1.25 more with the tree's `fill_mul`), so the days are read against that: the violent one pushes up to two days a night and keeps three, the steady one sells up to a day and a quarter and keeps two, the careful one moves half a day and keeps one, and the greedy one keeps the least that covers the night and the money; the issue's first numbers (2 / 1.5 / 1 / 1) sold out every night under the tree. A stash smaller than what the corners move in a night is sold whole, and the morning shows what the night left: the honest measure of a stocked city is the stash plus `SupplyDue`, the sell dialog's own number. `LieutenantActed` carries `Bought` (product, units, cost, off the morning's `SupplyBought` events naming them, `SupplyBought.Lieutenant` / `SupplyShort.Lieutenant`) and `Contracts`; the report's CREW line reads `Marta runs Bayport: bought 120 Weed for $2,500, sells normal tomorrow.`, a short one `Marta could not keep Weed stocked in Bayport: 30 under the level, …`, MONEY `Marta's restock: 120 Weed at $22.05 from Marisol = -$2,646` and, for a buy by hand through them, `Bought 120 Weed at $22.05 from Marisol through Marta = -$2,646`; the SALES line for a contract is yours only. **UI**: `b` on the market turned to a city a lieutenant runs opens the buy dialog for that city (`buyCity`: `dialogCity` for a buy; where you stand on the dashboard and everywhere else), the connect step listing `w.SuppliersIn(city)`, the title `BUY · BAYPORT · MARISOL · THROUGH MARTA` (`buyThrough`), the quantity's max the room there and the cash way at the markup (`affordFrom`, `World.Quote`), the price line `supplier $21 · +5% through Marta · street $38 · margin +73%` (the margin over what the buy pays; `priceFacts.base`, `markup`, `through`), `keep at` settable for that city as for any; turned to a city you are not in and nobody runs, a fresh `b` refuses with the pointer `Can't buy in Bayport: you are in Eastside and nobody runs it for you. Go there on the map screen (5), or give a lieutenant the city on the crew screen (4).` (`buyPointer`; the sell dialog's `b` still turns to a buy where you stand, #168, `TestDialogToggleNamesTheCity`); the market's `keep` column reads `120 (lt)` in `CrewText` (`level` in a `kInt` column) as `order` reads their order, the pane's contract row `keep at 120 (lt)` with no `x` (theirs to keep; `x` cancels only yours), the market's NOTES `You are in Eastside: Marta buys here for you at ×1.05 the connect's price, …`, the connect pane's `b  buy through Marta at ×1.05`, the dashboard's supply fact `supply 2 contracts +5 (lt)`, the cart's quantity step adds to a buy in their city through them; `b`'s help reads `buy where you stand or a lieutenant runs`, WORDS has `through`.
