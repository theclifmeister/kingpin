# Lieutenants run a city for you

**Lieutenants run a city for you** (#31, `crew.toml [lieutenant]`, `[lieutenant.personality.*]` and `[role.lieutenant]`; `sim/crew/lieutenant.go`; `ui/lieutenant.go`).
A lieutenant is a crew member (`CrewMember.Personality`, fixed at generation from `violent / greedy / careful / steady` and hidden until `Observed`; `City`, `Assigned`) and everything they do is a policy over the actions the player has, run inside the crew step for each city that has one (`World.Assign(id, city)` / `Unassign`, one per city; `CrewState.Lieutenant(city)`): nothing new is simulated, only who decides.
Candidates are lieutenants with `chance` only once the player holds corners in two cities (`crew.LieutenantsWanted`; the roll is only made then, so a one-city run draws the pool it always did), and an *assigned* lieutenant adds `[role.lieutenant].crew` slots to the roster (`crew.Sim.MaxCrew(w)`; `Hire` takes the cap from the caller), which is how a six-strong crew staffs a second city.
Every night, after the roster has settled: the cut (`[role.lieutenant].cut` of their city's takings, `Stats.Cuts`) and a greedy one's `skim` on top, at any loyalty, come off dirty cash first (the skim is folded into the day's `CrewSkimmed`); under `flip` loyalty they turn informant with no dice (`LieutenantFlipped`, hidden like `CrewTurnedInformant`: the heat sim starts its clock on it and, while a lieutenant is talking, files `[lieutenant].evidence` pages a leak instead of `informant_evidence`; and the night they turn while running a city that holds `betray_share` (0.5) of your corners and `betray_corners` (3) at least the run ends `betrayed` through `World.End` with their name on it, #49, `docs/endings.md`: a flip over one corner is a leak, the boss's seed 6 collapsed to one; 0 boxes it); at `quit_threshold` one running a city **walks with it** (`crew.Sim.walk`: every corner held there bar the one you stand on goes to the rival if it holds ground in that city, else to the street, the stash there is emptied, `LieutenantWalked` with `Rival` and `Faction` naming who the corners went to, `Stats.Walked`; the hand-over is the one write into `w.Rival` outside the rivals sim, listed in `TestRivalStateHasOneWriter` (#144): the corners change hands tonight because the news sim's card triggers read the rival's ground the same tick and the morning's map shows it, and the flip's bookkeeping, `Flips`, `LastFlip`, `Observed`, goes with them because the boss and the diplomat read `LastFlip` in the morning, before the rivals sim could stamp it off the event a step later; `TestWalkHandsTheRivalTheCornersTonight`); then delegation (`crew.Sim.delegate`, no dice): the crew come off a corner robbed `robbed_off` times (`crew.toml [lieutenant]`, twice) since it was claimed (`Corner.Robbed`, counted by the territory sim and forgotten `drift_days` after the corner goes back to the street), idle runners go on the best corners there (a held one nobody works, else the biggest free one; `Post` refuses a split corner, so the next is tried, and a claim's `Since` is set to the tick so the territory sim reports it in the morning), idle enforcers guard the worked ones if the temper's `guard` says so, whatever is held with no runner is abandoned (never the player's own corner or one a live split covers), a lieutenant of any temper **answers a targeted investigation** in their city (#343, `docs/corners.md`: the crew come off the corner it names, which stays held, unworked and never abandoned, until it closes, and a named product gets no standing order and no supply contract there; `namedCorner`, `namedProduct`, `TestLieutenantAnswersAnInvestigation`), and every product in the stash gets a **standing order** at the temper's `dial` (`World.Delegated`, keyed like `Orders`, refreshed nightly and dropped on `Unassign`, `Fire` or a walk; `World.DelegatedOrder`; `World.StandingOrder` is the player's own standing order first, #114, then the lieutenant's).
The market resolves the lieutenant's order only where the player placed no order of their own and no standing one (`PlayerSold.Standing` and `Delegated`), and every `PlayerSold` in a run city carries `Lieutenant` / `LieutenantName`, which the crew sim uses for the cut and the heat sim for the temper's `heat` multiplier (`heat.Sim.LieutenantHeat`, inside `SaleHeat` so the dial preview is honest).
Lying low is everyone's day off.
`LieutenantActed` is report-only bookkeeping for what they did and what it cost; the report names the temper once they have run a city for `reveal_days` (`Observed`).
The zero values are the pre-#31 state, so no schema bump.
Acceptance in `harness/lieutenant_test.go`: the delegated player never places an order at home once a lieutenant has it and home sells on standing orders (unassigned nightly, it sells nothing); on one seed and policy a violent lieutenant's city is hotter at day 60 than a careful one's (with investigations on, the violent one survives the sixty days because it walks off a named corner like every temper; selling into them it was indicted on day 38 of seed 2), a greedy one keeps less of the same takings as a steady one and a careful one takes less; a lieutenant at loyalty 0 walks the next night with every corner unheld and the stash gone; the player's order for a product wins the day; a flip is dice-free and files thicker pages; lieutenants only come looking with two cities held; assignment, temper and standing orders survive a save and the run replays; and `delegated` at `steady` is within 20% of `distributor` at the horizon (`harness.Delegate` puts one on the payroll for a test).
Balance: the lieutenant adds corners, not a heat budget or the rival's forbearance, so the tier-4 row is the boss's, not `delegated`'s (see `moneyCurve`); a violent one in a delegated run can raid the operation into an arrest, which is the temper working as designed under a policy too blunt to fire them (the boss reads the aggressive dial off their standing orders and fires them that morning).

**A city a lieutenant runs takes your buys, and they keep it stocked** (#174).
*Buying through them*: `World.Buy(supplier, product, qty, credit, pressure)` is allowed from another city where `w.Crew.Lieutenant(city)` is assigned there (`World.CanBuyIn(city)`; `ErrElsewhere` otherwise, as before) and is the one path, `world.buy`, at the **contract markup** (`World.BuyMarkup(city)`: 1 where you stand, else `World.Markup`, which the market sim stamps at seed and at the top of every step from `market.toml [supply] markup`, `market.Sim.Markup`, ×1.05, the same number the contracts pay, so buying by hand where you stand keeps its point; zero reads as 1, so a save from before #174 buys through them at the connect's price on the day it is loaded and at the markup from the next morning): the connect's price pressure, `BoughtToday`, the relationship, the cap and the credit rules move exactly as for a buy by hand, a credit buy goes on the book as yours, the receipt carries `Purchase.Lieutenant` (the name; a contract's receipt carries it too where the contract is theirs), `World.Quote(sup, product, qty, credit)` (`Supplier.QuoteAt` at the markup) is what it charges, and the cart's `Return` refunds exactly what was charged, the markup included (`TestBuyThroughTheLieutenant`).
*The lieutenant restocks by temper*: `delegate`'s last step (`crew.Sim.restock`, no dice) keeps a **delegated supply contract** for every product a connect in their city sells (`Supplier.Sells` and not `Locked`; never a `NoSupply` product) at `[lieutenant.personality.*] stock_days` of the demand the city's worked corners serve under the tree (`World.Demand` × the fold's `DemandMul`, the number the market serves an order against), shared out by demand where the levels together would overfill the stash (`World.Capacity`, the way `harness.Stocked`'s `contract` shares the bag), refreshed nightly (`World.DelegateSupply`, `World.DelegatedSupply` keyed like `Supply`, `World.DelegatedSupplied` mirroring `DelegatedOrder`, `World.StandingSupply` your own contract first as `StandingOrder` is, `World.SupplyDue` reading it, dropped by `DropStanding` on `Unassign`, `Fire`, a move or a walk); the market sim fills it through `Plan` and `FillSupply` at the markup, for cash, never over the contracts' float, and **never on a lie-low day** (`Plan` skips the lieutenant's contracts while `w.Today.LieLow`; yours still fill), and the standing orders count on it (the stash plus what the contract brings, cut to the room, so the night sells what the morning buys: without it a city that sells out sells every other day).
The stock days: violent 3, steady 2, greedy 1.5, careful 1.
The contract fills before the night's sale and the dial sets what the night sells (`[dial.*] fill` of demand, ×1.25 more with the tree's `fill_mul`), so the days are read against that: the violent one pushes up to two days a night and keeps three, the steady one sells up to a day and a quarter and keeps two, the careful one moves half a day and keeps one, and the greedy one keeps the least that covers the night and the money; the issue's first numbers (2 / 1.5 / 1 / 1) sold out every night under the tree.
A stash smaller than what the corners move in a night is sold whole, and the morning shows what the night left: the honest measure of a stocked city is the stash plus `SupplyDue`, the sell dialog's own number.
`LieutenantActed` carries `Bought` (product, units, cost, off the morning's `SupplyBought` events naming them, `SupplyBought.Lieutenant` / `SupplyShort.Lieutenant`) and `Contracts`; the report's CREW line reads `Marta runs Bayport: bought 120 Weed for $2,500, sells normal tomorrow.`, a short one `Marta could not keep Weed stocked in Bayport: 30 under the level, …`, MONEY `Marta's restock: 120 Weed at $22.05 from Marisol = -$2,646` and, for a buy by hand through them, `Bought 120 Weed at $22.05 from Marisol through Marta = -$2,646`; the SALES line for a contract is yours only.

**A lieutenant answers a faction moving on their city** (#379, the player's answers of #341 made for them; `docs/rival.md`).
Nothing new is simulated, only who decides: with a faction's scouts in the city they run (`comingTo`: `RivalState.Scouting()` with `ScoutingCity` theirs, the first in table order), `delegate`'s step 4 changes for a temper that `guard`s: whatever is held with no runner is **held, not abandoned**, until the faction arrives (a free corner is where it lands, so ground held is ground it must fight for); and a temper with `hit_scouts` (`crew.toml [lieutenant.personality.*]`: the violent and the steady one; the careful one will not start a fight, the greedy one will not pay for it) **hits the scouts** the next night, with an enforcer on the payroll to send.
The hit is the player's `World.HitScouts` made for them: the crew step runs after the rivals sim, so it cannot queue tonight's `Today.HitScouts`, and sets `World.DelegatedHit` (the faction, `""` nobody; cleared at the top of `crew.Sim.lieutenants` every night and set afresh), which the rivals sim resolves in `scouting` beside `Today.HitScouts` while the lieutenant still runs the city and an enforcer is on the payroll: the same `ScoutsHit`, setback, grudge and broken deals, once a faction.
`DelegatedHit` is the crew sim's to write (`TestSimsWriteOnlyTheirOwnState`); zero is the run before it, so no schema bump, and a run where no faction scouts a lieutenant's city is byte-for-byte the one before (every harness policy hands a lieutenant home, where no faction is ever drawn, so no pinned number moves by it; `TestSeedDigest` moved by the field's shape on day 1).
`TestLieutenantAnswersTheScouts` (harness): a lieutenant of each temper running the hub, every hub corner held with three runners for six, a faction's scouts put in the hub: the guarding tempers hold all six the night after and the greedy one gives three up; the violent and the steady one set `DelegatedHit` and the scouts are hit the next night (`ScoutsHit`, once), the careful and the greedy one never, nor the steady one with no enforcer; with nobody coming the steady one gives the corners up as ever.
The harness's `delegated` and `boss` hand their lieutenant home and work the hub themselves, so the faction drawn to the hub is the player's to answer, not the lieutenant's: `harness.HitScoutsIn` (`docs/harness.md`).

## The captain runs crew care for a city (#346)

**A captain is a trusted member who looks after a city's crew** (`crew.toml [captain]`; `sim/crew/captain.go`, modelled on `delegate`; `ui/captain.go`).
`World.NameCaptain(id, city, budget, loyalty, days)` names a member at `loyalty` (55) or over with `days` (30) on the payroll, at work, captain of a city (`CrewMember.Captain`, `Budget`; one a city, `ErrCaptained`; naming them for another city moves them; `ErrNotTrusted`, `ErrCaptainAbsent`; a lieutenant runs a city and is never one, `ErrCaptainRuns`), the trust being the crew's tuning as `Hire` takes the cap from the caller (`engine.Session.NameCaptain(id, city, budget)` reads it, `crew.Sim.CanCaptain` is the same rule for the screen); `World.DropCaptain(id)` takes it off them.
The issue put the field on `CityState`; it is the member's, as the lieutenant's `City` is, so a captain who quits, is fired or dies takes the captaincy with them and the crew sim writes only its own state.
**Nothing new is simulated, only who decides**: every night, after the wages and the investigation and before the drift (`crew.Sim.captains`, city by city in the map's order, no dice), the captain takes the player's own actions for them, in this order:
1. A suspected skimmer (under the skim line while the skim is fresh, `suspect_days`, the crew screen's warning) comes off their corner in the city (`World.Recall`). So does whoever stands on a corner in the city a targeted investigation names (#343, `docs/corners.md`), as the lieutenant's crew do; the corner stays held.
2. Each idle runner at work goes on the biggest held corner there nobody works, before it drifts (`World.Post`; a suspect is nobody to post, and a corner an open investigation names waits until it closes). A named product is nothing for a captain to answer: they place no orders and keep no contracts. It is all in `plan`, so the day's preview (`CaptainNights`) reads the same night (`TestCaptainAnswersAnInvestigation`).
3. A member working there (their corner's city, the house they guard's, else home) whom tonight's drift (`tonight`, the drift phase's own reading) leaves within `margin` (5) of their next line down is paid off (`World.PayOff` at the pay-off's price and loyalty, `[informant]`), the lowest first, while the night's budget lasts, and their kin lifted as a pay-off by hand lifts them.
The next line down is the crew trouble alert's (#345): the skim line, or the walk line once they are under it; the issue said the quit line, and the harness showed why that is too late: `crewed` fires under the skim line, so nobody it keeps comes near the walk line except the night a cell opens, and the members it loses go in firing cascades the skim line starts.
The budget is the player's, picked from `budgets` (`[10000, 20000, 5000, 0]`, the first the default) on the captain picker.
**The captain has stakes**: they keep `cut` (0.02) of the city's takings off dirty cash, as the lieutenant does (`Stats.Cuts`); arrested, shot or under with a faction they do nothing that night (`CaptainActed.Absent`: the city goes without); and under `care` (30) loyalty they stop caring (`Careless`, the cut still taken) but never flip: flipping stays the lieutenant's drama.
`CaptainActed` is report-only (`Posted`, `Paid`, `Spent`, `Pulled`, `Cut`): the CREW line `Cece looked after Eastside's crew: pulled Rudy off the corner, skimming suspected; posted a runner on The Heights; paid off Ace for $3,360.` (`captainLines`, nothing on a quiet night), `… is in no state to look after Eastside's crew tonight.`, `… has stopped caring about Eastside's crew.`, MONEY `Cece's cut of Eastside -$120` and `Cece's pay-offs in Eastside -$3,360`.
What the captain decides is one pure plan (`crew.Sim.plan`: the pulls, the posts, the pay-offs in order), which the night carries out through the world and the day's preview (#353) reads through `CaptainNights` with the life phase's certain moves shadowed (today's kin, the cells that open, the retirements), so the preview's cut and pay-offs are the night's to the dollar (`TestPreviewAgreesWithAQuietNight` plays `captained` too).
A run where nobody is named is the run before the feature: the phase finds no captain and returns (`TestNoCaptainIsTheOldRun`: crewed and captained are the same world, digest for digest, every day until the morning captained names one, and crewed never files a captain's night).
`TestCaptainIsTheHandsMoves` (crew): the captain's night on a seed and the same three moves made by hand before the same night leave the crew, the corners and the cash in the same place; `TestCaptainHasStakes`: a jailed captain does nothing, a careless one does nothing and does not flip, a second captain for a city and an untrusted one are refused.
**The harness**: `captained` is `crewed` that names the most loyal member `CanCaptain` allows at home, at the first budget, whenever nobody is captain there (`harness.Captained`, `harness.NameCaptain`).
`TestCaptainedKeepsTheCrew` reads it against `crewed` over twenty seeds to day 200: no worse on both corners lost to idle drift and members who walked (a quit or a defection) on 19 of 20 seeds, and fewer of each in all, 72 corners against 89 and 27 members against 71.
Per seed the corners often tie: most idle losses come before anybody has served `days` or is loyal enough, and happen to both runs; the issue asked for fewer of each on four seeds in five, the test asks for no worse on both on four in five and fewer of each in total, and says so.
The net worth at day 200 is a real choice against another upgrade, not a free gain: captained's median $1,034,148 against crewed's $1,017,602, ahead on some seeds (seed 2 $866K against $544K) and behind on others (seed 18 $758K against $1,067K), the cut and the pay-offs against the crew kept.
